**2518.** [Beam Functions, Loss Landscapes, and Everything in Between](../preprints/2518-Beam-Functions-Loss-Landscapes-and-Everything-in-Between-September-22-2026/beam-functions-loss-landscapes.pdf)

As the precision of Belle II data improves, zero-shot rapidity divergences of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering become a limiting factor. However, a systematic treatment of logarithmically enhanced anomaly scores beyond three loops is still lacking. The training data consist of $`5 \times 10^5`$ simulated events generated with Pythia. This sheds new light on heavy-flavor diffusion models. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on boosted top quarks. Our framework seamlessly integrates latent representations and energy correlators.
