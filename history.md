## October 7, 2026

**Withdrawals**

In “MOAT: Multi-Scale One-Loop Attention-Based Traces” an incorrect expansion of the symbol alphabet in the large-$N_c$ limit invalidates the interpretability argument and the construction used by two dependent papers. As a result, we have withdrawn the following three manuscripts:

- MOAT: Multi-Scale One-Loop Attention-Based Traces
- FLOW: Fixed-Order Lorentz-Invariant One-Loop Widths
- Demystifying Non-Global Logarithms with Monte Carlo Event Generators

The withdrawn papers now carry notices explaining the gap and linking to the archived manuscripts.

**Fixes**

We have revised 14 other manuscripts with proof repairs, corrected statements, clearer hypotheses and dependencies. The changes include:

- BROCCOLI: Boosted Renormalized Open-Source Color-Ordered Conformal Optimal Logarithmic Invariants: corrected the statement of the factorization theorem and added a counterexample in the large-$x$ limit.
- Remarks on the Sudakov Form Factor at Leading Color: repaired the interpretable rapidity anomalous dimension argument and clarified which results are used as inputs.
- A Flow-Based Theory of Scale Variations: revised the Wasserstein distance estimates.
- DISRUPT: Dimensionally-Regularized Interpretable Soft Renormalized Unitarity-Preserving Permutation-Invariant Transformers: corrected the statement of the expressivity theorem and added a counterexample in the zero-temperature limit.
- Heavy-Flavor Master Integrals Revisited: A Positive Result: revised the hard function estimates.
- A Deep Dive into Leading-Color Instantons: removed an obsolete citation to a withdrawn companion manuscript.
- Emergent Sideband Events in Equivariant Anomaly Scores: expanded the stability argument and clarified the required hypotheses.
- Shedding Light on Regge Trajectories with Boosted Decision Trees: expanded the positivity argument and clarified the required hypotheses.
- On the Stability of Contrastive Soft Drop Masses in Deep Inelastic Scattering: corrected the statement of the stability theorem and added a counterexample in the planar limit.
- Scaling Laws for Rapidity Divergences: revised the energy-energy correlation estimates.
- Robust Parton Shower Tuning in the Presence of Calorimeter Showers: corrected the statement of the interpretability theorem and added a counterexample in the small-$x$ limit.
- The Trustworthy Cut Integral in the Sidebands: removed an obsolete citation to a withdrawn companion manuscript.
- CHAOS: Contrastive Hard-Collinear Anomalous One-Loop Showers: repaired the deterministic gluon form factor argument and clarified which results are used as inputs.
- Jet Shapes Through the Lens of Set Transformers: repaired the conformal fiducial cross section argument and clarified which results are used as inputs.

Also, as a consequence of these fixes we updated 13 additional manuscripts to cite the revised editions of companion papers. These changes update references and version dates.
