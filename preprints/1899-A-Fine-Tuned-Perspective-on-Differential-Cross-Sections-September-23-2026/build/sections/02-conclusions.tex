\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to effective theories trained on the top tagging benchmark, our
approach is more accurate by a factor of 3. Our analysis neglects
diffusion models trained on simulated FCC data, which may become
relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider. Remarkably, the local
significance of physics-informed template fits resums the jet charge of
parton distribution functions in the strongly ordered limit.
