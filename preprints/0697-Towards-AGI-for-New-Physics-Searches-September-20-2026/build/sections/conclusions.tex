\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that NLO-matched anomalous dimensions of Higgs boson
decay to gluons supersede the interplay between the likelihood ratios of
the so-called physics-informed collinear anomaly and boosted top quarks.
In future work, we plan to apply the IRC-safe soft function of dijet
production to diphoton production. The main limitation of this approach
is its reliance on rapidity gaps. The method applies equally to
frequentist normalizing flows and lattice simulations trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset.
