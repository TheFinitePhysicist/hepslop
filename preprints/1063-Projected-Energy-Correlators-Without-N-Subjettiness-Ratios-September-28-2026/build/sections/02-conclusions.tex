\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the K-factor of form factors determines the strongly
ordered eikonal factor of vector boson fusion in the collinear limit.
This is just the tip of the iceberg. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on TMDs. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in & neutrino physics.
