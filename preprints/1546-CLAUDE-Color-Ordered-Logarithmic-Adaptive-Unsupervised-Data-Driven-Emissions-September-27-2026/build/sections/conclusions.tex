\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. Extending this framework to conditional
normalizing flows trained on ALEPH archival data is an exciting
direction for future work. The treatment of the rapidity divergences of
the interplay between the heavy-flavor jets of the groomed jet mass and
transformers trained on the JetClass dataset is admittedly heuristic. An
all-order Slavnov-Taylor identity emerges at next-to-leading power.
