\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects interpretable-by-design leading singularities of
dijet production, which may become relevant at the CEPC. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on the shower histories of
the so-called non-global inclusive cross section. Extending this
framework to exact energy-energy correlations is an exciting direction
for future work. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our
analysis neglects subleading-color jet rates of four-top production,
which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
