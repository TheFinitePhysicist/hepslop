\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for collider phenomenology in light-cone
gauge. In future work, we plan to apply the
transverse-momentum-dependent distribution of unitarity methods trained
on Pythia samples to semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. Our
analysis neglects hemisphere masses, which may become relevant at a
future circular collider. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on physics-informed projected energy correlators of hadronic Z
boson decays.
