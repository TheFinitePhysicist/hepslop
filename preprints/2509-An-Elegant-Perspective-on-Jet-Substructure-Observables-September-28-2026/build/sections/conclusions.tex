\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of light-flavor Ward identities of trijet production is
admittedly heuristic. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on boosted top quarks. We believe this constitutes a paradigm
shift in top-quark physics. A natural next step is to match Glauber
modes without any additional assumptions. In future work, we plan to
apply the perturbative cusp anomalous dimension to deep inelastic
scattering.
