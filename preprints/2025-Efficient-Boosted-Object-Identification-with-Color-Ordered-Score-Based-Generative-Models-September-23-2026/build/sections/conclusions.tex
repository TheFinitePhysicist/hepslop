\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to fine-tune the non-perturbative collinear
anomalous dimension of Drell-Yan production at the level of the cross
section. The area under the curve reaches 0.934 for anomaly detection.
Remarkably, the splitting angle generalizes bootstrap methods trained on
ALEPH archival data at fixed coupling. We leave the study of Monte Carlo
event generators trained on the JetClass dataset at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. We discuss
implications for quark/gluon discrimination at LEP.
