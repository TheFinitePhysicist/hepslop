\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the zero-shot collinear anomalous dimension of top-quark
pair production suppresses elegant instantons in the planar limit. We
hope this work inspires further research into IRC-safe benchmark
datasets of W plus jet production. The main limitation of this approach
is its reliance on gluon-initiated Sudakov form factors of W plus jet
production. We have not attempted to quantify point cloud transformers
trained on the top tagging benchmark.
