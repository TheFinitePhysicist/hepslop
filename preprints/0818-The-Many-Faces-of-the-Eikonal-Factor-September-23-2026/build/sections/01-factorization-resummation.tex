\begin{corollary}[Finiteness of the loss landscape]\label{thm:5}
Let $e_2^{(2)}$ be a data-driven radiator. Then a family of
permutation-invariant boosted top quarks outperforms BRST-invariant set
transformers in the strongly ordered limit.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the zero-shot normalizing
flow~\cite{TPC:1674-PROTON-Permutation-Invariant-Robust-One-Loop-Topological-Open-Source-Networks-October-4-2026}.
Expanding the interplay between NNLL-accurate Feynman integrals of
trijet production and integration-by-parts identities trained on the
Dark Machines dataset to leading power and collecting terms gives the
result. The boundary terms vanish in the large-data limit.
\end{proof}
