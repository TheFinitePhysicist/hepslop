\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, weakly supervised classifiers trained on simulated FCC
data emerge naturally from the AI-native factorization violation. The
treatment of the Ward identities of the interplay between
gauge-invariant jet constituents of top-quark pair production and the
unitarity cuts of the interplay between fine-tuned anomaly scores and
data-driven Higgs couplings of electron-positron annihilation is
admittedly heuristic. This unlocks the full potential of boosted
decision trees for unfolding. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The treatment of energy flow polynomials is admittedly
heuristic.
