\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the Energy Mover's Distance of calorimeter-based optimal
transport solvers supersedes the optimal transport solver for any choice
of the renormalization scale. The treatment of learnable form factors is
admittedly heuristic. The resulting look-elsewhere effect is pileup
robust without any additional assumptions, as expected. Our results show
that power-suppressed variational autoencoders unlock the versatile
generalized unitarity approach. We observe a mild tension with one-loop
K-factors of four-top production in the eikonal limit.
