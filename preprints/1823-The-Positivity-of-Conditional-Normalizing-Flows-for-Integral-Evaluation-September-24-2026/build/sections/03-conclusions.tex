\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of cut integrals is admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the
AI-native running coupling differs from the gauge-covariant splitting
function by less than a part in a thousand for arbitrary jet algorithms.
The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We hope this work inspires further research into the latent
representations of the so-called interpretable anomalous dimension
matrix. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on theory
uncertainties.
