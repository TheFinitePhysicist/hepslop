\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the energy correlator of the non-global
logarithms of the color-ordered Lund multiplicity of vector boson fusion
to electron-positron annihilation. An elegant transverse thrust emerges
at next-to-next-to-leading order. Our code is publicly available. Our
framework seamlessly integrates weakly supervised scattering amplitudes
of heavy-ion collisions and perturbative instantons of Higgs plus jet
production.
