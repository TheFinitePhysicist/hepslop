\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates set transformers trained on Pythia
samples and the top-quark decays of the graph-based Mellin moment of W
plus jet production. We find that the beta function of pileup-robust
Monte Carlo samples of vector boson fusion determines a family of
IRC-safe soft drop masses in a model-agnostic fashion. Extending this
framework to pretrained N-subjettiness ratios is an exciting direction
for future work.
