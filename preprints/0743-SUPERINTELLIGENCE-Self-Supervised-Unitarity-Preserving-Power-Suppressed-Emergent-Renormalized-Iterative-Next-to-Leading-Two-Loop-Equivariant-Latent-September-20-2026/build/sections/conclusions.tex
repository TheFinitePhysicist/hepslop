\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects subleading-color beam functions, which may become
relevant at the LHC. We find that the classifier-based reweighting
enhances generative template fits in the planar limit. In future work,
we plan to apply a family of likelihood-free Monte Carlo samples to
Higgs plus jet production. We discuss implications for anomaly detection
at the High-Luminosity LHC.
