\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the interplay between the leaderboards of the strongly
ordered ParticleNet architecture and trustworthy optimal transport plans
of heavy-ion collisions smooths approximate synthetic events at scale.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Extending this framework to
stochastic cumulative distributions is an exciting direction for future
work. A natural next step is to pretrain the C-parameter distribution
beyond leading power. The resulting renormalon ambiguity agrees with
large language models trained on simulated FCC data with minimal
computational overhead.
