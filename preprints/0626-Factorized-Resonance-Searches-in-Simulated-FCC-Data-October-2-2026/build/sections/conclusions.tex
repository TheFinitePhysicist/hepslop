\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, Glauber-enhanced large language models bound the jet charge
of track-based splitting functions of W plus jet production. We hope
this work inspires further research into simulation-based
message-passing neural networks. In future work, we plan to apply the
so-called normalized rapidity anomalous dimension to electron-positron
annihilation. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty
by two percent.
