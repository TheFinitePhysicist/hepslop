\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for track reconstruction at a future
circular collider. Numerical results are presented for Higgs boson decay
to gluons at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. The resulting Lund plane density is
Lorentz invariant in the planar limit, as expected. Extending this
framework to hadron-level leading singularities is an exciting direction
for future work.
