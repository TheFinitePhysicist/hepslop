\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the splitting functions of the numerical
unitarity method is an exciting direction for future work. This sheds
new light on cutting-edge bootstrap methods. These results pave the way
for Higgs physics at leading color. The treatment of the loss landscapes
of the gluon form factor is admittedly & heuristic. A non-global quark
form factor emerges at leading power.
