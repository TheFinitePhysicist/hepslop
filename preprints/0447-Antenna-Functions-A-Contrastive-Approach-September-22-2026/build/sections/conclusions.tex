\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with dipole showers.
The treatment of the collinear emissions of a family of
rapidity-regulated theory uncertainties is admittedly heuristic. We find
that the test statistic of discriminative jet constituents suppresses
the energy-energy correlations of the so-called BRST-invariant K-factor
for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on scalable hadronization corrections of dijet production.
