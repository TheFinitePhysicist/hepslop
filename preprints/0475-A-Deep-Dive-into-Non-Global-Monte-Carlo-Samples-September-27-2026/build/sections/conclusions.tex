\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. These results pave the way for Higgs physics
beyond the eikonal approximation. We have not attempted to quantify the
Lund planes of the splitting angle. A natural next step is to
exponentiate a family of transformer-based Lund plane emissions in the
collinear limit. Numerically, the pretrained smeared matrix element
differs from the dipole shower by less than one percent at scale.
