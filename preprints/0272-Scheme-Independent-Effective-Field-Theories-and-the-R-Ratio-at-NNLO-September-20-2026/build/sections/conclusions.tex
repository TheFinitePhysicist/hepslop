\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The fragmentation function exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$. Our
analysis neglects smeared N-subjettiness ratios, which may become
relevant at HERA. Our findings open new avenues for jet substructure at
HERA. This work paves the way for a new generation of unitarity methods.
This sheds new light on interpretable sphalerons.
