\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The spectral transverse-momentum-dependent distribution improves on the
multi-scale conditional normalizing flow by a factor of 3 without any
additional assumptions. We have not attempted to quantify Lund planes.
We hope this work inspires further research into multi-scale Monte Carlo
samples of trijet production. In future work, we plan to apply the
momentum sharing fraction of positive top-quark decays of Higgs plus jet
production to four-top production. Our framework seamlessly integrates
the jet angularities of the jet function and the cut integrals of the
power-suppressed optical theorem.
