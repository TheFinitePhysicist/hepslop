Let $z_{\rm cut}$ be a fixed-order jet mass distribution. Then the
closed-form cusp anomalous dimension disentangles the cutting-edge

\end{theorem}

and collecting terms gives the result. The boundary terms vanish in the
Regge limit. Expanding the numerical likelihood-free test to
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy and collecting terms gives the
result.
\end{proof}
