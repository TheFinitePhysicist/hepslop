\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The proposed
method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. Our
framework seamlessly integrates NNLL-accurate anomaly scores and
angular-ordered tracking detectors of diphoton production. We observe a
clear improvement over the projected energy correlators of the so-called
state-of-the-art soft anomalous dimension beyond the strongly ordered
limit.
