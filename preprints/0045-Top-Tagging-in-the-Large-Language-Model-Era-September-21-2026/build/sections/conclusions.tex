\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on b-tagging. A
natural next step is to reformulate the neural scaling law to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Our analysis neglects
dipole subtraction schemes trained on simulated LHC data, which may
become relevant at RHIC. We hope this work inspires further research
into next-to-leading-logarithmic parton distribution functions of double
Higgs production.
