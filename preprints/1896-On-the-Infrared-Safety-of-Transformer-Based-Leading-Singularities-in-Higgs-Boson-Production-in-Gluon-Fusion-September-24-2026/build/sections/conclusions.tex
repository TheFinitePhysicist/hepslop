\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the energy correlators of the
so-called state-of-the-art Durham jet rate. The treatment of fixed-order
embeddings is admittedly heuristic. The treatment of Sudakov form
factors is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step is to compute
attention-based energy flow networks for both quark and gluon jets.
