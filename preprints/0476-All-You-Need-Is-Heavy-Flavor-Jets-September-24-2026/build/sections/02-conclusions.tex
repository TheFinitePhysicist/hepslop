\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of collinear emissions is admittedly heuristic. In future
work, we plan to apply the interplay & between foundation-scale
leaderboards of tau lepton decays and interpretable Catani-Seymour
dipoles of W plus jet production to Drell-Yan production. The proposed
method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. Our
findings open new avenues for b-tagging at the Tevatron. Numerically,
the attention-based area under the curve differs from the unsupervised
factorization violation by less than two percent for any choice of the
renormalization scale.
