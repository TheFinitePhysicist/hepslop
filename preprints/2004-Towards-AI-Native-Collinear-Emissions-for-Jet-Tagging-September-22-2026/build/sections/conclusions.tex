\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to bootstrap the elegant soft anomalous dimension
of B meson decays in the large-$N_c$ limit. Compared to explainable
zero-bin subtractions, our approach is more stable by a factor of 2.
This work paves the way for a new generation of Monte Carlo event
generators. The area under the curve reaches 0.942 for integral
evaluation.
