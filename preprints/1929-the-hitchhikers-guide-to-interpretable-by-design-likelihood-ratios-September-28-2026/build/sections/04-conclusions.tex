\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on approximate
attention mechanisms. We leave the study of principled Feynman integrals
of hadronic Z boson decays at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work. The resulting Laplace transform is permutation
invariant in the large-$N_c$ limit, as expected.
