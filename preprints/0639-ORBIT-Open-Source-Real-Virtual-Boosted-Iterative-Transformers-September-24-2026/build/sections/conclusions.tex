\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the jet broadening to Drell-Yan
production. Numerically, the hierarchical JADE three-jet rate differs
from the AI-native Glauber phase by less than one percent in the planar
limit. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by a
part in a thousand. We leave the study of gauge-covariant optimal
transport solvers at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work.
