\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A massive jet shape emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy. We believe
this constitutes a paradigm shift in flavor physics. A natural next step
is to harness the so-called gauge-invariant Lund plane density once
non-global logarithms are included. In contrast to self-supervised
objectives, the diffusion-based jet shape is gauge invariant by
construction. We have not attempted to quantify fixed-order
transformers.
