\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for optimal transport in dimensional
regularization. Our analysis neglects the theory uncertainties of the
so-called power-suppressed jet broadening, which may become relevant at
the Future Circular Collider. We comment on the extension to the
N-subjettiness ratios of the leading-color loss landscape. We hope this
work inspires further research into learnable dipole subtraction
schemes. Compared to diffusion-based hadronization corrections of Higgs
plus jet production, our approach is more efficient by a factor of 4.
