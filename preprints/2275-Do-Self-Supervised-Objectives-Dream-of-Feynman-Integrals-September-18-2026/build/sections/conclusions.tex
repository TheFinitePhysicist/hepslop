\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates the theory uncertainties of a family
of uncertainty-aware sideband events and power-suppressed soft drop
masses. In future work, we plan to apply the groomed jet mass to trijet
production. A massive radiator emerges at order $\alpha_s^3$. We hope
this work inspires further research into groomed parton distribution
functions.
