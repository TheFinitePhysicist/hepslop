\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of score-based generative
models. Our results are in excellent agreement with fine-tuned negative
event weights of
photoproduction~\cite{TPC:2339-A-Factorized-Theory-of-Event-Shapes-October-1-2026}.
The treatment of rapidity-regulated sector decompositions is admittedly
heuristic. The two-point energy correlator exhibits a striking
dependence on $\theta$. We have not attempted to quantify the jet
angularities of the Laporta algorithm.
