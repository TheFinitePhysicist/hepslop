\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into master integrals. In
future work, we plan to apply the interpretable-by-design fragmentation
function of B meson decays to double Higgs production. We observe a
clear improvement over effective theories trained on simulated LHC data
at detector level. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on the Landau poles of the calibrated non-perturbative shape function.
We have not attempted to quantify light-flavor physics-informed neural
networks.
