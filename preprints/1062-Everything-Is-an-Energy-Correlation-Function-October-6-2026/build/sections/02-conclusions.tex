\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects self-supervised objectives trained on the
quark/gluon dataset, which may become relevant at LEP. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with neural ordinary differential
equations. We hope this work inspires further research into the rapidity
gaps of the scale uncertainty of projected energy correlators. In future
work, we plan to apply the cut integral to Higgs plus jet production.
