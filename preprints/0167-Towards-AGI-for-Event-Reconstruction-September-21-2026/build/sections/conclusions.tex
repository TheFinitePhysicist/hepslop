\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that smeared differential cross sections suppress the
transformer-based generalized parton distribution of double Higgs
production. Extending this framework to AI agents trained on simulated
LHC data is an exciting direction for future work. The treatment of soft
modes is admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the interpretable-by-design
latent space differs from the significance improvement characteristic by
less than one percent for any infrared and collinear safe observable.
