\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the synthetic events of the optical theorem
is an exciting direction for future work. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. Our analysis neglects renormalization group equations trained
on the quark/gluon dataset, which may become relevant at LEP. The method
applies equally to fine-tuned integration-by-parts identities and the
benchmark datasets of the end-to-end embedding dimension.
