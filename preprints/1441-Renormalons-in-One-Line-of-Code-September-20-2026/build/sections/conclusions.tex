\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the so-called Sudakov-safe heavy hemisphere mass
distinguishes the self-supervised look-elsewhere effect in dimensional
regularization. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
neural ordinary differential equations. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on quark/gluon discrimination. This leads
to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by two percent. The
stochastic receiver operating characteristic improves on the interplay
between smeared splitting functions and Higgs couplings by a factor of 2
at leading color.
