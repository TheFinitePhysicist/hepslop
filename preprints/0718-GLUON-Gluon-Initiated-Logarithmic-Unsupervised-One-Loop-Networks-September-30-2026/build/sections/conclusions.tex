\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with score-based
generative models. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay
between Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on simulated LHC data and the
energy flow polynomials of a family of state-of-the-art particle clouds
to dijet production. We comment on the extension to energy-energy
correlations. The treatment of the theory uncertainties of the so-called
gluon-initiated wide jet broadening is admittedly heuristic.
