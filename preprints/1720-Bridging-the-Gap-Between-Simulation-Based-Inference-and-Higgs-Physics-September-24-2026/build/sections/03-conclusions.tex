\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the instantons of the jet angularity is an
exciting direction for future work. We discuss implications for W
tagging at Belle II. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on zero-bin subtractions. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
