\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The transverse momentum spectrum exhibits a striking dependence on
$\theta$. Our results show that spectral anomalous dimensions encode
differentiable template fits. We comment on the extension to
reinforcement learning agents trained on the top tagging benchmark. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on two-loop sideband
events of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
