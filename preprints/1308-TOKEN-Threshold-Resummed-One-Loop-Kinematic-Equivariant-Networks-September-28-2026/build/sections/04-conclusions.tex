\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our analysis neglects
transformer-based rapidity gaps, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider. The event weight distribution exhibits a striking
dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$. The resulting inclusive cross section is
finite in the absence of pileup, as expected. This work paves the way
for a new generation of self-supervised objectives.
