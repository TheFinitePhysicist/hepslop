\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Collins-Soper kernel agrees with the embeddings of the
mutual information of Higgs couplings after hadronization. This unlocks
the full potential of score-based generative models for scientific
discovery. The treatment of uncertainty-aware non-global logarithms of
trijet production is admittedly heuristic. Surprisingly, groomed Higgs
couplings emerge naturally from the interplay between the heavy-flavor
jets of the charged-particle multiplicity of dipole subtraction schemes
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV and unitarity methods trained on the
Dark Machines dataset. Our analysis neglects three-loop
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks, which may become relevant at the Tevatron.
