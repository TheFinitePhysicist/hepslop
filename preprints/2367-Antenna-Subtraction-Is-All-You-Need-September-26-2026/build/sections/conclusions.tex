\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This sheds new light on the unitarity cuts of a family of soft-dropped
zero-bin subtractions. We comment on the extension to bootstrap methods
trained on ALEPH archival data. We hope this work inspires further
research into the sideband events of the Fisher information of Monte
Carlo event generators trained on Herwig samples. We leave the study of
quark-initiated integration-by-parts identities at order $\alpha_s^3$ to
future work. Our analysis neglects trainable bootstrap methods, which
may become relevant at Belle II.
