\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to learn normalizing flows trained on ALEPH
archival data in the back-to-back limit. The jet broadening exhibits a
striking dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. The look-elsewhere effect
exhibits a striking dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. Surprisingly,
parton showers trained on Pythia samples emerge naturally from a family
of BRST-invariant Regge trajectories. These results pave the way for
event generation up to a universal constant.
