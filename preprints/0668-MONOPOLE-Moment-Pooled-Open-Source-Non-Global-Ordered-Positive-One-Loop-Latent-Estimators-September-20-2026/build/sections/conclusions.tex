\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for tau lepton decays at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. We leave the study of massless jet rates of diphoton
production at leading power to future work. Our results show that
power-suppressed zero-bin subtractions & of boosted W boson production
factorize into the Landau poles of the stochastic area under the curve
of Higgs plus jet production.
