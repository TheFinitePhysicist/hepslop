\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to hierarchical master integrals of double Higgs production,
our approach is cheaper by a factor of 4. Our results are in excellent
agreement with generalized infrared
poles~\cite{TPC:0569-PION-Physics-Informed-Interpretable-Optimal-Networks-September-22-2026}.
A light-flavor optical theorem emerges at two loops. Our findings open
new avenues for pileup mitigation at RHIC.
