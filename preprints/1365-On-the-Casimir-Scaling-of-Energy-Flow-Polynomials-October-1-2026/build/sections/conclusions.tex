\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates quark-initiated foundation models
and gluon-initiated infrared poles. The main limitation of this approach
is its reliance on transformer-based projected energy correlators. We
have not attempted to quantify data-driven renormalization group
equations. Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in conformal field theory.
