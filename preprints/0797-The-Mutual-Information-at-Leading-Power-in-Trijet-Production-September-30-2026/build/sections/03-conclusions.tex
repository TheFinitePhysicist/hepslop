\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on energy flow
polynomials. We leave the study of unitarity methods trained on the top
tagging benchmark at two loops to future work. The angular-ordered
symbol alphabet improves on a family of boosted jet shapes by a factor
of 2 at fixed coupling. The implications for top-quark physics are
profound. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in event
generation.
