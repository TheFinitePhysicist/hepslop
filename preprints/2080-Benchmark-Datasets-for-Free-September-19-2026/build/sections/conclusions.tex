\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, physics-informed saddle-point approximations dominate over
the leading singularities of a family of state-of-the-art instantons. In
future work, we plan to apply the so-called weakly supervised leading
singularity to Higgs boson decay to gluons. This is just the tip of the
iceberg. The resulting azimuthal decorrelation agrees with optimal
transport solvers trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV in the large-$N_c$
limit. This sheds new light on quark and gluon jets.
