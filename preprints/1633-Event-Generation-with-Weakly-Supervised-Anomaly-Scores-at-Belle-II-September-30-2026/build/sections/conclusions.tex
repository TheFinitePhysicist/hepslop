\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. We leave the study of
Lorentz-equivariant normalizing flows at leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. Our analysis neglects Glauber-enhanced jet substructure
observables, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. In
future work, we plan to apply the multi-jet merging procedure to
Drell-Yan production.
