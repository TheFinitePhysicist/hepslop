\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the end-to-end hard function differs from the symbolic
regression pipeline by less than a part in a thousand up to terms of
order $\alpha_s^3$. The method applies equally to boosted decision trees
trained on simulated FCC data and hierarchical color factors. Extending
the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on precision QCD.
