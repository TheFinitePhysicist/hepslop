\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to boost-invariant attention weights of
four-top production and the instantons of the so-called power-suppressed
master integral. We hope this work inspires further research into
heavy-flavor jets. The nuisance parameter exhibits a striking dependence
on $e_2^{(2)}$. The treatment of renormalization group equations trained
on the top tagging benchmark is admittedly heuristic. Our code is
publicly available.
