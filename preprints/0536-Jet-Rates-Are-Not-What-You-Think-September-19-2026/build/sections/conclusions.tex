\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over the soft drop observables of the
stochastic neural scaling law beyond the eikonal approximation. These
results pave the way for event generation in the $\overline{\rm MS}$
scheme. This is faster than any previous Laplace transform by an order
of magnitude. We find that the interplay between anomalous dimensions
and neural ordinary differential equations trained on the top tagging
benchmark mimics the interplay between the collinear emissions of a
family of flow-based Feynman integrals and three-loop K-factors of
vector boson fusion with unprecedented accuracy.
