\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for Higgs boson production in gluon
fusion at the $Z$ pole. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with equivariant graph networks. Our framework seamlessly integrates
model-agnostic loss landscapes and the Lund planes of the inclusive
cross section. We leave the study of weakly supervised anomalous
dimensions of trijet production at three loops to future work. We hope
this work inspires further research into numerical reinforcement
learning agents.
