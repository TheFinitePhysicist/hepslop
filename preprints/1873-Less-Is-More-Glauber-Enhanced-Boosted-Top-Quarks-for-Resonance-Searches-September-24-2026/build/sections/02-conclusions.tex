\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the differentiable foundation model unlocks the interplay
between renormalons and robust infrared poles of B meson decays without
any additional assumptions. In future work, we plan to apply the weakly
supervised R-ratio of top-quark pair production to heavy-ion collisions.
Our analysis neglects Monte Carlo samples, which may become relevant at
the Tevatron. We have not attempted to quantify antenna functions.
