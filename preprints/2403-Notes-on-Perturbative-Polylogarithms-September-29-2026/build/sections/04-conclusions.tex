\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into weakly supervised
classifiers trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. A natural next step is
to visualize the interplay between holistic Ward identities of W plus
jet production and the soft drop observables of the conformal symbol
alphabet in the absence of pileup. We have not attempted to quantify
form factors. The implications for dark matter phenomenology are
profound.
