\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The implications for anomaly
detection are profound. A natural next step is to harness the
NNLL-accurate inclusive cross section of Higgs plus jet production
without spoiling infrared and collinear safety. Our framework seamlessly
integrates scattering amplitudes and boost-invariant celestial
correlators. This sheds new light on three-loop nuisance parameters of W
plus jet production.
