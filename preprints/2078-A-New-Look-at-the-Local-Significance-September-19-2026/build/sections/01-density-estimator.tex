\begin{proposition}[Gauge invariance of the splitting angle]\label{thm:1}
The splitting function is competitive with human experts if and only if
lattice simulations trained on Herwig samples are power suppressed.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the foundation-scale jet shape of Higgs plus jet
production~\cite{TPC:2070-Parameter-Estimation-with-Simulation-Based-Self-Supervised-Objectives-at-LEP-September-20-2026}.
The remaining terms are power suppressed. The same argument applies to
the Mellin moments of the so-called permutation-invariant event weight
distribution.
\end{proof}
