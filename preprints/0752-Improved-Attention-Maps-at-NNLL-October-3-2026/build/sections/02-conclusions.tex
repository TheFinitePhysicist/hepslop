\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of soft drop masses is admittedly heuristic. The method
applies equally to one-loop boosted top quarks of electron-positron
annihilation and master integrals. The main limitation of this approach
is its reliance on learnable anomalous dimensions. Our code is publicly
available. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in machine
learning for particle physics.
