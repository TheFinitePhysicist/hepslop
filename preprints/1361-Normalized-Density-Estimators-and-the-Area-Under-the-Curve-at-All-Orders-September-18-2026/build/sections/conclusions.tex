\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects score-based generative models trained on Herwig
samples, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. This
leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by two percent.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The energy-energy correlation
exhibits a striking dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$. The treatment of robust
cumulative distributions is admittedly heuristic.
