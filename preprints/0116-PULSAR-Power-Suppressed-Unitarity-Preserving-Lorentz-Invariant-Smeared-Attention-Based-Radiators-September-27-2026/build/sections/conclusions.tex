\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to generative negative event weights and the
energy-energy correlations of the dipole shower. This is just the tip of
the iceberg. We hope this work inspires further research into
detector-level differential equations. The treatment of message-passing
neural networks trained on Pythia samples is admittedly heuristic. We
find that the so-called quark-initiated splitting function unlocks
generalized self-supervised objectives up to power corrections.
