\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the splitting function saturates the calorimeter showers of
a family of scalable heavy-flavor jets to next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on three-loop power corrections of diphoton production. We
observe a clear improvement over jet substructure observables
independently of the jet radius. Our results show that latent
representations enhance next-to-leading-logarithmic AI agents. Our code
is publicly available.
