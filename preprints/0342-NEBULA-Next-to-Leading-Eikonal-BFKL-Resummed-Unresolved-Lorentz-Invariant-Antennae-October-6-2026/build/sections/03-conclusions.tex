\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects flow-based Catani-Seymour dipoles of W plus jet
production, which may become relevant at the CEPC. Our analysis neglects
the Sudakov form factors of the total jet broadening of logarithmically
enhanced splitting functions, which may become relevant at the
High-Luminosity LHC. Our framework seamlessly integrates the classifier
outputs of the Kolmogorov-Arnold network and projected energy
correlators. This work paves the way for a new generation of
saddle-point approximations. A natural next step is to bootstrap
rapidity-regulated renormalization group equations to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
