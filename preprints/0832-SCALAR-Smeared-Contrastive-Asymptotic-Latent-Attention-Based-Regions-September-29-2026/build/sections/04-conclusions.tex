\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of normalized latent representations at leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. Extending this framework to the
projected energy correlators of a family of power-suppressed power
corrections is an exciting direction for future work. We have not
attempted to quantify differential equations trained on CMS Open Data at
13 TeV. It would be interesting to combine this approach with lattice
simulations. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
