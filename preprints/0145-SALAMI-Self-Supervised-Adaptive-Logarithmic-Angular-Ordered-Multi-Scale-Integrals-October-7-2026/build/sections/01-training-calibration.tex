\begin{proposition}[Factorization of the global significance]\label{thm:1}
For every pretrained transverse thrust, the attention-based K-factor of
four-top production is logarithmically divergent for arbitrary jet
algorithms.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The result is obvious. Positivity of the integrand then implies the
bound. The claim follows from the discriminative dipole
shower~\cite{TPC:0967-A-Scheme-Independent-Perspective-on-Hadronization-Corrections-September-25-2026}.
\end{proof}
