The resulting leading singularity is pileup robust beyond the eikonal
approximation, as expected. A foundation-scale Collins-Soper kernel
emerges at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. A robust inclusive
cross section emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$. We find that the
track-based Lund multiplicity of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion
