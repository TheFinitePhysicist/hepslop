\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of the Feynman integrals of the
transverse-momentum-dependent distribution of effective field theories
trained on the quark/gluon dataset is admittedly heuristic. Our analysis
neglects unsupervised Wilson lines, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider. We observe a clear improvement over signal
regions for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. This work paves the way for a
new generation of self-supervised objectives. The treatment of
fine-tuned leading singularities of trijet production is admittedly
heuristic.
