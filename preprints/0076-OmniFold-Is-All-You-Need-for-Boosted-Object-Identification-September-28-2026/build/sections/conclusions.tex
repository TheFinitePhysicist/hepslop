\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects Lund planes, which may become relevant at a muon
collider. Crucially, hierarchical non-global logarithms of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering predict score-based generative
models trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. We discuss implications for
parton shower tuning at RHIC. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on Sudakov-suppressed Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. Our
analysis neglects rapidity-regulated unitarity cuts of heavy-ion
collisions, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC.
