\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify tracking detectors. We leave the study
of attention mechanisms trained on the LHC Olympics dataset at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on model discovery. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. Extending the
method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
