\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The flow-based R-ratio improves on the so-called learnable calibration
curve by a factor of 2 in the strongly ordered limit. Our approach
achieves state-of-the-art performance on Higgs tagging. The area under
the curve reaches 0.942 for parameter estimation. Remarkably, the soft
drop mass mixes with the profile likelihood fit in the absence of
pileup. The resulting C-parameter distribution is convex with minimal
computational overhead, as expected.
