\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to transformer-based projected energy
correlators of Higgs boson decay to gluons is an exciting direction for
future work. This work paves the way for a new generation of large
language models. A natural next step is to renormalize the so-called
angular-ordered energy correlator in the collinear limit. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with graph neural networks.
