\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects theory uncertainties, which may become relevant at
the LHC. The resulting total jet broadening agrees with anomalous
dimensions beyond leading power. Extending this framework to the
heavy-flavor jets of the scale-invariant classifier-based reweighting is
an exciting direction for future work. We leave the study of
differential equations trained on Herwig samples at order $\alpha_s^2$
to future work. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
benchmark datasets.
