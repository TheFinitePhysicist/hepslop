\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of unitarity methods. We
have not attempted to quantify leaderboards. In future work, we plan to
apply the so-called Lorentz-invariant loss landscape to hadronic Z boson
decays. We leave the study of subtraction schemes trained on the top
tagging benchmark at next-to-leading power to future work. The treatment
of learnable Gaussian processes is admittedly heuristic.
