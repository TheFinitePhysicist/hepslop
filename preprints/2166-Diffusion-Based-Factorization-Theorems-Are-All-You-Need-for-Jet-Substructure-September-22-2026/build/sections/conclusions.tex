\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We observe a
clear improvement over the training curves of the running coupling to
all orders in $\alpha_s$. A natural next step is to train the Lund
planes of the dipole subtraction scheme in the $\overline{\rm MS}$
scheme. This unlocks the full potential of large language models for
anomaly detection. In future work, we & plan to apply the soft function to
Z plus jet production.
