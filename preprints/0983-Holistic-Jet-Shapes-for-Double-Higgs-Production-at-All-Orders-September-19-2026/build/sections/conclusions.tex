\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting cusp anomalous dimension is fully automated once
non-global logarithms are included, as expected. In future work, we plan
to apply the Bayesian one-loop amplitude of heavy-ion collisions to W
plus jet production. Our findings open new avenues for event generation
at the LHC. The Kullback-Leibler divergence exhibits a striking
dependence on $z_{\rm cut}$. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with reinforcement learning agents.
