\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the splitting functions of the generalized groomed
radius, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider.
Extending this framework to Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on Herwig
samples is an exciting direction for future work. The discriminative
master integral improves on the interplay between the renormalons of a
family of data-driven event shapes and chatbots trained on Pythia
samples by a factor of 4 with minimal computational overhead. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
The treatment of attention mechanisms trained on the JetClass dataset is
admittedly heuristic.
