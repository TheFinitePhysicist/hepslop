Unfortunately, the nuisance parameters of the so-called BRST-invariant
beam function are notoriously difficult to compute in the threshold
limit. As a byproduct, we obtain a multi-scale generalization of the
hemisphere mass difference for pileup mitigation. However, a systematic
treatment of principled theory uncertainties of Z plus jet production
beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is still lacking.
