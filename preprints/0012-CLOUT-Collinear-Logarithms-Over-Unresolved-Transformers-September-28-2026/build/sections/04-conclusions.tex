\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the robust C-parameter distribution differs from the
leading-color R-ratio by less than five percent at scale. Extending the
method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. Our analysis neglects perturbative cut
integrals, which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
