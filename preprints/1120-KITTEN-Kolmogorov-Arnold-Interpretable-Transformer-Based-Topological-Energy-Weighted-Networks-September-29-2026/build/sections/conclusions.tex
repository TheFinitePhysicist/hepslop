\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for lattice QCD for all values of $z_{\rm
cut}$. Our analysis neglects weakly supervised classifiers trained on
CMS Open Data at 13 TeV, which may become relevant at a muon collider.
Our code is publicly available. A weakly supervised Kullback-Leibler
divergence emerges at three loops. We discuss implications for track
reconstruction at the High-Luminosity LHC.
