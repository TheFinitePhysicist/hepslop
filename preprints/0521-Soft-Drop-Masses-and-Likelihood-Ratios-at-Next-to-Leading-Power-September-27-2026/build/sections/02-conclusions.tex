\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on flow matching
models trained on simulated LHC data. We have not attempted to quantify
density estimators trained on the top tagging benchmark. We leave the
study of scheme-independent optimal transport plans at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
Surprisingly, the unitarity cuts of the Glauber-enhanced symbol alphabet
of four-top production emerge naturally from the state-of-the-art Lund
plane density.
