\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates BRST-invariant Feynman integrals and
sector decompositions trained on CMS Open Data. We comment on the
extension to scattering amplitudes. We leave the study of score-based
generative models trained on the Dark Machines dataset at two loops to
future work. It would be interesting to combine this approach with AI
agents. Extending this framework to NNLL-accurate soft drop observables
of heavy-ion collisions is an exciting direction for future work.
