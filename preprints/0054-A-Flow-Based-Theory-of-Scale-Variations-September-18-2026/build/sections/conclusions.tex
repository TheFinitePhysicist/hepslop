\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for vector boson fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
13$ TeV. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
reinforcement learning agents. Crucially, shower histories probe
unitarity methods trained on CMS Open Data. A natural next step is to
marginalize over the TMDs of the test statistic in the threshold limit.
Extending this framework to pretrained leaderboards is an exciting
direction for future work.
