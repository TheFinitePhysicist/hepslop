\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to explainable likelihood ratios of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering and embeddings. Surprisingly,
attention mechanisms trained on the quark/gluon dataset emerge naturally
from the contrastive beam function. We have not attempted to quantify
autoregressive models trained on ALEPH archival data. The resulting jet
function is exactly solvable to arbitrary precision, as expected. This
sheds new light on unitarity-preserving optimal transport solvers.
