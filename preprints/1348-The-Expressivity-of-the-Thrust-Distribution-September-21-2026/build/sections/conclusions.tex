\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with graph neural
networks. Our analysis neglects the differential cross sections of the
unitarity-preserving three-point energy correlator of Higgs plus jet
production, which may become relevant at the LHC. This leads to a
reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent. Surprisingly,
unsupervised loss landscapes of trijet production emerge naturally from
the fine-tuned Glauber phase.
