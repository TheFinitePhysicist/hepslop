\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on stochastic
cumulative distributions of dijet production. Our analysis neglects
boosted top quarks, which may become relevant at HERA. A natural next
step is to harness Lorentz-invariant projected energy correlators of
deep inelastic scattering up to a universal & constant. We hope this work
inspires further research into theory uncertainties.
