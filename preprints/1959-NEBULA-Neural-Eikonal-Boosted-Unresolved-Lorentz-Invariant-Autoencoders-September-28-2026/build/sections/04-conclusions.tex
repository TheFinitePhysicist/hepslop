\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with foundation models.
Remarkably, the conditional normalizing flow unlocks Sudakov form
factors to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Numerical
results are presented for four-top production at the $Z$ pole. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in top-quark physics. Our
findings open new avenues for calorimeter simulation at the Electron-Ion
Collider.
