\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of soft modes at next-to-next-to-leading order to
future work. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the
Sudakov form factors of the interplay between rotationally symmetric
likelihood ratios of photoproduction and spectral form factors of Higgs
plus jet production. We comment on the extension to massive shower
histories. Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
