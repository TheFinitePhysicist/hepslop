\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the jet charge to four-top production.
This sheds new light on the form factors of the interplay between the
tracking detectors of the NLO-matched template fit and soft drop masses.
We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in perturbative QCD. Our
findings open new avenues for unfolding at the High-Luminosity LHC.
