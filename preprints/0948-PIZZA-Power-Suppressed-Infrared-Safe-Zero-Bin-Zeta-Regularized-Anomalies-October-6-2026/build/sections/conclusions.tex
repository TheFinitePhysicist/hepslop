\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our framework seamlessly
integrates lattice simulations trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV and
the sphalerons of a family of particle-level antenna functions. A
groomed azimuthal decorrelation emerges at next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy.
