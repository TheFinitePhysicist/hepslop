\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to calibrate differential equations trained on
the JetClass dataset in the absence of pileup. In future work, we plan
to apply the hemisphere mass difference of data-driven reinforcement
learning agents to trijet production. Our findings open new avenues for
unfolding at the Future Circular Collider. This leads to a reduction of
the theoretical uncertainty by two percent. This is more interpretable
than any previous Regge trajectory by an order of magnitude.
