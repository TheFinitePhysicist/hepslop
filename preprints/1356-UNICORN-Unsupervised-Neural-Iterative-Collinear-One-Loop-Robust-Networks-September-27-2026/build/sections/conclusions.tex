\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into state-of-the-art
K-factors. The model-agnostic rapidity gap fraction improves on the
novel Energy Mover's Distance by a factor of 4 for all values of $z_{\rm
cut}$. An all-order Lund multiplicity emerges at leading power. These
results pave the way for event generation for any number of emissions.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on boosted top
quarks.
