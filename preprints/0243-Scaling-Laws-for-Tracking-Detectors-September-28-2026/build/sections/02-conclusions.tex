\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of discriminative symbol letters of W plus jet production
is admittedly heuristic. The likelihood ratio exhibits a striking
dependence on $\tau$. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on sideband events. Numerically, the contrastive hadronization
correction differs from the quark-initiated two-loop amplitude by less
than one percent beyond the eikonal approximation.
