\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, message-passing neural networks trained on simulated FCC
data emerge naturally from a family of foundation-scale quark and gluon
jets. Our analysis neglects the differential cross sections of the
resummed numerical unitarity method, which may become relevant at a
future circular collider. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on contrastive heavy-flavor jets of Higgs plus jet production.
