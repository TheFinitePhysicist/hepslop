\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for physics beyond the Standard Model are profound. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The area under the curve
reaches 0.934 for bump hunting. We hope this work inspires further
research into IRC-safe Sudakov form factors.
