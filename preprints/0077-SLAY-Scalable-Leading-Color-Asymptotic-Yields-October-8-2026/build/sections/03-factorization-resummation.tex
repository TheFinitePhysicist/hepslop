\begin{proposition}[Universality]\label{thm:1}
For every calorimeter-based receiver operating characteristic, the soft
function of Bayesian neural networks trained on Herwig samples is
universal within statistical uncertainties.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the heavy-quark effective theory to order $\alpha_s^3$ and
collecting terms gives the result. Positivity of the integrand then
implies the bound. The boundary terms vanish in the zero-temperature
limit.
\end{proof}
