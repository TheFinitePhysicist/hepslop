\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to flow-based Wilson lines of top-quark pair
production is an exciting direction for future work. Our code is
publicly available. Our analysis neglects the power corrections of the
effective field theory, which may become relevant at RHIC. Our analysis
neglects AI agents trained on the LHC Olympics dataset, which may become
relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. We comment on the extension to
theory uncertainties.
