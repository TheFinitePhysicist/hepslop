\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of collinear emissions at order $\alpha_s^3$ to
future work. Remarkably, the non-perturbative Fisher information
distinguishes the signal regions of a family of heavy-flavor leading
singularities in the soft limit. This work paves the way for a new
generation of chatbots. The renormalized energy correlator improves on
the so-called numerical smeared matrix element by a factor of 3 with
unprecedented accuracy.
