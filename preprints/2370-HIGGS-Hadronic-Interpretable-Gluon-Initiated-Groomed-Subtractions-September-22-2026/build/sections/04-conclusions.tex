\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This sheds new light on angular-ordered negative event weights of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with effective field theories. This unlocks the
full potential of score-based generative models for boosted object
identification. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty
by one percent. A natural next step is to merge the double-real
correction to all orders in $\alpha_s$.
