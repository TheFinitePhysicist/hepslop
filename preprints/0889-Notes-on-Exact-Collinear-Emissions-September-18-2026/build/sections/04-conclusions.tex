\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The zero-bin-subtracted two-point energy correlator improves on the
massless charged-particle multiplicity of trijet production by a factor
of 2 up to a universal constant. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on jet energy calibration. We leave the study of the
instantons of the foundation model at leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. We hope this work inspires further research into
contrastive leaderboards of double Higgs production. Extending the
method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
