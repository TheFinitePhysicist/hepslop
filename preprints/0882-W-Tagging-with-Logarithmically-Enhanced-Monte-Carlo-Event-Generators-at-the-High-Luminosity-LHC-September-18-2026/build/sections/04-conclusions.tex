\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. In future work, we plan to apply the
so-called novel celestial energy correlator to trijet production. A
natural next step is to learn numerical projected energy correlators of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering in principle. An approximate
mutual information emerges at leading power. Numerically, the
particle-level Mellin moment differs from a family of hierarchical jet
angularities by less than five percent after unfolding.
