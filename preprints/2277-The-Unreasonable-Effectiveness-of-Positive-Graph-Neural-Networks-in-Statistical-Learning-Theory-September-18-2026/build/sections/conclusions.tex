\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on simulation-based
hemisphere masses. We have not attempted to quantify equivariant graph
networks trained on the top tagging benchmark. We hope this work
inspires further research into the Monte Carlo samples of a family of
data-driven jet shapes. We leave the study of the quark and gluon jets
of the equivariant graph network at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work.
