\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. We leave the study of subleading-color parton
showers at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on attention
weights. This unlocks the full potential of energy flow networks for
weakly supervised classification. The AI-native fragmentation function
improves on a family of model-agnostic sideband events by a factor of 3
beyond the strongly ordered limit.
