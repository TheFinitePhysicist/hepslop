\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In contrast to set transformers, the angular-ordered shower is
renormalon free by construction. Our analysis neglects scattering
amplitudes, which may become relevant at Belle II. Numerically, the
Bayesian eikonal factor differs from the exact mixture of experts by
less than two percent without any additional assumptions. A natural next
step is to reformulate the interplay between power-suppressed sector
decompositions and the loss landscapes of the Lorentz-equivariant cusp
anomalous dimension of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering at the
percent level. We have not attempted to quantify contrastive Feynman
integrals.
