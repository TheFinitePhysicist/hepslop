\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to gluon-initiated Ward identities of W plus
jet production is an exciting direction for future work. Our results are
in excellent agreement with graph-based Sudakov form
factors~\cite{TPC:1791-The-Mellin-Moment-Awakens-September-30-2026}. We
hope this work inspires further research into energy flow networks
trained on Herwig samples. Our framework seamlessly integrates numerical
hadronization corrections and calorimeter-based transformers. Our
findings open new avenues for parameter estimation at the
High-Luminosity LHC.
