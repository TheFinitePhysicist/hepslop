\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for automated theorem proving at the
Electron-Ion Collider. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Extending
this framework to fine-tuned anomalous dimensions of Higgs boson decay
to gluons is an exciting direction for future work. We have not
attempted to quantify zero-bin subtractions. We hope this work inspires
further research into zero-bin subtractions.
