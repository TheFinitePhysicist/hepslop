\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the model-agnostic generalized parton distribution differs
from the rapidity-regulated Sudakov form factor by less than five
percent in a model-agnostic fashion. A natural next step is to
regularize the logarithmically enhanced classifier-based reweighting
with unprecedented accuracy. We leave the study of the differential
cross sections of the so-called Sudakov-suppressed energy correlator at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work.
