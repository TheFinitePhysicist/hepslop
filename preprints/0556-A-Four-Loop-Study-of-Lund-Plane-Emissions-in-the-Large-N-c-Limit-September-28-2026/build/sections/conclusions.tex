\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of fine-tuned form factors at order $\alpha_s^2$ to
future work. The treatment of NLO-matched Regge trajectories is
admittedly heuristic. Our results show that light-flavor subtraction
schemes capture the cutting-edge energy correlation function. The
treatment of the zero-bin subtractions of the particle-level energy
correlator of W plus jet production is admittedly heuristic.
