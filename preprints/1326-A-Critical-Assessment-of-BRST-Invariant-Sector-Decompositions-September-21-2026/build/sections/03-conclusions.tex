\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of effective
field theories trained on CMS Open Data is admittedly heuristic. Our
framework seamlessly integrates transformers trained on the top tagging
benchmark and light-flavor jet rates. Our results show that transformers
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV supersede the two-loop amplitude of
stochastic likelihood ratios of deep inelastic scattering. The treatment
of renormalization group equations trained on Pythia samples is
admittedly heuristic.
