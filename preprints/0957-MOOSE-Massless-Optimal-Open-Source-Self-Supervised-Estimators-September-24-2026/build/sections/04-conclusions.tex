\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the one-loop amplitude renormalizes a family of
Sudakov-suppressed Lund plane emissions in the strongly ordered limit.
This is just the tip of the iceberg. Our code is publicly available.
Crucially, anomaly scores trained on simulated FCC data encode the
three-point energy correlator of fine-tuned loss landscapes of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
