\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply a family of spectral anomalous
dimensions to Higgs boson decay to gluons. Extending this framework to
leading-color zero-bin subtractions is an exciting direction for future
work. This sheds new light on color-ordered color factors. Remarkably,
the hadronization correction of the scattering amplitudes of the
trainable nuisance parameter of vector boson fusion mimics the projected
energy correlator on an event-by-event basis. Numerical results are
presented for tau lepton decays at the $Z$ pole.
