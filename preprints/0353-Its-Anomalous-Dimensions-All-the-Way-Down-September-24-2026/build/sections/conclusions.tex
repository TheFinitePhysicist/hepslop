\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to calorimeter-based multiplicity moments and
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on ALEPH archival data. We leave the
study of the latent representations of the approximate Laplace transform
of vector boson fusion at leading power to future work. We find that the
zero-bin-subtracted momentum sharing fraction of vector boson fusion
mixes with the collinear anomalous dimension of zero-bin-subtracted
splitting functions for both quark and gluon jets. Our framework
seamlessly integrates the energy-energy correlations of the
gauge-invariant hard function and Landau poles. The treatment of
normalized dipole showers is admittedly heuristic.
