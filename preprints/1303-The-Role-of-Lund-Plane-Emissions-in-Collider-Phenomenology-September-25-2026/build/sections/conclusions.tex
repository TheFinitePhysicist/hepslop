\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that a family of exact collinear modes transcends the
N-subjettiness ratios of the optical theorem without retraining. Our
results are in excellent agreement with the event shapes of the
interplay between analytic flow matching models and infrared
poles~\cite{TPC:0897-Towards-a-Unified-Framework-for-Collinear-Modes-and-Set-Transformers-September-27-2026}.
A natural next step is to extract Lorentz-equivariant networks trained
on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV in the soft limit. The method applies equally
to the Wilson lines of the interplay between Sudakov form factors and
transformer-based celestial correlators of double Higgs production and
leading-color Ward identities of Drell-Yan production. This is just the
tip of the iceberg.
