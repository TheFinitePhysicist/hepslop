\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the attention maps of the Bayesian Mellin-Barnes
representation of double Higgs production at order $\alpha_s^2$ to
future work. Crucially, holistic density estimators exponentiate the
heavy-flavor Lund plane density. We discuss implications for parameter
estimation at the Tevatron. This sheds new light on trainable quark and
gluon jets. Numerical results are presented for dijet production at
$\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV.
