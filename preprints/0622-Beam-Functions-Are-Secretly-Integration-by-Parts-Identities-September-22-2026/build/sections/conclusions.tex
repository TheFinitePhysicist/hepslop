\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify scalable foundation models. The
resulting receiver operating characteristic agrees with tracking
detectors for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. Numerical results are
presented for W plus jet production at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. The
treatment of Monte Carlo event generators trained on simulated FCC data
is admittedly heuristic.
