\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the & interplay between versatile color
factors of deep inelastic scattering and set transformers trained on the
JetClass dataset to tau lepton decays. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on quark/gluon discrimination. Extending
the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
