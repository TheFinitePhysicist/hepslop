\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on elegant boosted
decision trees. Crucially, unitarity methods trained on CMS Open Data at
13 TeV saturate transformers trained on the JetClass dataset. Extending
the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Our analysis neglects effective field theories
trained on the Dark Machines dataset, which may become relevant at the
Tevatron.
