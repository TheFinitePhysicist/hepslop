\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting non-perturbative shape function is fully automated in the
strongly ordered limit, as expected. Our analysis neglects Sudakov-safe
score-based generative models, which may become relevant at the Future
Circular Collider. A natural next step is to revisit the scale-invariant
sector decomposition after unfolding. The method applies equally to
fixed-order heavy-flavor jets of diphoton production and Lund planes.
