\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Numerical results are
presented for double Higgs production at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. The
treatment of Bayesian scaling laws is admittedly heuristic. A natural
next step is to parametrize Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV beyond the & strongly ordered limit.
