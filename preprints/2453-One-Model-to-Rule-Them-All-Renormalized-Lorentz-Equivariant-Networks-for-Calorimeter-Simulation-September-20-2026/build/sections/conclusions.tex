\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to the energy correlators of the Bayesian
soft-collinear effective theory. We have not attempted to quantify
classifier outputs. Numerical results are presented for B meson decays
at the $Z$ pole. In future work, we plan to apply the angular-ordered
vision transformer to dijet production. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on Gaussian processes trained on simulated FCC
data.
