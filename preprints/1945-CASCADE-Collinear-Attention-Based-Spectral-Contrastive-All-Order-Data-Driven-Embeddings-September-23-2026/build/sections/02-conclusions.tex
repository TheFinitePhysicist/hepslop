\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with sector
decompositions. Our analysis neglects gauge-covariant theory
uncertainties of electron-positron annihilation, which may become
relevant at a future circular collider. In future work, we plan to apply
the non-perturbative shape function of cutting-edge Landau poles to
Higgs boson decay to gluons.
