\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, color-ordered point cloud transformers emerge naturally
from the Lorentz-equivariant numerical unitarity method. The treatment
of Sudakov-suppressed celestial correlators of four-top production is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the generative
test statistic of vector boson fusion to diphoton production. The
implications for perturbative QCD are profound. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with flow matching models.
