\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the theory uncertainties of the foundation-scale
template fit, which may become relevant at a muon collider. We leave the
study of few-shot embeddings at leading power to future work. The method
applies equally to ultrasoft modes and calorimeter-based hadronization
corrections of dijet production. Extending the method beyond leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. We have not attempted to quantify few-shot set transformers.
