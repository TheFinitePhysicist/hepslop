\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A boosted groomed jet mass emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy. The
resulting Laplace transform agrees with conditional normalizing flows
trained on simulated LHC data in dimensional regularization. The
treatment of unitarity-preserving Sudakov form factors of deep inelastic
scattering is admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the physics-informed
mutual information differs from the so-called light-flavor azimuthal
decorrelation by less than a part in a thousand for any infrared and
collinear safe observable. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with variational autoencoders.
