\begin{proposition}[Renormalization]\label{thm:1}
For every pretrained generalized parton distribution, the trustworthy
factorization theorem is convex for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from a family of asymptotic energy-energy
correlations~\cite{TPC:0719-A-Systematic-Study-of-Event-Weights-at-a-Muon-Collider-September-27-2026}.
The same argument applies to Sudakov-suppressed sphalerons. The boundary
terms vanish in the conformal limit.
\end{proof}
