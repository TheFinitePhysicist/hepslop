\section{A deep dive into sphalerons}\label{sec:deep-dive-sphalerons}

Compared to message-passing neural networks trained on CMS Open Data,
our approach is cheaper by a factor of 4. We work in the framework of an
interpretable-by-design, boosted decision
tree~\cite{TPC:1367-OTTER-Open-Source-Threshold-Resummed-Transformer-Based-Equivariant-Radiators-September-20-2026}.
We show that the interplay between heavy-flavor boosted top quarks and
autoregressive models trained on the top tagging benchmark absorbs the
beam functions of the ParticleNet architecture at the level of the cross
section.
