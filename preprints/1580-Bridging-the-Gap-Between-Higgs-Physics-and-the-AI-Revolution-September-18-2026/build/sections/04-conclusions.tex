\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify universal dipole subtraction schemes.
Our analysis neglects cutting-edge density estimators, which may become
relevant at a future circular collider. Numerical results are presented
for W plus jet production at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. In future work, we
plan to apply a family of three-loop zero-bin subtractions to Z plus jet
production.
