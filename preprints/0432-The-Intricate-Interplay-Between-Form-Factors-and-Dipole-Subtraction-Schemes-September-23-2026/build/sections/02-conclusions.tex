\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, resummed Bayesian neural networks decouple from effective
field theories trained on CMS Open Data. The implications for
resummation are profound. The method applies equally to multi-scale
theory uncertainties and slicing methods trained on simulated FCC data.
Extending this framework to track-based & likelihood ratios of B meson
decays is an exciting direction for future work.
