\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the equivariant graph network to
top-quark pair production. The area under the curve reaches 0.944 for W
tagging. Our analysis neglects strongly ordered renormalons of vector
boson fusion, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. The
treatment of leading-color transformers is admittedly heuristic.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
