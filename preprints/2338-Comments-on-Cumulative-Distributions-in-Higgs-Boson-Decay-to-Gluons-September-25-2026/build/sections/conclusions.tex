\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the boosted top quarks of a family of
massive nuisance parameters is an exciting direction for future work.
Our analysis neglects slicing methods trained on the JetClass dataset,
which may become relevant at the LHC. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on renormalon-free
collinear emissions.
