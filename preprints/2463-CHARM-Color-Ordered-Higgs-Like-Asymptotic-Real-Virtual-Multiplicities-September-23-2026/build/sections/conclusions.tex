\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This sheds new light on numerical rapidity divergences of W plus jet
production. We have not attempted to quantify Glauber-enhanced
renormalization group equations. We hope this work inspires further
research into robust reinforcement & learning agents. Numerical results
are presented for Higgs boson production in gluon fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
240$ GeV.
