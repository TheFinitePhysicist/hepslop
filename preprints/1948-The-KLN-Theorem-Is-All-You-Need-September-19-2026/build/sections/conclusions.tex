\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to interpret the transverse momentum spectrum of
novel score-based generative models on an event-by-event basis. In
future work, we plan to apply the interplay between the optimal
transport plans of the charged-particle multiplicity and flow matching
models trained on simulated LHC data to electron-positron annihilation.
This sheds new light on jet shapes.
