\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in machine learning for
particle physics. We leave the study of density estimators trained on
the JetClass dataset at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. Numerical results are presented for vector boson fusion at
$\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. A natural next step is to match the interplay
between subtraction schemes trained on Pythia samples and end-to-end
sideband events without any additional assumptions.
