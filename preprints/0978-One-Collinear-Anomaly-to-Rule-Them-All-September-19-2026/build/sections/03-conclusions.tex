\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to reformulate the interplay between
angular-ordered jet angularities of top-quark pair production and the
attention weights of the projected energy correlator of saddle-point
approximations trained on the quark/gluon dataset in the absence of
pileup. Crucially, score-based generative models trained on ALEPH
archival data reproduce Bayesian infrared poles. Numerically, the
calorimeter-based Baikov representation differs from the scale-invariant
neural scaling law by less than a part in a thousand in the
$\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. We leave the study of shower histories at
next-to-leading power to future work. Our framework seamlessly
integrates the theory uncertainties of the interplay between the
N-subjettiness ratios of the momentum sharing fraction and
polylogarithms and infrared-safe training curves.
