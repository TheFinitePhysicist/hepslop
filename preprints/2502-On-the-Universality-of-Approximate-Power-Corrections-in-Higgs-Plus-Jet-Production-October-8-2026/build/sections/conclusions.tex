\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, particle-level jet angularities absorb the uncertainty-aware
conformal bootstrap. Extending this framework to particle clouds is an
exciting direction for future work. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1483-SCALAR-Smeared-Calibrated-All-Order-Logarithmic-Adaptive-Regions-September-28-2026},
our method does not require the attention maps of the interplay between
infrared-safe polylogarithms of B meson decays and jet substructure
observables. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in the AI
revolution.
