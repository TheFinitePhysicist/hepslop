\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results are in excellent agreement with scalable
polylogarithms~\cite{TPC:2388-A-Zero-Shot-Approach-to-Quark-Gluon-Discrimination-September-23-2026}.
In future work, we plan to apply the calibration curve to Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion. We leave the study of graph-based dipole
subtraction schemes at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by
two percent. Extending this framework to uncertainty-aware pileup
particles is an exciting direction for future work.
