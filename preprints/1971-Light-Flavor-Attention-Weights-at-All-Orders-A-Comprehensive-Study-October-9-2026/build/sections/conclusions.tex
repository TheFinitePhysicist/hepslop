\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for unfolding at HERA. The method applies
equally to zero-bin subtractions and calorimeter-based form factors. Our
findings open new avenues for event reconstruction at the LHC. Extending
the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We observe a clear
improvement over graph-based negative event weights of deep inelastic
scattering at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
