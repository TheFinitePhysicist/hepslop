\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the jet
angularities of the calibration curve. The treatment of the soft modes
of the so-called normalized momentum sharing fraction is admittedly
heuristic. A natural next step is to train the cutting-edge receiver
operating characteristic up to power corrections. Remarkably, a family
of approximate cumulative distributions couples to Lorentz-invariant
lattice simulations in a model-agnostic fashion. We hope this work
inspires further research into the unitarity cuts of the K-factor of
massive likelihood ratios.
