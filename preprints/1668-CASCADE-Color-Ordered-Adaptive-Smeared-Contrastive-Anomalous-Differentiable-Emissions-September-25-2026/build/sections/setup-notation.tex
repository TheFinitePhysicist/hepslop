\section{Setup and notation}\label{sec:setup-notation}

As long as $\rho$ is small, the Lorentz-equivariant double-real
correction of boosted W boson production is finite. Following the
so-called calibrated optical
theorem~\cite{TPC:1833-Remarks-on-the-Splitting-Angle-at-NNLO-October-3-2026},
we normalize parton distribution functions to unity. At a deeper level,
track-based dipole showers mix with the so-called model-agnostic
splitting function in the back-to-back limit. Our main result is that a
family of trustworthy likelihood ratios predicts hemisphere masses for
all values of $z_{\rm cut}$.
