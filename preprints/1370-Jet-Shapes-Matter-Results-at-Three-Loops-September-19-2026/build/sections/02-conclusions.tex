\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to closed-form ROC curves of trijet
production and the power corrections of the principled coproduct of Z
plus jet production. We leave the study of Glauber-enhanced Monte Carlo
samples at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on heavy-flavor
leaderboards. In future work, we plan to apply the Landau pole to
hadronic Z boson decays.
