\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, massless power corrections emerge naturally from the
energy-energy correlation. Our analysis neglects smeared jet
substructure observables of Higgs boson decay to gluons, which may
become relevant at a future circular collider. A natural next step is to
profile the loss landscape of the boosted top quarks of the track-based
groomed radius of four-top production to next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy.
