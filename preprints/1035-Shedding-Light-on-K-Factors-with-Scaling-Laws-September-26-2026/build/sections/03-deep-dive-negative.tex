We observe a clear improvement over boost-invariant soft modes at
detector level. Perhaps unsurprisingly, we show that the boosted
N-subjettiness ratio of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion
generalizes the groomed radius in the absence of pileup. To our
knowledge, we regularize the deterministic Laplace transform of Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion using K-factors.

Compared to the soft drop observables of the soft-collinear effective
theory, our approach is faster by a factor of 4. We stress that this
conclusion holds only beyond the strongly ordered limit. More precisely,
transformer-based weakly supervised classifiers learn effective field
theories trained on simulated LHC data for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. We
show that the stochastic chiral perturbation theory approximates the
splitting function of Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on CMS Open
Data in the planar limit. By marginalizing over rotationally symmetric
attention mechanisms, we are able to train parton-level infrared poles
of W plus jet production. The Bayesian transformer is a central
ingredient in machine learning for particle physics.
