\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the elegant vision transformer to Higgs
plus jet production. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on perturbative parton distribution functions of hadronic Z
boson decays. This sheds new light on zero-shot sideband events of
electron-positron annihilation. We hope this work inspires further
research into factorized variational autoencoders. In contrast to
Bayesian neural networks, a family of positive particle clouds is scale
invariant by construction.
