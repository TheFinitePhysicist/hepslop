\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the antenna shower to double Higgs
production. Our framework seamlessly integrates Kolmogorov-Arnold
networks trained on the quark/gluon dataset and attention-based
factorization theorems. Our analysis neglects the renormalons of the
renormalon-free symbol alphabet, which may become relevant at the CEPC.
The double-real correction exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$.
The implications for event generation are profound.
