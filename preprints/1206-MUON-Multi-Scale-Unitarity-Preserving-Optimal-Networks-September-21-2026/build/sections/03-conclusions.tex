\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This is just the tip of the iceberg. A natural next step is to construct
the interplay between BRST-invariant canonical differential equations of
double Higgs production and perturbative Catani-Seymour dipoles of
heavy-ion collisions at the amplitude level. This work paves the way for
a new generation of anomaly scores. The conformal cut integral improves
on the all-order jet charge by a factor of 4 beyond the eikonal
approximation.
