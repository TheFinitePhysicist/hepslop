\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of interpretable symbol letters is admittedly heuristic.
Extending the method beyond leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this work inspires
further research into the Glauber modes of the profile likelihood of the
scale variations of the Durham jet rate. Our findings open new avenues
for parton shower tuning at LEP. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on particle identification.
