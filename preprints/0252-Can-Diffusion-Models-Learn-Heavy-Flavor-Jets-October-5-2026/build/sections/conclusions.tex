\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to factorize a family of elegant soft drop
observables for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. The treatment of
Lorentz-invariant instantons of W plus jet production is admittedly
heuristic. Extending this framework to beam functions is an exciting
direction for future work. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with flow matching models.
