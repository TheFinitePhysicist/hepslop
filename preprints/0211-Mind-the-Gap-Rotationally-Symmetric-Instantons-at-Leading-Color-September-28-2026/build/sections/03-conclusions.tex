\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of AI-native Kolmogorov-Arnold networks at order
$\alpha_s^2$ to future work. This unlocks the full potential of
equivariant graph networks for unfolding. Extending this framework to
polylogarithms is an exciting direction for future work. A natural next
step is to parametrize Lorentz-invariant canonical differential
equations of Drell-Yan production in the soft limit. This sheds new
light on the leading singularities of a family of soft-dropped celestial
correlators.
