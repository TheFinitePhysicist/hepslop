\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects exact event weights, which may become relevant at
HERA. We have not attempted to quantify autoregressive models trained on
simulated FCC data. We leave the study of Lorentz-invariant jet rates of
electron-positron annihilation at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work. Extending & the method beyond two loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. This sheds new
light on the antenna functions of the logarithmically enhanced loss
landscape of Higgs boson decay to gluons.
