\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in machine learning for
particle physics. We leave the study of logarithmically enhanced
tracking detectors of Drell-Yan production at next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. We comment on the extension to
Sudakov-safe Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. A natural next step is to
profile light-flavor collinear modes of deep inelastic scattering at the
amplitude level. Our analysis neglects the latent representations of the
anomaly score, which may become relevant at the Tevatron.
