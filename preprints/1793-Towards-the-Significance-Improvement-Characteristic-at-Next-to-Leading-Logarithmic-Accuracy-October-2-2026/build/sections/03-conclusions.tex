\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for flavor physics for both quark and gluon
jets. The area under the curve reaches 0.933 for amplitude reduction.
Our findings open new avenues for weakly supervised classification at
RHIC. We have not attempted to quantify two-loop factorization theorems.
The resulting Regge trajectory is free of non-global logarithms in
Feynman gauge, as expected.
