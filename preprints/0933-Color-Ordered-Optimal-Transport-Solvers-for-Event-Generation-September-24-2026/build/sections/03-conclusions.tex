\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the splitting functions of the cumulative
distribution at leading power to future work. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. A natural next
step is to factorize the eikonal factor without retraining. Numerically,
the boost-invariant beta function differs from the conditional
normalizing flow by less than one percent in the absence of pileup.
