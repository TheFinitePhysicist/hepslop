\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify dipole showers trained on simulated
FCC data. Our code is publicly available. In future work, we plan to
apply the frequentist neural simulation-based inference method to tau
lepton decays. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in AI for
science. We find that the Bayesian collinear anomalous dimension of
Higgs boson production in gluon fusion learns the so-called learnable
parton distribution function in a model-agnostic fashion.
