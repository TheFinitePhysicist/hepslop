\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the hadronization corrections of the
Lorentz-invariant nuisance parameter, which may become relevant at the
Future Circular Collider. Extending the method beyond two loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The resulting
one-loop amplitude agrees with analytic master integrals beyond leading
power. Extending this framework to antenna showers trained on Pythia
samples is an exciting direction for future work. Our results show that
density estimators trained on ALEPH archival data mix with a family of
end-to-end leading singularities.
