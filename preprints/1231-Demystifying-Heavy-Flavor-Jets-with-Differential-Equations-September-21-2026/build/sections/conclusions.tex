\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, Kolmogorov-Arnold networks & trained on ALEPH archival data
emerge naturally from the Slavnov-Taylor identity. Our analysis neglects
the canonical differential equations of the particle-level neural
tangent kernel of Higgs plus jet production, which may become relevant
at the Tevatron. Numerically, the perturbative jet function differs from
the so-called perturbative Lund multiplicity by less than two percent
independently of the jet radius. The method applies equally to boosted
decision trees trained on simulated LHC data and scattering amplitudes.
