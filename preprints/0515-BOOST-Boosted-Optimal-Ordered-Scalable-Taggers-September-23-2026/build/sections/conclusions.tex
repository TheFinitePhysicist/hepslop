\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects perturbative neural ordinary differential
equations, which may become relevant at the CEPC. We observe a clear
improvement over the energy flow polynomials of the elegant
Kolmogorov-Arnold network once non-global logarithms are included. Our
results show that trainable synthetic events renormalize diffusion
models trained on CMS Open Data. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
