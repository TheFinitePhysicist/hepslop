\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the calorimeter-based total jet broadening differs from a
family of Lorentz-invariant soft drop masses by less than five percent
for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with boosted decision trees. The resulting hemisphere mass
difference is remarkably stable beyond the eikonal approximation, as
expected. In future work, we plan to apply the Ward identity of
multiplicity moments to Higgs plus jet production. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in large language model research.
