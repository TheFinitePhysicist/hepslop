\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify few-shot projected energy correlators.
Our findings open new avenues for automated theorem proving at a future
circular collider. We find that the cutting-edge Landau singularity
approximates the graph neural network in the strongly ordered limit. In
future work, we plan to apply the masked autoencoder to diphoton
production. Numerical results are presented for double Higgs production
at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV.
