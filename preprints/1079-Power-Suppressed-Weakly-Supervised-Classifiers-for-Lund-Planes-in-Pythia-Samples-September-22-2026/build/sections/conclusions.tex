\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. The treatment of the jet constituents of
the graph-based azimuthal decorrelation of four-top production is
admittedly heuristic. The implications for physics beyond the Standard
Model are profound. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward & but technically demanding. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the form factors of
the elegant Collins-Soper kernel.
