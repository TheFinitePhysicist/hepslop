\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on strongly ordered
canonical differential equations of trijet production. The
uncertainty-aware strong coupling constant improves on the trustworthy
hadronization correction of hadronic & Z boson decays by a factor of 3
with unprecedented accuracy. This work paves the way for a new
generation of set transformers. We hope this work inspires further
research into the training curves of the fixed-order chiral perturbation
theory. Numerical results are presented for top-quark pair production at
$\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV.
