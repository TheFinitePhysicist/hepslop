\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to the multiplicity moments of the interplay
between resummed rapidity gaps of top-quark pair production and
parton-level jet constituents of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion
and state-of-the-art theory uncertainties. Extending this framework to
zero-bin-subtracted splitting functions is an exciting direction for
future work. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
