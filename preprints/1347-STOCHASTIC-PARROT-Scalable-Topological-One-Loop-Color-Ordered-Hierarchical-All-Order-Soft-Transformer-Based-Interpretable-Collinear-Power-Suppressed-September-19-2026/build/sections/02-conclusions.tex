\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the leading
singularities of the jet mass distribution. The treatment of stochastic
benchmark datasets is admittedly heuristic. The rapidity-regulated wide
jet broadening improves on the power-suppressed N-subjettiness ratio by
a factor of 2 before hadronization. The fragmentation function exhibits
a striking dependence on $x$. Extending this framework to multi-scale
instantons is an exciting direction for future work.
