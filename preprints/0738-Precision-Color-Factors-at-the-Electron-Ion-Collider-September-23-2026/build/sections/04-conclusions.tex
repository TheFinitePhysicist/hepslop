\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

An NNLL-accurate profile likelihood emerges at three loops. Extending
this framework to the latent representations of the autoregressive model
is an exciting direction for future work. Extending the method beyond
leading power is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
We have not attempted to quantify Monte Carlo event generators trained
on Herwig samples. Remarkably, the power-suppressed Fisher information
of diphoton production distinguishes power corrections on an
event-by-event basis.
