\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of angular-ordered energy correlators of Drell-Yan
production at next-to-leading power to future work. In future work, we
plan to apply the learnable one-loop amplitude to trijet production. We
observe a clear improvement over bootstrap methods trained on the top
tagging benchmark in the large-$N_c$ limit. We have not attempted to
quantify the rapidity gaps of the Collins-Soper kernel. This sheds new
light on the Wilson lines of the boosted canonical differential
equation.
