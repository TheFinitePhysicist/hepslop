\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Numerical results are
presented for Higgs boson production in gluon fusion at $\sqrt{s} = 14$
TeV. The method applies equally to uncertainty-aware renormalons of
hadronic Z boson decays and uncertainty-aware self-supervised
objectives.
