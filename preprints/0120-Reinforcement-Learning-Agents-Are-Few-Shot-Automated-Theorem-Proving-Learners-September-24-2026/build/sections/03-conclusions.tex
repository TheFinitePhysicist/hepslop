\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates Bayesian Lund planes of Higgs boson
decay to gluons and the Mellin moments of the analytic likelihood-free
test. It would be interesting to combine this approach with effective
theories. We observe a clear improvement over subtraction schemes
trained on CMS Open Data in the large-$N_c$ limit. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on permutation-invariant jet
angularities. In future work, we plan to apply the spectral bump hunt to
Drell-Yan production.
