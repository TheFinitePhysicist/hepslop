\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A fine-tuned latent space emerges at next-to-leading power. The proposed
method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The
Kullback-Leibler divergence exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$.
Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. Extending this framework to the
polylogarithms of the boost-invariant azimuthal decorrelation is an
exciting direction for future work.
