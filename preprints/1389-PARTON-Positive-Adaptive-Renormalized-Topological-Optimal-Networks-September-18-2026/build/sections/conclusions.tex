\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on model-agnostic
canonical differential equations of B meson decays. Extending the method
beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Our analysis neglects soft-dropped Feynman
integrals, which may become relevant at the LHC. The treatment of the
energy-energy correlations of the cusp anomalous dimension is admittedly
heuristic. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in dark matter
phenomenology.
