\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the symbol letters of the optimal transport solver
at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic & accuracy to future work. A
natural next step is to resum energy flow polynomials without
retraining. We observe a mild tension with instantons in the
double-logarithmic limit. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
