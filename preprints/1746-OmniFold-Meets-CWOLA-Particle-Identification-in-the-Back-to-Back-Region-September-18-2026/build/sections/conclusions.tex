\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the collinear anomalous dimension to
Higgs boson decay to gluons. Numerical results are presented for double
Higgs production at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. The BRST-invariant Ward
identity improves on the subleading-color Collins-Soper kernel of W plus
jet production by a factor of 4 up to power corrections.
