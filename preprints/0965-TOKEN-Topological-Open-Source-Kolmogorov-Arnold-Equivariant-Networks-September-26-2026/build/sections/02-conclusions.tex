\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over jet angularities after unfolding.
Extending this framework to score-based generative models trained on CMS
Open Data is an exciting direction for future work. A natural next step
is to unfold the one-loop C-parameter distribution without any
additional assumptions. We have not attempted to quantify Bayesian
neural networks trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
