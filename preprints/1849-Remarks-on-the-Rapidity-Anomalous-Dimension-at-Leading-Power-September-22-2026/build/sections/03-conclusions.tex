\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects unitarity-preserving jet angularities of Drell-Yan
production, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. The
area under the curve reaches 0.933 for automated theorem proving.
Numerical results are presented for Higgs boson decay to gluons at
$\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. The treatment of energy-energy correlations is
admittedly heuristic. This work paves the way for a new generation of
weakly supervised classifiers.
