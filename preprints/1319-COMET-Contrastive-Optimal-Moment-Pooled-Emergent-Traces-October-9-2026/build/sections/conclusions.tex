\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the projected energy correlator couples to scattering
amplitudes within statistical uncertainties. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with parton showers. Extending this framework to
weakly supervised density estimators is an exciting direction for future
work. We leave the study of beam functions at order $\alpha_s^2$ to
future work. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
differentiable rapidity gaps of deep inelastic scattering.
