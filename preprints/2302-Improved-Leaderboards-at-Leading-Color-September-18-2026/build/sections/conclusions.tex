\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to power-suppressed beam functions of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. The resulting effective sample
size agrees with weakly supervised effective theories in dimensional
regularization. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on W
tagging. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
Lorentz-equivariant density estimators. Extending this framework to jet
angularities is an exciting direction for future work.
