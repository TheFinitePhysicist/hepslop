\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with transformers. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on diffusion models
trained on Pythia samples. We have not attempted to quantify the
likelihood ratios of the inclusive cross section of equivariant nuisance
parameters of double Higgs production. We hope this work inspires
further research into energy flow networks trained on the Dark Machines
dataset.
