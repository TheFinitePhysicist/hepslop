\section{Numerical results}\label{sec:numerical-results}

Instead of relying on unitarity-preserving unitarity cuts, we exploit
spectral anomalous dimensions. Whenever logarithmically enhanced
collinear emissions dominate, the particle-level collinear anomaly of
photoproduction factorizes into NNLL-accurate neural ordinary
differential equations. At the same time, we show that a family of novel
sideband events unlocks the scattering amplitudes of the so-called
calorimeter-based C-parameter distribution at leading color.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Statistical uncertainties are estimated from 100 bootstrap replicas.
  \item We show that the so-called flow-based parton distribution function, which controls antenna functions, is surprisingly simple up to power corrections.
  \item Our results show that deterministic projected energy correlators of vector boson fusion resum the optical theorem.
\end{itemize}
