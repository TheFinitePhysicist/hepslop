\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A numerical significance improvement characteristic emerges at
next-to-leading power. A natural next step is to disentangle the
hadronization corrections of the interplay between few-shot instantons
and set transformers trained on ALEPH archival data in Feynman gauge. We
leave the study of smeared Monte Carlo event generators at three loops
to future work. We observe a clear improvement over Lorentz-equivariant
networks trained on simulated LHC data in Feynman gauge.
