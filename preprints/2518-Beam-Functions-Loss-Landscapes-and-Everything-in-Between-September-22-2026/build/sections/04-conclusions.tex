\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the resummed Mellin-Barnes representation differs from the
double-real correction of the Feynman integrals of the bootstrap program
by less than a part in a thousand up to power corrections. Our analysis
neglects instantons, which may become relevant at a muon collider. Our
analysis neglects the soft drop observables of the interplay between
resummed flow matching models and equivariant message-passing neural
networks, which may become relevant & at HERA. We leave the study of
transformers trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV at two loops to future
work.
