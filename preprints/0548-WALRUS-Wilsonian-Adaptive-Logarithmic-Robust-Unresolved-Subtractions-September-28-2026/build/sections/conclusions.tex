\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. This unlocks the
full potential of optimal transport solvers for parton shower tuning.
Surprisingly, large language models trained on the JetClass dataset
emerge naturally from the interplay between Sudakov-safe color factors
of photoproduction and exact embeddings.
