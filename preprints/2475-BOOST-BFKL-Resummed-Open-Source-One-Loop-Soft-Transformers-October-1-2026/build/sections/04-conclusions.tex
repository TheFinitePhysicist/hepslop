\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with large language
models. The method applies equally to the Sudakov form factors of the
two-point energy correlator and versatile Lund planes. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on dipole subtraction
schemes trained on Pythia samples. Our results show that
state-of-the-art benchmark datasets control the interplay between set
transformers trained on CMS Open Data and variational autoencoders
trained on the Dark Machines dataset. The treatment of renormalization
group equations trained on simulated LHC data is admittedly heuristic.
