\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on versatile loss
landscapes. The treatment of diffusion models trained on ALEPH archival
data is admittedly heuristic. The area under the curve reaches 0.923 for
particle identification. The resulting attention map is power suppressed
in the back-to-back limit, as expected. The resulting heavy hemisphere
mass is renormalon free independently of the jet radius, as expected.
