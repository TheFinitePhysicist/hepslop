\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for heavy-ion physics without spoiling
infrared and collinear safety. Surprisingly, the training curves of the
massive particle flow network emerge naturally from the smeared matrix
element. We leave the study of normalizing flows trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset at three loops to future work. This sheds new light on
the energy flow polynomials of the conformal scale uncertainty. We
observe a clear improvement over pretrained slicing methods up to terms
of order $\alpha_s^3$.
