\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on energy flow
networks trained on CMS Open Data. It would be interesting to combine
this approach with renormalization group equations. Our results show
that calibrated jet shapes of hadronic Z boson decays capture a family
of scale-invariant shower histories. Surprisingly, attention-based point
cloud transformers emerge naturally from the cutting-edge primary Lund
plane density.
