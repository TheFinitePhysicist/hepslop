\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for dijet production at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our analysis
neglects projected energy correlators, which may become relevant at LEP.
Our analysis neglects non-global logarithms, which may become relevant
at the CEPC.
