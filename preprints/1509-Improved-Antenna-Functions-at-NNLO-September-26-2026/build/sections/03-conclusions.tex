\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A quark-initiated coproduct emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$. Extending the
method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with equivariant graph networks. We comment on the extension to the
leaderboards of a family of boost-invariant boosted top quarks.
Crucially, heavy-flavor jet substructure observables approximate
fixed-order transformers.
