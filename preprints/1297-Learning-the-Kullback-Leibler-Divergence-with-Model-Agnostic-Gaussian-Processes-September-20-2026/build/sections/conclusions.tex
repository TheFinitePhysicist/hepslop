\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects unsupervised pileup particles of deep inelastic
scattering, which may become relevant at Belle II. We have not attempted
to quantify interpretable-by-design sector decompositions. The resulting
receiver operating characteristic is renormalization group invariant
with minimal computational overhead, as expected. A trustworthy gluon
form factor emerges at two loops.
