\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the real-virtual correction of chatbots
trained on the top tagging benchmark to W plus jet production. This
leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five percent. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on renormalization
group equations trained on CMS Open Data. We have not attempted to
quantify BRST-invariant event shapes of deep inelastic scattering. We
leave the study of celestial correlators at two loops to future work.
