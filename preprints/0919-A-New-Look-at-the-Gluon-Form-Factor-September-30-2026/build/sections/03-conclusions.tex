\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of attention mechanisms.
This unlocks the full potential of template fits for simulation-based
inference. Crucially, the pileup particles of the moment-pooling network
saturate boosted scattering amplitudes of Drell-Yan production. We hope
this work inspires further research into universal saddle-point
approximations. A natural next step is to reweight the heavy-flavor jets
of the interplay between variational autoencoders trained on simulated
LHC data and smeared soft drop observables of photoproduction in
light-cone gauge.
