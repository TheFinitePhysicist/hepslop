\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with chatbots. Our
findings open new avenues for event reconstruction at the Electron-Ion
Collider. We have not attempted to quantify Sudakov-suppressed jet
rates. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
soft-dropped canonical differential equations. Extending the method
beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
