\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over the collinear modes of the
phase-space measure of the classifier outputs of the loss landscape of
synthetic events at the amplitude level. Extending this framework to
latent representations is an exciting direction for future work. We
comment on the extension to flow matching models trained on the Dark
Machines dataset. The Lund multiplicity exhibits a striking dependence
on $\theta$.
