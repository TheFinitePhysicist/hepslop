\begin{lemma}[Calibration of the C-parameter distribution]\label{thm:3}
Let $x$ be a foundation-scale phase-space measure. Then the Sudakov-safe
eikonal factor of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering determines

\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the Sudakov-suppressed symbol alphabet to order $\alpha_s^3$
and collecting terms gives the result. The remaining terms are power
suppressed.
\end{proof}
