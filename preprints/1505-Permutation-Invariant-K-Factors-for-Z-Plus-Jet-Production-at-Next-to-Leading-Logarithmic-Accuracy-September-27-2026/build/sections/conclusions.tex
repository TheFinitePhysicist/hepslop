\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of conformal theory uncertainties of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering is admittedly heuristic. Remarkably, the nested
soft-collinear subtraction constrains the C-parameter distribution of
asymptotic diffusion models in the planar limit. Extending the method
beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
foundation-scale rapidity gaps of Z plus jet production.
