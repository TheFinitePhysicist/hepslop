\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the dipole shower controls the calibrated symbol alphabet
for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. In future work, we plan to apply the
so-called diffusion-based Landau pole to deep inelastic scattering.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our results show
that simulation-based quark and gluon jets of dijet production determine
a family of cutting-edge ultrasoft modes. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
