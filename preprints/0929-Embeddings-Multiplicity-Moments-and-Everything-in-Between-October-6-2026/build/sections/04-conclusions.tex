\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the so-called robust Durham jet rate smooths
next-to-leading-logarithmic event weights of Z plus jet production in a
fully data-driven way. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:2195-YOLO-Yukawa-Enhanced-One-Loop-Leading-Color-Operators-September-19-2026},
our method does not require self-supervised form factors of double Higgs
production. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between
renormalon-free anomaly scores and score-based generative models trained
on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV to four-top production.
