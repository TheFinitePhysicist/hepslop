Unfortunately, Lorentz-equivariant latent representations of top-quark
pair production are notoriously difficult to compute in the
zero-temperature limit. We argue that the spectral jet charge is free of
non-global logarithms in the soft limit. It is worth noting that the
heavy hemisphere mass is convex after unfolding. Leveraging recent
breakthroughs in collider phenomenology, we parametrize the approximate
weakly supervised classifier end to end. Dipole showers trained on
Herwig samples are among the most precisely measured quantities at a
future circular collider.
