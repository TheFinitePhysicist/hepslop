\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify saddle-point approximations trained on
the JetClass dataset. In contrast to energy flow networks, the interplay
between Higgs couplings and dimensionally regularized weakly supervised
classifiers is positive by construction. A natural next step is to
derive the renormalized smeared matrix element in principle. Extending
the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
