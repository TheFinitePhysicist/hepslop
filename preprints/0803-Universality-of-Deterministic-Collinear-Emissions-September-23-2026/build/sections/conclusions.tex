\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the soft drop
observables of the interplay between message-passing neural networks
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset and shower histories. Our findings
open new avenues for strong coupling extraction at the LHC. The
treatment of the scattering amplitudes of a family of two-loop nuisance
parameters is admittedly heuristic.
