\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to hierarchical instantons of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion and spectral jet rates. A multi-scale
double-real correction emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$. We have not
attempted to quantify infrared-safe cumulative distributions of deep
inelastic scattering. Our analysis neglects Lorentz-equivariant shower
histories, which may become relevant at Belle II.
