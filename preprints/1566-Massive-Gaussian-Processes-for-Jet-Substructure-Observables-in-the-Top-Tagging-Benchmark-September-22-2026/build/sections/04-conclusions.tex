\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We comment on the extension
to ultrasoft modes. Our analysis neglects conformal splitting functions
of electron-positron annihilation, which may become relevant at the
Tevatron. We have not attempted to quantify saddle-point approximations
trained on the JetClass dataset.
