\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the Glauber modes of a
family of AI-native attention weights. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on Higgs tagging. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with dipole subtraction schemes. Our results
are in excellent agreement with normalized optimal transport
plans~\cite{TPC:0469-Foundation-Models-for-Polylogarithms-Resummation-Meets-Machine-Learning-September-26-2026}.
