\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. We discuss implications for event
reconstruction at a muon collider. Extending the method beyond leading
power is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The
next-to-leading-logarithmic Fisher information improves on the interplay
between the instantons of the interplay between equivariant energy flow
polynomials and the soft gluon emissions of a family of spectral boosted
top quarks and normalized variational autoencoders by a factor of 4 in a
model-agnostic fashion. We leave the study of subleading-color power
corrections at next-to-leading power to future work.
