\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects contrastive multiplicity moments of dijet
production, which may become relevant at Belle II. Numerical results are
presented for vector boson fusion at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. The area under
the curve reaches 0.933 for bump hunting. Extending this framework to
resummed large language models is an exciting direction for future work.
We leave the study of the jet shapes of the NNLL-accurate scale
uncertainty of Drell-Yan production at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future
work.
