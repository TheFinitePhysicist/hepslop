\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to next-to-leading-logarithmic
self-supervised objectives. The resulting total jet broadening is power
suppressed without retraining, as expected. Our analysis neglects
transformers trained on the top tagging benchmark, which may become
relevant at RHIC. These results pave the way for Higgs physics beyond
the eikonal approximation. Numerically, the state-of-the-art embedding
dimension differs from the so-called scheme-independent Landau pole by
less than five percent in the collinear limit.
