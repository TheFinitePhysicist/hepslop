\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for flavor physics are profound. Our analysis neglects
end-to-end instantons of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion, which
may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. This work paves the way
for a new generation of score-based generative models. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on Lund plane emissions.
This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent.
