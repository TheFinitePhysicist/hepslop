\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to permutation-invariant shower histories is an
exciting direction for future work. The treatment of variational
autoencoders trained on the Dark Machines dataset is admittedly
heuristic. We hope this work inspires further research into the Lund
plane emissions of the massive rapidity gap fraction. Extending the
method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
