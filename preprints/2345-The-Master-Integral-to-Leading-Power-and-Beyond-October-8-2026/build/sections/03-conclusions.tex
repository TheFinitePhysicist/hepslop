\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects message-passing neural networks trained on the top
tagging benchmark, which may become relevant at a future circular
collider. We observe a clear improvement over equivariant TMDs for any
number of emissions. Our analysis neglects the soft drop observables of
the infrared-safe groomed jet mass, which may become relevant at a muon
collider. This work paves the way for a new generation of point cloud
transformers.
