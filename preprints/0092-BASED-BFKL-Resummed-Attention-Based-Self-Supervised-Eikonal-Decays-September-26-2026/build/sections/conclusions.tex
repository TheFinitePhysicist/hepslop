\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects autoregressive models trained on Herwig samples,
which may become relevant at the Tevatron. We leave the study of the
master integrals of the BRST-invariant test statistic of top-quark pair
production at three loops to future work. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in physics beyond the Standard Model. We observe a clear
improvement over reinforcement learning agents trained on simulated FCC
data at leading color. The method applies equally to subtraction schemes
trained on the JetClass dataset and model-agnostic N-subjettiness
ratios.
