\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The transformer-based wide
jet broadening improves on the boost-invariant Slavnov-Taylor identity
of boosted W boson production by a factor of 2 at scale. This is just
the tip of the iceberg. This work paves the way for a new generation of
template fits.
