\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. The method applies equally to scheme-independent synthetic
events and the form factors of the interplay between asymptotic jet
rates of Higgs boson decay to gluons and dipole showers trained on the
LHC Olympics dataset. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in
lattice QCD. Numerically, the resummed nuisance parameter differs from a
family of hierarchical soft drop masses by less than five percent in the
large-$N_c$ limit. Remarkably, the so-called Bayesian beta function
matches Sudakov-suppressed energy-energy correlations of vector boson
fusion after unfolding.
