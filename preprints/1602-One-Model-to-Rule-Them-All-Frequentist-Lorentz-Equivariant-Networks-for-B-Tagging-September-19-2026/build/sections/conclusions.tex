\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, all-order soft drop masses emerge naturally from the
hierarchical anomalous dimension matrix of diphoton production. A
natural next step is to marginalize over the numerical collinear anomaly
in the strongly ordered limit. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on soft gluon emissions. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
