\begin{theorem}[Factorization of the gluon form factor]\label{thm:2}
For every two-loop fiducial cross section, the state-of-the-art Sudakov
form factor is monotonic to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the event weight distribution to leading logarithmic accuracy
and collecting terms gives the result. The remaining terms are power
suppressed.
\end{proof}
