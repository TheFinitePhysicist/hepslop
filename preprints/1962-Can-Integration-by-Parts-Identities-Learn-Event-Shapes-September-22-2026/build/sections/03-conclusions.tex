\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on spectral
scattering amplitudes of trijet production. & The resulting transverse
momentum spectrum agrees with TMDs in the back-to-back limit. The
resulting inclusive cross section is gauge invariant for any infrared
and collinear safe observable, as expected. The treatment of approximate
energy correlators of dijet production is admittedly heuristic.
