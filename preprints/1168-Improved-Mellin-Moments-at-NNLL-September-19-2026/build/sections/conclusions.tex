\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We observe a clear improvement over the latent representations
of the so-called light-flavor Sudakov form factor in the strongly
ordered limit. We observe a mild tension with master integrals in the
threshold limit. Extending this framework to three-loop instantons is an
exciting direction for future work. The treatment of the signal regions
of the parton distribution function is admittedly heuristic.
