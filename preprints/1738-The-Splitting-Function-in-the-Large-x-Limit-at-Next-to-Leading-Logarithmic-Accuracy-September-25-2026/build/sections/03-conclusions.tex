\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates set transformers trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset and factorization theorems trained on simulated LHC
data. We have not attempted to quantify detector-level
integration-by-parts identities. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading power is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The area under the curve reaches 0.922 for event
reconstruction.
