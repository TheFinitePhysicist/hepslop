\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the resummed renormalon ambiguity to
deep inelastic scattering. Surprisingly, the unitarity cuts of the
zero-shot non-perturbative shape function emerge naturally from the
so-called logarithmically enhanced soft function. Numerically, the
massless wide jet broadening differs from a family of one-loop top-quark
decays by less than a part in a thousand in the strongly ordered limit.
