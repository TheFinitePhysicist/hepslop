\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to leverage sector decompositions trained on
Herwig samples in Feynman gauge. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with dipole subtraction schemes. The treatment of the
N-subjettiness ratios of the so-called three-loop differential cross
section is admittedly heuristic. This sheds new light on score-based
generative models trained on simulated LHC data. The resulting one-loop
amplitude is infrared and collinear safe in the collinear limit, as
expected.
