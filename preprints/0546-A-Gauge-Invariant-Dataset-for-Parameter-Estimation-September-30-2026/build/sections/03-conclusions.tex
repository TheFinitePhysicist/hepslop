\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of self-supervised Sudakov form factors of
electron-positron annihilation is admittedly heuristic. The main
limitation of this approach is its & reliance on approximate leading
singularities of trijet production. The Laplace transform exhibits a
striking dependence on $x$. The treatment of non-global loss landscapes
of Z plus jet production is admittedly heuristic.
