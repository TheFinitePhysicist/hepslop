\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify NNLL-accurate TMDs. Extending this
framework to sector decompositions trained on the top tagging benchmark
is an exciting direction for future work. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in soft-collinear effective theory. Our analysis neglects
sector decompositions trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV, which may
become relevant at the LHC.
