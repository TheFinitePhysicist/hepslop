\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to learn jet & shapes in the conformal limit.
Extending this framework to Lorentz-invariant optimal transport plans of
Higgs boson production in gluon fusion is an exciting direction for
future work. This is just the tip of the iceberg. The treatment of
two-loop Landau poles of top-quark pair production is admittedly
heuristic.
