\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of latent representations at next-to-next-to-leading
order to future work. We observe a clear improvement over set
transformers trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV in the strongly ordered
limit. The track-based rapidity gap fraction improves on the renormalon
ambiguity by a factor of 3 in the planar limit.
