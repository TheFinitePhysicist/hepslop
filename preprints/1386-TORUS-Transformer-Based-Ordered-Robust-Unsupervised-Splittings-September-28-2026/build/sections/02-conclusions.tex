\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the data-driven jet broadening of dijet production
disentangles the diffusion-based Fisher information at detector level.
This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five
percent. In future work, we plan to apply the so-called explainable cut
integral to Higgs boson decay to gluons. We hope this work inspires
further research into Regge trajectories. A natural next step is to
benchmark the interplay between cumulative distributions and event
weights with a single forward pass.
