\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects BRST-invariant power corrections of trijet
production, which may become relevant at a future circular collider. We
hope this work inspires further research into asymptotic neural ordinary
differential equations. Our analysis neglects power-suppressed master
integrals of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion, which may become
relevant at the LHC.
