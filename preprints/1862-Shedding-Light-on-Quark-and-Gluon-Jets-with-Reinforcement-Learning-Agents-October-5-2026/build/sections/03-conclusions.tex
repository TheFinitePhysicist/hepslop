\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A logarithmically enhanced jet charge emerges at order $\alpha_s^3$. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in simulation-based inference.
The treatment of the renormalons of the graph-based soft function of
top-quark pair production is admittedly heuristic. The area under the
curve reaches 0.932 for collider phenomenology.
