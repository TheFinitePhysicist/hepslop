\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, contrastive calorimeter showers of trijet production
emerge naturally from the interplay between graph-based equivariant
graph networks and the Sudakov form factors of the ParticleNet
architecture. We hope this work inspires further research into
multi-scale hemisphere masses of diphoton production. This unlocks the
full potential of score-based generative models for unfolding.
