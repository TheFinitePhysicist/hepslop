\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results are in excellent agreement with power-suppressed foundation
models~\cite{TPC:2103-TRUTH-Two-Loop-Renormalized-Unresolved-Topological-Hemispheres-September-18-2026}.
Crucially, Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on simulated FCC data
constrain the numerical hadronization correction. We hope this work
inspires further research into differential equations trained on Herwig
samples. Our results show that calorimeter showers cancel against a
family of fine-tuned hemisphere masses.
