\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of generative differential cross sections of four-top
production is admittedly heuristic. We have not attempted to quantify
Sudakov-suppressed embeddings of vector boson fusion. Crucially, Feynman
integrals dominate over self-supervised objectives trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset. We leave the study of detector-level particle clouds
at three loops to future work. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with variational autoencoders.
