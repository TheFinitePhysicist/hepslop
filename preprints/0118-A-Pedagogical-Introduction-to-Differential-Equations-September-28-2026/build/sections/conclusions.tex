\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify calibrated Monte Carlo event
generators. The implications for Higgs physics are profound. We discuss
implications for top tagging at a future circular collider. Our approach
achieves state-of-the-art performance on jet tagging. The treatment of
end-to-end embeddings of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering is
admittedly heuristic.
