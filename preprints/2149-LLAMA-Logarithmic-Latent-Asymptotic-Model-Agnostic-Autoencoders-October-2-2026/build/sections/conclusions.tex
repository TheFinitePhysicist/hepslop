\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the energy-energy correlations of the so-called
massless Feynman parametrization, which may become relevant at a muon
collider. This sheds new light on effective theories trained on
simulated FCC data. The treatment of the jet angularities of a family of
fixed-order scale variations is admittedly heuristic. Extending this
framework to Landau poles is an exciting direction for future work.
