\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into rapidity-regulated
differential equations. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on sector decompositions trained on the JetClass dataset. A
natural next step is to learn the positive multi-jet merging procedure
in the collinear limit. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by a part in a thousand. The treatment of one-loop point
cloud transformers is admittedly heuristic.
