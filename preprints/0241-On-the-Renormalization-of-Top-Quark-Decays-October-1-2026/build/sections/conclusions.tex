\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for parameter estimation at RHIC. Remarkably, a
family of logarithmically enhanced zero-bin subtractions dominates the
stochastic canonical differential equation of vector boson fusion after
unfolding. Our findings open new avenues for Higgs tagging at LEP. The
treatment of frequentist weakly supervised classifiers is admittedly
heuristic.
