\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates factorized Monte Carlo event
generators and color-ordered point cloud transformers. We have not
attempted to quantify non-global negative event weights. The treatment
of jet rates is admittedly heuristic. It would be interesting to combine
this approach with neural ordinary differential equations. The method
applies equally to end-to-end density estimators and message-passing
neural networks trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
