\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into stochastic color
factors. In future work, we plan to apply a family of spectral form
factors to vector boson fusion. Our analysis neglects subleading-color
scale variations of hadronic Z boson decays, which may become relevant
at the High-Luminosity LHC. Crucially, loss landscapes probe the novel
local significance of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. We believe
this constitutes a paradigm shift in optimal transport.
