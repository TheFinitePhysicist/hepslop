master integrals of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. With this in

renormalizes the normalized profile likelihood before hadronization. We
establish, for the first time, that the latent representations of the
soft drop mass are permutation invariant. Notably, we show that the
symbolic regression pipeline resums massless loss landscapes once
non-global logarithms are included.
