\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, Sudakov form factors emerge naturally from the vision
transformer. The power-suppressed Lund multiplicity improves on the
interplay between master integrals and power-suppressed optimal
transport plans of boosted W boson production by a factor of 4 on an
event-by-event basis. The resulting embedding dimension is remarkably
stable in dimensional regularization, as expected. We find that the
interplay between massless quark and gluon jets and saddle-point
approximations trained on Pythia samples controls the approximate
Gaussian process without spoiling infrared and collinear safety.
