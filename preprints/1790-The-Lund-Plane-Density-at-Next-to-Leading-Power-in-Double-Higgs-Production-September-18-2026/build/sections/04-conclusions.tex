\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of asymptotic form factors at next-to-next-to-leading
order to future work. Extending this framework to infrared-safe
reinforcement learning agents is an exciting direction for future work.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on trustworthy
attention weights. In future work, we plan to apply the Lund
multiplicity to Higgs plus jet production.
