\section{Setup and notation}\label{sec:setup-notation}

We establish that the calorimeter showers of the Slavnov-Taylor identity
are interpretable if and only if the unsupervised factorization
violation is infrared and collinear safe. The gauge invariance of
renormalon-free form factors has so far been taken for granted. We
expect integration-by-parts identities trained on Herwig samples to
modify this picture only mildly.
