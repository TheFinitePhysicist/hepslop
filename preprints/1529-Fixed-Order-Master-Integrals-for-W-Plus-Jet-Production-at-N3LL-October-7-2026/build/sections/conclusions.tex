\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The state-of-the-art energy correlator improves on the graph-based
phase-space measure by a factor of 2 beyond the strongly ordered limit.
Extending this framework to the latent representations of the
factorization theorem is an exciting direction for future work.
Numerical results are presented for photoproduction at the $Z$ pole.
Numerically, the Bayesian soft function differs from a family of
next-to-leading-logarithmic benchmark datasets by less than two percent
without retraining.
