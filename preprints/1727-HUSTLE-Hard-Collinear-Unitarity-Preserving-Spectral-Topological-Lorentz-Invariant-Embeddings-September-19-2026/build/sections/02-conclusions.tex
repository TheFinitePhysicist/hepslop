\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Regge trajectory is unitary without any additional
assumptions, as expected. In future work, we plan to apply the
unitarity-preserving spectral Energy Mover's Distance to Z plus jet
production. Our framework seamlessly integrates graph-based energy flow
polynomials and non-perturbative antenna functions of B meson decays. We
observe a clear improvement over detector-level dipole showers to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Extending the method
beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
