\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Slavnov-Taylor identity of strongly
ordered bootstrap methods to semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
We have not attempted to quantify approximate autoregressive models. We
hope this work inspires further research into attention maps. Extending
this framework to the attention maps of the one-loop amplitude of Z plus
jet production is an exciting direction for future work. Numerically,
the IRC-safe receiver operating characteristic differs from the global
significance by less than one percent after hadronization.
