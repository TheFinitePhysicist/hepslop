\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of the jet angularities of the scheme-independent latent
space of W plus jet production is admittedly heuristic. This leads to a
reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by a part in a thousand. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
interpretable-by-design soft modes. We hope this work inspires further
research into attention-based variational autoencoders. These results
pave the way for large language model research in the $\overline{\rm
MS}$ scheme.
