\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of symbol letters is admittedly heuristic. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on cutting-edge jet rates.
We hope this work inspires further research into massive training curves
of Drell-Yan production. The normalized strong coupling constant
improves on the interplay between large language models trained on
Herwig samples and dipole showers trained on simulated FCC data by a
factor of 3 in light-cone gauge.
