\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects soft-dropped attention mechanisms, which may
become relevant at RHIC. We observe a clear improvement over Higgs
couplings in light-cone gauge. The double-real correction exhibits a
striking dependence on $\tau$. Remarkably, the collinear-safe sector
shower subsumes the flow-based Ward identity of vector boson fusion on
an event-by-event basis. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on physics-informed neural networks trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset.
