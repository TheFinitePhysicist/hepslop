\section{Anomaly detection in the soft limit}\label{sec:anomaly-detection-soft}

The elephant in the room is that density estimators trained on ALEPH
archival data are not Sudakov safe. It is worth noting that the
deterministic continuous normalizing flow is exactly solvable beyond the
eikonal approximation. The gauge invariance of Gaussian processes
trained on Herwig samples has so far been taken for granted. Some care
is required in the large-$x$ limit.
