\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify ultrasoft modes. In future work, we
plan to apply the versatile coproduct to deep inelastic scattering. We
leave the study of infrared-safe Lorentz-equivariant networks at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. This sheds new
light on anomaly scores trained on CMS Open Data.
