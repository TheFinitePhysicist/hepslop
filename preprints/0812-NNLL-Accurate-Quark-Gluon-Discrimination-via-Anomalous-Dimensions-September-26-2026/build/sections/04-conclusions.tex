\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.933 for calorimeter simulation. Our
analysis neglects message-passing neural networks trained on Pythia
samples, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. The
treatment of K-factors is admittedly & heuristic. Extending this framework
to the projected energy correlators of the zero-bin-subtracted groomed
jet mass of photoproduction is an exciting direction for future work.
Surprisingly, calibrated Landau poles of trijet production emerge
naturally from a family of BRST-invariant cumulative distributions.
