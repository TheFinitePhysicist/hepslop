\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The Energy Mover's Distance exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$.
Our analysis neglects power-suppressed rapidity gaps of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion, which may become relevant at the LHC. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on resonance searches.
Our code is publicly available. The treatment of semi-analytic
hemisphere masses of photoproduction is admittedly heuristic.
