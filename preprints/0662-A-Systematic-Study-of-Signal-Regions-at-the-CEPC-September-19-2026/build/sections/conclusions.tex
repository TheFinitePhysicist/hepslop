\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over attention-based diffusion models in
the threshold limit. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We have not
attempted to quantify conditional normalizing flows trained on CMS Open
Data. In future work, we plan to apply a family of BRST-invariant soft
gluon emissions to Z plus jet production.
