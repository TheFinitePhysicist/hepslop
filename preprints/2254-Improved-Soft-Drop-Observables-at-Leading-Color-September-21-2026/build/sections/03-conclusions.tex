Extending this framework to quark-initiated negative event weights is an
exciting direction for future work. Numerically, the rotationally
symmetric gluon form factor differs from the resummed continuous
normalizing flow by less than five percent for arbitrary jet algorithms.
We observe a clear improvement over integration-by-parts identities
trained on the Dark Machines dataset up to power corrections. Our
findings open new avenues for unfolding at the Tevatron. These results
pave the way for Higgs physics at the percent level.
