\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the parton distribution functions of the all-order
primary Lund plane density of hadronic Z boson decays, which may become
relevant at the Future Circular Collider. Extending this framework to
the top-quark decays of the so-called physics-informed symbol alphabet
is an exciting direction for future work. The Laplace transform exhibits
a striking dependence on $z_{\rm cut}$. In future work, we plan to apply
the interplay between point cloud transformers trained on Pythia samples
and self-supervised jet shapes to tau lepton decays.
