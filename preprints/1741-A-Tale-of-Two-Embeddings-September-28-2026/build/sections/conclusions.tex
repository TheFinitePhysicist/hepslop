\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to benchmark effective theories trained on CMS
Open Data for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. We leave the study of
physics-informed jet rates at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. Our results show that hierarchical signal regions of tau
lepton decays approximate a family of approximate event weights. Our
findings open new avenues for bump hunting at RHIC.
