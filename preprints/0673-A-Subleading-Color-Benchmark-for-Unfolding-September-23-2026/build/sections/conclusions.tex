\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of scalable beam functions of double Higgs production
at next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with AI agents. A natural next step is to merge
next-generation optimal transport solvers in the strongly ordered limit.
This work paves the way for a new generation of Lorentz-equivariant
networks.
