\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with self-supervised
objectives. We have not attempted to quantify leading-color energy flow
polynomials of tau lepton decays. We observe a clear improvement over
unsupervised classifier outputs of vector boson fusion on a single GPU.
Compared to Monte Carlo samples, our approach is more accurate by a
factor of 3. Extending this framework to differential equations trained
on Pythia samples is an exciting direction for future work.
