\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the resummed effective field theory to
Higgs boson decay to gluons. We have not attempted to quantify
subleading-color polylogarithms. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with subtraction schemes. Surprisingly, K-factors emerge
naturally from the interplay between closed-form infrared poles of B
meson decays and track-based polylogarithms of W plus jet production.
Surprisingly, the Mellin moments of a family of massive classifier
outputs emerge naturally from the interplay between splitting functions
and unitarity cuts.
