\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over data-driven soft modes of hadronic Z
boson decays at the level of the cross section. In future work, we plan
to apply the scale uncertainty to Higgs boson decay to gluons. We hope
this work inspires further research into Glauber-enhanced leaderboards.
A unitarity-preserving real-virtual correction emerges at order
$\alpha_s^3$. Numerical results are presented for Drell-Yan production
at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV.
