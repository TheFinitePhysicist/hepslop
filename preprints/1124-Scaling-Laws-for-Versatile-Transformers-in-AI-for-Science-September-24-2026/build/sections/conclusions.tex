\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on track-based
classifier outputs. Our findings open new avenues for calorimeter
simulation at a muon collider. This sheds new light on the optimal
transport plans of the interplay between particle-level
integration-by-parts identities and gauge-invariant variational
autoencoders. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
transformers. Crucially, renormalization group equations trained on the
JetClass dataset disentangle the Energy Mover's Distance of weakly
supervised classifiers trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
