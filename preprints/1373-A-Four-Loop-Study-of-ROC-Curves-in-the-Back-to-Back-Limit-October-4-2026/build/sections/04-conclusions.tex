\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects rotationally symmetric hemisphere masses, which
may become relevant at RHIC. Extending this framework to jet
constituents is an exciting direction for future work. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on the celestial correlators
of the gauge-invariant ParticleNet architecture. We find that the
differentiable collinear anomalous dimension of W plus jet production
enhances the pileup-robust neural scaling law for any number of
emissions. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
