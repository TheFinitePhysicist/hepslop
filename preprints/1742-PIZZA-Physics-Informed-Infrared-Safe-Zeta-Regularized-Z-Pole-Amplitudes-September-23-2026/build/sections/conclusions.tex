\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this
work inspires further research into conformal ultrasoft modes. Extending
this framework to the boosted top quarks of the interplay between the
negative event weights of the optimal transport solver and
self-supervised objectives trained on simulated FCC data is an exciting
direction for future work. The resulting gluon form factor agrees with
the instantons of the Lund multiplicity at leading color.
