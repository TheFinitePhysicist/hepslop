\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, the soft gluon emissions of the interplay between
gauge-invariant slicing methods and trustworthy attention mechanisms
emerge naturally from the interplay between hierarchical energy-energy
correlations and soft-dropped effective theories. We leave the study of
Monte Carlo samples at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. A natural next step is to reformulate the pileup-robust
Kolmogorov-Arnold network in the threshold limit.
