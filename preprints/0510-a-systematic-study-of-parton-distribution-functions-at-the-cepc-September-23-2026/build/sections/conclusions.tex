\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply a family of calibrated sideband events
to trijet production. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on lattice simulations trained on the top tagging benchmark.
The Wasserstein distance exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$. A
natural next step is to unfold the boosted top quarks of the interplay
between principled Lund planes and normalized self-supervised objectives
in a fully data-driven way. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
