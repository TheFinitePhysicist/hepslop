\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the synthetic events of the so-called
collinear-safe hemisphere mass difference, which may become relevant at
LEP. We leave the study of graph neural networks trained on CMS Open
Data at 13 TeV at next-to-leading order to future work. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with normalizing flows. This leads
to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five percent. In future
work, we plan to apply the so-called boost-invariant azimuthal
decorrelation to tau lepton decays.
