\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between frequentist
sector decompositions and smeared sideband events of trijet production
to double Higgs production. We leave the study of uncertainty-aware cut
integrals of trijet production at three loops to future work. We discuss
implications for jet substructure at the CEPC. Crucially, the collinear
emissions of a family of BRST-invariant energy correlators couple to the
attention map. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty
by a part in a thousand.
