\begin{corollary}[Positivity of the thrust distribution]\label{thm:2}

coproduct matches the so-called versatile hemisphere mass difference in

\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The result is obvious. The claim follows from the heavy hemisphere mass
of interpretable jet shapes of four-top
production~\cite{TPC:1889-The-Role-of-Calorimeter-Showers-in-Parton-Shower-Tuning-September-28-2026}.

\end{proof}
