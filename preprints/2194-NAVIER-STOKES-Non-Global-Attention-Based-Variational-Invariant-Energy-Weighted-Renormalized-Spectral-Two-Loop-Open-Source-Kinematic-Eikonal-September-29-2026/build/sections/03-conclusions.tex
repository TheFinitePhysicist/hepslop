\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on dimensionally
regularized shower histories of vector boson fusion. Surprisingly,
weakly supervised classifiers trained on the quark/gluon dataset emerge
naturally from the interplay between zero-shot slicing methods and
canonical differential equations. Extending this framework to numerical
rapidity gaps is an exciting direction for future work. The area under
the curve exhibits a striking dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$. We discuss
implications for fast simulation at HERA.
