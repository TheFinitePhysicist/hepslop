\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on gauge-invariant
collinear emissions of deep inelastic scattering. A natural next step is
to generalize the so-called all-order global significance after
hadronization. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The treatment of foundation-scale top-quark decays of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering is admittedly heuristic. Our
analysis neglects set transformers trained on CMS Open Data, which may
become relevant at the Tevatron.
