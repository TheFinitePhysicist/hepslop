\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results are in excellent agreement with elegant antenna
showers~\cite{TPC:1715-STRANGE-Smeared-Threshold-Resummed-Renormalized-Anomalous-Neural-Generative-Embeddings-October-4-2026}.
Our findings open new avenues for simulation-based inference at the
High-Luminosity LHC. Extending the method beyond three loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We have not
attempted to quantify one-loop projected energy correlators of Higgs
plus jet production.
