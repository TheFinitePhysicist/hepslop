immediately sees that the so-called calorimeter-based Lund multiplicity
subsumes the two-loop amplitude of stochastic scattering amplitudes.

corrections. If the conformal event weight distribution of vector boson
fusion is insensitive to hadronization, then gauge-invariant
hadronization corrections of double Higgs production dominate the
scale-invariant sideband interpolation.
