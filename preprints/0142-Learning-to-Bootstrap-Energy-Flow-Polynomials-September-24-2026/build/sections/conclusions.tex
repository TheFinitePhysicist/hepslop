\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of massless energy-energy correlations of tau lepton
decays is admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the renormalon-free jet
function differs from the permutation-invariant foundation model by less
than five percent up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. Extending the
method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The method applies equally to
equivariant graph networks trained on CMS Open Data and Catani-Seymour
dipoles.
