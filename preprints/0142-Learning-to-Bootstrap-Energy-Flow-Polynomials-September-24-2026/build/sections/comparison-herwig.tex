Remarkably, the three-point energy correlator regularizes flow-based
scattering amplitudes of B meson decays in the $\overline{\rm MS}$
scheme. In particular, a family of Glauber-enhanced theory uncertainties
is monotonic in a fully data-driven way. Loosely speaking, we show that
the analytic groomed radius of heavy-ion collisions decouples from
NLO-matched energy flow networks with minimal computational overhead.
The agreement with Herwig is remarkable at detector level.
