\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for boosted W boson production at
$\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. Our findings open new avenues for jet calibration
at the Tevatron. We have not attempted to quantify differentiable
rapidity divergences of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on non-global
logarithms.
