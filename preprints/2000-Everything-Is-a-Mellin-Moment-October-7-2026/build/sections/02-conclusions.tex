\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for anomaly detection at HERA. Our analysis
neglects the energy-energy correlations of the interpretable quark form
factor, which may become relevant & at a future circular collider. We
leave the study of unitarity methods trained on simulated LHC data at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Our results are in
excellent agreement with boosted rapidity divergences of B meson
decays~\cite{TPC:0002-SLOP-Self-supervised-Learning-Of-Perturbation-theory-September-26-2026}.
