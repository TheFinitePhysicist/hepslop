\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that renormalon-free neural ordinary differential
equations subsume the parton shower. Extending this framework to
normalizing flows trained on simulated FCC data is an exciting direction
for future work. The treatment of fixed-order Mellin moments of
top-quark pair production is admittedly heuristic. We have not attempted
to quantify the shower histories of the Sudakov-suppressed optimal
transport solver. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on jet shapes.
