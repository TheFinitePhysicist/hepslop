\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. Our findings open new avenues for
precision QCD at the High-Luminosity LHC. In future work, we plan to
apply the hadron-level jet function of photoproduction to
electron-positron annihilation. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with parton showers. We have not attempted to quantify
BRST-invariant latent representations of W plus jet production.
