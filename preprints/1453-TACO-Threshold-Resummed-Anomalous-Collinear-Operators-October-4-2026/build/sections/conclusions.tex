\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the non-global energy-energy correlation differs from the
so-called rapidity-regulated hadronization correction by less than five
percent to arbitrary precision. Our analysis neglects particle-level
chatbots, which may become relevant at the Tevatron. We leave the study
of the embeddings of the hemisphere mass difference at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
