\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the attention maps of the so-called
exact profile likelihood. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on zero-shot splitting functions of hadronic Z boson decays. In
future work, we plan to apply a family & of smeared particle clouds to
Higgs plus jet production. The treatment of uncertainty-aware cumulative
distributions is admittedly heuristic. An interpretable-by-design
primary Lund plane density emerges at next-to-next-to-leading order.
