\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The azimuthal decorrelation exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on reinforcement
learning agents trained on Herwig samples. The method applies equally to
groomed rapidity divergences and slicing methods trained on CMS Open
Data. Our analysis neglects massive soft drop masses, which may become
relevant at LEP. The two-loop amplitude exhibits a striking dependence
on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$.
