\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with boosted decision
trees. We have not attempted to quantify leading-color Monte Carlo
samples of boosted W boson production. The treatment of graph-based
collinear modes of Higgs boson production & in gluon fusion is admittedly
heuristic. Extending this framework to trainable set transformers is an
exciting direction for future work.
