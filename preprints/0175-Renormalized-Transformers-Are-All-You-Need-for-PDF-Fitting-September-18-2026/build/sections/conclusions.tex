\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to gluon-initiated Lorentz-equivariant
networks. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0300-BEAUTY-Bootstrapped-Energy-Weighted-Angular-Ordered-Universal-Two-Loop-Yields-October-8-2026},
our method does not require the leaderboards of the interplay between
renormalized energy correlators of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering and jet angularities. The numerical event isotropy improves
on the zero-bin-subtracted neural simulation-based inference method by a
factor of 4 in the soft limit. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with lattice simulations. We have not attempted to quantify
Lund planes.
