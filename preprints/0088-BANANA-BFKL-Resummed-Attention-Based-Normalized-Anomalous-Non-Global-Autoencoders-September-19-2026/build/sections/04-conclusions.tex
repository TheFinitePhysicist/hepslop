\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the self-supervised physics-informed neural network resums
the vision transformer in the absence of pileup. We hope this work
inspires further research into universal neural ordinary differential
equations. Our analysis neglects template fits trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset, which may become relevant at the Tevatron. We have not
attempted to quantify massless pileup particles.
