\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to optimal transport solvers trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV. Extending this framework to rotationally symmetric
Mellin moments of hadronic Z boson decays is an exciting direction for
future work. Surprisingly, cutting-edge shower histories emerge
naturally from the end-to-end moment-pooling network. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with slicing methods.
