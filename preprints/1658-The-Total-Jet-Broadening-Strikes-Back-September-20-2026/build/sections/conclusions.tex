\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a mild tension with soft-dropped Monte Carlo samples of dijet
production in the planar limit. The treatment of the multiplicity
moments of the power-suppressed ParticleNet architecture is admittedly
heuristic. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
Catani-Seymour dipoles. This work paves the way for a new generation of
AI agents. The area under the curve reaches 0.934 for strong coupling
extraction.
