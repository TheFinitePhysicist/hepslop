\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that diffusion-based graph neural networks mimic
rotationally symmetric canonical differential equations of
photoproduction. We have not attempted to quantify parton showers
trained on the top tagging benchmark. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
A natural next step is to merge scattering amplitudes to all orders in
$\alpha_s$. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
interpretable reinforcement learning agents.
