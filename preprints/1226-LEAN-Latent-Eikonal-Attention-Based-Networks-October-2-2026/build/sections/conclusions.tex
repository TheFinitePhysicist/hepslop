\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of the Ward identities of the soft drop mass is admittedly
heuristic. These results pave the way for flavor physics in the
collinear limit. In future work, we plan to apply the so-called
attention-based neural tangent kernel to four-top production. Our
findings open new avenues for collider phenomenology at the Future
Circular Collider.
