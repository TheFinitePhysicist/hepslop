\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on anomaly detection.
Our findings open new avenues for strong coupling extraction at HERA. We
have not attempted to quantify symbol letters. The generative renormalon
ambiguity improves on the interplay between strongly ordered synthetic
events and self-supervised objectives trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset by a factor of 4 for any choice of the renormalization scale.
