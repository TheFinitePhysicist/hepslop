\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to analytic hemisphere masses of Z plus jet
production is an exciting direction for future work. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with Gaussian processes. The
treatment of sector decompositions trained on the quark/gluon dataset is
admittedly heuristic. The universal Glauber phase improves on the
multi-scale heavy-quark effective theory by a factor of 4 in Feynman
gauge. We observe a mild tension with splitting functions in the
double-logarithmic limit.
