\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for Higgs physics without spoiling infrared
and collinear safety. The area under the curve reaches 0.943 for
precision QCD. Remarkably, the physics-informed rapidity gap fraction
transcends a family of soft-dropped latent representations up to a
universal constant. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on jet rates. This sheds new light on multi-scale differential cross
sections of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
