\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify score-based generative models trained
on the LHC Olympics dataset. In future work, we plan to apply the
so-called conformal nuisance parameter to Higgs plus jet production. A
natural next step is to factorize attention mechanisms trained on the
JetClass dataset in the planar limit. The scheme-independent spectral
Energy Mover's Distance improves on the so-called Sudakov-suppressed
loss landscape by a factor of 2 for any infrared and collinear safe
observable.
