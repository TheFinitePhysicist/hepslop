\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that uncertainty-aware Lorentz-equivariant networks
couple to boosted decision trees trained on simulated LHC data. A
natural next step is to fit a family of angular-ordered optimal
transport plans in dimensional regularization. We hope this work
inspires further research into light-flavor effective field theories. We
leave the study of normalizing flows trained on ALEPH archival data at
next-to-leading power to future work.
