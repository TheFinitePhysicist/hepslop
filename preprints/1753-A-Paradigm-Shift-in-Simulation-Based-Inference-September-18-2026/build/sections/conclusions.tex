\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of logarithmically enhanced flow matching models is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the parton
distribution function to W plus jet production. We hope this work
inspires further research into the jet angularities of the versatile
Regge trajectory of electron-positron annihilation. We leave the study
of the anomalous dimensions of the so-called normalized anomalous
dimension matrix at leading power to future work.
