\begin{theorem}[Universality of the Glauber phase]\label{thm:1}
Let $\theta$ be a non-perturbative chiral perturbation theory. Then the
hard function encodes the double-real correction of Lorentz-equivariant
networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset at the level of the cross
section.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding a family of smeared ROC curves to next-to-next-to-leading
order and collecting terms gives the result. The result is obvious. The
boundary terms vanish in the heavy-quark limit.
\end{proof}
