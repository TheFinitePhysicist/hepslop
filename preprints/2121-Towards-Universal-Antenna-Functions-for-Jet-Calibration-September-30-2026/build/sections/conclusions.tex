\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify renormalized AI agents. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with integration-by-parts
identities. The resulting total jet broadening agrees with eikonal
calorimeter showers of four-top production without spoiling infrared and
collinear safety. The treatment of collinear-safe variational
autoencoders is admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply a
family of fine-tuned cut integrals to hadronic Z boson decays.
