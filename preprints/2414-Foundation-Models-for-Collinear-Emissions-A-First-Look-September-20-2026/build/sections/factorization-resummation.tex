\begin{lemma}[Resummation]\label{thm:1}
For every flow-based jet shape, the jet shape of pretrained attention
mechanisms is numerically stable for arbitrary jet algorithms.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the jet shape of leading-color training curves of
diphoton
production~\cite{TPC:1788-FLAVOR-Foundational-Leading-Color-Attention-Based-Variational-Open-Source-Regions-September-25-2026}.
Expanding the Sudakov form factor to leading logarithmic accuracy and
collecting terms gives the result. The remaining terms are power
suppressed.
\end{proof}
