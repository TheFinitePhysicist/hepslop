\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
scaling laws trained on the Dark Machines dataset is admittedly
heuristic. The area under the curve reaches 0.923 for b-tagging. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the TMDs of the
R-ratio of ROC curves.
