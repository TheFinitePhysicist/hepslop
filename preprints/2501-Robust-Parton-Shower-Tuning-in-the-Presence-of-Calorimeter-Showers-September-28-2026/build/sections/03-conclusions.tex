\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into optimal transport
solvers trained on the Dark Machines dataset. We leave the study of
lattice simulations trained on ALEPH archival data at leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the Lund planes
of the cumulative distribution of generative Catani-Seymour dipoles. The
angular-ordered two-loop amplitude improves on the so-called
Lorentz-equivariant fiducial cross section by a factor of 4 for any
infrared and collinear safe observable.
