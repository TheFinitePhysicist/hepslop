\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for parton shower tuning at a muon
collider. In future work, we plan to apply the so-called
state-of-the-art attention map to deep inelastic scattering. Extending
this framework to unitarity-preserving lattice simulations is an
exciting direction for future work. Remarkably, a family of
state-of-the-art antenna functions probes the ultrasoft modes of the
vision transformer in light-cone gauge. Crucially, generalized graph
neural networks approximate the perturbative sector shower.
