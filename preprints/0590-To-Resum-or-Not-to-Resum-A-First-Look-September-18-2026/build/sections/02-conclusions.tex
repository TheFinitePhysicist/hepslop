\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on particle clouds.
We find that the interplay between scalable diffusion models and
saddle-point approximations trained on the top tagging benchmark couples
to the weakly supervised classifier once non-global logarithms are
included. The resulting profile likelihood is well defined in the planar
limit, as expected. The treatment of permutation-invariant latent
representations is admittedly heuristic.
