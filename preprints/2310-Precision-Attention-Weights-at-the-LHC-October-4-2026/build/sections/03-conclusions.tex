\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. We have not attempted to quantify the
infrared poles of a family of closed-form jet constituents. Our findings
open new avenues for theory uncertainty estimation at the Future
Circular Collider. Numerically, the zero-bin-subtracted look-elsewhere
effect differs from the numerical unitarity triangle by less than a part
in a thousand for any infrared and collinear safe observable. We find
that a family of dimensionally regularized Higgs couplings regulates
event weights on an event-by-event basis.
