\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. We hope this work inspires further
research into K-factors. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in
precision jet physics. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on color-ordered infrared poles of Higgs plus jet production.
Numerical results are presented for Z plus jet production at $\sqrt{s} =
240$ GeV.
