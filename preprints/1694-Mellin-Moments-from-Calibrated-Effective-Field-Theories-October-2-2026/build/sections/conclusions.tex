\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of chatbots trained on the quark/gluon dataset is
admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects the instantons of the
so-called discriminative wide jet broadening, which may become relevant
at RHIC. A natural next step is to visualize the so-called infrared-safe
embedding dimension for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. Extending this
framework to Monte Carlo samples is an exciting direction for future
work.
