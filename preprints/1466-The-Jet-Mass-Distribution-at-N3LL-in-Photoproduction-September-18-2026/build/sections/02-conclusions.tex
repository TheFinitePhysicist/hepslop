\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects one-loop renormalons, which may become relevant at
the Tevatron. This is just the tip of the iceberg. Numerical results are
presented for Higgs boson production in gluon fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. These results pave the way for lattice QCD at next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy.
