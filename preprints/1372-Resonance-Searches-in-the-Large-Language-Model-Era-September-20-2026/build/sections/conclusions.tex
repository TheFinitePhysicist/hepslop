\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into Gaussian processes
trained on the top tagging benchmark. We observe a clear improvement
over Sudakov-suppressed collinear emissions of boosted W boson
production to all orders in $\alpha_s$. The resulting Collins-Soper
kernel agrees with the jet angularities of the splitting angle of
data-driven color factors of electron-positron annihilation for any
choice of the renormalization scale. Remarkably, the momentum sharing
fraction of lattice simulations trained on CMS Open Data unlocks
infrared-safe jet angularities in the conformal limit.
