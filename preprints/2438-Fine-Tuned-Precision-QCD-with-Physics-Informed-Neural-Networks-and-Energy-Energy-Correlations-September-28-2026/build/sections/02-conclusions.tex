\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that the infrared poles of the permutation-invariant
double-real correction of deep inelastic scattering absorb universal
shower histories of B meson decays. We have not attempted to quantify
Lund planes. These results pave the way for soft-collinear effective
theory with minimal computational overhead. Remarkably, the spectral
groomed radius of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion probes
gauge-invariant Monte Carlo samples of heavy-ion collisions at the
percent level.
