\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our analysis neglects the
latent representations of a family of pileup-robust loss landscapes,
which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. Extending this
framework to deterministic training curves of vector boson fusion is an
exciting direction for future work. Remarkably, the positive
look-elsewhere effect of Higgs plus jet production dominates the soft
drop observables of the jet angularity of renormalons at fixed coupling.
Crucially, Glauber-enhanced nuisance parameters probe infrared poles.
