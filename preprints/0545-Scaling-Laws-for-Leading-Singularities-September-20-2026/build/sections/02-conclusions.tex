\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to reformulate the angular-ordered shower for any
number of emissions. Our analysis neglects splitting functions, which
may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider. Our findings open new
avenues for W tagging at the CEPC. Compared to variational autoencoders
trained on ALEPH archival data, our approach is more stable by a factor
of 3. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
