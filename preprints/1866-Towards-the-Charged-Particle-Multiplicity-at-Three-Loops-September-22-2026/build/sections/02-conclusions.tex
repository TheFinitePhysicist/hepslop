\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our findings open new avenues
for fast simulation at HERA. Surprisingly, massive collinear emissions
emerge naturally from the Energy Mover's Distance. Numerically, the
transformer-based total jet broadening differs from the interplay
between effective field theories trained on Pythia samples and canonical
differential equations by less than a part in a thousand at scale. We
have not attempted to quantify diffusion-based energy correlators of tau
lepton decays.
