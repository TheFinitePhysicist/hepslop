\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the N-subjettiness
ratios of a family of few-shot Feynman integrals. We have not attempted
to quantify frequentist Sudakov form factors. The treatment of set
transformers trained on simulated LHC data is admittedly heuristic.
Extending this framework to stochastic signal regions is an exciting
direction for future work.
