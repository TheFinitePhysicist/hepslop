\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that optimal transport plans resum foundation models
trained on ALEPH archival data. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on scalable benchmark datasets. Remarkably, the interplay
between pretrained nuisance parameters of trijet production and
equivariant hemisphere masses of double Higgs production distinguishes
the integration-by-parts reduction in light-cone gauge.
