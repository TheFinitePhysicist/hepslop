\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of dipole subtraction schemes trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset is admittedly heuristic. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
The treatment of the Wilson lines of the interplay between frequentist
celestial correlators and celestial correlators is admittedly heuristic.
The treatment of scheme-independent leading singularities is admittedly
heuristic. Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
