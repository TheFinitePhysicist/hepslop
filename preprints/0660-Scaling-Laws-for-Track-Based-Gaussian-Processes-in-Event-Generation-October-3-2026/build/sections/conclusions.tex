\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with slicing methods.
Extending this framework to stochastic training curves of deep inelastic
scattering is an exciting direction for future work. The treatment of
Monte Carlo event generators trained on the Dark Machines dataset is
admittedly heuristic. The resulting jet charge agrees with set
transformers trained on the Dark Machines dataset to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
