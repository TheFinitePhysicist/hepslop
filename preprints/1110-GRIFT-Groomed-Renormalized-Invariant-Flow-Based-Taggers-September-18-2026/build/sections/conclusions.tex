\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects next-to-leading-logarithmic jet rates of top-quark
pair production, which may become relevant at the LHC. Our analysis
neglects uncertainty-aware jet rates of diphoton production, which may
become relevant at a muon collider. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
In future work, we plan to apply the self-supervised nuisance parameter
to W plus jet production. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on factorization theorems trained on Herwig samples.
