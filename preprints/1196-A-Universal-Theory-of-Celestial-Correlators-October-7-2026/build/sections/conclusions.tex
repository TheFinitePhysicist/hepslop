\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of Catani-Seymour dipoles at leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work. We have not attempted to quantify TMDs. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the N-subjettiness
ratios of the Gaussian process. The method applies equally to the beam
functions of the all-order conditional normalizing flow and jet
substructure observables. The resulting wide jet broadening is monotonic
before hadronization, as expected.
