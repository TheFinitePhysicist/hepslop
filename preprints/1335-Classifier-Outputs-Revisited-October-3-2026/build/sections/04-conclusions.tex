\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to marginalize over generalized rapidity gaps
beyond the eikonal approximation. We leave the study of heavy-flavor
event weights at three loops to future work. We have not attempted to
quantify power corrections. In future work, we plan to apply the
so-called renormalized canonical differential equation to
electron-positron annihilation. We hope this work inspires further
research into zero-shot saddle-point approximations.
