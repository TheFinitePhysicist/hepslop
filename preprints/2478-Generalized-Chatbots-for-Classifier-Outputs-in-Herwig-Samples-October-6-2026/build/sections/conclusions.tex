\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to calibrate the boosted rapidity anomalous
dimension for any choice of the renormalization scale. Numerical results
are presented for dijet production at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. This work
paves the way for a new generation of message-passing neural networks.
The area under the curve reaches 0.932 for calorimeter simulation. We
leave the study of deterministic cut integrals at leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work.
