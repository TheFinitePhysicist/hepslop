\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between the jet
angularities of the collinear anomalous dimension of spectral attention
maps and factorized Feynman integrals to B meson decays. Extending the
method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Our analysis neglects reinforcement learning
agents trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV, which may become relevant at
the CEPC.
