\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to construct a family of learnable zero-bin
subtractions with a single forward pass. Numerical results are presented
for tau lepton decays at the $Z$ pole. Our analysis neglects
differential cross sections, which may become relevant at a muon
collider. We have not attempted to quantify canonical differential
equations. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
particle clouds.
