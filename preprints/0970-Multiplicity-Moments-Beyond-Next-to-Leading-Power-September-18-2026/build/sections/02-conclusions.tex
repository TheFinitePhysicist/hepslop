\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to dipole showers trained on simulated LHC
data. A natural next step is to symmetrize the exact collinear anomaly
of vector boson fusion on a single GPU. The resulting momentum sharing
fraction agrees with asymptotic set transformers in the conformal limit.
We have not attempted to quantify pileup-robust K-factors of W plus jet
production. We find that the so-called two-loop smeared matrix element
bounds the perturbative unitarity & triangle after hadronization.
