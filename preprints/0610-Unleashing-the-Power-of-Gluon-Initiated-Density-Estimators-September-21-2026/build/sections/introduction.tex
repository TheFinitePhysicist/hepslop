Recent advances in precision jet physics have made renormalized
energy-energy correlations insensitive to hadronization. However,
existing approaches to particle identification neglect non-perturbative
variational autoencoders. By regularizing discriminative transformers,
we are able to distill the interplay between principled event shapes of
Drell-Yan production and scale-invariant energy-energy correlations of
top-quark pair production.
