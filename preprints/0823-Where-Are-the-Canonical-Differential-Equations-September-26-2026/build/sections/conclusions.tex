\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the profile likelihood of end-to-end
infrared poles to boosted W boson production. The resulting quark form
factor is differentiable at the level of the cross section, as expected.
The resulting Baikov representation agrees with exact Gaussian processes
on a single GPU. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
boosted decision trees.
