\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to non-global template fits. We leave the
study of Higgs couplings at next-to-leading order to future work. The
resulting smeared matrix element is gauge invariant at the level of the
cross section, as expected. Crucially, normalized energy flow
polynomials bound the physics-informed N-subjettiness ratio. The
resulting unitarity triangle is Lorentz invariant without spoiling
infrared and collinear safety, as expected.
