\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to interpret the interplay between
next-to-leading-logarithmic subtraction schemes and state-of-the-art jet
rates for any infrared and collinear safe observable. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in physics beyond the Standard Model. The
area under the curve reaches 0.944 for bump hunting. Our analysis
neglects projected energy correlators, which may become relevant at the
LHC.
