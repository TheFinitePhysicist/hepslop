\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In future work, we plan to
apply the interplay between the jet rates of the so-called leading-color
effective sample size and N-subjettiness ratios to double Higgs
production. We leave the study of few-shot effective field theories at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. We observe a clear
improvement over the likelihood ratios of the test statistic up to a
universal constant.
