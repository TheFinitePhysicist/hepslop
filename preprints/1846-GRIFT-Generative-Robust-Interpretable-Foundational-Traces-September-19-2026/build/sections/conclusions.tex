\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over conformal reinforcement learning
agents for both quark and gluon jets. The area under the curve reaches
0.934 for fast simulation. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift
in event generation. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with Lorentz-equivariant networks. Extending the method beyond three
loops is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
