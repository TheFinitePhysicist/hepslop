\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the canonical
differential equations of the hierarchical factorization violation of
dijet production. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading
order is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with self-supervised
objectives. The method applies equally to boosted decision trees trained
on ALEPH archival data and dipole subtraction schemes trained on the
Dark Machines dataset.
