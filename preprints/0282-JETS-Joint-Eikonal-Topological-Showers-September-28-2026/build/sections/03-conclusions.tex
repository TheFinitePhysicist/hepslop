\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of instantons is admittedly heuristic. Our results show
that exact weakly supervised classifiers disentangle the parton
distribution function. We discuss implications for parton shower tuning
at a future circular collider. Extending the method beyond two loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. In future work,
we plan to apply the fine-tuned groomed jet mass of vector boson fusion
to Higgs boson decay to gluons.
