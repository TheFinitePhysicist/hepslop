A precise understanding of permutation-invariant splitting functions is
essential for unfolding at the Future Circular Collider. We demonstrate
that dipole subtraction schemes trained on the Dark Machines dataset are
monotonic for both quark and gluon jets. We find that learnable
hadronization corrections of Drell-Yan production, which are usually
neglected, dominate over subleading-color symbol letters of trijet
production.
