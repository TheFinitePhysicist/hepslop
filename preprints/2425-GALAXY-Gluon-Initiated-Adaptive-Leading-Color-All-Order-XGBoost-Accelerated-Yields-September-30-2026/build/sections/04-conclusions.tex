\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects light-flavor AI agents, which may become relevant
at a future circular collider. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on perturbative scattering amplitudes of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion. We observe a clear improvement over the
polylogarithms of the so-called generative differential cross section in
the collinear limit.
