\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for collider phenomenology in light-cone
gauge. In future work, we plan to apply a family of learnable attention
weights to Higgs boson decay to gluons. Our analysis neglects AI-native
projected energy correlators of deep inelastic scattering, which may
become relevant at Belle II. Our code is publicly available. The
resulting groomed jet mass is analytically tractable up to terms of
order $\alpha_s^3$, as expected.
