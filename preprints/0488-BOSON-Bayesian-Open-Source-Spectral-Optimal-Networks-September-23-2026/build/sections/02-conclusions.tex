\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with dipole subtraction
schemes. Extending this framework to boosted score-based generative
models is an exciting direction for future work. Extending the method
beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We comment on the extension to normalizing flows
trained on Herwig samples. We hope this work inspires further research
into leading-color normalizing flows.
