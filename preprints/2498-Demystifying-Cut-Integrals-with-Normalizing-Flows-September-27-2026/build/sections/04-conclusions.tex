\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of explainable antenna showers is admittedly heuristic.
The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We comment on the extension to the cut integrals of the
interplay between jet substructure observables and the cumulative
distributions of the likelihood-free jet charge. This leads to a
reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five percent. These results
pave the way for perturbative QCD beyond the strongly ordered limit.
