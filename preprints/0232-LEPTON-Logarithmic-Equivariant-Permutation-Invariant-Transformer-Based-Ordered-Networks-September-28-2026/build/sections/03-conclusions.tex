\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to NLO-matched Monte Carlo event generators.
A natural next step is to learn end-to-end integration-by-parts
identities in principle. In future work, we plan to apply the
track-based double-real correction to deep inelastic scattering. Our
analysis neglects the cut integrals of the so-called heavy-flavor heavy
hemisphere mass, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider.
This unlocks the full potential of effective field theories for top
tagging.
