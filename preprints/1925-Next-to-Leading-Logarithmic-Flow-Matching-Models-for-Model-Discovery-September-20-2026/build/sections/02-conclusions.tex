\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of zero-shot anomaly scores at order $\alpha_s^3$ to
future work. This is just the tip of the iceberg. Extending this
framework to the event shapes of the rapidity-regulated projected energy
correlator of trijet production is an exciting direction for future
work. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
