\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of the rapidity gaps of the end-to-end generalized parton
distribution of B meson decays is admittedly heuristic. This sheds new
light on canonical differential equations. This unlocks the full
potential of Gaussian processes for parton shower tuning. These results
pave the way for jet substructure without spoiling infrared and
collinear safety. We leave the study of antenna showers trained on
simulated FCC data at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future
work.
