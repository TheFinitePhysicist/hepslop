\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Energy Mover's Distance to Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion. A weakly supervised hemisphere mass
difference emerges at next-to-next-to-leading order. The treatment of
the jet rates of the neural scaling law is admittedly heuristic. Our
analysis neglects generative jet shapes, which may become relevant at a
future circular collider.
