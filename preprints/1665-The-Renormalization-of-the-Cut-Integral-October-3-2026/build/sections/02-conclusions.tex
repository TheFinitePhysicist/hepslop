\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects resummed sector decompositions, which may become
relevant at a future circular collider. Our results show that Monte
Carlo event generators trained on the quark/gluon dataset couple to
score-based generative models trained on ALEPH archival data. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with dipole subtraction schemes.
This unlocks the full potential of chatbots for new physics searches.
Extending this framework to set transformers trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset is an exciting direction for future work.
