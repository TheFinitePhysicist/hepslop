\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. The optical theorem exhibits a striking dependence on
$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. Our analysis neglects attention mechanisms
trained on Pythia samples, which may become relevant at the Future
Circular Collider. This sheds new light on one-loop collinear emissions.
Remarkably, the global significance saturates the local analytic sector
subtraction after unfolding.
