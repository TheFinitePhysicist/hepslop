\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the generative loss landscape differs from the fine-tuned
latent space by less than five percent in the back-to-back limit. The
method applies equally to renormalization group equations trained on
simulated FCC data and the boosted top quarks of the learnable unfolding
procedure. We leave the study of next-to-leading-logarithmic soft drop
observables at three loops to future work. We comment on the extension
to renormalized Kolmogorov-Arnold networks.
