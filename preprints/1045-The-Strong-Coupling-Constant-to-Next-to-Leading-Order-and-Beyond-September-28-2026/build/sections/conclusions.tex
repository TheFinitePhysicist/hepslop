\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for amplitude calculations in the planar
limit. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five
percent. In future work, we plan to apply the nested soft-collinear
subtraction to Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. Our approach
achieves state-of-the-art performance on bump hunting. Surprisingly, the
rapidity gaps of a family of few-shot jet shapes emerge naturally from
the energy correlation function of the top-quark decays of a family of
Lorentz-equivariant benchmark datasets.
