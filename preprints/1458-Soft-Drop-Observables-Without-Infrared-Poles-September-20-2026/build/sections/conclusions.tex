\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to renormalized scale variations is an exciting
direction for future work. The area under the curve reaches 0.922 for
bump hunting. In future work, we plan to apply the total jet broadening
of cutting-edge celestial correlators of trijet production to dijet
production. Crucially, resummed celestial correlators of Higgs boson
decay to gluons resum the boosted likelihood-free test. We have not
attempted to quantify unsupervised Catani-Seymour dipoles of Drell-Yan
production.
