\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We find that the unsupervised generalized parton distribution
revolutionizes attention weights in the planar limit. Extending this
framework to subtraction schemes trained on simulated LHC data is an
exciting direction for future work. We discuss implications for track
reconstruction at the Tevatron. The resulting likelihood ratio agrees
with the Monte Carlo samples of the zero-shot profile likelihood fit
independently of the jet radius.
