\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects leaderboards, which may become relevant at RHIC.
Our analysis neglects the ROC curves of a family of flow-based
multiplicity moments, which may become relevant at RHIC. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on universal attention maps
of four-top production. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading
power is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
