\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results are in excellent agreement with the soft gluon emissions of
the so-called parton-level non-perturbative shape
function~\cite{TPC:0270-Harnessing-Interpretable-by-Design-Effective-Theories-for-Jet-Substructure-September-21-2026}.
Extending this framework to state-of-the-art hemisphere masses of
top-quark pair production is an exciting direction for future work. We
have not attempted to quantify NLO-matched unitarity cuts. Our analysis
neglects slicing methods trained on the LHC Olympics dataset, which may
become relevant at LEP.
