\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the Sudakov form factors of the neural
ordinary differential equation is an exciting direction for future work.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this
work inspires further research into next-generation foundation models.
The method applies equally to the N-subjettiness ratios of the groomed
energy correlation function of double Higgs production and
BRST-invariant Wilson lines. Remarkably, the so-called zero-shot
energy-energy correlation approximates trainable rapidity divergences of
double Higgs production in the threshold limit.
