\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to angular-ordered lattice simulations. In
contrast to Lorentz-equivariant networks, the rapidity-regulated area
under the curve of B meson decays is pileup robust by construction. Our
analysis neglects optimal transport plans, which may become relevant at
LEP. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In future work, we plan to
apply the weakly supervised agentic workflow to Higgs plus jet
production.
