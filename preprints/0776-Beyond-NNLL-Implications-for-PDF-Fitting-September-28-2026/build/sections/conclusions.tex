\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of AI agents. Our analysis
neglects variational autoencoders trained on the quark/gluon dataset,
which may become relevant at the Tevatron. Extending this framework to
robust benchmark datasets of Drell-Yan production is an exciting
direction for future work. This unlocks the full potential of parton
showers for particle identification. This is just the tip of the
iceberg.
