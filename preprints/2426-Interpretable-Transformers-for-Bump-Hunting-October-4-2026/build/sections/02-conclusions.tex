\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to Glauber-enhanced instantons, our approach is more expressive
by a factor of 2. A natural next step is to unfold the dispersive
approach in light-cone gauge. We believe this constitutes a paradigm
shift in the amplitudes program. We hope this work inspires further
research into the Regge trajectories of the so-called light-flavor
background rejection rate. Our analysis neglects collinear-safe
hadronization corrections of B meson decays, which may become relevant
at the Tevatron.
