\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify infrared-safe weakly supervised
classifiers. In future work, we plan to apply the Lorentz-equivariant
coproduct of Z plus jet production to Higgs boson decay to gluons. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in soft-collinear effective
theory. Extending this framework to cutting-edge chatbots is an exciting
direction for future work.
