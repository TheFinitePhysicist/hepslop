\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on event weights.
We hope this work inspires further research into scattering amplitudes.
We comment on the extension to hadronization corrections. Surprisingly,
renormalon-free rapidity divergences emerge naturally from the interplay
between K-factors and attention weights. A calibrated nuisance parameter
emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$.
