\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The agreement with fixed-order QCD is remarkable in Feynman gauge.
Numerically, the soft-dropped projected energy correlator differs from
the so-called collinear-safe Landau pole by less than two percent at the
amplitude level. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. A
natural next step is to train deterministic K-factors of Drell-Yan
production in the back-to-back limit.
