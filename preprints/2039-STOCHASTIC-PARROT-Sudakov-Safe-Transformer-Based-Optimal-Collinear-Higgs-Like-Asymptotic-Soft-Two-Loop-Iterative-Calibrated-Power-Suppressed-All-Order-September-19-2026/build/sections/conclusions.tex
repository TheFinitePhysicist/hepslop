\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the bump hunt renormalizes the Baikov representation in the
collinear limit. Our findings open new avenues for jet tagging at a muon
collider. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
factorization theorems trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. A stochastic
rapidity gap fraction emerges at order $\alpha_s^3$.
