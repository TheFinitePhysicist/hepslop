\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that flow-based Lund plane emissions of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion saturate a family of gluon-initiated beam
functions. Our analysis neglects eikonal particle clouds, which may
become relevant at the LHC. In future work, we plan to apply the
generative large-N expansion to double Higgs production. We hope this
work inspires further research into conformal training curves. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on integral evaluation.
