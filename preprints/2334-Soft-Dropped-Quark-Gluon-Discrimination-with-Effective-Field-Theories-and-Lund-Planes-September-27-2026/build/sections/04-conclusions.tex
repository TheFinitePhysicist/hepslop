\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects likelihood-free self-supervised objectives, which
may become relevant at the CEPC. We hope this work inspires further
research into autoregressive models & trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV.
It would be interesting to combine this approach with energy flow
networks. Our analysis neglects approximate normalizing flows, which may
become relevant at the CEPC.
