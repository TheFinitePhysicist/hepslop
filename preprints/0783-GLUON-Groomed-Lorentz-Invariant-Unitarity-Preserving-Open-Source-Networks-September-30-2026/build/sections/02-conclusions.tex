\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of trainable beam functions of photoproduction at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with normalizing flows. The
treatment of BRST-invariant negative event weights is admittedly
heuristic. Extending this framework to rotationally symmetric lattice
simulations is an exciting direction for future work.
