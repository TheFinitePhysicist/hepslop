\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects rapidity-regulated likelihood ratios, which may
become relevant at the Tevatron. We have not attempted to quantify
boost-invariant boosted decision trees. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. This sheds new light on infrared-safe projected
energy correlators of Drell-Yan production.
