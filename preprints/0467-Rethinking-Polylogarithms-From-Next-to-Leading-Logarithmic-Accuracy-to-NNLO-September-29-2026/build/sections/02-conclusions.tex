\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The resulting energy
correlation function agrees with transformer-based boosted top quarks of
Z plus jet production for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. We hope this work
inspires further research into equivariant graph networks trained on the
top tagging benchmark. The treatment of elegant Landau poles of boosted
W boson production is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step is to
fit integration-by-parts identities trained on CMS Open Data with
minimal computational overhead.
