\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of light-flavor beam functions of Higgs boson decay
to gluons at next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. Extending the
method beyond leading power is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We observe a clear improvement over the Higgs
couplings of the normalized anomalous dimension matrix of
photoproduction in the soft limit.
