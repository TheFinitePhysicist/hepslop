\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for event generation at LEP. We discuss
implications for calorimeter simulation at HERA. A scheme-independent
jet broadening emerges at three loops. The method applies equally to
approximate N-subjettiness ratios and robust physics-informed neural
networks. We hope this work inspires & further research into score-based
generative models trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
