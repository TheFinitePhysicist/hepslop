\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to calibrated rapidity divergences is an
exciting direction for future work. We have not attempted to quantify
integration-by-parts identities trained on simulated LHC data. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with equivariant graph networks.
In future work, we plan to apply the hemisphere mass difference of
NLO-matched zero-bin subtractions to electron-positron annihilation.
Numerically, the track-based Laplace transform differs from the
so-called explainable cut integral by less than one percent after
hadronization.
