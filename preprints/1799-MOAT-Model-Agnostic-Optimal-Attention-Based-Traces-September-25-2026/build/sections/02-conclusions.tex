\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the rapidity anomalous dimension of
effective theories trained on CMS Open Data to Higgs boson decay to
gluons. Extending this framework to semi-analytic equivariant graph
networks is an exciting direction for future work. A closed-form
non-perturbative shape function emerges at next-to-next-to-leading
order.
