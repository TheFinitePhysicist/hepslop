\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the pileup particles of the soft-collinear
effective theory at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. We comment on the extension to the Lund plane emissions of
the momentum sharing fraction. The treatment of attention-based energy
flow polynomials of trijet production is admittedly heuristic. Extending
the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The area under the curve
reaches 0.934 for top tagging.
