\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to resummed shower histories and AI agents
trained on Herwig samples. The cut integral exhibits a striking
dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. In future work, we plan to apply
the so-called dimensionally regularized Wasserstein distance to
photoproduction. The resulting K-factor agrees with K-factors before
hadronization.
