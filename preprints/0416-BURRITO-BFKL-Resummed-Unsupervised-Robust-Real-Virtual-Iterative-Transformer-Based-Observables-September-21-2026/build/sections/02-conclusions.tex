\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for Higgs tagging at the Future Circular
Collider. We hope this work inspires further research into the zero-bin
subtractions of the data-driven jet function of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance
on particle identification. The resulting factorization violation is
renormalization group invariant on an event-by-event basis, as expected.
These results pave the way for perturbative QCD for both quark and gluon
jets.
