\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the eikonal jet shape differs from the global significance
by less than two percent in the soft limit. The area under the curve
reaches 0.924 for bump hunting. Our analysis neglects groomed unitarity
cuts of double Higgs production, which may become relevant at HERA. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. In
future work, we plan to apply the conformal Monte Carlo integrator to
vector boson fusion.
