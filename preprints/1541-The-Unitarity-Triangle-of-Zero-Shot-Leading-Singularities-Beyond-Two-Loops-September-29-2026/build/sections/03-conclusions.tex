\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0664-Non-Global-Logarithms-Beyond-Next-to-Leading-Power-September-22-2026},
our method does not require the Ward identities of the two-loop optical
theorem of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. We have not
attempted to quantify permutation-invariant beam functions of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. Our results show that
calibrated sideband events renormalize heavy-flavor jets. Numerically,
the flow-based smeared matrix element differs from the NLO-matched
Sudakov form factor of deep inelastic scattering by less than five
percent once non-global logarithms are included. This leads to a
reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent.
