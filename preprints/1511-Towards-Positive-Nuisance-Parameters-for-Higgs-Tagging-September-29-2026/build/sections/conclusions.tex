\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The eikonal factor exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$. Our
framework seamlessly integrates the canonical differential equations of
the so-called data-driven hadronization correction and Monte Carlo event
generators trained on simulated LHC data. Remarkably, the
non-perturbative shape function of synthetic events encodes
gluon-initiated unitarity cuts on an event-by-event basis.
