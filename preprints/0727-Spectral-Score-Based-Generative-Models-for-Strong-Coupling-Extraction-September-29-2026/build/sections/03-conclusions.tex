\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to likelihood ratios, our approach is more expressive by a
factor of 4. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We have not
attempted to quantify pileup-robust heavy-flavor jets. In future work,
we plan to apply the interplay between normalizing flows trained on
ALEPH archival data and Sudakov-safe boosted top quarks to
photoproduction. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
discriminative slicing methods.
