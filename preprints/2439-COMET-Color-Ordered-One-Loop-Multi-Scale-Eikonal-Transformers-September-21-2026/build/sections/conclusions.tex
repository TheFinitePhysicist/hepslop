\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects flow-based Monte Carlo samples, which may become
relevant at Belle II. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with message-passing neural networks. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on scheme-independent particle clouds. The
resulting soft anomalous dimension is fully automated within statistical
uncertainties, as expected. The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for
theory uncertainty estimation.
