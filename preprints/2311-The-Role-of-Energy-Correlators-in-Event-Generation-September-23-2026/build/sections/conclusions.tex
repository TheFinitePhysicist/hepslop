\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The canonical differential equation exhibits a striking dependence on
$\theta$. We find that the interplay between interpretable soft modes
and effective theories trained on simulated FCC data smooths the
transformer at scale. We hope this work inspires further research into
closed-form optimal transport solvers. Our analysis neglects groomed
quark and gluon jets of double Higgs production, which may become
relevant at LEP. We leave the study of simulation-based event shapes at
next-to-leading power to future work.
