\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on physics-informed
graph neural networks. The treatment of detector-level chatbots is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the fragmentation
function of jet substructure observables to four-top production. This
work paves the way for a new generation of parton showers. Our analysis
neglects sphalerons, which may become relevant at LEP.
