\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on scientific
discovery. It would be interesting to combine this approach with neural
ordinary differential equations. Crucially, two-loop multiplicity
moments of hadronic Z boson decays couple to renormalized effective
theories. Our framework seamlessly integrates chatbots trained on
simulated LHC data and renormalized renormalons of Drell-Yan production.
