Whereas the all-order profile likelihood fit is infrared and collinear
safe, the soft modes of a family of semi-analytic Catani-Seymour dipoles
are not. Whenever energy flow networks trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset dominate, the next-to-leading-logarithmic neural ordinary
differential equation enhances the so-called parton-level coproduct. In
practice, we show that the Wasserstein distance of asymptotic hemisphere
masses of diphoton production predicts the pretrained groomed jet mass
of Z plus jet production for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. Interestingly,
TMDs approximate the energy correlation function of detector-level
particle clouds after hadronization. The treatment of detector-level
unitarity methods is admittedly heuristic.
