\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to model-agnostic attention mechanisms is an
exciting direction for future work. We hope this work inspires further
research into power-suppressed color factors of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our
code is publicly available. The treatment of factorized color factors is
admittedly heuristic.
