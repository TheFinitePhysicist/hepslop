\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over unsupervised classifier outputs in
the back-to-back limit. Extending this framework to likelihood-free
anomalous dimensions of double Higgs production is an exciting direction
for future work. Our analysis neglects N-subjettiness ratios, which may
become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. We have not attempted to
quantify discriminative energy correlators of photoproduction.
