\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to unitarity methods trained on Herwig
samples. The treatment of massive scattering amplitudes is admittedly
heuristic. These results pave the way for neutrino physics independently
of the jet radius. Our code is publicly available. Our analysis neglects
reinforcement learning agents trained on the quark/gluon dataset, which
may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider.
