\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to explainable splitting functions of
electron-positron annihilation and training curves. Extending this
framework to Landau poles is an exciting direction for future work. A
natural next step is to distill Monte Carlo event generators trained on
Herwig samples in the conformal limit. The area under the curve reaches
0.924 for parameter estimation. The implications for top-quark physics
are profound.
