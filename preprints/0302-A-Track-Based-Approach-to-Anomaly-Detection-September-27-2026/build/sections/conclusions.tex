\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of hemisphere masses is admittedly heuristic. The area
under the curve reaches 0.943 for PDF fitting. Compared to the form
factors of the soft function of cutting-edge Feynman integrals, our
approach is more accurate by a factor of 3. A permutation-invariant
profile likelihood emerges at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
