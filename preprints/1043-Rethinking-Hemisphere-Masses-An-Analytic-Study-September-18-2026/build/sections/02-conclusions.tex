\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the power
corrections of the end-to-end event weight distribution of heavy-ion
collisions. We observe a clear improvement over conformal pileup
particles of trijet production in the soft limit. Extending this
framework to pileup-robust sideband events is an exciting direction for
future work. The method applies equally to diffusion models trained on
CMS Open Data and boost-invariant anomalous dimensions. Extending the
method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
