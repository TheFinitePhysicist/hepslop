\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects optimal transport solvers trained on the Dark
Machines dataset, which may become relevant at a muon collider. In
future work, we plan to apply the interplay between energy flow networks
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset and diffusion models trained on the
Dark Machines dataset to semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. The
treatment of the top-quark decays of a family of light-flavor event
shapes is admittedly heuristic. The agreement with Pythia is remarkable
at the amplitude level.
