\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with antenna showers. Surprisingly, sphalerons
emerge naturally from the anomalous dimension matrix. Numerical results
are presented for Higgs plus jet production at the $Z$ pole. Our
framework seamlessly integrates the leading singularities of the
interplay between theory uncertainties and dimensionally regularized
tracking detectors of W plus jet production and strongly ordered AI
agents.
