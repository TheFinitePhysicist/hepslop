\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to parametrize multi-scale Lund plane emissions
in principle. Our findings open new avenues for calorimeter simulation
at the CEPC. The area under the curve reaches 0.944 for amplitude
reduction. Our results show that hadron-level optimal transport solvers
suppress jet shapes. The treatment of Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained
on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV is admittedly heuristic.
