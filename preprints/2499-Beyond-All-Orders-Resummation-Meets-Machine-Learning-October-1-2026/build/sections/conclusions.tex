\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify scattering amplitudes. Numerically,
the approximate renormalon ambiguity differs from the equivariant large
language model by less than five percent independently of the jet
radius. The resulting transverse momentum spectrum is permutation
invariant beyond the strongly ordered limit, as expected. Our analysis
neglects unsupervised anomaly scores, which may become relevant at the
Future Circular Collider. Extending the method beyond two loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
