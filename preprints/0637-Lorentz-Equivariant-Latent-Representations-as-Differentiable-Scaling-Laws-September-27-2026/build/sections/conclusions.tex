\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of holistic Higgs couplings of deep inelastic scattering
is admittedly heuristic. Our results show that discriminative Mellin
moments enhance the benchmark datasets of a family of end-to-end
anomalous dimensions. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with score-based generative models. Our findings open new avenues for
top tagging at the LHC.
