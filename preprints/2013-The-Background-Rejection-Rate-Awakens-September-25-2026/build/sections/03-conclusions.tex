\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of slicing methods trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV
at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Our analysis
neglects attention mechanisms trained on CMS Open Data, which may become
relevant at a muon collider. We discuss implications for integral
evaluation at RHIC. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
