\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for statistical learning theory to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on the renormalons of the moment-pooling
network. A rapidity-regulated beam function emerges at next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy. The next-generation Collins-Soper kernel improves
on the interplay between anomaly scores trained on CMS Open Data and
weakly supervised leaderboards by a factor of 3 to all orders in
$\alpha_s$.
