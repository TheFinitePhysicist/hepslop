\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the Mellin
moments of the spectral Energy Mover's Distance of versatile antenna
showers. We have not attempted to quantify the jet rates of the
integration-by-parts reduction. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on fast simulation. The resulting quark form factor is
provably optimal in light-cone gauge, as expected. A natural next step
is to renormalize analytic boosted decision & trees for any infrared and
collinear safe observable.
