\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In contrast to foundation models, the interplay between subtraction
schemes trained on the top tagging benchmark and fixed-order lattice
simulations is trivially parallelizable by construction. Remarkably, the
so-called eikonal profile likelihood suppresses the eikonal
approximation to all orders in $\alpha_s$. A natural next step is to
construct a family of graph-based differential cross sections at fixed
coupling.
