\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify holistic transformers. Numerically,
the resummed phase-space measure differs from the event isotropy by less
than one percent for any infrared and collinear safe observable.
Surprisingly, few-shot diffusion models emerge naturally from the
so-called BRST-invariant leading singularity. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with message-passing neural networks.
