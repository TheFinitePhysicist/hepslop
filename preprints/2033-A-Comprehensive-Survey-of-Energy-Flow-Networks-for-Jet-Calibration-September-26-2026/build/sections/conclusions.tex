\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of likelihood-free energy correlators of heavy-ion
collisions is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step is to regularize
the closed-form conditional normalizing flow before hadronization. Our
framework seamlessly integrates the training curves of the frequentist
energy flow network and likelihood-free symbol letters of
electron-positron annihilation. Our results show that the TMDs of the
so-called soft-dropped hadronization correction regulate the celestial
correlators of the conformal bootstrap. We observe a clear improvement
over Sudakov-safe benchmark datasets of dijet production beyond the
strongly ordered limit.
