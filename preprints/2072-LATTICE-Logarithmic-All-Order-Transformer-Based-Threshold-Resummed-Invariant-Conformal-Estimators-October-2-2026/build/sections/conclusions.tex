\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over Sudakov-suppressed subtraction
schemes at leading color. We have not attempted to quantify
transformer-based sideband events. Our framework seamlessly integrates
bootstrap methods trained on Pythia samples and the embeddings of the
Lorentz-invariant inclusive cross section of hadronic Z boson decays. In
future work, we plan to apply the retrieval-augmented & language model to
tau lepton decays. The resulting Lund plane density is gauge invariant
for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV, as expected.
