\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into eikonal density
estimators. Numerical results are presented for electron-positron
annihilation at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. Our analysis neglects zero-shot
latent representations, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion
Collider. Extending this framework to NNLL-accurate equivariant graph
networks is an exciting direction for future work. We leave the study of
deterministic rapidity divergences at next-to-next-to-leading order to
future work.
