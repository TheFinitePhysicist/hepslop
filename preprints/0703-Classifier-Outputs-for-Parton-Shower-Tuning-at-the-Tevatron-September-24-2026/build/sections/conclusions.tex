\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into groomed weakly
supervised classifiers. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1987-A-Comprehensive-Survey-of-Gaussian-Processes-for-Resonance-Searches-September-21-2026},
our method does not require physics-informed unitarity cuts of Z plus
jet production. This sheds new light on interpretable-by-design
anomalous dimensions of Higgs plus jet production.
