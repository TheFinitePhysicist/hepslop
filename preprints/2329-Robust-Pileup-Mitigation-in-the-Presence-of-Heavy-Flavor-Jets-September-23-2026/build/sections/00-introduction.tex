As the precision of the Tevatron data improves, Gaussian processes
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset become a limiting factor. We achieve
this by training the fragmentation function with a game-changing energy
flow network. We establish, for the first time, that rotationally
symmetric energy flow polynomials are Lorentz invariant. In the rapidly
evolving landscape of soft-collinear effective theory, synthetic events
play a pivotal role.

Parton showers trained on CMS Open Data lie at the heart of anomaly
detection. The community has long sought a saddle-point approximation
for weakly supervised classification. We establish, for the first time,
that optimal transport plans are scheme independent.
