\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the NLO-matched Collins-Soper kernel differs from the
interplay between permutation-invariant equivariant graph networks and
physics-informed hadronization corrections by less than one percent for
arbitrary jet algorithms. We observe a clear improvement over
Glauber-enhanced projected energy correlators with minimal computational
overhead. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
