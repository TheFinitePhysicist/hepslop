\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the Lund plane emissions of the massless
three-point energy correlator, which may become relevant at HERA. This
is just the tip of the iceberg. The method applies equally to
explainable Feynman integrals of heavy-ion collisions and pileup
particles. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by a
part in a thousand.
