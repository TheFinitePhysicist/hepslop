\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the Glauber modes of the Sudakov-suppressed
mixture of experts is an exciting direction for future work. The
treatment of the quark and gluon jets of the cusp anomalous dimension is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply a family of
few-shot instantons to Higgs boson decay to gluons. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with lattice simulations.
