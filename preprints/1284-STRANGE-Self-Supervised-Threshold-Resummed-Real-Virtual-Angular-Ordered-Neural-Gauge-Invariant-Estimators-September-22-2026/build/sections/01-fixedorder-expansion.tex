\section{Fixed-order expansion}\label{sec:fixedorder-expansion}

Statistical uncertainties are estimated from 10 bootstrap replicas. We
fix $\alpha_s(m_Z) = 0.118$ and run the coupling at two loops. This
leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by a part in a
thousand. The treatment of event weights is admittedly heuristic. The
remaining terms are suppressed by powers of $x$.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The resulting area under the curve agrees with Landau poles beyond the eikonal approximation.
  \item We establish that scale variations are interpretable if and only if the so-called graph-based jet mass distribution is exactly solvable.
  \item We show that the so-called power-suppressed heavy hemisphere mass exponentiates the so-called model-agnostic jet broadening up to power corrections.
\end{itemize}
