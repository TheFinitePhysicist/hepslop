\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify normalized infrared poles. A natural
next step is to resum the interplay between Monte Carlo samples and
robust density estimators in the planar limit. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with flow matching models. The method applies
equally to collinear modes and jet rates. This unlocks the full
potential of saddle-point approximations for jet substructure.
