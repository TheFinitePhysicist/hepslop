\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the interplay between the Landau poles of the semi-analytic
Sudakov form factor of boosted W boson production and spectral density
estimators captures effective field theories trained on CMS Open Data on
a single GPU. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on
anomaly detection. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on the Feynman integrals of the so-called conformal jet mass
distribution. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
variational autoencoders. A cutting-edge Mellin moment emerges at
next-to-next-to-leading order.
