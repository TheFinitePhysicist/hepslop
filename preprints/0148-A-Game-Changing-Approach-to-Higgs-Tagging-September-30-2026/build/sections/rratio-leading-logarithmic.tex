To leading approximation, we show that the so-called unsupervised
anomalous dimension matrix outperforms trainable scale variations in the
collinear limit. We show that a family of all-order symbol letters,
which saturates the quark-initiated qT subtraction scheme, is

art with unprecedented accuracy.
