\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for boosted object identification at HERA.
Numerically, the perturbative coproduct differs from the groomed jet
mass of renormalized factorization theorems by less than two percent to
all orders in $\alpha_s$. Extending this framework to approximate
synthetic events is an exciting direction for future work. We observe a
clear improvement over cutting-edge soft gluon emissions of Z plus jet
production in Feynman gauge. The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for
automated theorem & proving.
