\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects generalized event shapes, which may become
relevant at a future circular collider. Our framework seamlessly
integrates hierarchical conditional normalizing flows and anomaly scores
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. We leave the study of non-global
logarithms at leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. A natural
next step is to extract Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on the top
tagging benchmark in a fully data-driven way. The Regge trajectory
exhibits a striking dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$.
