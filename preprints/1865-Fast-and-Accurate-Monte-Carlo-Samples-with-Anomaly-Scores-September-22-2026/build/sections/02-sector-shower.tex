To regulate the infrared poles, we work in $d = 4 - 2\epsilon$
dimensions. Despite their success, conditional normalizing flows remain
largely black boxes. Modern analyses at the Tevatron rely heavily on
smeared unitarity cuts of photoproduction. To date, no definition of the
state-of-the-art antenna shower is known to be boost invariant at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading power is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding.
