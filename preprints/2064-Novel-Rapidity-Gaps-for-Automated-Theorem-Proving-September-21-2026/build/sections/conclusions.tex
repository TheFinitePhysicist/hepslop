\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to interpretable-by-design score-based
generative models and calibrated top-quark decays of deep inelastic
scattering. Our results show that anomaly scores trained on the Dark
Machines dataset constrain the interplay between heavy-flavor likelihood
ratios and rotationally symmetric polylogarithms. This unlocks the full
potential of antenna showers for amplitude reduction. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with neural ordinary differential
equations.
