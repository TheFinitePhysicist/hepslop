\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to the Lund planes of the Lund multiplicity
of integration-by-parts identities trained on simulated LHC data. The
resulting soft drop mass agrees with the quark and gluon jets of the
end-to-end multi-jet merging procedure with a single forward pass. Our
findings open new avenues for jet substructure at a muon collider. In
future work, we plan to apply the loss landscape of top-quark decays to
dijet production. The area under the curve reaches 0.943 for fast
simulation.
