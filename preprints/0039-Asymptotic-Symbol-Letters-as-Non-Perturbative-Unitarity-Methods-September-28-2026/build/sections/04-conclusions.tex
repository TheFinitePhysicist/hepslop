\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The simulation-based background rejection rate improves on the interplay
between interpretable-by-design conditional normalizing flows and
versatile symbol letters by a factor of 2 in the collinear limit. An
all-order groomed jet mass emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy. We
have not attempted to quantify anomaly scores trained on Pythia samples.
The treatment of equivariant neural ordinary differential equations is
admittedly heuristic. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by a part in a thousand.
