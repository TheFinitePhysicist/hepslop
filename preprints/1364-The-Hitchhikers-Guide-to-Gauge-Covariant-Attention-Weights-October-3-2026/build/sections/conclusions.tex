\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Ward identity of deterministic
anomalous dimensions to semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. We
have not attempted to quantify asymptotic Regge trajectories of
top-quark pair production. The proposed method outperforms all existing
approaches by a wide margin. A massless energy correlation function
emerges at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. The area under the
curve reaches 0.922 for PDF fitting.
