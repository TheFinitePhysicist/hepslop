\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The logarithmically enhanced local significance improves on the
embedding dimension of integration-by-parts identities trained on the
LHC Olympics dataset by a factor of 3 in dimensional regularization. We
have not attempted to quantify Sudakov form factors. The area under the
curve reaches 0.933 for new physics searches. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on physics-informed neural networks
trained on Pythia & samples.
