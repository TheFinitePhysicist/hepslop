\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The latent space exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$. The
resulting inclusive cross section is scale invariant up to terms of
order $\alpha_s^3$, as expected. Surprisingly, infrared-safe parton
distribution functions emerge naturally from the dispersive approach.
Remarkably, the multi-jet merging procedure matches differential
equations trained on Herwig samples at detector level.
