\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of simulation-based dipole subtraction schemes at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Extending the
method beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Extending this framework to two-loop shower
histories of dijet production is an exciting direction for future work.
We observe a clear improvement over diffusion models trained on Pythia
samples at scale. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:2059-Bridging-the-Gap-Between-Quantum-Computing-and-String-Theory-September-24-2026},
our method does not require rapidity-regulated Sudakov form factors.
