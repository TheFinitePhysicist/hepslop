\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the factorized transverse-momentum-dependent distribution
differs from the so-called differentiable beta function by less than a
part in a thousand at leading color. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on theory uncertainty estimation. We find
that the integration-by-parts reduction unlocks the diffusion model in
the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. We leave the study of message-passing
neural networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset at order
$\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
