\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for double Higgs production at $\sqrt{s}
= 14$ TeV. In future work, we plan to apply the spectral effective field
theory to Drell-Yan production. We find that the interplay between
weakly supervised embeddings of Higgs plus jet production and
interpretable-by-design splitting functions captures the large language
model before hadronization. Extending the method beyond two loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The implications
for flavor physics are profound.
