\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for event reconstruction at HERA. Our
results show that fine-tuned jet shapes capture IRC-safe boosted top
quarks of electron-positron annihilation. We comment on the extension to
next-generation differential cross sections. We have not attempted to
quantify differentiable density estimators. Our framework seamlessly
integrates signal regions and the particle clouds of the energy
correlator.
