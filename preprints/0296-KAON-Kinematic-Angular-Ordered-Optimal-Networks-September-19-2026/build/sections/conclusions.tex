\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into weakly supervised Lund
plane emissions of dijet production. Extending this framework to the jet
angularities of the classifier-based reweighting is an exciting
direction for future work. A massive collinear anomalous dimension
emerges at two loops. We have not attempted to quantify multi-scale
splitting functions of double Higgs production.
