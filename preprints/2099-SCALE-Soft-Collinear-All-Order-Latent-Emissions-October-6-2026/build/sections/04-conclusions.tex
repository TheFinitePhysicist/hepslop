\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on rapidity
divergences. Our code is publicly available. In future work, we plan to
apply the so-called frequentist jet angularity to diphoton production.
The resulting receiver operating characteristic is provably optimal for
all values of $z_{\rm cut}$, as expected.
