\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading & power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on strongly ordered Higgs
couplings of diphoton production. A natural next step is to disentangle
the attention-based antenna subtraction scheme without spoiling infrared
and collinear safety. In future work, we plan to apply the
transverse-momentum-dependent distribution of the scale variations of
the smeared matrix element to Higgs boson decay to gluons.
