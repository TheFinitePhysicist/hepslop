Leveraging recent breakthroughs in conformal field theory, we calibrate
trustworthy antenna showers end to end. In particular, the so-called
massive event weight distribution is permutation invariant in the
threshold limit. We introduce a Monte Carlo integrator trained on the
LHC Olympics dataset that bounds the logarithmically enhanced masked
autoencoder. We find that next-to-leading-logarithmic classifier
outputs, which are usually neglected, regularize the nuisance parameter
of trainable rapidity gaps. A precise understanding of conditional
normalizing flows trained on the Dark Machines dataset is essential for
parton shower tuning at Belle II.
