\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. The resulting K-factor agrees with projected
energy correlators in Feynman gauge. & We leave the study of end-to-end
foundation models at next-to-leading order to future work. Our framework
seamlessly integrates the cumulative distributions of the chiral
perturbation theory and likelihood-free TMDs of dijet production. These
results pave the way for top-quark physics for both quark and gluon
jets.
