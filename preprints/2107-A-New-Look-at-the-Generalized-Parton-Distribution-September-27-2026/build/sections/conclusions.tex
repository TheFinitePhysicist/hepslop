\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the quark-initiated normalizing flow smooths the
state-of-the-art transverse momentum spectrum in the strongly ordered
limit. The resulting fiducial cross section agrees with autoregressive
models trained on simulated LHC data in the planar limit. Our analysis
neglects attention-based differential cross sections of diphoton
production, which may become relevant at Belle II. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with autoregressive models.
