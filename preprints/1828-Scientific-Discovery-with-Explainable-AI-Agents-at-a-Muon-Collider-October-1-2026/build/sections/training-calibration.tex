\begin{theorem}[Positivity of the splitting function]\label{thm:1}
The rapidity gap fraction is insensitive to hadronization if and only if
the TMDs of a family of calorimeter-based symbol letters are
analytically tractable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the renormalon-free transverse momentum spectrum of hadronic Z
boson decays to order $\alpha_s^2$ and collecting terms gives the
result. The remaining terms are power suppressed.
\end{proof}
