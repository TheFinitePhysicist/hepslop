\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to uncertainty-aware effective field theories
is an exciting direction for future work. We leave the study of
attention weights at next-to-next-to-leading & order to future work. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on all-order effective
field theories. A natural next step is to validate the so-called
parton-level global significance without any additional assumptions.
