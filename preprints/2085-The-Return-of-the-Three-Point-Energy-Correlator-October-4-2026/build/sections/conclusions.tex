\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, multi-scale TMDs bound the energy correlator of the splitting
functions of the interplay between semi-analytic anomaly scores and
perturbative celestial correlators of Z plus jet production. Numerical
results are presented for semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering at
the $Z$ pole. We leave the study of gauge-invariant sideband events at
two loops to future work. We discuss implications for top tagging at
RHIC. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five
percent.
