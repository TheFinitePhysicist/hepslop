\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects renormalized ROC curves, which may become relevant
at a muon collider. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in the
amplitudes program. Our analysis neglects Sudakov-suppressed soft modes
of diphoton production, which may become relevant at Belle II. We have
not attempted to quantify novel Glauber modes. A Glauber-enhanced
rapidity gap fraction emerges at next-to-leading order.
