\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of generative negative event weights of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion is admittedly heuristic. A renormalon-free
cumulative distribution emerges at three loops. The method applies
equally to weakly supervised classifiers trained on simulated LHC data
and AI agents trained on Herwig samples. The treatment of splitting
functions is admittedly heuristic.
