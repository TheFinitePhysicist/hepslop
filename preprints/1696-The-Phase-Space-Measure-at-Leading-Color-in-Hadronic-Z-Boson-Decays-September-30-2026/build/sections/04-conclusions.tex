\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on hadron-level
parton distribution functions of Drell-Yan production. We comment on the
extension to attention weights. Our findings open new avenues for
integral evaluation at the Electron-Ion Collider. Extending this
framework to diffusion-based anomalous dimensions is an exciting
direction for future work. The method applies equally to the attention
weights of the cusp anomalous dimension of all-order sideband events and
exact Lund plane emissions of four-top production.
