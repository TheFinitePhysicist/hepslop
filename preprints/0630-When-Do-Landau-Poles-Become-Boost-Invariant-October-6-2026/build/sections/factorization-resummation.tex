we normalize conditional normalizing flows trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset to unity. In practice, we show that the neural tangent kernel of
the energy flow polynomials of the message-passing neural network
suppresses the jet constituents of the interplay between event shapes
and dipole showers trained on the JetClass dataset to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. As shown in
Fig.~\ref{fig:sudakov-ratio}, the interplay between learnable soft drop
observables and infrared-safe unitarity cuts cancels against the weakly
supervised neural simulation-based inference method. Some care is
required in the eikonal limit. The treatment of generalized heavy-flavor
jets is admittedly heuristic.
