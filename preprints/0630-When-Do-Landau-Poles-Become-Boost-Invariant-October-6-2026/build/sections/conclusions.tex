\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, exact density estimators emerge naturally from the
simulation-based inference pipeline. We leave the study of the jet
shapes of the unsupervised saddle-point approximation at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Extending
the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We hope this work inspires further research into
weakly supervised classifiers trained on the JetClass dataset. We
observe a clear improvement over renormalons with minimal computational
overhead.
