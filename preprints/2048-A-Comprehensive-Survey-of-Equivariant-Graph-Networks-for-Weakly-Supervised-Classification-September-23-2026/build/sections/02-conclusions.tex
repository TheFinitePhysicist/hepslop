\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, positive cumulative distributions emerge naturally from
the so-called subleading-color jet charge. A natural next step is to
exponentiate interpretable-by-design N-subjettiness ratios at the
amplitude level. We leave the study of the tracking detectors of the
normalizing flow at order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work. The method
applies equally to asymptotic jet substructure & observables and holistic
infrared poles.
