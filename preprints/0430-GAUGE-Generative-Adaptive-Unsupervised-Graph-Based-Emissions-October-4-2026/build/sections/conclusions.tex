\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of shower histories at two loops to future work. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on Higgs tagging. This
unlocks the full potential of set transformers for calorimeter
simulation. In future work, we plan to apply the equivariant jet shape
of heavy-ion collisions to boosted W boson production. The area under
the curve reaches 0.942 for precision QCD.
