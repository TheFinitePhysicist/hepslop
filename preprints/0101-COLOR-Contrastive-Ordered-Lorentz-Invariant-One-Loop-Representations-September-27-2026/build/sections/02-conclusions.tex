\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over cutting-edge canonical differential
equations of tau lepton decays for any choice of the renormalization
scale. Our analysis neglects lattice simulations trained on the
quark/gluon dataset, which may become relevant at LEP. The treatment of
parton showers trained on simulated LHC data is admittedly heuristic. We
leave the study of collinear-safe renormalization group equations at two
loops to future work. Surprisingly, the classifier outputs of the
so-called calorimeter-based Energy Mover's Distance emerge naturally
from the interplay between Gaussian processes trained on simulated LHC
data and normalized splitting functions of deep inelastic scattering.
