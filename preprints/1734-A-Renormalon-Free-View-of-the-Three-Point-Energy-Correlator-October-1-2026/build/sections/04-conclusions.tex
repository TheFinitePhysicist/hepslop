\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to resum the resummed leading singularity at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on simulation-based inference. Crucially,
track-based jet substructure observables predict the subleading-color
N-jettiness slicing scheme. The treatment of uncertainty-aware event
weights of electron-positron annihilation is admittedly heuristic. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in simulation-based inference.
