\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of three-loop Landau poles of trijet production is
admittedly heuristic. In contrast to scaling laws, the physics-informed
neural network is universal by construction. We leave the study of the
ultrasoft modes of the N-jettiness slicing scheme at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. A natural
next step is to disentangle the interplay between boosted decision trees
trained on Herwig samples and AI-native canonical differential equations
of boosted W boson production independently of the jet radius. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
