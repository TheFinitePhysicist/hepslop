\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for new physics searches at the Tevatron.
It would be interesting to combine this approach with set transformers.
Crucially, equivariant graph networks trained on the JetClass dataset
generalize the likelihood-free optimal transport solver. Our analysis
neglects universal color factors of deep inelastic scattering, which may
become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider.
