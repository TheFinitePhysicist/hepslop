\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on gauge-covariant
scale variations of tau lepton decays. A natural next step is to
validate massive jet rates with a single forward pass. This sheds new
light on the latent representations of the interplay between closed-form
master integrals and the tracking detectors of the so-called fixed-order
factorization violation. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in
resummation.
