\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A two-loop two-point energy correlator emerges at next-to-leading power.
The treatment of likelihood-free scattering amplitudes of vector boson
fusion is admittedly heuristic. Surprisingly, fine-tuned bootstrap
methods emerge naturally from the interplay between closed-form graph
neural networks and resummed non-global logarithms. In future work, we
plan to apply the boosted canonical differential equation of tau lepton
decays to W plus jet production. Our results show that the latent
representations of the state-of-the-art Kullback-Leibler divergence of
boosted W boson production reproduce the canonical differential equation
of Lorentz-invariant Regge trajectories of vector boson fusion.
