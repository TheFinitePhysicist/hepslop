\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for Higgs plus jet production at
$\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. Extending this framework to diffusion models
trained on Herwig samples is an exciting direction for future work.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We have not
attempted to quantify loss landscapes. These results pave the way for
machine learning for particle physics independently of the jet radius.
