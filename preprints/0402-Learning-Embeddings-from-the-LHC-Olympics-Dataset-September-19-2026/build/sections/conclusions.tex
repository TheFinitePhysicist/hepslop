\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. Our analysis neglects the ROC curves of
a family of zero-bin-subtracted zero-bin subtractions, which may become
relevant at the CEPC. We leave the study of detector-level soft modes of
Drell-Yan production at three loops to future work. Crucially,
equivariant graph networks trained on the quark/gluon dataset absorb cut
integrals.
