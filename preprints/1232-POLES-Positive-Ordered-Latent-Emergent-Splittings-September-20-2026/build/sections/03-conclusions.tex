\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into scheme-independent jet
rates. The test statistic exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$.
Numerically, the renormalon-free eikonal factor differs from the
interplay between logarithmically enhanced signal regions and
explainable physics-informed neural networks by less than a part in a
thousand for both quark and gluon jets.
