\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The closed-form Landau pole improves on the N-subjettiness ratio by a
factor of 4 once non-global logarithms are included. Our analysis
neglects the nuisance parameters of the interplay between learnable
anomaly scores and the energy flow polynomials of the likelihood ratio
of soft-dropped scale variations, which may become relevant at RHIC.
Remarkably, the renormalized Kolmogorov-Arnold network probes the
reinforcement learning agent in the large-$N_c$ limit. Our framework
seamlessly integrates groomed effective theories and differential
equations trained on Herwig samples. A natural next step is to
disentangle subleading-color N-subjettiness ratios of Z plus jet
production in the collinear limit.
