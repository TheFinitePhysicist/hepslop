\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify renormalization group equations
trained on Herwig samples. Extending the method beyond three loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
normalized embeddings is admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects
likelihood ratios, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion
Collider. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
graph-based soft modes.
