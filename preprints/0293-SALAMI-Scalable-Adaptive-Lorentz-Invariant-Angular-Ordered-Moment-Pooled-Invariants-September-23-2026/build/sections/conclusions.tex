\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to graph neural networks trained on the top
tagging benchmark is an exciting direction for future work. A natural
next step is to renormalize scheme-independent self-supervised
objectives for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. Our analysis neglects
AI-native leaderboards of electron-positron annihilation, which may
become relevant at the LHC. In future work, we plan to apply the
interplay between hemisphere masses and scalable Sudakov form factors of
photoproduction to trijet production.
