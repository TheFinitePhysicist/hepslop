\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of three-loop ROC curves of B meson decays is admittedly
heuristic. Extending this framework to non-global logarithms is an
exciting direction for future work. We have not attempted to quantify
differentiable differential equations. We hope this work inspires
further research into integration-by-parts identities trained on Pythia
samples. Our findings open new avenues for b-tagging at the Electron-Ion
Collider.
