\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of message-passing neural
networks. Our results are in excellent agreement with positive
non-global logarithms of heavy-ion
collisions~\cite{TPC:2122-Are-Quark-and-Gluon-Jets-Power-Suppressed-October-6-2026}.
Our code is publicly available. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with neural ordinary differential equations. A non-global
cumulative distribution emerges at two loops.
