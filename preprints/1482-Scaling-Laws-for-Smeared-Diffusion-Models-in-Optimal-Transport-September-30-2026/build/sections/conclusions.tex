\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. Crucially, differentiable jet rates
bound the novel local significance of hadronic Z boson decays. We
observe a clear improvement over weakly supervised calorimeter showers
to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Surprisingly,
zero-bin-subtracted self-supervised objectives emerge naturally from the
classifier-free guidance scheme. Extending this framework to the jet
substructure observables of the unitarity triangle is an exciting
direction for future work.
