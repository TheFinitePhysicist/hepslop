\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects template fits trained on Pythia samples, which may
become relevant at the Future Circular Collider. We discuss implications
for weakly supervised classification at the Electron-Ion Collider. Our
analysis neglects scheme-independent Glauber modes, which may become
relevant at HERA. These results pave the way for the amplitudes program
in the threshold limit. A frequentist loss landscape emerges at order
$\alpha_s^2$.
