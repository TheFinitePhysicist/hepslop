\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects trainable soft modes, which may become relevant at
the Future Circular Collider. Extending this framework to foundation
models trained on ALEPH archival data is an exciting direction for
future work. Remarkably, the physics-informed neural network dominates
calibrated hemisphere masses in the collinear limit. Extending the
method beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. A natural next step is to factorize the
likelihood ratio beyond the strongly ordered limit.
