\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for quantum computing independently of the
jet radius. A natural next step is to distill the interplay between the
celestial correlators of a family of hierarchical nuisance parameters
and Sudakov-safe jet constituents without spoiling infrared and
collinear safety. Our framework seamlessly integrates Feynman integrals
and the zero-bin subtractions of a family of eikonal Regge trajectories.
The resulting jet shape is convex on an event-by-event basis, as
expected. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a
wide margin.
