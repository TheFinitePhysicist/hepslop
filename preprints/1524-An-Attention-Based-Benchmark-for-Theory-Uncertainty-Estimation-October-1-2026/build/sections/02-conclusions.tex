\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for the AI revolution for both quark and
gluon jets. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
versatile soft drop observables. The area under the curve reaches 0.934
for parameter estimation. The Ward identity exhibits a striking
dependence on $x$. The treatment of weakly supervised classifiers
trained on simulated FCC data is admittedly heuristic.
