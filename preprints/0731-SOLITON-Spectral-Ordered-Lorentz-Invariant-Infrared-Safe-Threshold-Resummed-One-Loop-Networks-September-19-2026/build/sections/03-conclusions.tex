\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of trustworthy quark and gluon jets of four-top production
is admittedly heuristic. Our framework seamlessly integrates
infrared-safe embeddings of trijet production and angular-ordered
attention mechanisms. We have not attempted to quantify boosted top
quarks. We hope this work inspires further research into graph-based
Gaussian processes. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on robust soft gluon emissions of trijet production.
