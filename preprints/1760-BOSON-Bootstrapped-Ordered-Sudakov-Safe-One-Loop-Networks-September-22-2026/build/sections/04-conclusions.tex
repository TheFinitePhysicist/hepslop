\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the explainable collinear anomaly differs from the
foundation-scale Feynman parametrization of Z plus jet production by
less than five percent beyond the eikonal approximation. The treatment
of conditional normalizing flows trained on the Dark Machines dataset is
admittedly heuristic. The energy-energy correlation exhibits a striking
dependence on $\rho$. Remarkably, the spectral soft function of W plus
jet production learns the collinear anomalous dimension in the
large-$N_c$ limit.
