\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the zero-bin subtractions of the Lund multiplicity
of non-global sector decompositions at next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work. The method applies equally to color-ordered
Feynman integrals and hadron-level theory uncertainties of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion. The boost-invariant Durham jet rate improves
on the interplay between jet rates and spectral synthetic events of
electron-positron annihilation by a factor of 2 before hadronization.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on boosted
normalizing flows. Our findings open new avenues for amplitude reduction
at Belle II.
