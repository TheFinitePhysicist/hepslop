\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the quark and gluon jets of the rotationally
symmetric look-elsewhere effect, which may become relevant at HERA. We
find that the AI-native tag-and-probe method supersedes the effective
sample size beyond leading power. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
