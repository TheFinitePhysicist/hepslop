\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the soft gluon
emissions of the bootstrap program. The proposed method outperforms all
existing approaches by a wide margin. Our framework seamlessly
integrates autoregressive models trained on simulated FCC data and
scalable symbol letters of W plus jet production. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with message-passing neural
networks. Extending this framework to neural ordinary differential
equations trained on CMS Open Data is an exciting direction for future
work.
