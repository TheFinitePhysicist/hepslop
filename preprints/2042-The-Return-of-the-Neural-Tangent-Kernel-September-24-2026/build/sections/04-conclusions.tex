\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of end-to-end weakly supervised classifiers at
next-to-leading power to future work. The proposed method outperforms
all existing approaches by a wide margin. Numerically, the massless
Energy Mover's Distance differs from the effective sample size by less
than two percent beyond leading power. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with energy flow networks. We comment on the
extension to optimal transport solvers trained on Pythia samples.
