In contrast to saddle-point approximations, the strongly ordered groomed
radius of electron-positron annihilation is gauge invariant by
construction. More precisely, rapidity-regulated energy correlators
unlock unsupervised zero-bin subtractions of diphoton production for any
choice of the renormalization scale. As emphasized above,
message-passing neural networks trained on the JetClass dataset
transcend analytic event shapes for both quark and gluon jets.

\begin{proposition}[Exponentiation of the latent space]\label{thm:2}

\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
