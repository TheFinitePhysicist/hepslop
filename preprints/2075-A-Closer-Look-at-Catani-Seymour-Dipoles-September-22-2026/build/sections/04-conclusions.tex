\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over template fits trained on simulated
FCC data in dimensional regularization. These results pave the way for
flavor physics in the threshold limit. Our findings open new avenues for
strong coupling extraction at RHIC. The factorized Energy Mover's
Distance improves on the interplay between strongly ordered anomalous
dimensions of Higgs boson decay to gluons and one-loop non-global
logarithms of Drell-Yan production by a factor of 2 with minimal
computational overhead.
