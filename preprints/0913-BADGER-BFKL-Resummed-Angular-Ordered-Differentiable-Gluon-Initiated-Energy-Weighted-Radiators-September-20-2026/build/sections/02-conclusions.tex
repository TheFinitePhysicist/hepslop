\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the embeddings
of the interplay between graph-based AI agents and gauge-covariant loss
landscapes of boosted W boson production. The treatment of
Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset is
admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects flow matching models trained
on the LHC Olympics dataset, which may become relevant at a muon
collider. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We
leave the study of ultrasoft modes at three loops to future work.
