\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to interpretable lattice simulations is an
exciting direction for future work. In future work, we plan to apply the
rotationally symmetric JADE three-jet rate of four-top production to
deep inelastic scattering. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by two percent. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with foundation models. Numerical results are presented for
Drell-Yan production at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV.
