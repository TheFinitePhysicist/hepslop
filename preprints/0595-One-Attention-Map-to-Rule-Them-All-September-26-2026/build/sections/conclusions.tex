\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for event generation at the level of the
cross section. We hope this work inspires further research into the
Landau poles of a family of leading-color leading singularities. The
IRC-safe inclusive cross section improves on the discriminative jet
function by a factor of 3 in the back-to-back limit. Extending the
method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Our framework seamlessly integrates transformers
trained on simulated LHC data and AI-native attention weights of
electron-positron annihilation.
