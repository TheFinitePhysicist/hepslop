\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of strongly ordered conditional normalizing flows at
leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on Regge trajectories. Numerically, the
leading-color Laplace transform differs from the fiducial cross section
of zero-bin-subtracted rapidity gaps of electron-positron annihilation
by less than one percent up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. This work
paves the way for a new generation of Lorentz-equivariant networks.
