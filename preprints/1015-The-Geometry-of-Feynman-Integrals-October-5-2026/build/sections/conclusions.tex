\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to the jet constituents of the leading-color
wide jet broadening of boosted W boson production. Extending the method
beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We observe a clear improvement over attention
weights at detector level. We have not attempted to quantify
heavy-flavor Catani-Seymour dipoles.
