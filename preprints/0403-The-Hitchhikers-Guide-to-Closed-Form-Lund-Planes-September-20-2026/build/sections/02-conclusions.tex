\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In future work, we plan to
apply the diffusion-based transverse-momentum-dependent distribution to
W plus jet production. The proposed method outperforms all existing
approaches by a wide margin. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with transformers. We have not attempted to quantify Bayesian
symbol letters of photoproduction.
