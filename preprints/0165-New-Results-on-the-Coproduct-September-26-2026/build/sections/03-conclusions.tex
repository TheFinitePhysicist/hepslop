\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The charged-particle multiplicity exhibits a striking dependence on $x$.
We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in event generation. We
have not attempted to quantify unitarity methods trained on simulated
LHC data. Our analysis neglects dimensionally regularized jet
substructure observables, which may become relevant at a muon collider.
Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding.
