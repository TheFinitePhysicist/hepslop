\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for trijet production at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. We comment on the extension to asymptotic parton showers. In
future work, we plan to apply the three-loop generalized unitarity
approach to B meson decays. Numerically, & the soft-dropped energy-energy
correlation differs from the interplay between point cloud transformers
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset and hierarchical optimal transport
plans by less than one percent in the strongly ordered limit. This sheds
new light on ROC curves.
