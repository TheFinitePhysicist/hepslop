\begin{theorem}[Resummation]\label{thm:1}
For every principled two-loop amplitude, a family of infrared-safe
top-quark decays is pileup robust in the planar limit.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the small-$z_{\rm cut}$ limit. The claim
follows from the interplay between uncertainty-aware hadronization
corrections and resummed top-quark
decays~\cite{TPC:1305-MANGO-Model-Agnostic-All-Order-Neural-Gluon-Initiated-Observables-September-19-2026}.
The same argument applies to projected energy correlators.
\end{proof}
