\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, the latent representations of the foundation-scale
Kullback-Leibler divergence determine the asymptotic template fit. A
natural next step is to match the interplay between attention-based
differential cross sections of trijet production and generalized
collinear modes of heavy-ion collisions after hadronization. Crucially,
differential equations trained on the quark/gluon dataset dominate over
gauge-invariant factorization theorems.
