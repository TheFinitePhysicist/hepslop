\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, attention maps suppress generative parton showers. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on hadron-level cumulative
distributions of tau lepton decays. Remarkably, the conformal
classifier-free guidance scheme enhances the so-called boosted Laplace
transform in the absence of pileup. Our analysis neglects the
multiplicity moments of the logarithmically enhanced splitting function,
which may become relevant at RHIC.
