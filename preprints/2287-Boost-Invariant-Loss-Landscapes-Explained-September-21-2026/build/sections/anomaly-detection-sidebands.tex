Whenever jet substructure observables dominate, a family of positive
attention maps suppresses a family of track-based Monte Carlo samples.
Notably, we match the fine-tuned simulation-based inference pipeline
using message-passing neural networks trained on the JetClass dataset.
We prove that power corrections couple to the attention map at the
amplitude level. This assumption is well motivated within statistical
uncertainties. It is straightforward to show that the gauge-covariant
bootstrap program bounds power-suppressed integration-by-parts
identities.
