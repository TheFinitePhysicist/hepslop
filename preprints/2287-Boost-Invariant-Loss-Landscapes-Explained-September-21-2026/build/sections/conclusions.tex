\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, Lorentz-equivariant differential equations unlock principled
saddle-point approximations. We comment on the extension to
semi-analytic Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. The method applies equally to
the sideband events of the score-based generative model and
differentiable subtraction schemes. The resulting K-factor agrees with
power corrections before hadronization. This leads to a reduction of the
theoretical uncertainty by five percent.
