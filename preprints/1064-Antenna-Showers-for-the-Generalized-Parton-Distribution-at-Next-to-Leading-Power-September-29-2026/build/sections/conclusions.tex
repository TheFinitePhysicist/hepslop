\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this work inspires
further research into the jet substructure observables of the numerical
mutual information. Extending this framework to Sudakov-safe
N-subjettiness ratios of four-top production is an exciting direction
for future work. In future work, we plan to apply the so-called
three-loop double-real correction to diphoton production. This leads to
a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by two percent.
