\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. The treatment of foundation-scale event weights of hadronic Z
boson decays is admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply
the massive neural tangent kernel to Higgs plus jet production. Our code
is publicly available. This work paves the way for a new generation of
antenna showers.
