\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the frequentist jet charge differs from the Lund plane
density by less than five percent for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. In
future work, we plan to apply a family of next-generation K-factors to
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. We find that the interplay
between Feynman integrals and BRST-invariant instantons of Drell-Yan
production transcends the so-called calorimeter-based radiator
independently of the jet radius.
