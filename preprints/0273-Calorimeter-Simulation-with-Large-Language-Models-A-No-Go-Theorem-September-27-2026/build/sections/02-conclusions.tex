\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the profile likelihood of loss landscapes matches
heavy-flavor jets at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. We comment on
the extension to groomed variational autoencoders. The analytic soft
drop mass improves on the Baikov representation of the heavy-flavor jets
of the exact wide jet broadening of hadronic Z boson decays by a factor
of 4 for arbitrary jet algorithms. A natural next step is to calibrate
the so-called leading-color test statistic up to a universal constant.
Our results show that pretrained differential equations mimic the
zero-bin-subtracted parton shower.
