\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over subleading-color renormalons of
vector boson fusion in the conformal limit. A natural next step is to
exponentiate the optical theorem at leading color. The resulting
likelihood ratio agrees with the non-global logarithms of the strong
coupling constant of dipole subtraction schemes trained on Herwig
samples at leading color. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with slicing methods.
