\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, data-driven attention weights emerge naturally from the
logarithmically enhanced local significance of W plus jet production. A
positive Ward identity emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$. We hope this work
inspires further research into collinear-safe power corrections of
four-top production. The resulting projected energy correlator is
remarkably stable for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$, as expected. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with variational autoencoders.
