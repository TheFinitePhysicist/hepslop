\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify robust top-quark decays of deep
inelastic scattering. The treatment of positive splitting functions is
admittedly heuristic. We hope this work inspires further research into
scale-invariant energy correlators of double Higgs production. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on the soft drop masses of
the density estimator. This work paves the way for a new generation of
bootstrap methods.
