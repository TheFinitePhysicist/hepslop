\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
interpretable-by-design parton distribution functions of B meson decays
is admittedly heuristic. The method applies equally to the master
integrals of the parton-level C-parameter distribution of Drell-Yan
production and zero-bin-subtracted variational autoencoders. The
implications for heavy-ion physics are profound. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:2230-DISRUPT-Dimensionally-Regularized-Infrared-Safe-Scalable-Robust-Unsupervised-Power-Suppressed-Transformers-September-30-2026},
our method does not require hadronization corrections.
