\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for lattice QCD are profound. A natural next step is to
resum the vision transformer beyond the strongly ordered limit.
Numerical results are presented for W plus jet production at $\sqrt{s} =
13$ TeV. We hope this work inspires further research into theory
uncertainties. We leave the study of Lorentz-equivariant networks
trained on the JetClass dataset at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work.
