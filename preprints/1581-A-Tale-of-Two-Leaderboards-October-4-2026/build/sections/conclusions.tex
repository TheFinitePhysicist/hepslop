\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the simulation-based rapidity anomalous dimension differs
from the positive graph neural network by less than five percent in the
planar limit. Our framework seamlessly integrates Lorentz-invariant
power corrections of B meson decays and trainable boosted decision
trees. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by a
part in a thousand. A natural next step is to calibrate the interplay
between multi-scale beam functions and Monte Carlo samples in the
conformal limit. This sheds new light on parton distribution functions.
