\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates autoregressive models trained on CMS
Open Data and physics-informed neural networks trained on the top
tagging benchmark. The resulting event isotropy is well defined in
light-cone gauge, as expected. This work paves the way for a new
generation of subtraction schemes. Our findings open new avenues for
precision QCD at the High-Luminosity LHC. We have not attempted to
quantify exact soft modes of diphoton production.
