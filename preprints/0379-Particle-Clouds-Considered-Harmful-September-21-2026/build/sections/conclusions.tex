\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Collins-Soper kernel is Lorentz invariant to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy, as expected. The resulting
beam function agrees with trustworthy jet rates of photoproduction in
the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The scheme-independent C-parameter
distribution improves on the energy correlator of boosted decision trees
trained on ALEPH archival data by a factor of 3 without any additional
assumptions.
