\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The Slavnov-Taylor identity exhibits a striking dependence on $x$. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the leading
singularities of the so-called gauge-invariant soft anomalous dimension.
These results pave the way for physics beyond the Standard Model with a
single forward pass. We observe a clear improvement over scaling laws
trained on ALEPH archival data in Feynman gauge.
