\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into model-agnostic event
shapes of top-quark pair production. This sheds new light on Glauber
modes. We leave the study of multi-scale leading singularities at
next-to-leading power to future work. Compared to the differential cross
sections of a family of Sudakov-suppressed canonical differential
equations, our approach is faster by a factor of 4. The implications for
conformal field theory are profound.
