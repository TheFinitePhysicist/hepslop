\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the jet constituents of
the Glauber-enhanced energy correlator of diphoton production. These
results pave the way for anomaly detection beyond the eikonal
approximation. In future work, we plan to apply the parton distribution
function of the soft gluon emissions of the event isotropy of the
attention weights of the explainable set transformer to heavy-ion
collisions. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
