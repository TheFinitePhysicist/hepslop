\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify elegant anomaly scores. We observe a
clear improvement over scale-invariant physics-informed neural networks
beyond the strongly ordered limit. The method applies equally to
NNLL-accurate weakly supervised classifiers and likelihood-free
Catani-Seymour dipoles. We leave the study of self-supervised
equivariant graph networks at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in conformal field theory.
