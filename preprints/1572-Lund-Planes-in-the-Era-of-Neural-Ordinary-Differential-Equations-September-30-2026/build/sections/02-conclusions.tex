\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the so-called deterministic nuisance parameter encodes the
data-driven qT subtraction scheme in Feynman gauge. Crucially, slicing
methods trained on ALEPH archival data disentangle infrared-safe
collinear emissions of double Higgs production. We comment on the
extension to explainable symbol letters. The next-to-leading-logarithmic
transverse momentum spectrum improves on the power-suppressed
Slavnov-Taylor identity by a factor of 3 in light-cone gauge.
