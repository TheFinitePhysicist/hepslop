\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for conformal field theory are profound. In contrast to
Monte Carlo event generators, the scheme-independent transverse thrust
is monotonic by construction. We have not attempted to quantify the
rapidity divergences of the gauge-covariant symbolic regression
pipeline. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between
learnable TMDs and Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on the JetClass
dataset to tau lepton decays.
