\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of antenna showers trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV
at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on perturbative jet angularities of dijet
production.
