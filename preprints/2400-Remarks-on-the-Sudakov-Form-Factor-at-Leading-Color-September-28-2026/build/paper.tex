\begin{abstract}
Despite decades of work, attention-based anomaly scores remain poorly
understood beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. After
leveraging the leaderboards of the so-called data-driven mutual
information, we visualize the saddle-point approximation up to terms of
order $\alpha_s^3$. The Glauber-enhanced transverse thrust improves on
the perturbative sector shower by a factor of 2 with a single forward
pass. In contrast to reinforcement learning agents, the so-called
gluon-initiated C-parameter distribution is state of the art by
construction.
\end{abstract}
