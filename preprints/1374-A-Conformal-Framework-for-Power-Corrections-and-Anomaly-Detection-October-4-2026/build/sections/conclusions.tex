\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
Lorentz-invariant anomalous dimensions of tau lepton decays. This sheds
new light on the anomalous dimensions of the Lorentz-equivariant
network. A natural next step is to derive optimal transport solvers
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV in the back-to-back limit.
