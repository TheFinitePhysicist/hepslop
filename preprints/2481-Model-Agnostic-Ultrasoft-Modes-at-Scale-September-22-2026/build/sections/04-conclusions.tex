\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of holistic neural ordinary differential equations at
order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. Our results are in excellent
agreement with foundation-scale soft drop
observables~\cite{TPC:0237-Towards-AGI-for-Jet-Calibration-September-29-2026}.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on canonical
differential equations. Extending this framework to density estimators
trained on the JetClass dataset is an exciting direction for future
work.
