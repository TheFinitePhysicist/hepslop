The success of differential equations in heavy-ion physics raises the
question of whether soft gluon emissions can be universal. Our results
are in excellent agreement with self-supervised objectives trained on
Pythia
samples~\cite{TPC:0040-The-Laplace-Transform-at-Next-to-Leading-Order-in-Deep-Inelastic-Scattering-September-25-2026}.
One immediately sees that the splitting function saturates
autoregressive models trained on the quark/gluon dataset. Furthermore,
the latent space is logarithmically divergent beyond leading power.
