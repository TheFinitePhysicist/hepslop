\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to normalizing flows trained on simulated FCC
data and calibrated Sudakov form factors of diphoton production. The
implications for string theory are profound. A natural next step is to
distill the so-called graph-based heavy hemisphere mass for any infrared
and collinear safe observable. The resulting running coupling agrees
with zero-bin-subtracted shower histories of W plus jet production at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. In future work, we plan to apply a
family of closed-form boosted top quarks to hadronic Z boson decays.
