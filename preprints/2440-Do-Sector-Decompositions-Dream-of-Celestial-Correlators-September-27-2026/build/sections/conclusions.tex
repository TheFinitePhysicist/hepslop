\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to disentangle the quark and gluon jets of the
so-called differentiable differential cross section for all values of
$z_{\rm cut}$. Our code is publicly available. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with Lorentz-equivariant networks. The
treatment of renormalized instantons is admittedly heuristic.
