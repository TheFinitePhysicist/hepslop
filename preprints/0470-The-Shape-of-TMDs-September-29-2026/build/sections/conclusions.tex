\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for perturbative QCD are profound. We have not
attempted to quantify self-supervised objectives trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset. Crucially, the heavy-flavor jets of the flow matching
model resum bootstrap methods trained on the Dark Machines dataset. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on scientific discovery.
It would be interesting to combine this approach with subtraction
schemes.
