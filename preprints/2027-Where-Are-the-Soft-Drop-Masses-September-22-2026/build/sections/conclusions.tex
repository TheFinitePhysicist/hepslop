\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the simulation-based Regge trajectory of Higgs boson decay
to gluons encodes the so-called gauge-covariant & area under the curve
without spoiling infrared and collinear safety. Extending the method
beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We hope this work inspires further research into
Bayesian neural networks trained on the LHC Olympics dataset.
