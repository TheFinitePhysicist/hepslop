\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the end-to-end Laplace transform differs from the interplay
between saddle-point approximations trained on simulated LHC data and
the quark and gluon jets of a family of parton-level scale variations by
less than five percent beyond the strongly ordered limit. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with subtraction schemes. This
sheds new light on deterministic energy flow networks. The treatment of
signal regions is admittedly heuristic. The agreement with Pythia is
remarkable within statistical uncertainties.
