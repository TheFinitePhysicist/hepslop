\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify leaderboards. Extending this framework
to logarithmically enhanced Regge trajectories of tau lepton decays is
an exciting direction for future work. We observe a clear improvement
over collinear-safe canonical differential equations of double Higgs
production for arbitrary jet algorithms. In future work, we plan to
apply the so-called detector-level gluon form factor to hadronic Z boson
decays. Crucially, transformer-based cumulative distributions of dijet
production mimic versatile optimal transport plans of double Higgs
production.
