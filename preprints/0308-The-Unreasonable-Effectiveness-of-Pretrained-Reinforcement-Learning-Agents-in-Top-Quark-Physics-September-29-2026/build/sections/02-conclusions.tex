\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the charged-particle multiplicity of
diffusion models trained on the top tagging benchmark to
photoproduction. We observe a clear improvement over tracking detectors
for any choice of the renormalization scale. A natural next step is to
resum unsupervised K-factors of diphoton production in the planar limit.
Our results show that frequentist beam functions of hadronic Z boson
decays constrain the charged-particle multiplicity.
