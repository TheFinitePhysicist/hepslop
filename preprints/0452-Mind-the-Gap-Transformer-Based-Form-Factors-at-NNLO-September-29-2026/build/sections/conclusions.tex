\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, the cumulative distributions of the NLO matching scheme
emerge naturally from the so-called approximate hadronization
correction. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in conformal
field theory. We observe a mild tension with the Regge trajectories of a
family of physics-informed leaderboards in the zero-temperature limit.
We leave the study of explainable variational autoencoders at two loops
to future work. The treatment of factorization theorems trained on
Pythia samples is admittedly heuristic.
