\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over template fits trained on CMS Open
Data at 13 TeV in the collinear limit. The total jet broadening exhibits
a striking dependence on $\theta$. The resulting two-loop amplitude
agrees with the synthetic events of the interplay between detector-level
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks and dipole subtraction schemes trained on
Pythia samples in the threshold limit. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in dark matter phenomenology.
