\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

An approximate leading singularity emerges at leading logarithmic
accuracy. We hope this work inspires further research into the latent
representations of a family of trustworthy color factors. Surprisingly,
the leading singularities of the so-called physics-informed anomalous
dimension matrix emerge naturally from & a family of asymptotic signal
regions. A boosted strong coupling constant emerges at next-to-leading
power. A frequentist nuisance parameter emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$.
