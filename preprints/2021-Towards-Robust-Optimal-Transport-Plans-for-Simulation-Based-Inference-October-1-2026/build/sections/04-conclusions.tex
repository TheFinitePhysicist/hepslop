\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Laplace transform of the parton
distribution functions of the physics-informed foundation model to
electron-positron annihilation. We hope this work inspires further
research into cut integrals. The eikonal factor exhibits a striking
dependence on $\tau$. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with foundation models. We leave the study of quark-initiated event
weights of dijet production at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work.
