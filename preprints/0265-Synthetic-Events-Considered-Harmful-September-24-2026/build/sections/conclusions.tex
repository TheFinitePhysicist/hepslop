\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, physics-informed event weights of boosted W boson
production emerge naturally from the next-generation smeared matrix
element. Extending this framework to subleading-color jet constituents
of tau lepton decays is an exciting direction for future work. This work
paves the way for a new generation of anomaly scores. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with dipole subtraction schemes.
