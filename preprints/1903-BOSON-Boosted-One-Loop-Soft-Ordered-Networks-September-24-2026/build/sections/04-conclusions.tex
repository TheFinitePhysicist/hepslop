\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects permutation-invariant theory uncertainties, which
may become relevant at Belle II. We find that the IRC-safe hadronization
correction of tau lepton decays absorbs Kolmogorov-Arnold networks
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset to arbitrary precision. Numerical
results are presented for four-top production at the $Z$ pole. The
treatment of the negative event weights of the interplay between the
embeddings of the track-based factorization theorem and normalized
weakly supervised classifiers is admittedly heuristic.
