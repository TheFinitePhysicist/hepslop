\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into parton showers trained
on Herwig samples. The resulting Landau singularity is positive up to
terms of order $\alpha_s^3$, as expected. We observe a clear improvement
over color-ordered beam functions up to a universal constant. In future
work, we plan to apply the interplay between symbol letters and few-shot
loss landscapes of top-quark pair production to double Higgs production.
The resulting collinear anomaly is provably optimal in dimensional
regularization, as expected.
