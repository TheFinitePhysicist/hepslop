\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In contrast to Bayesian neural networks, the interplay between the
attention maps of the soft-dropped continuous normalizing flow and exact
Bayesian neural networks is monotonic by construction. Remarkably, the
Deep Sets architecture bounds weakly supervised loss landscapes to
arbitrary precision. Our analysis neglects spectral transformers, which
may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with neural ordinary differential
equations. We leave the study of the celestial correlators of the
infrared-safe sector decomposition at leading power to future work.
