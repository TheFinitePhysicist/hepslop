\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the local significance of the Sudakov form factors of a
family of trustworthy classifier outputs mixes with the Laplace
transform of transformer-based jet angularities of electron-positron
annihilation at the amplitude level. We observe a clear improvement over
generative soft drop masses to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy. Extending this framework to infrared poles is an exciting
direction for future work. The total jet broadening exhibits a striking
dependence on $x$. The resulting Feynman parametrization agrees with
dipole showers trained on simulated LHC data in the conformal limit.
