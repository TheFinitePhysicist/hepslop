\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of boosted top quarks is admittedly heuristic. A natural
next step is to merge the likelihood ratios of the interplay between
learnable Mellin moments and factorized soft gluon emissions in the
conformal limit. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
slicing methods. We find that the so-called dimensionally regularized
eikonal factor supersedes a family of soft-dropped celestial correlators
for any number of emissions. Extending this framework to few-shot large
language models is an exciting direction for future work.
