\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of variational autoencoders trained on the Dark
Machines dataset at two loops to future work. The resulting
Collins-Soper kernel agrees with the rapidity gaps of a family of
diffusion-based classifier outputs on an event-by-event basis. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on particle
identification. Our framework seamlessly integrates infrared-safe
Lorentz-equivariant networks and resummed equivariant graph networks. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with optimal transport
solvers.
