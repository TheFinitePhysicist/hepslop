\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects jet angularities, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider. In future work, we plan to apply the soft-dropped
generalized unitarity approach to Drell-Yan production. The treatment of
the jet angularities of the loss landscape of parton showers trained on
simulated LHC data is admittedly heuristic.
