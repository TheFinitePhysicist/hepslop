\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify self-supervised objectives trained on
the Dark Machines dataset. We hope this work inspires further research
into particle clouds. Numerical results are presented for hadronic Z
boson decays at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. We comment on the extension to
foundation-scale diffusion models. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in machine learning for particle physics.
