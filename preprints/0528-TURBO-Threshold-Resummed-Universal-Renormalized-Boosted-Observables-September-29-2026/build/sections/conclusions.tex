\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify equivariant hemisphere masses. A
natural next step is to benchmark the Lund plane density for any
infrared and collinear safe observable. Our findings open new avenues
for parton shower tuning at the Electron-Ion Collider. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on the polylogarithms of the
scalable antenna shower. We discuss implications for jet tagging at a
muon collider.
