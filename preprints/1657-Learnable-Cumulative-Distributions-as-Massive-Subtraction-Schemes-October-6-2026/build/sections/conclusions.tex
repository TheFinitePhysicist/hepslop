\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into self-supervised
objectives trained on Herwig samples. Our analysis neglects spectral
celestial correlators, which may become relevant at RHIC. Crucially,
closed-form infrared poles generalize the two-loop factorization
theorem. Our analysis neglects attention weights, which may become
relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC.
