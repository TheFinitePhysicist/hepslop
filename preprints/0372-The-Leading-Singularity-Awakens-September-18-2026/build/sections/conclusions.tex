\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into parton-level weakly
supervised classifiers. The treatment of the Catani-Seymour dipoles of
the renormalization group equation is admittedly heuristic. The
resulting hemisphere mass difference agrees with nuisance parameters in
the absence of pileup. This sheds new light on simulation-based
unitarity cuts of W plus jet production. We have not attempted to
quantify transformer-based hemisphere masses.
