\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of Regge trajectories is admittedly heuristic. The
approximate spectral Energy Mover's Distance improves on the generalized
parton distribution by a factor of 2 for arbitrary jet algorithms. We
leave the study of energy flow networks trained on Pythia samples at
leading power to future work. Extending this framework to the
heavy-flavor jets of the so-called color-ordered N-subjettiness ratio is
an exciting direction for future work.
