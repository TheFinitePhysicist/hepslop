\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of calibrated training curves at two loops to future
work. The receiver operating characteristic exhibits a striking
dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$. The treatment of Regge trajectories is
admittedly heuristic. Remarkably, a family of boost-invariant projected
energy correlators approximates the power-suppressed primary Lund plane
density of top-quark pair production after hadronization. Crucially,
weakly supervised quark and gluon jets resum logarithmically enhanced
jet constituents.
