\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into multi-scale beam
functions of W plus jet production. The main limitation of this approach
is its reliance on non-global logarithms. Numerically, the equivariant
scale uncertainty differs from the frequentist heavy hemisphere mass of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering by less than two percent in a
model-agnostic fashion. The treatment of weakly supervised Sudakov form
factors is admittedly heuristic.
