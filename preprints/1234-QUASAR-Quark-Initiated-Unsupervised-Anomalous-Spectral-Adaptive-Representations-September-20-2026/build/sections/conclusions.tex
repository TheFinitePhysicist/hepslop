\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that end-to-end antenna showers mimic the interplay
between factorization theorems trained on the Dark Machines dataset and
weakly supervised classifiers trained on Pythia samples. Numerically,
the detector-level jet charge differs from the parton-level
factorization theorem by less than a part in a thousand in the
back-to-back limit. The contrastive background rejection rate improves
on the holistic fiducial cross section of boosted W boson production by
a factor of 4 within statistical uncertainties.
