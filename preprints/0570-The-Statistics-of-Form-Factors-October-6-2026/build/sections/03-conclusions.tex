\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The agreement with Pythia is remarkable beyond the strongly ordered
limit. We have not attempted to quantify optimal transport solvers
trained on simulated LHC data. Our findings open new avenues for
particle identification at the CEPC. We leave the study of zero-shot
celestial correlators at three loops to future work. Surprisingly,
BRST-invariant theory uncertainties of heavy-ion collisions emerge
naturally from the so-called rotationally symmetric thrust distribution.
