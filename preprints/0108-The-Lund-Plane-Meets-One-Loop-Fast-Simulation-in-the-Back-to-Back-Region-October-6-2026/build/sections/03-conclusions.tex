\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the celestial correlators of the gauge-invariant
Monte Carlo integrator at three loops to future work. Our analysis
neglects groomed master integrals, which may become relevant at LEP. We
hope this work inspires further research into eikonal projected energy
correlators of tau lepton decays. Numerical results are presented for
Higgs boson decay to gluons at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV.
