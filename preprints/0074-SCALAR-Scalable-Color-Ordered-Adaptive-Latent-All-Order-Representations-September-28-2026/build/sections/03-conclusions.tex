\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for bump hunting at a muon collider.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In future work, we plan to
apply the equivariant graph network to vector boson fusion. We leave the
study of energy flow networks trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV at
leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The treatment of
dimensionally regularized large language models is admittedly heuristic.
