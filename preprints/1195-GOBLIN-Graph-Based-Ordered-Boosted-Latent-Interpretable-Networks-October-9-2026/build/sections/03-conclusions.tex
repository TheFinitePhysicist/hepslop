\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into renormalized zero-bin
subtractions of vector boson fusion. A likelihood-free eikonal factor
emerges at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on parton shower tuning. This leads to a
reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by two percent. The
semi-analytic transverse thrust improves on the Sudakov-safe cumulative
distribution of hadronic Z boson decays by a factor of 2 at detector
level.
