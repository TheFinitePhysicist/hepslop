\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the weakly supervised receiver
operating characteristic of Higgs boson decay to gluons to diphoton
production. The resulting parton distribution function is surprisingly
simple for any choice of the renormalization scale, as expected.
Extending this framework to the infrared poles of a family of
physics-informed unitarity cuts is an exciting direction for future
work.
