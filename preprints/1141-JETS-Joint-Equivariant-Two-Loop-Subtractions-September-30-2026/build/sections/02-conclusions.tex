\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the area under the curve probes the collinear-safe neural
tangent kernel at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. This sheds new
light on set transformers trained on the Dark Machines dataset. In
future work, we plan to apply the spectral Energy Mover's Distance of
shower histories to semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on automated theorem
proving. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by
five percent.
