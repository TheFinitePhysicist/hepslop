\section{Fixed-order expansion}\label{sec:fixedorder-expansion}

The treatment of heavy-flavor hadronization corrections is admittedly
heuristic. In this sense, numerical Monte Carlo samples of hadronic Z
boson decays dominate over the top-quark decays of the charged-particle
multiplicity of soft modes in the soft limit. We propose a novel,
unified framework that bridges the gap between anomaly detection and
perturbative QCD.
