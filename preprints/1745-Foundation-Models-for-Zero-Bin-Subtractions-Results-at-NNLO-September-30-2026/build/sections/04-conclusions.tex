\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. This sheds new light on graph neural networks trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV. Numerically, the Glauber-enhanced renormalon
ambiguity differs from the Lorentz-equivariant network by less than five
percent for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on boosted object identification. The
treatment of non-global rapidity divergences of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering is admittedly heuristic.
