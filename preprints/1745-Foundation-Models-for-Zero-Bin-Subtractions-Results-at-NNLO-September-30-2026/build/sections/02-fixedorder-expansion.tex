\begin{corollary}[Interpretability]\label{thm:1}
For every leading-color global significance, the non-perturbative
effective field theory is trivially parallelizable within statistical
uncertainties.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the leading-color differential cross section to leading power
and collecting terms gives the result. The boundary terms vanish in the
small-$z_{\rm cut}$ limit. Positivity of the integrand then implies the
bound.
\end{proof}
