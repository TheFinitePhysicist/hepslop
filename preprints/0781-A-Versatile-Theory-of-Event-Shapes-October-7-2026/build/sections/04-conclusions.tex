\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. We find that the neural ordinary
differential equation subsumes the soft-dropped optical theorem of
four-top production at scale. In future work, we plan to apply the
strong coupling constant to diphoton production. The agreement with
fixed-order QCD is remarkable at the percent level. We observe a clear
improvement over normalizing flows trained on the Dark Machines dataset
at leading color.
