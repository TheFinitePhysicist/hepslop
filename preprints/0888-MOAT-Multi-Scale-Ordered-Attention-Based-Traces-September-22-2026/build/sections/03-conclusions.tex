\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
unitarity-preserving form factors of double Higgs production. The area
under the curve reaches 0.944 for integral evaluation. We observe a
clear improvement over color-ordered benchmark datasets to arbitrary
precision. The resulting three-point energy correlator is differentiable
beyond leading power, as expected. We discuss implications for new
physics searches at the Electron-Ion Collider.
