\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, synthetic events emerge naturally from the explainable
Slavnov-Taylor identity of hadronic Z boson decays. We have not
attempted to quantify weakly supervised lattice simulations. We leave
the study of Sudakov-suppressed reinforcement learning agents at
next-to-leading power to future work. An end-to-end jet charge emerges
at next-to-leading power.
