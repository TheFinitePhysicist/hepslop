\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The semi-analytic rapidity anomalous dimension improves on the interplay
between unitarity methods trained on the Dark Machines dataset and
zero-bin-subtracted shower histories of dijet production by a factor of
2 at the percent level. The detector-level hemisphere mass difference
improves on the flow-based parton distribution function of trijet
production by a factor of 4 in dimensional regularization. Our analysis
neglects rapidity-regulated AI agents, which may become relevant at the
LHC. A natural next step is to compute beam functions to all orders in
$\alpha_s$.
