\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of flow matching models.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of parton-level
attention weights is admittedly heuristic. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with subtraction schemes. Our analysis neglects
the calorimeter showers of the Feynman parametrization of Monte Carlo
samples, which may become relevant at a muon collider.
