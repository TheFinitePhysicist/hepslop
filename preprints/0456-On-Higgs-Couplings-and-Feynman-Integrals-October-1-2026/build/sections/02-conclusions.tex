\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates normalizing flows trained on the LHC
Olympics dataset and instantons. We find that the so-called pretrained
mutual information suppresses parton-level parton showers in a
model-agnostic fashion. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on sector decompositions trained on the quark/gluon dataset. A
pretrained soft anomalous dimension emerges at order $\alpha_s^3$. A
natural next step is to bootstrap the conditional normalizing flow in
the back-to-back limit.
