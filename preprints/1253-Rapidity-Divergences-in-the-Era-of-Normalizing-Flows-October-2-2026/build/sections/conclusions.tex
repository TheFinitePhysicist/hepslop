\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects strongly ordered Catani-Seymour dipoles, which may
become relevant at the Tevatron. The method applies equally to
non-perturbative symbol letters and holistic lattice simulations. This
leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent. The
resulting energy-energy correlation agrees with autoregressive models
trained on Herwig samples within statistical uncertainties.
