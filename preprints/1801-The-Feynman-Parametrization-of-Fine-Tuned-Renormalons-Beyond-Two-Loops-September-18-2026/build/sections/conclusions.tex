\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on b-tagging.
Numerical results are presented for vector boson fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
14$ TeV. Extending this framework to the zero-bin subtractions of the
so-called normalized primary Lund plane density is an exciting direction
for future work. The treatment of normalized multiplicity moments of
vector boson fusion is admittedly heuristic.
