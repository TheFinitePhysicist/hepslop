\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with autoregressive
models. Our framework seamlessly integrates groomed factorization
theorems and fine-tuned cumulative distributions. We hope this work
inspires further research into detector-level Higgs couplings. Numerical
results are presented for Higgs plus jet production at $\sqrt{s} = 13$
TeV. The permutation-invariant one-loop amplitude improves on the
autoregressive model by a factor of 4 on an event-by-event basis.
