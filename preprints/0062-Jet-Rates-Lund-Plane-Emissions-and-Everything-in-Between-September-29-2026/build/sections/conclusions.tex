\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into event weights. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with chatbots. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on integration-by-parts
identities trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. Extending the method
beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Extending this framework to
transformer-based template fits is an exciting direction for future
work.
