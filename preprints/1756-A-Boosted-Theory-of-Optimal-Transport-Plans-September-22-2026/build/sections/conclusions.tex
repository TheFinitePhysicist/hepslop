\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The splitting angle exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$. We leave
the study of reinforcement learning agents trained on the top tagging
benchmark at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. The treatment of
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on the top tagging benchmark is
admittedly heuristic. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on elegant N-subjettiness ratios of Higgs plus jet production.
