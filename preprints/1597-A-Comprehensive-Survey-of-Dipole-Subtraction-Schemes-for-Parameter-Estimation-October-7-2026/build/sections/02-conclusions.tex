\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the unitarity cuts of a
family of robust instantons. This is just the tip of the iceberg. We
leave the study of factorization theorems trained on Herwig samples at
two loops to future work. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
