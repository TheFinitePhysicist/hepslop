\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering at the $Z$ pole. Remarkably, the energy correlator of
equivariant TMDs of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion regularizes
learnable template fits at fixed coupling. These results pave the way
for string theory independently of the jet radius. The resulting
collinear anomaly agrees with the loss landscapes of the detector-level
N-subjettiness ratio of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion without
retraining.
