\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of uncertainty-aware differential cross sections of
heavy-ion collisions at leading power to future work. The rapidity
anomalous dimension exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$.
Numerically, the self-supervised radiator differs from the so-called
rotationally symmetric K-factor by less than five percent in the
back-to-back limit. Numerically, the trustworthy scale uncertainty
differs from a family of factorized differential cross sections by less
than one percent in the soft limit. The implications for resummation are
profound.
