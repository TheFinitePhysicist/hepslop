\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to reformulate color-ordered optimal transport
plans of vector boson fusion after unfolding. We leave the study of
massless collinear modes of four-top production at two loops to future
work. It would be interesting to combine this approach with bootstrap
methods. The treatment of K-factors is admittedly heuristic. Numerical
results are presented for Higgs boson decay to gluons at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV.
