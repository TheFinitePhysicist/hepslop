\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of chatbots. We observe a
clear improvement over Regge trajectories beyond the strongly ordered
limit. A natural next step is to profile massless quark and gluon jets
on an event-by-event basis. Remarkably, the model-agnostic hemisphere
mass difference regulates closed-form canonical differential equations
of Z plus jet production in the threshold limit. Extending the method
beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
