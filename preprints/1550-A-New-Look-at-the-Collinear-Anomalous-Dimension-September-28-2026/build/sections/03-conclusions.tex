\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

An exact splitting angle emerges at next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy. In future work, we plan to apply a family of generalized
N-subjettiness ratios to deep inelastic scattering. Numerical results
are presented for four-top production at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. A natural
next step is to match the energy correlator of large language models
trained on the quark/gluon dataset to arbitrary precision.
