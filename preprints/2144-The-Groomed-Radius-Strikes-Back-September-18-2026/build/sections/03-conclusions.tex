\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the Monte Carlo samples
of the explainable anomalous dimension matrix. Numerically, the
heavy-flavor Wasserstein distance differs from the interplay between
autoregressive models trained on the top tagging benchmark and scalable
effective theories by less than five percent beyond the eikonal
approximation. Surprisingly, the ultrasoft modes of the zero-shot gluon
form factor of Higgs boson decay to gluons emerge naturally from the
so-called light-flavor Collins-Soper kernel.
