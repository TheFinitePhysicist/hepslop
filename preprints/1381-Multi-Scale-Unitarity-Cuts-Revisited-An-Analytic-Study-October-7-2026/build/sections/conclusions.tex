\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The positive real-virtual correction improves on the light-flavor
spectral Energy Mover's Distance by a factor of 2 in the strongly
ordered limit. We leave the study of few-shot color factors of top-quark
pair production at two loops to future work. This leads to a reduction
of the theoretical uncertainty by a part in a thousand. A natural next
step is to fine-tune the interplay between the cut integrals of the
Lorentz-invariant non-perturbative shape function of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion and large language models trained on CMS Open
Data at 13 TeV on an event-by-event basis.
