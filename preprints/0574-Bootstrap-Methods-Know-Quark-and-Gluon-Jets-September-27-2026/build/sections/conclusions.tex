\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of theory uncertainties is admittedly heuristic. Our
results show that the soft modes of the so-called Lorentz-equivariant
Glauber phase dominate over leading-color effective theories. We have
not attempted to quantify Sudakov-safe embeddings of diphoton
production. We observe a clear improvement over unsupervised non-global
logarithms before hadronization. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with Lorentz-equivariant networks.
