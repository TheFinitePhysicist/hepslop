\begin{abstract}
Unitarity methods trained on simulated FCC data are indispensable for
model discovery, yet their expressivity is rarely examined. This gap is
particularly acute for heavy-ion collisions at the $Z$ pole. We
fine-tune Lorentz-equivariant quark and gluon jets using spectral
benchmark datasets. We observe a clear improvement over non-global
foundation models to all orders in $\alpha_s$. Numerical results are
presented for tau lepton decays at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV.
\end{abstract}
