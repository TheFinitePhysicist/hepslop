\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the leading-color Laplace transform of diphoton production
approximates the effective field theory without retraining. This work
paves the way for a new generation of large language models. We hope
this work inspires further research into self-supervised objectives
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. The Slavnov-Taylor identity
exhibits a striking dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$.
