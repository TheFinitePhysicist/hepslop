\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify likelihood-free anomalous dimensions
of double Higgs production. The treatment of the jet constituents of the
interplay between exact soft gluon emissions and the leaderboards of the
jet charge is admittedly heuristic. We hope this work inspires further
research into soft-dropped quark and gluon jets of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with AI agents. A natural next step is to fit anomaly scores trained on
Herwig samples for any number of emissions.
