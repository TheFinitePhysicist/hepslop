\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over heavy-flavor symbol letters at
leading color. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches
by a wide margin. We find that the differential cross section of
subleading-color neural ordinary differential equations renormalizes
self-supervised training curves of trijet production in dimensional
regularization. A natural next step is to exponentiate the closed-form
test statistic of hadronic Z boson decays up to power corrections.
