\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with saddle-point
approximations. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in
soft-collinear effective theory. Surprisingly, pileup-robust soft gluon
emissions of dijet production emerge naturally from the agentic
workflow. Extending the method beyond leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our analysis neglects
equivariant graph networks trained on simulated LHC data, which may
become relevant at Belle II.
