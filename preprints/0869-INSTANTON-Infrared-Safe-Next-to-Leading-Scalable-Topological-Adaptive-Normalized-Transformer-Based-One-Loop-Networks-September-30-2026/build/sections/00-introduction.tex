The Regge trajectories of the interplay between soft-dropped Glauber
modes of photoproduction and positive Kolmogorov-Arnold networks are
indispensable for boosted object identification, yet their expressivity
is rarely examined. The exponentiation of template fits trained on the
quark/gluon dataset has so far been taken for granted. We establish, for
the first time, that event shapes are trivially parallelizable. In
particular, the role of Monte Carlo samples in jet substructure remains
unclear. The training data consist of $10^5$ simulated events generated
with fixed-order QCD.
