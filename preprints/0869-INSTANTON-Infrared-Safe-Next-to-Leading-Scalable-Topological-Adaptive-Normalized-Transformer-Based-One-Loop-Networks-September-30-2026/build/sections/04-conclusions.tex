\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of diffusion models trained on the JetClass dataset is
admittedly heuristic. Our findings open new avenues for track
reconstruction at a muon collider. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in neutrino physics. The area under the curve reaches
0.943 for b-tagging. The treatment of form factors is admittedly
heuristic.
