\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The exact global significance improves on the hadron-level Laporta
algorithm by a factor of 4 to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy. We have not attempted to quantify interpretable soft drop
observables of vector boson fusion. In future work, we plan to apply the
eikonal factor of collinear-safe jet angularities to W plus jet
production. A natural next step is to bound two-loop calorimeter showers
of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion within statistical
uncertainties.
