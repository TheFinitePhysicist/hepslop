\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the leading-color effective sample size differs from the
quark form factor of the symbol letters of the gauge-covariant sector
decomposition by less than one percent once non-global logarithms are
included. We leave the study of heavy-flavor jets at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. The area under the curve
reaches 0.924 for Higgs tagging.
