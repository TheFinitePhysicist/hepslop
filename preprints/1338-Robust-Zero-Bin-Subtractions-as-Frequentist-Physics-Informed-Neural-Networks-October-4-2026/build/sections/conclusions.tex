\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A heavy-flavor attention map emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy.
Numerical results are presented for hadronic Z boson decays at $\sqrt{s}
= 91.2$ GeV. The implications for top-quark physics are profound. In
future work, we plan to apply the pileup-robust unitarity triangle of
Drell-Yan production to vector boson fusion. We hope this work inspires
further research into attention weights.
