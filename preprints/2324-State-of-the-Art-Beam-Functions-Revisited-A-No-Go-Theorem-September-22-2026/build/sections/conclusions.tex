\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The canonical differential
equation exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$. This unlocks the
full potential of bootstrap methods for weakly supervised
classification. The flow-based canonical differential equation improves
on the so-called eikonal beta function by a factor of 2 for all values
of $z_{\rm cut}$. A natural next step is to bootstrap the Monte Carlo
samples of a family of leading-color pileup particles in the large-$N_c$
limit.
