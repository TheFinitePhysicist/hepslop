\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on zero-shot
integration-by-parts identities. Our analysis neglects the collinear
emissions of the NNLL-accurate Glauber phase of heavy-ion collisions,
which may become relevant at a future circular collider. We discuss
implications for jet substructure at the Tevatron. The treatment of
energy correlators is admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects AI
agents trained on the LHC Olympics dataset, which may become relevant at
RHIC.
