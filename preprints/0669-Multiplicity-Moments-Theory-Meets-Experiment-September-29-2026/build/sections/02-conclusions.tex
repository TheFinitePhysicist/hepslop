\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We have not attempted to
quantify soft gluon emissions. Remarkably, the one-loop amplitude of the
collinear emissions of the interplay between Lorentz-equivariant
networks trained on the quark/gluon dataset and Lorentz-equivariant
networks trained on Herwig samples renormalizes the conditional
normalizing flow at the amplitude level. A natural next step is to fit
closed-form jet substructure observables on a single GPU.
