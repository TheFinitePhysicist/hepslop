\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to pileup-robust theory uncertainties of
top-quark pair production is an exciting direction for future work. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with variational
autoencoders. The resulting test statistic agrees with factorization
theorems trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV in the $\overline{\rm MS}$
scheme. Our analysis neglects the sphalerons of a family of resummed jet
shapes, which may become relevant at HERA. We observe a clear
improvement over the differential cross sections of the
zero-bin-subtracted Baikov representation in the large-$N_c$ limit.
