\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates equivariant graph networks trained
on simulated LHC data and scalable cumulative distributions of top-quark
pair production. Our findings open new avenues for fast simulation at
the High-Luminosity LHC. We have not attempted to quantify parton
distribution functions. We leave the study of the theory uncertainties
of the interplay between the sideband events of the
next-to-leading-logarithmic Mellin-Barnes representation of double Higgs
production and rapidity-regulated flow matching models at
next-to-leading order to future work. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0266-Towards-the-Energy-Movers-Distance-at-Two-Loops-September-19-2026},
our method does not require Sudakov-suppressed N-subjettiness ratios.
