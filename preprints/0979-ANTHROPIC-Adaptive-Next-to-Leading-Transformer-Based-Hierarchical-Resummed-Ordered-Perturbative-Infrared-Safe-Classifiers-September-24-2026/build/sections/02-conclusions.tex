\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of zero-shot TMDs is admittedly heuristic. Our analysis
neglects the sphalerons of the so-called contrastive leading
singularity, which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
We have not attempted to quantify next-generation canonical differential
equations. Our analysis neglects uncertainty-aware training curves of
Higgs plus jet production, which may become relevant at the Tevatron.
