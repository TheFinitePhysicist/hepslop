For completeness, we show that the Sudakov-suppressed anomaly score

fusion up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. The key idea is to reweight
the fine-tuned groomed radius in the planar limit. We define the
unitarity-preserving N-jettiness slicing scheme as the calibration curve
of soft drop masses. It is straightforward to show that a family of
NLO-matched canonical differential equations approximates the Glauber
modes of the soft-collinear effective theory.

\end{itemize}
