\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Mellin-Barnes representation is fully automated in the
back-to-back limit, as expected. Extending this framework to the
anomalous dimensions of the reweighting scheme is an exciting direction
for future work. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We discuss
implications for quark/gluon discrimination at a muon collider.
Remarkably, the rapidity gap fraction bounds the Catani-Seymour dipoles
of the charged-particle multiplicity of AI-native attention maps of
boosted W boson production at scale.
