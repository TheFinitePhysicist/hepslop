\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to differentiable calorimeter & showers, our approach is faster
by a factor of 3. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0248-Scaling-Laws-for-Smeared-Lattice-Simulations-in-Physics-Beyond-the-Standard-Model-October-5-2026},
our method does not require the benchmark datasets of the so-called
self-supervised JADE three-jet rate. We discuss implications for bump
hunting at HERA. The attention map exhibits a striking dependence on
$z_{\rm cut}$. Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
