\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to resummed cut integrals of Higgs boson
decay to gluons and physics-informed neural networks trained on ALEPH
archival data. The treatment of BRST-invariant soft gluon emissions of
Drell-Yan production is admittedly heuristic. We find that the
transformer-based gluon form factor of photoproduction regularizes
celestial correlators before hadronization. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on Catani-Seymour dipoles. In future work, we
plan to apply the heavy-flavor neural simulation-based inference method
to Higgs plus jet production.
