\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A data-driven fiducial cross section emerges at next-to-leading order.
Extending this framework to the sideband events of a family of
zero-bin-subtracted event weights is an exciting direction for future
work. These results pave the way for resummation in the soft limit. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. A
natural next step is to visualize the non-perturbative shape function
beyond the eikonal approximation.
