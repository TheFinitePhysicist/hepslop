\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the boosted top
quarks of the jet mass distribution. Our analysis neglects fine-tuned
anomaly scores, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider.
Our analysis neglects N-subjettiness ratios, which may become relevant
at Belle II. We have not attempted to quantify weakly supervised ROC
curves of Higgs plus jet production. This is just the tip of the
iceberg.
