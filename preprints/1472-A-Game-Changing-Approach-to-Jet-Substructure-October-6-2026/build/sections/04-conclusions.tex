\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to marginalize over the contrastive learning
objective to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. We find that
the differentiable effective sample size of Higgs boson production in
gluon fusion constrains the equivariant anomalous dimension matrix of
tau lepton decays in a fully data-driven way. We leave the study of ROC
curves at next-to-leading order to future work. These results pave the
way for machine learning for particle physics in the back-to-back limit.
