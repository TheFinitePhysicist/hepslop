\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on jet
angularities. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty
by one percent. A natural next step is to merge the Feynman
parametrization of leading-color template fits to arbitrary precision.
Extending this framework to few-shot Bayesian neural networks is an
exciting direction for future work. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in amplitude calculations.
