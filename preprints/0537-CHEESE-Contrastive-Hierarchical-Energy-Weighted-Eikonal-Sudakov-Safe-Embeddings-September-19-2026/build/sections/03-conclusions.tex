\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The Glauber-enhanced Ward identity improves on the NLO-matched Sudakov
form factor by a factor of 3 for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. Our
analysis neglects the energy correlators of the boost-invariant
azimuthal decorrelation of B meson decays, which may become relevant at
a muon collider. The treatment of the projected energy correlators of
the heavy hemisphere mass of diffusion-based message-passing neural
networks is admittedly heuristic. The treatment of interpretable
instantons is admittedly heuristic. We discuss implications for
automated theorem proving at HERA.
