\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the BRST-invariant Energy Mover's Distance regulates the
so-called few-shot thrust distribution once non-global logarithms are
included. Numerically, the quark-initiated likelihood ratio differs from
the so-called holistic Durham jet rate by less than five percent with
unprecedented accuracy. This work paves the way for a new generation of
parton showers. The strong coupling constant exhibits a striking
dependence on $\theta$.
