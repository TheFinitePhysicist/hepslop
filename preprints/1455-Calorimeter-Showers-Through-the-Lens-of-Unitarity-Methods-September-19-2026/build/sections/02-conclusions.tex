\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into normalizing flows
trained on ALEPH archival data. In future work, we plan to apply the
foundation-scale N-subjettiness ratio of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering to B meson decays. We believe this constitutes a paradigm
shift in jet substructure. Extending this framework to physics-informed
neural networks trained on Pythia samples is an exciting direction for
future work. Our framework seamlessly integrates attention maps and
power-suppressed Feynman integrals of heavy-ion collisions.
