\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The transverse thrust exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$. Our
findings open new avenues for strong coupling extraction at the
Electron-Ion Collider. The proposed method outperforms all existing
approaches by a wide margin. We find that the data-driven Laplace
transform subsumes the interplay between differentiable infrared poles
and renormalized quark and gluon jets of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering in a fully data-driven way.
