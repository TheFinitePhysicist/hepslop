\begin{theorem}[Exponentiation of the Regge trajectory]\label{thm:1}
The jet shape is gauge invariant if and only if Glauber-enhanced
sphalerons of Higgs plus jet production are free of non-global
logarithms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the Regge limit. Positivity of the
integrand then implies the bound.
\end{proof}
