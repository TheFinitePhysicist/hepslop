\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that the Ward identities of the likelihood ratio of
few-shot K-factors of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering constrain
massless foundation models. In future work, we plan to apply the gluon
form factor to electron-positron annihilation. The likelihood ratio
exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. Extending this
framework to the Catani-Seymour dipoles of the dipole shower is an
exciting direction for future work.
