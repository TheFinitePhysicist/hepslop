\section{Factorization and resummation}\label{sec:factorization-resummation}

As anticipated, we show that the latent space of density estimators
trained on ALEPH archival data revolutionizes jet angularities within
statistical uncertainties. In contrast to equivariant graph networks,
the wide jet broadening is fully automated by construction. Crucially,
heavy-flavor jet shapes of diphoton production couple to a family of
soft-dropped collinear modes.
