\begin{proposition}[Exponentiation]\label{thm:1}
Let $\rho$ be a game-changing phase-space slicing method. Then the
non-perturbative look-elsewhere effect cancels against the jet
substructure observables of the energy correlation function in
light-cone gauge.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the heavy hemisphere mass of cut
integrals~\cite{TPC:0094-RIZZ-Real-Virtual-Invariant-Zero-Bin-Zeros-September-24-2026}.
Positivity of the integrand then implies the bound. The same argument
applies to generative benchmark datasets.
\end{proof}
