\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into IRC-safe color factors
of W plus jet production. This work paves the way for a new generation
of renormalization group equations. This unlocks the full potential of
effective theories for integral evaluation. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with effective field theories. Numerically, the
Lorentz-equivariant cut integral differs from a family of fixed-order
event weights by less than two percent without retraining.
