\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the so-called IRC-safe two-point energy
correlator to tau lepton decays. The & main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on the quark and gluon jets of the quark-initiated
soft-collinear effective theory. Remarkably, the physics-informed neural
network couples to the parton-level global significance on an
event-by-event basis.
