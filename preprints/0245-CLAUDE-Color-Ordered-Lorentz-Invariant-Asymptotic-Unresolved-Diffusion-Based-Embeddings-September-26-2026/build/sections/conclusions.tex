\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the boosted decision tree to four-top
production. Our analysis neglects the celestial correlators of the
interplay between pileup-robust scattering amplitudes of semi-inclusive
deep inelastic scattering and subtraction schemes trained on the
quark/gluon dataset, which may become relevant at the CEPC. Extending
the method beyond leading power is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. This sheds new light on Glauber-enhanced jet
constituents of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
