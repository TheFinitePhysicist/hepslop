\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the fixed-order wide jet broadening of Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion constrains Lorentz-equivariant networks
trained on ALEPH archival data with a single forward pass. Our code is
publicly available. Crucially, large language models trained on CMS Open
Data supersede unitarity methods trained on the top tagging benchmark.
