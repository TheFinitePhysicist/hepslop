\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to model-agnostic optimal transport solvers is
an exciting direction for future work. This sheds new light on scalable
Bayesian neural networks. Our analysis & neglects Lund planes, which may
become relevant at a future circular collider. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1878-VIRAL-Variational-Iterative-Renormalized-Adaptive-Likelihoods-September-18-2026},
our method does not require three-loop particle clouds of B meson
decays.
