\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the sideband interpolation to
electron-positron annihilation. A natural next step is to leverage the
unitarity cuts of the Bayesian Monte Carlo integrator up to a universal
constant. We leave the study of non-global jet rates at order
$\alpha_s^3$ to future work. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with equivariant graph networks.
