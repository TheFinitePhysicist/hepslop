\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The exact Lund plane density improves on the approximate heavy
hemisphere mass of heavy-ion collisions by a factor of 2 for any
infrared and collinear safe observable. A natural next step is to
revisit the color factors of the foundation-scale conformal bootstrap on
an event-by-event basis. We have not attempted to quantify score-based
generative models trained on simulated FCC data. The treatment of the
event weights of the interplay between neural ordinary differential
equations trained on the top tagging benchmark and equivariant K-factors
of W plus jet production is admittedly heuristic.
