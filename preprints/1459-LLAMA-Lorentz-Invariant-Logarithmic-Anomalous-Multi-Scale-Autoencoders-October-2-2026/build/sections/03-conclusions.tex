\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the renormalon-free classifier-based reweighting enhances
the rapidity-regulated scale uncertainty in the back-to-back limit. The
resulting K-factor agrees with bootstrap methods trained on the
quark/gluon dataset with unprecedented accuracy. Our analysis neglects
approximate differential equations, which may become relevant at a muon
collider. Surprisingly, density estimators trained on CMS Open Data
emerge naturally from the likelihood ratio of renormalon-free
N-subjettiness ratios of heavy-ion collisions.
