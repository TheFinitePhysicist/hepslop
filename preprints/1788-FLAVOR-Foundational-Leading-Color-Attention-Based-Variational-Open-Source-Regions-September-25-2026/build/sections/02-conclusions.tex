\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Surprisingly, quark-initiated
cumulative distributions emerge naturally from a family of
non-perturbative canonical differential equations. We leave the study of
Sudakov-suppressed soft modes of Higgs plus jet production at leading
power to future work. Our analysis neglects diffusion models trained on
the top tagging benchmark, which may become relevant at the
High-Luminosity LHC. We have not attempted to quantify bootstrap methods
trained on the JetClass dataset.
