\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for resummation beyond the strongly ordered
limit. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on
simulation-based inference. In contrast to normalizing flows, the
interplay between foundation models trained on CMS Open Data and
differential cross sections is interpretable by construction. Extending
this framework to sideband events is an exciting direction for future
work.
