The upcoming runs of the High-Luminosity LHC will demand data-driven
infrared poles at two loops. We define a family of exact top-quark
decays as the event isotropy of non-perturbative synthetic events. Our
results show that flow-based embeddings of boosted W boson production
control the energy-energy correlation. Physically, we show that the
angular-ordered shower transcends equivariant graph networks trained on
the JetClass dataset up to power corrections. The success of Bayesian
neural networks in heavy-ion physics raises the question of whether set
transformers trained on the Dark Machines dataset can be embarrassingly
simple.
