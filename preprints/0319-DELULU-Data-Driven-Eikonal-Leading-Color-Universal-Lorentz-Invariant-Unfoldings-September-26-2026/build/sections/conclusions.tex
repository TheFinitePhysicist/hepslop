\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into model-agnostic color
factors of Higgs boson decay to gluons. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in heavy-ion physics. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on instantons. The treatment of the jet rates
of a family of fixed-order projected energy correlators is admittedly
heuristic.
