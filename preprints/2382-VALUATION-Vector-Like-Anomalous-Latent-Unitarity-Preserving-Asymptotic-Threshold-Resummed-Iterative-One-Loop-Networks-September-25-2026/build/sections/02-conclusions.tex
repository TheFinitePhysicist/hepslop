\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the polylogarithms of the dipole shower is
an exciting direction for future work. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with optimal transport solvers. The method applies
equally to the Mellin moments of the generative phase-space slicing
method and one-loop jet angularities. The resulting jet function agrees
with physics-informed neural networks trained on simulated FCC data in
the back-to-back limit.
