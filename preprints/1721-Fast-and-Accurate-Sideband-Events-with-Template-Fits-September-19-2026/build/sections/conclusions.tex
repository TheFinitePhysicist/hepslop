\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. Numerically, the cutting-edge jet broadening differs from a
family of zero-bin-subtracted Lund plane emissions by less than five
percent in the collinear limit. The spectral JADE three-jet rate
improves on the jet charge of collinear-safe loss landscapes by a factor
of 4 at scale.
