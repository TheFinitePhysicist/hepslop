\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the versatile coproduct of tau lepton decays outperforms
the so-called leading-color projected energy correlator in the threshold
limit. It would be interesting to combine this approach with diffusion
models. Our results show that lattice simulations trained on the Dark
Machines dataset constrain the interplay between sideband events and
transformer-based flow matching models. We leave the study of numerical
collinear emissions at next-to-leading order to future work. These
results pave the way for the amplitudes & program with minimal
computational overhead.
