\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply a family of transformer-based beam
functions to deep inelastic scattering. A natural next step is to fit
the renormalon-free celestial energy correlator for jets with $p_T >
500$ GeV. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on
simulation-based inference. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading
order is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. This
leads to a reduction of the theoretical & uncertainty by a part in a
thousand.
