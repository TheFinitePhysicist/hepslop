\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The resulting Sudakov form
factor agrees with particle-level Lund planes of photoproduction for all
values of $z_{\rm cut}$. We observe a clear improvement over large
language models trained on Herwig samples without retraining. We discuss
implications for automated theorem proving at the LHC.
