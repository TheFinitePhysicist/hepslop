\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over score-based generative models
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. A
particle-level two-point energy correlator emerges at next-to-leading
order. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
saddle-point approximations trained on CMS Open Data. These results pave
the way for perturbative QCD without any additional assumptions.
