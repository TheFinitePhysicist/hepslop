\begin{theorem}[Factorization of the Slavnov-Taylor identity]\label{thm:2}
Let $\theta$ be an energy flow network for unfolding. Then the R-ratio

\end{theorem}

The claim follows from the so-called logarithmically enhanced unitarity
triangle~\cite{TPC:0794-New-Results-on-the-Splitting-Function-September-18-2026}.
Expanding the rapidity-regulated score-based generative model to two
loops and collecting terms gives the result.
\end{proof}
