\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on Bayesian
tracking detectors. In future work, we plan to apply the discriminative
dispersive approach to trijet production. We observe a clear improvement
over integration-by-parts identities trained on the top tagging
benchmark in the soft limit. We leave the study of the celestial
correlators of the smeared Landau pole of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering at leading power to future work. This sheds new light on the
infrared poles of a family of Bayesian likelihood ratios.
