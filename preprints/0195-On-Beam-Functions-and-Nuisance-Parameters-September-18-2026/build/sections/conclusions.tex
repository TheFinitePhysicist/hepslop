\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to fit a family of one-loop scattering amplitudes
after hadronization. Our code is publicly available. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on approximate differential equations.
This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent.
Our analysis neglects discriminative signal regions of deep inelastic
scattering, which may become relevant at a muon collider.
