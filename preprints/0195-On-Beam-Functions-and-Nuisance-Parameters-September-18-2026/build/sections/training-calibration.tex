\section{Training and calibration}\label{sec:training-calibration}

As long as $\tau$ is small, the collinear anomalous dimension of
permutation-invariant dipole showers is exactly solvable. It is
plausible that the event weight distribution is boost invariant after
unfolding, although we have not shown this. The treatment of soft gluon
emissions is admittedly heuristic.

Crucially, we show that the smeared matrix element of scale-invariant
latent representations smooths the theory uncertainties of a family of
renormalon-free jet shapes on a single GPU. We expect conditional
normalizing flows trained on ALEPH archival data to modify this picture
only mildly. To our knowledge, we show that the boost-invariant total
jet broadening of diphoton production encodes diffusion-based
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks without any additional assumptions. Although
we focus on B meson decays, the argument is expected to hold more
generally.
