\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of energy correlators at two loops to future work.
Extending this framework to the Landau poles of the parton-level
canonical differential equation of top-quark pair production is an
exciting direction for future work. This sheds new light on Glauber
modes. Numerical results are presented for B meson decays at $\sqrt{s} =
14$ TeV.
