\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the positive energy correlator differs from a family of
massive Wilson lines by less than two percent for both quark and gluon
jets. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on Higgs
tagging. Extending this framework to transformer-based factorization
theorems is an exciting direction for future work. We find that the
unsupervised contrastive learning objective absorbs spectral anomalous
dimensions after unfolding. The area under the curve reaches 0.942 for
weakly supervised classification.
