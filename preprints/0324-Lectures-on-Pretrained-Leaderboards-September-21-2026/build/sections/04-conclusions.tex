\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. Our analysis neglects rapidity divergences, which may become
relevant at HERA. Our analysis neglects neural ordinary differential
equations trained on the JetClass dataset, which may become relevant at
the High-Luminosity LHC. Extending this framework to boosted top quarks
is an exciting direction for future work. This work paves the way for a
new generation of renormalization group equations.
