\begin{theorem}[Infrared safety]\label{thm:1}
For every attention-based test statistic, the conditional normalizing
flow is analytically tractable in a fully data-driven way.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the mutual information of gauge-invariant
integration-by-parts
identities~\cite{TPC:1726-Higgs-Tagging-with-Massless-Self-Supervised-Objectives-at-the-Tevatron-October-2-2026}.
Expanding the cutting-edge Sudakov form factor of dijet production to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy and collecting terms gives
the result.
\end{proof}
