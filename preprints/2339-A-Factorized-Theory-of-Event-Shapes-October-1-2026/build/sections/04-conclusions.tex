\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to closed-form projected energy correlators of vector boson
fusion, our approach is more accurate by a factor of 2. The treatment of
jet shapes is admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the angular-ordered
profile likelihood differs from the cutting-edge three-point energy
correlator of Z plus jet production by less than two percent for both
quark and gluon jets. Our analysis neglects non-global chatbots, which
may become relevant at the LHC.
