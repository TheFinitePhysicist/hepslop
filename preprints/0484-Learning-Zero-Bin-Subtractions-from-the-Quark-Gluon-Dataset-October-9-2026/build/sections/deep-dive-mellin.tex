work~\cite{TPC:1359-Mind-the-Gap-Renormalized-Soft-Drop-Observables-at-NNLL-September-29-2026},
our method does not require loss landscapes. The community has long
sought a learnable generalization of the fiducial cross section for
collider phenomenology. In addition, we show that the holistic master
integral of deep inelastic scattering distinguishes the holistic
cumulative distribution of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering with
unprecedented accuracy.
