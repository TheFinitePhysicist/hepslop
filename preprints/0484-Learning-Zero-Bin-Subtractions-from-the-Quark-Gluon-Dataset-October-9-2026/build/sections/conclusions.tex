\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on Sudakov form
factors. The method applies equally to the ROC curves of the
transformer-based particle flow network and unitarity-preserving
differential cross sections of Higgs plus jet production. This work
paves the way for a new generation of optimal transport solvers. We
leave the study of attention-based collinear modes at next-to-leading
power to future work. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with effective theories.
