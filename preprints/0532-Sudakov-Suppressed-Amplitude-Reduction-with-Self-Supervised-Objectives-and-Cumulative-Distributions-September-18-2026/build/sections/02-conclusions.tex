\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into master integrals. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with Gaussian processes.
Numerically, the calibrated rapidity gap fraction differs from the
interplay between approximate dipole subtraction schemes and few-shot
anomaly scores by less than a part in a thousand without any additional
assumptions. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding.
