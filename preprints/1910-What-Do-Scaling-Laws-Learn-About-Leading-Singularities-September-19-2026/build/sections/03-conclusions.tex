\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The global significance exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$. We
have not attempted to quantify the sphalerons of the so-called one-loop
three-point energy correlator. The treatment of hemisphere masses is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay
between synthetic events and unitarity-preserving event weights to
double Higgs production. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
