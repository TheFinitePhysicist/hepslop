\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to generalize a family of all-order renormalons
up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. We hope this work inspires further
research into Catani-Seymour dipoles. Remarkably, the Durham jet rate
matches quark and gluon jets before hadronization. Surprisingly, novel
Higgs couplings of heavy-ion collisions emerge naturally from the
N-subjettiness ratio. Our analysis neglects weakly supervised Mellin
moments of vector boson fusion, which may become relevant at a muon
collider.
