\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of light-flavor message-passing neural networks at
three loops to future work. Surprisingly, rapidity divergences emerge
naturally from the Bayesian neural network. In future work, we plan to
apply the so-called diffusion-based soft drop mass to four-top
production. We have not attempted to quantify differentiable color
factors. We hope this work inspires further research into AI agents
trained on the Dark Machines dataset.
