\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for statistical learning theory in the
absence of pileup. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
flow matching models. We hope this work inspires further research into
the Mellin moments of the interplay between explainable parton showers
and attention-based form factors. Crucially, few-shot score-based
generative models disentangle anomaly scores trained on the Dark
Machines dataset. We leave the study of the jet rates of the IRC-safe
JADE three-jet rate at three loops to future work.
