\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
gauge-invariant weakly supervised classifiers is admittedly heuristic.
In future work, we plan to apply the conformal quark form factor to
electron-positron annihilation. Extending this framework to
rapidity-regulated boosted top quarks of top-quark pair production is an
exciting direction for future work.
