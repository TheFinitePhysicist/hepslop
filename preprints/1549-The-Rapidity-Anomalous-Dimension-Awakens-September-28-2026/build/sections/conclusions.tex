\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on versatile
unitarity cuts of Drell-Yan production. Extending the method beyond
three loops is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
This is just the tip of the iceberg. In future work, we plan to apply
the approximate soft-collinear effective theory to dijet production.
Extending this framework to the jet substructure observables of the
K-factor is an exciting direction for future work.
