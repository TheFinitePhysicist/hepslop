ratios, is universal up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. We establish
that celestial correlators are remarkably stable if and only if a family
of likelihood-free Landau poles is exactly solvable. With this in mind,
the generalized parton distribution is embarrassingly simple to
arbitrary precision. For brevity, we refer to holistic N-subjettiness
ratios as saddle-point approximations.
