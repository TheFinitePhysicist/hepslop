\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, physics-informed large language models emerge naturally
from a family of exact event weights. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on resonance searches. Our framework
seamlessly integrates subtraction schemes trained on simulated FCC data
and collinear-safe anomaly scores. We hope this work inspires further
research into Lorentz-equivariant jet shapes of diphoton production.
