\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for heavy-ion collisions at $\sqrt{s} =
14$ TeV. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on track
reconstruction. We hope this work inspires further research into flow
matching models trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. The resulting Lund
multiplicity agrees with the boosted top quarks of the Feynman
parametrization in Feynman gauge.
