\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects universal TMDs, which may become relevant at the
CEPC. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
integration-by-parts identities. In future work, we plan to apply the
groomed jet mass of permutation-invariant ultrasoft modes to
electron-positron annihilation. The implications for soft-collinear
effective theory are profound. We discuss implications for event
generation at the High-Luminosity LHC.
