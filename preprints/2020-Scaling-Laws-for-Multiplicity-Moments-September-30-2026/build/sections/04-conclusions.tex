\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our findings
open new avenues for integral evaluation at RHIC. A generative azimuthal
decorrelation emerges at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
Our analysis neglects dipole subtraction schemes trained on the Dark
Machines dataset, which may become relevant at the Future Circular
Collider.
