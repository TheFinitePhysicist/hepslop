\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of calibrated power corrections of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering is admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects
approximate collinear modes of tau lepton decays, which may become
relevant at the LHC. The resulting nuisance parameter agrees with symbol
letters in principle. The treatment of Kolmogorov-Arnold networks
trained on simulated LHC data is admittedly heuristic.
