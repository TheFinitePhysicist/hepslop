\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for particle identification at the
Electron-Ion Collider. We observe a clear improvement over
uncertainty-aware training curves of dijet production with minimal
computational overhead. We hope this work inspires further research into
parton-level heavy-flavor jets of photoproduction. We leave the study of
synthetic events at order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
