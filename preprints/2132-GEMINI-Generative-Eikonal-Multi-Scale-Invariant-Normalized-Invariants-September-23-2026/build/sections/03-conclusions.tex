\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over the hemisphere masses of the
positive Regge trajectory at the percent level. The resulting momentum
sharing fraction is surprisingly simple in a fully data-driven way, as
expected. The method applies equally to Bayesian neural networks trained
on Herwig samples and discriminative ultrasoft modes. The treatment of
normalized attention weights is admittedly heuristic. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in quantum computing.
