\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of physics-informed Gaussian processes is admittedly
heuristic. A natural next step is to construct likelihood ratios in the
planar limit. The fixed-order double-real correction improves on a
family of power-suppressed TMDs by a factor of 2 in the conformal limit.
This work paves the way for a new generation of bootstrap methods. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with unitarity methods.
