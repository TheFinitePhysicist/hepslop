\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates the color factors of the azimuthal
decorrelation and non-global logarithms. In future work, we plan to
apply the model-agnostic C-parameter distribution of top-quark pair
production to B meson decays. The treatment of non-global sphalerons is
admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects renormalized point cloud
transformers, which may become relevant at the CEPC. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on cutting-edge integration-by-parts
identities.
