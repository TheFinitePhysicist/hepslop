\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to BRST-invariant reinforcement learning
agents and normalizing flows trained on the JetClass dataset. In future
work, we plan to apply the stochastic unfolding procedure to Higgs boson
production in gluon fusion. We have not attempted to quantify
fixed-order antenna showers. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
