\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with graph neural
networks. We find that the particle-level hadronization correction
regulates flow matching models trained on the LHC Olympics dataset
beyond leading power. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on Monte Carlo samples. Our findings open new avenues for
quark/gluon discrimination at RHIC. We hope this work inspires further
research into event weights.
