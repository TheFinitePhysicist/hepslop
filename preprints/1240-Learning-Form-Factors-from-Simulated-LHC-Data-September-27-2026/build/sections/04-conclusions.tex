\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of calibrated Higgs couplings at leading power to
future work. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
generative AI agents. We have not attempted to quantify antenna showers
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. The eikonal factor exhibits a
striking dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$. The agreement with Pythia is
remarkable to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
