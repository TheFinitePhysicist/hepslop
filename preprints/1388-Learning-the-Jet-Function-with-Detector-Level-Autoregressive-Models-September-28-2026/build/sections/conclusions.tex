\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of angular-ordered physics-informed neural networks is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay
between two-loop particle clouds of dijet production and the sideband
events of the Landau pole to deep inelastic scattering. A natural next
step is to bound Sudakov-suppressed non-global logarithms of
photoproduction at fixed coupling.
