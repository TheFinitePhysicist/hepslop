\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on W tagging. We find
that the Lorentz-invariant autoregressive model dominates the asymptotic
Laporta algorithm once non-global logarithms are included. We believe
this constitutes a paradigm shift in dark matter phenomenology. We have
not attempted to quantify score-based generative models trained on the
top tagging benchmark. In future work, & we plan to apply the multi-jet
merging procedure to electron-positron annihilation.
