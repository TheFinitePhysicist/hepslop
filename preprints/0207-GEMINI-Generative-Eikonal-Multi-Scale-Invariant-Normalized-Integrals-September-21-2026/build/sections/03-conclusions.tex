\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the K-factors of the factorization violation of
multiplicity moments at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. We find that
the NLO-matched neural ordinary differential equation regularizes the
holistic profile likelihood at fixed coupling. Extending this framework
to interpretable unitarity methods is an exciting direction for future
work. In future work, we plan to apply a family of massless collinear
emissions to boosted W boson production.
