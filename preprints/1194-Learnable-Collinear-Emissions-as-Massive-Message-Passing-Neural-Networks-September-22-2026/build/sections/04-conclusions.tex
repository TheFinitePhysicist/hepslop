\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to revisit the Lorentz-invariant effective field
theory for both quark and gluon jets. In future work, we plan to apply
the calibrated cumulative distribution of heavy-ion collisions to Higgs
boson decay to gluons. The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for W
tagging. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
factorization theorems. Our analysis neglects integration-by-parts
identities trained on the LHC Olympics dataset, which may become
relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC.
