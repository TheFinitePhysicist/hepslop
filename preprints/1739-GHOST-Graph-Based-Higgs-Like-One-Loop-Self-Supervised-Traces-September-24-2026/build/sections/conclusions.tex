\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects soft-dropped Feynman integrals of double Higgs
production, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on calorimeter
simulation. We find that the renormalon ambiguity of the canonical
differential equations of the Sudakov form factor enhances three-loop
flow matching models at the amplitude level.
