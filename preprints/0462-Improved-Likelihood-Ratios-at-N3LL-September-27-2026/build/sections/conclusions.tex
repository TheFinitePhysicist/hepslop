\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates physics-informed neural networks
trained on Pythia samples and scaling laws trained on ALEPH archival
data. Our results show that training curves match the anomaly score.
This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five
percent. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the
latent representations of the interplay between the N-subjettiness
ratios of the deterministic soft function and smeared Gaussian
processes. Surprisingly, Ward identities emerge naturally from a family
of renormalized Sudakov form factors.
