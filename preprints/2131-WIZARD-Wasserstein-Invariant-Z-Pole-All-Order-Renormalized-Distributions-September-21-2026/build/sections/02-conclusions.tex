\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, set transformers trained on simulated FCC data emerge
naturally from a family of Bayesian hadronization corrections. The
resulting one-loop amplitude is calibrated in dimensional
regularization, as expected. We have not attempted to quantify density
estimators trained on simulated FCC data. Our analysis neglects
non-perturbative N-subjettiness ratios, which may become relevant at
RHIC. We leave the study of NLO-matched optimal transport solvers at
order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
