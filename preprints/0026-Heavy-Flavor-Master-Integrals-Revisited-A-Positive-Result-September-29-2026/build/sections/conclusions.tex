\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over renormalized hemisphere masses of W
plus jet production within statistical uncertainties. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0380-STOCHASTIC-PARROT-Spectral-Topological-One-Loop-Calibrated-Higgs-Like-Anomalous-Sudakov-Safe-Transformer-Based-Invariant-Contrastive-Positive-All-September-27-2026},
our method does not require jet angularities. Extending the method
beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. This work paves the way for a new generation of
point cloud transformers.
