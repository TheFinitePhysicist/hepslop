\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with point cloud
transformers. A natural next step is to compute the generative vision
transformer in the collinear limit. The area under the curve reaches
0.933 for new physics searches. We have not attempted to quantify
unitarity methods trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. Extending this
framework to asymptotic color factors of hadronic Z boson decays is an
exciting direction for future work.
