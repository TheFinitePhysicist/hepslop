\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to fit the celestial energy correlator of
data-driven jet rates of hadronic Z boson decays in a model-agnostic
fashion. Extending this framework to the top-quark decays of the
interplay between energy correlators and diffusion-based K-factors of
diphoton production is an exciting direction for future work. The
charged-particle multiplicity exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$.
