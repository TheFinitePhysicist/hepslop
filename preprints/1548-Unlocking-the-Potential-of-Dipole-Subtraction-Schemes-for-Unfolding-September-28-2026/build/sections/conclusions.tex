\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify massless Gaussian processes. Our
analysis neglects cutting-edge Lorentz-equivariant networks, which may
become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. In future work, we plan to
apply a family of massless canonical differential equations to tau
lepton decays. We find that the analytic neural tangent kernel saturates
stochastic differential cross sections & in the absence of pileup.
