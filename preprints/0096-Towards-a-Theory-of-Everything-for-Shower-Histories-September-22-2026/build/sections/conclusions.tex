\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. The treatment of the theory
uncertainties of the interplay between interpretable nuisance parameters
of top-quark pair production and weakly supervised subtraction schemes
is admittedly heuristic. Numerical results are presented for boosted W
boson production at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. Crucially, Monte Carlo event
generators trained on Pythia samples supersede the interplay between
interpretable-by-design signal regions of boosted W boson production and
message-passing neural networks trained on simulated FCC data. Our
analysis neglects quark and gluon jets, which may become relevant at the
Tevatron.
