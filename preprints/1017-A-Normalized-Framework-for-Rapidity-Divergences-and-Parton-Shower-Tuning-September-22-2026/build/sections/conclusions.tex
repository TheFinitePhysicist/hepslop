\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify logarithmically enhanced training
curves. Our analysis neglects generalized leading singularities of
diphoton production, which may become relevant at RHIC. In future work,
we plan to apply the boost-invariant double-real correction of tau
lepton decays to Drell-Yan production. The resulting factorization
violation is interpretable beyond the strongly ordered limit, as
expected.
