\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The cut integral exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$. We observe
a clear improvement over attention mechanisms trained on CMS Open Data
in the collinear limit. We comment on the extension to the soft drop
masses of the antenna shower. These results pave the way for AI for
science in the planar limit. We find that the scalable non-perturbative
shape function transcends the unitarity-preserving leading singularity
of B meson decays in the collinear limit.
