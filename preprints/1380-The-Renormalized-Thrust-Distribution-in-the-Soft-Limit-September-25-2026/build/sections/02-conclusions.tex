\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for jet tagging. We leave the
study of massive parton showers at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Extending this framework to
energy flow networks trained on ALEPH archival data is an exciting
direction for future work.
