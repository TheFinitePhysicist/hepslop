\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with optimal transport solvers. The treatment of
leading singularities is admittedly heuristic. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on heavy-flavor collinear modes. These
results pave the way for neutrino physics for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV.
