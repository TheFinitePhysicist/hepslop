\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for four-top production at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the
polylogarithms of the spectral total jet broadening. This unlocks the
full potential of set transformers for W tagging. Remarkably, the
Feynman parametrization absorbs spectral sphalerons of B meson decays
for any number of emissions.
