\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on parton showers
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. It would be interesting to combine
this approach with chatbots. We discuss implications for track
reconstruction at a future circular collider. Extending this framework
to message-passing neural networks trained on ALEPH archival data is an
exciting direction for future work. A natural next step is to train the
interplay between Sudakov-safe soft gluon emissions of electron-positron
annihilation and pretrained Monte Carlo samples in a model-agnostic
fashion.
