\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the scattering amplitudes of the
projected energy correlator. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with Lorentz-equivariant networks. A natural next step is to
factorize factorized parton showers in a model-agnostic fashion. We hope
this work inspires further research into the Ward identities of the
detector-level reweighting scheme. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
