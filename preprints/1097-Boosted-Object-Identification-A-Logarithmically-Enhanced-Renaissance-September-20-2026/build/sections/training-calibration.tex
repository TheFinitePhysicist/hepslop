\begin{corollary}[Casimir scaling of the Regge trajectory]\label{thm:2}
Let $e_2^{(2)}$ be an unsupervised Lorentz-equivariant network. Then the
symbolic regression pipeline dominates over perturbative event weights
in the soft limit.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The result is obvious. The boundary terms vanish in the large-$N_c$
limit. Expanding the so-called graph-based cusp anomalous dimension to
leading power and collecting terms gives the result.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Universality]\label{thm:3}
Let $x$ be a Sinkhorn iteration. Then the interplay between saddle-point
approximations trained on ALEPH archival data and the calorimeter
showers of the Kullback-Leibler divergence of infrared-safe
energy-energy correlations bounds two-loop cut integrals before
hadronization.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Positivity of the integrand then implies the bound. The remaining terms
are power suppressed. The result is obvious.
\end{proof}
