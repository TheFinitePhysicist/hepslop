\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the Lorentz-equivariant autoregressive model enhances
asymptotic soft modes of photoproduction in light-cone gauge. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with slicing methods. Extending
this framework to conformal multiplicity moments is an exciting
direction for future work. We leave the study of principled energy flow
polynomials at next-to-leading order to future work. Our findings open
new avenues for fast simulation at the Future Circular Collider.
