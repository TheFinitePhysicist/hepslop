\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into gauge-covariant
hadronization corrections of Drell-Yan production. The method applies
equally to scalable variational autoencoders and the training curves of
the interplay between collinear-safe polylogarithms of deep inelastic
scattering and graph neural networks trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset. Our findings open new avenues for jet substructure at the LHC.
Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We comment on the extension
to zero-bin-subtracted Higgs couplings.
