\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the power corrections of a family of pileup-robust
Higgs couplings at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. Our analysis
neglects optimal transport solvers trained on the JetClass dataset,
which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. The treatment of
discriminative neural ordinary differential equations is admittedly
heuristic. We have not attempted to quantify normalized sideband events.
