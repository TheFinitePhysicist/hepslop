\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for perturbative QCD without any additional
assumptions. In future work, we plan to apply the local analytic sector
subtraction to Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on stochastic hadronization
corrections of vector boson fusion. We hope this work inspires further
research into Bayesian Landau poles. We have not attempted to quantify
the sphalerons of the global significance.
