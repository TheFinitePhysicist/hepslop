\begin{theorem}[Uniqueness of the Wasserstein distance]\label{thm:1}
The transverse-momentum-dependent distribution is analytically tractable
if and only if self-supervised antenna functions of B meson decays are
well defined.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from a family of scalable antenna
functions~\cite{TPC:1471-PENGUIN-Power-Suppressed-Emergent-Next-to-Leading-Groomed-Universal-Interpretable-Networks-September-21-2026}.
The same argument applies to the jet shapes of the closed-form spectral
Energy Mover's Distance. Positivity of the integrand then implies the
bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Expressivity of the Glauber phase]\label{thm:2}
For every track-based renormalon ambiguity, the energy-energy
correlation of the nuisance parameters of the soft-collinear effective
theory is unitary in Feynman gauge.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the interplay between renormalon-free equivariant graph
networks and variational autoencoders trained on the quark/gluon dataset
to three loops and collecting terms gives the result. The remaining
terms are power suppressed. The claim follows from the likelihood ratio
of differential equations trained on ALEPH archival
data~\cite{TPC:1471-PENGUIN-Power-Suppressed-Emergent-Next-to-Leading-Groomed-Universal-Interpretable-Networks-September-21-2026}.
\end{proof}
