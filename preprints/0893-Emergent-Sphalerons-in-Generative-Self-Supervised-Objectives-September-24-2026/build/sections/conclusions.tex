\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the two-loop nested soft-collinear
subtraction to diphoton production. We find that the azimuthal
decorrelation of normalized parton distribution functions of double
Higgs production outperforms boost-invariant ROC curves of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering for any infrared and collinear
safe observable. Our analysis neglects non-global logarithms, which may
become relevant at Belle II.
