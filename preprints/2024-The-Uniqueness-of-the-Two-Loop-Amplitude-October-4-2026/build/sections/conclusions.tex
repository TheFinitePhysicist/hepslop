\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. Extending this framework to the optimal
transport plans of the point cloud transformer is an exciting direction
for future work. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between
positive collinear modes and power-suppressed Wilson lines to Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion. Extending the method beyond leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
physics-informed neural networks.
