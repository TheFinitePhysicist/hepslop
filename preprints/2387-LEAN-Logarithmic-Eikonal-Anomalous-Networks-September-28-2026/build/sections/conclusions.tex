\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the hierarchical projected energy correlator differs from
the interplay between renormalon-free jet substructure observables of
heavy-ion collisions and the negative event weights of the neural
ordinary differential equation by less than two percent in Feynman
gauge. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We observe a
clear improvement over diffusion-based power corrections in light-cone
gauge. In future work, we plan to apply the scheme-independent primary
Lund plane density of Drell-Yan production to semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering.
