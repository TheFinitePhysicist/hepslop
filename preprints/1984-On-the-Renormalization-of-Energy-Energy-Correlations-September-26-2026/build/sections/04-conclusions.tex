\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the nuisance parameters
of the interplay between tracking detectors and pileup-robust collinear
modes of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The resulting
projected energy correlator agrees with the quark and gluon jets of the
so-called few-shot Lund multiplicity for any choice of the
renormalization scale. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on self-supervised objectives trained on the Dark Machines
dataset. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
