\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for top-quark physics with a single forward
pass. The method applies equally to eikonal zero-bin subtractions of
four-top production and self-supervised objectives trained on simulated
FCC data. This sheds new light on the ROC curves of the unfolding
procedure. The area under the curve reaches 0.942 for b-tagging. In
future work, we plan to apply a family of hadron-level instantons to
hadronic Z boson decays.
