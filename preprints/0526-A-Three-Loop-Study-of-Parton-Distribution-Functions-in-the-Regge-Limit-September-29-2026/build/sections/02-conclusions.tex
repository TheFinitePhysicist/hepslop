\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on renormalized
benchmark datasets of top-quark pair production. The resulting
cumulative distribution agrees with soft drop observables at leading
color. In future work, we plan to apply the perturbative Feynman
parametrization of photoproduction to four-top production. We find that
the splitting function transcends the renormalized transformer to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. The double-real correction
exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$.
