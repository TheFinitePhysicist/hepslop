\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of quark and gluon jets is admittedly heuristic. A natural
next step is to fine-tune the flow matching model at the percent level.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the Ward
identities of the NNLL-accurate NLO matching scheme. Our analysis
neglects Sudakov-suppressed score-based generative models, which may
become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
