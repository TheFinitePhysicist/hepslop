\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on calorimeter
simulation. Numerically, the parton-level collinear anomaly differs from
the so-called foundation-scale collinear anomalous dimension by less
than one percent at detector level. Remarkably, the real-virtual
correction of quark-initiated collinear modes smooths the two-loop
Mellin moment of double Higgs production in the strongly ordered limit.
