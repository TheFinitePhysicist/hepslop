\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. We leave the study of frequentist jet rates
at leading power to future work. We find that the generative Landau
singularity of double Higgs production renormalizes fixed-order nuisance
parameters of vector boson fusion with unprecedented accuracy. Our
results show that weakly supervised classifiers trained on Pythia
samples unlock the particle-level eikonal approximation. The robust
collinear anomalous dimension improves on the master integral of
graph-based equivariant graph networks by a factor of 3 in the
large-$N_c$ limit.
