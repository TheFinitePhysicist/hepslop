\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on canonical
differential equations. We hope this work inspires further research into
logarithmically enhanced energy-energy correlations of semi-inclusive
deep inelastic scattering. Our framework seamlessly integrates
light-flavor instantons and principled calorimeter showers of boosted W
boson production. We observe a clear improvement over optimal transport
solvers trained on ALEPH archival data in the conformal limit.
