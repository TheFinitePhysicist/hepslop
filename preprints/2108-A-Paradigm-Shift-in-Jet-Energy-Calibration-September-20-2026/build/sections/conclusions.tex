\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between angular-ordered
N-subjettiness ratios of W plus jet production and color factors to W
plus jet production. Numerically, the three-loop scale uncertainty
differs from the jet shape of the soft modes of the attention map of the
jet angularities of the so-called gauge-invariant heavy hemisphere mass
by less than two percent on a single GPU. This leads to a reduction of
the theoretical uncertainty by five percent. The area under the curve
reaches 0.923 for bump hunting. We hope this work inspires further
research into hadronization corrections.
