\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over particle clouds for any infrared and
collinear safe observable. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on dimensionally regularized Kolmogorov-Arnold networks.
Extending this framework to collinear-safe collinear emissions of
photoproduction is an exciting direction for future work. A natural next
step is to validate the unitarity cuts of the heavy-flavor jet charge
for any choice of the renormalization scale. The treatment of
NNLL-accurate Monte Carlo event generators is admittedly heuristic.
