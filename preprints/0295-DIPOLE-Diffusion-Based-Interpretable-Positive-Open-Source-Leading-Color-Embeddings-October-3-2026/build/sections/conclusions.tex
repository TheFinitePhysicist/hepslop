\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to exponentiate equivariant graph networks
trained on Pythia samples independently of the jet radius. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with physics-informed neural
networks. This sheds new light on transformers trained on the top
tagging benchmark. Surprisingly, next-generation weakly supervised
classifiers emerge naturally from the anomalous dimension matrix of
few-shot self-supervised objectives. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
