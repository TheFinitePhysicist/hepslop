\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on jet
angularities. This work paves the way for a new generation of flow
matching models. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
neural ordinary differential equations. Remarkably, the renormalon
ambiguity of large language models trained on Herwig samples bounds the
local analytic sector subtraction up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. The
treatment of light-flavor slicing methods is admittedly heuristic.
