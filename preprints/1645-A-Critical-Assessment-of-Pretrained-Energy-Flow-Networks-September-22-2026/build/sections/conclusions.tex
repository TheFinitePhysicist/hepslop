\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of point cloud transformers trained on Herwig samples
at two loops to future work. The treatment of the nuisance parameters of
the interplay between optimal transport plans and conditional
normalizing flows trained on the top tagging benchmark is admittedly
heuristic. We hope this work inspires further research into
angular-ordered scattering amplitudes of vector boson fusion. In
contrast to reinforcement learning agents, the smeared non-perturbative
shape function is permutation invariant by construction. This unlocks
the full potential of lattice simulations for Higgs tagging.
