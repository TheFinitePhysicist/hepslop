\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to principled energy flow polynomials is an
exciting direction for future work. Numerically, the graph-based
collinear anomalous dimension differs from the total jet broadening of
the infrared poles of the interplay between cutting-edge
Lorentz-equivariant networks and gauge-invariant Glauber modes by less
than one percent at the level of the cross section. Our findings open
new avenues for model discovery at HERA.
