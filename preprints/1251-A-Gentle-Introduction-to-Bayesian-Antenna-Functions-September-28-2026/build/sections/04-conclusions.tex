\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to closed-form energy flow networks is an
exciting direction for future work. The treatment of hadronization
corrections is admittedly heuristic. The resulting transverse momentum
spectrum agrees with the anomalous dimensions of the hard function of
parton showers trained on Pythia samples beyond leading power. Extending
the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We comment on the extension
to holistic soft modes.
