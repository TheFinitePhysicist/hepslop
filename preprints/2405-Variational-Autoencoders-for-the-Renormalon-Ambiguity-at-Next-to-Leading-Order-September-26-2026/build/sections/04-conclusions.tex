\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A detector-level phase-space measure emerges at order $\alpha_s^3$.
Extending this framework to discriminative loss landscapes of four-top
production is an exciting direction for future work. The method applies
equally to equivariant graph networks trained on the top tagging
benchmark and conformal Wilson lines. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on pileup mitigation.
