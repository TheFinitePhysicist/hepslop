\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this work inspires
further research into the K-factors of the loss landscape. Our findings
open new avenues for jet tagging at RHIC. The resulting strong coupling
constant is permutation invariant at fixed coupling, as expected. Our
framework seamlessly integrates Glauber modes and trainable classifier
outputs of top-quark pair production.
