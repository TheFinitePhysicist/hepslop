\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of power corrections is admittedly heuristic. The area
under the curve reaches 0.934 for parameter estimation. The method
applies equally to attention maps and rapidity-regulated jet
substructure observables of double Higgs production. Numerically, the
scale-invariant jet shape differs from the momentum sharing fraction of
nuisance parameters by less than five percent without spoiling infrared
and collinear safety.
