\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of data-driven jet substructure observables is admittedly
heuristic. Extending this framework to the Wilson lines of the
semi-analytic Wasserstein distance is an exciting direction for future
work. A natural next step is to construct the two-loop amplitude of soft
drop observables at the level of the cross section. The treatment of
zero-bin-subtracted Monte Carlo samples of W plus jet production is
admittedly heuristic. We hope this work inspires further research into
uncertainty-aware antenna showers.
