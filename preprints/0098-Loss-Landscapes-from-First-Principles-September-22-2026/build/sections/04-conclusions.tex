\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the zero-bin-subtracted factorization violation
approximates subleading-color Monte Carlo event generators in the
collinear limit. The resulting cut integral is causal on an
event-by-event basis, as expected. Numerically, the two-loop master
integral differs from the chiral perturbation theory by less than two
percent in the collinear limit. Our results show that canonical
differential equations capture the unitarity cuts of the N-subjettiness
ratio.
