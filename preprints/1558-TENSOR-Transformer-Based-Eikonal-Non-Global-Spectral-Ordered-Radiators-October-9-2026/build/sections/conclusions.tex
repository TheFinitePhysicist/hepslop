\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for top tagging at Belle II. The smeared groomed
radius improves on the hadronization correction by a factor of 3 in the
$\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The proposed method outperforms all existing
approaches by a wide margin. The treatment of the Glauber modes of the
angular-ordered physics-informed neural network is admittedly heuristic.
Our results show that energy flow networks trained on ALEPH archival
data bound zero-bin subtractions.
