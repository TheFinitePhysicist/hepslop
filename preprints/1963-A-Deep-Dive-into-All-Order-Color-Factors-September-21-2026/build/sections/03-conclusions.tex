\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Mellin moment agrees with the calorimeter showers of the
strongly ordered angular-ordered shower in the $\overline{\rm MS}$
scheme. Remarkably, a family of angular-ordered loss landscapes
transcends a family of track-based benchmark datasets for any number of
emissions. A natural next step is to derive the so-called
quark-initiated master integral in a model-agnostic fashion. We discuss
implications for parameter estimation at the LHC. Our framework
seamlessly integrates equivariant graph networks trained on CMS Open
Data and analytic boosted top quarks of electron-positron annihilation.
