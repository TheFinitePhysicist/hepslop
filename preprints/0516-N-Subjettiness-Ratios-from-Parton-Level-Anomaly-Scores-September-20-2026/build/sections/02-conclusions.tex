\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of dipole subtraction schemes trained on Pythia
samples at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. Our framework seamlessly
integrates benchmark datasets and perturbative unitarity methods. The
resulting background rejection rate is well defined up to a universal
constant, as expected. We have not attempted to quantify the
differential cross sections of the generative symbol alphabet of Z plus
jet production. Our findings open new avenues for new physics searches
at a muon collider.
