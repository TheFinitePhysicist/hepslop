\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects physics-informed neural networks trained on the
quark/gluon dataset, which may become relevant at the Future Circular
Collider. These results pave the way for collider phenomenology
independently of the jet radius. We have not attempted to quantify
infrared-safe set transformers. In future work, we plan to apply a
family of spectral classifier outputs to boosted W boson production.
