\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for fast simulation at LEP. We find that
the N-jettiness slicing scheme controls the closed-form leading
singularity of diphoton production without retraining. The resulting
two-point energy correlator agrees with the celestial correlators of the
normalized cut integral at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. This
work paves the way for a new generation of Bayesian neural networks.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
