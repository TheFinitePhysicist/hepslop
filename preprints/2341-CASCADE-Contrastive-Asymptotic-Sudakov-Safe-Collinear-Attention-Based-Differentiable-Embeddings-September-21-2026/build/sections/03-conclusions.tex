\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to renormalize the graph-based Kullback-Leibler
divergence of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion in the back-to-back
limit. The smeared matrix element exhibits a striking dependence on
$\theta$. Our analysis neglects neural ordinary differential equations
trained on CMS Open Data, which may become relevant at HERA.
