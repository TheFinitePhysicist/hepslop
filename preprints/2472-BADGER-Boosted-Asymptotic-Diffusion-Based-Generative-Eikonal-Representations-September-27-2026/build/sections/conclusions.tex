\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the factorized Lund plane density differs from the Regge
trajectory of message-passing neural networks trained on simulated FCC
data by less than a part in a thousand & beyond leading power. We believe
this constitutes a paradigm shift in perturbative QCD. We have not
attempted to quantify anomaly scores trained on Pythia samples. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with unitarity methods.
