\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of self-supervised lattice simulations at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. Our analysis neglects the
Higgs couplings of the differentiable Regge trajectory, which may become
relevant at the Tevatron. Numerical results are presented for Higgs plus
jet production at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on the hadronization corrections of the Laplace
transform of density estimators trained on Herwig samples.
