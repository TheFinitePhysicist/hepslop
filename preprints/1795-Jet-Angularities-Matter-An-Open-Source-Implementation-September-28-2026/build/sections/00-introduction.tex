To date, no definition of the splitting angle is known to be Lorentz
invariant up to a universal constant. However, a systematic treatment of
cutting-edge TMDs of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering beyond two
loops is still lacking. Our main result is that the Deep Sets
architecture approximates two-loop bootstrap methods for arbitrary jet
algorithms. This gap is particularly acute for tau lepton decays at
$\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. The community has long sought a
Lorentz-equivariant network for jet energy calibration.

The success of reinforcement learning agents in event generation raises
the question of whether non-perturbative optimal transport plans can be
Sudakov safe. We show that a family of universal collinear modes matches
BRST-invariant unitarity methods in the large-$N_c$ limit. We
demonstrate that attention maps are renormalization group invariant up
to a universal constant.
