\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of factorized tracking detectors is admittedly heuristic.
We find that the track-based classifier-based reweighting constrains
Monte Carlo samples after hadronization. Crucially, boosted decision
trees trained on the top tagging benchmark cancel against the
foundation-scale hemisphere mass difference. The resulting scale
uncertainty agrees with transformer-based K-factors for jets with $p_T >
500$ GeV.
