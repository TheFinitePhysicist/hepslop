\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for boosted W boson production at
$\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. This work paves the way for a new generation of
lattice simulations. We leave the study of factorized Lund planes at
leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. We have not attempted to
quantify the embeddings of the conditional normalizing flow. Extending
this framework to foundation models trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV
is an exciting direction for future work.
