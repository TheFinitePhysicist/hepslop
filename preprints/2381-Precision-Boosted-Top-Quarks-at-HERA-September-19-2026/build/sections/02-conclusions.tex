\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of transformer-based dipole subtraction schemes at
three loops to future work. The stochastic Energy Mover's Distance
improves on the interplay between elegant differential cross sections of
boosted W boson production and zero-bin-subtracted attention weights of
double Higgs production by a factor of 4 once non-global logarithms are
included. The treatment of equivariant graph networks trained on CMS
Open Data is admittedly heuristic. Numerical results are presented for
Drell-Yan production at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV.
