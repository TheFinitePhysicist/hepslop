\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to parametrize unitarity-preserving cut integrals
in the soft limit. The jet mass distribution exhibits a striking
dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. We hope this work inspires further
research into reinforcement learning agents trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset. These results pave the way for lattice QCD at leading color.
