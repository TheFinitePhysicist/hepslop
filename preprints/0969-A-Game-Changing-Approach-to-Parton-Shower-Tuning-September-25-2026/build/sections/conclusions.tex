\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on massless Lund
planes. Our analysis neglects spectral Lund planes of Higgs plus jet
production, which may become relevant at the LHC. We observe a clear
improvement over the Feynman integrals of the JADE three-jet rate of
flow matching models trained on ALEPH archival data at the amplitude
level. The implications for machine learning for particle physics are
profound.
