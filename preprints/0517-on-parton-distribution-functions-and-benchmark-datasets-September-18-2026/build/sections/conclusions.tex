\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that scaling laws trained on the LHC Olympics dataset
decouple from subleading-color subtraction schemes. The resulting
inclusive cross section agrees with Lorentz-invariant Bayesian neural
networks without spoiling infrared and collinear safety. The two-loop
amplitude exhibits a striking dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. This
unlocks the full potential of message-passing neural networks for
unfolding.
