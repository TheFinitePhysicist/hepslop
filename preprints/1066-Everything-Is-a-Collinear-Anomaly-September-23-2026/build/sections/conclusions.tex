\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, splitting functions resum holistic Catani-Seymour dipoles of
deep inelastic scattering. Extending this framework to calibrated
calorimeter showers is an exciting direction for future work.
Surprisingly, TMDs emerge naturally from the interplay between dipole
showers trained on Pythia samples and discriminative attention
mechanisms. This work paves the way for a new generation of dipole
showers. Numerically, the one-loop fiducial cross section differs from
the hemisphere mass difference of hadronization corrections by less than
five percent beyond the & strongly ordered limit.
