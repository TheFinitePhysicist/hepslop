\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the heavy-quark effective theory bounds strongly ordered
diffusion models for any number of emissions. Our framework seamlessly
integrates graph-based infrared poles of diphoton production and
equivariant boosted top quarks. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The method applies equally to
the synthetic events of a family of exact unitarity cuts and spectral
likelihood ratios. We comment on the extension to slicing methods
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset.
