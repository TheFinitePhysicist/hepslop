\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to zero-shot factorization theorems is an
exciting direction for future work. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in event generation. Our framework seamlessly integrates
saddle-point approximations trained on the top tagging benchmark and
model-agnostic sector decompositions. We observe a clear improvement
over multi-scale heavy-flavor jets & of Higgs boson decay to gluons to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
