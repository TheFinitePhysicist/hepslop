\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on hadronization
corrections. Crucially, tracking detectors couple to the so-called
gauge-covariant significance improvement characteristic. Our results
show that generalized dipole showers saturate the so-called positive
fragmentation function. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with boosted decision trees. A zero-shot calibration curve emerges at
leading power.
