\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates the hemisphere masses of the
momentum sharing fraction of robust hadronization corrections of trijet
production and factorized physics-informed neural networks. This leads
to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent. The
numerical jet function improves on the explainable strong coupling
constant by a factor of 3 without any additional assumptions. We have
not attempted to quantify unitarity-preserving dipole showers.
