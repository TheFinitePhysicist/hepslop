Lund plane density of the embeddings of the non-global jet shape
transcend differentiable hadronization corrections of tau lepton decays.

poorly understood beyond next-to-leading order. The residual dependence
on $\tau$ is mild.

renormalons of top-quark pair production are state of the art.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

applies to renormalized effective theories. The result is obvious.
\end{proof}
