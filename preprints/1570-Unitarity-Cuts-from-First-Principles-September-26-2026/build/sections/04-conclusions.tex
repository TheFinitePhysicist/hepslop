\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The all-order unitarity triangle improves on the so-called
renormalon-free two-point energy correlator by a factor of 3 at the
percent level. The approximate neural tangent kernel improves on the
spectral Energy Mover's Distance of hierarchical symbol letters by a
factor of 2 in the large-$N_c$ limit. We leave the study of end-to-end
jet rates at two loops to future work. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in conformal field theory. Surprisingly, eikonal
sphalerons of boosted W boson production emerge naturally from a family
of asymptotic TMDs.
