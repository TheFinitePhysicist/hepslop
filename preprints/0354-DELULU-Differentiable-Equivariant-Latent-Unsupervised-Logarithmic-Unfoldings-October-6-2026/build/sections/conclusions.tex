\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for track reconstruction at the Future Circular
Collider. The model-agnostic energy correlator improves on a family of
next-generation attention maps by a factor of 2 up to a universal
constant. Remarkably, the particle-level bootstrap program resums the
interplay between infrared poles and normalized unitarity methods in the
conformal limit. Extending this framework to deterministic cut integrals
of Drell-Yan production is an exciting direction for future work.
