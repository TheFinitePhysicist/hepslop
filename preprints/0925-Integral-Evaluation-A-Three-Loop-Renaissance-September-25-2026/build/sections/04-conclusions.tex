\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on state-of-the-art
top-quark decays of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. Extending
this framework to multi-scale boosted top quarks is an exciting
direction for future work. This is just the tip of the iceberg. The
implications for resummation are profound. In future work, we plan to
apply the weakly supervised smeared matrix element to photoproduction.
