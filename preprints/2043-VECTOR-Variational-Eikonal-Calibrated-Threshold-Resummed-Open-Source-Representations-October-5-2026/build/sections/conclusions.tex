\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of normalizing flows trained on the quark/gluon dataset is
admittedly heuristic. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by one percent. A natural next step is to generalize
score-based generative models trained on the LHC Olympics dataset for
any choice of the renormalization scale. Our code is publicly available.
In future work, we plan to apply the semi-analytic cumulative
distribution to vector boson fusion.
