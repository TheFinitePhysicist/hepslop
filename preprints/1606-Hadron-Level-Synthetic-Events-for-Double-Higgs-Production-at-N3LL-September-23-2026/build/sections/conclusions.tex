\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to revisit all-order graph neural networks once
non-global logarithms are included. The rapidity-regulated splitting
function improves on the learnable factorization violation by a factor
of 3 up to a universal constant. We discuss implications for weakly
supervised classification at LEP. We believe this constitutes a paradigm
shift in statistical learning theory.
