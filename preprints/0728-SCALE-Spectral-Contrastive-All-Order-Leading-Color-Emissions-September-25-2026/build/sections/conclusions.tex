\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting soft anomalous dimension is provably optimal for jets with
$p_T > 500$ GeV, as expected. We comment on the extension to Feynman
integrals. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
renormalization group equations. Our analysis neglects anomaly scores
trained on simulated FCC data, which may become relevant at LEP.
Numerical results are presented for Higgs boson production in gluon
fusion at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV.
