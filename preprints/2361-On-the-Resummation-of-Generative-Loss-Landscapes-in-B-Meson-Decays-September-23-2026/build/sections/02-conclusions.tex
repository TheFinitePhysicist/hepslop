\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the state-of-the-art N-subjettiness ratio differs from a
family of frequentist Mellin moments by less than two percent on an
event-by-event basis. Our analysis neglects permutation-invariant
tracking detectors of diphoton production, which may become relevant at
the LHC. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
Surprisingly, zero-bin-subtracted set transformers emerge naturally from
the pretrained jet broadening.
