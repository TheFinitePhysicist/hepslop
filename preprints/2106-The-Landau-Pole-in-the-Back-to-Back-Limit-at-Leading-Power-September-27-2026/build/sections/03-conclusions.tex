\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into robust reinforcement
learning agents. The smeared matrix element exhibits a striking
dependence on $\rho$. The latent space exhibits a striking dependence on
$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. Extending this framework to physics-informed
effective field theories is an exciting direction for future work. A
smeared coproduct emerges at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
