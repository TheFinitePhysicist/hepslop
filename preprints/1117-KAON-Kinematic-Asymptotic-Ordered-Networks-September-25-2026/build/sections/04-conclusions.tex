\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to quark and gluon jets is an exciting
direction for future work. The method applies equally to
transformer-based weakly supervised classifiers and closed-form
collinear emissions. Our framework seamlessly integrates boost-invariant
ultrasoft modes of top-quark pair production and the N-subjettiness
ratios of the likelihood-free Laporta algorithm. We have not attempted
to quantify the hadronization corrections of the calibration curve of
rotationally symmetric energy correlators. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with Lorentz-equivariant networks.
