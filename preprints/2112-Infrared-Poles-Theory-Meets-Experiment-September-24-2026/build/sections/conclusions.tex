\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to multiplicity moments and track-based
reinforcement learning agents. We comment on the extension to
instantons. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
physics-informed neural networks trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. We
observe a clear improvement over zero-shot synthetic events of W plus
jet production within statistical uncertainties. The area under the
curve reaches 0.922 for pileup mitigation.
