\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Numerically, the holistic
soft function differs from the unfolding procedure by less than five
percent in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. This unlocks the full
potential of self-supervised objectives for event generation. It would
be interesting to combine this approach with renormalization group
equations.
