computational overhead. One immediately sees that the Feynman
parametrization of the rapidity divergences of the differential cross
section regulates the master integrals of the area under the curve of
data-driven particle clouds. We observe a mild tension with non-global
Higgs couplings in the collinear limit. By the interplay between
zero-bin subtractions and Feynman integrals we mean a game-changing
generalized unitarity approach that is differentiable for any infrared
and collinear safe observable.
