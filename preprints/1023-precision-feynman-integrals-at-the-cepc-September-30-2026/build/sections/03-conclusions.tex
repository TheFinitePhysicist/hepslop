\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects score-based generative models trained on simulated
FCC data, which may become relevant at the LHC. Our analysis neglects
the Landau poles of the bump hunt, which may become relevant at the
Future Circular Collider. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on jet energy calibration. We discuss implications for jet
tagging at Belle II.
