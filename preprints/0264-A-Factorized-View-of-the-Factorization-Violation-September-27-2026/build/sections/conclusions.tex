\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with saddle-point
approximations. Numerically, the parton-level beam function differs from
the moment-pooling network by less than one percent for jets with $p_T >
500$ GeV. We have not attempted to quantify quark-initiated Lund plane
emissions. A natural next step is to resum resummed sideband events of W
plus jet production for any number of emissions. We hope this work
inspires further research into scale-invariant benchmark datasets.
