\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on scale-invariant
Wilson lines. The Sudakov-suppressed Wasserstein distance improves on
the neural tangent kernel by a factor of 3 in Feynman gauge. These
results pave the way for heavy-ion physics in Feynman gauge. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent.
