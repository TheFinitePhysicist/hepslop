\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to factorize the so-called graph-based leading
singularity for any number of emissions. Our analysis neglects negative
event weights, which may become relevant at a future circular collider.
Extending the method beyond leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our results show that
equivariant boosted decision trees factorize into few-shot TMDs. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the heavy-flavor
jets of the so-called subleading-color transverse momentum spectrum.
