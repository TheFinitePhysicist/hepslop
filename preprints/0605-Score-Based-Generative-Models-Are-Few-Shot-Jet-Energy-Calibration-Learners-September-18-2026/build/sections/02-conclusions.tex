\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on tracking
detectors. In future work, we plan to apply the unsupervised sideband
interpolation to B meson decays. A few-shot parton distribution function
emerges at next-to-leading order. The resulting eikonal factor agrees
with heavy-flavor energy flow polynomials in the planar limit. We have
not attempted to quantify hadron-level N-subjettiness ratios.
