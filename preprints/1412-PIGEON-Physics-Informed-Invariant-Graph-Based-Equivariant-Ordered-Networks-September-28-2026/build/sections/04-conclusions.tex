\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The power-suppressed calibration curve improves on the smeared matrix
element of variational autoencoders trained on the Dark Machines dataset
by a factor of 3 after unfolding. The proposed method outperforms all
existing approaches by a wide margin. The implications for anomaly
detection are profound. We have not attempted to quantify
Sudakov-suppressed parton showers.
