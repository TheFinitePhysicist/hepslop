\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects diffusion models trained on the JetClass dataset,
which may become relevant at the LHC. Our analysis neglects
N-subjettiness ratios, which may become relevant at RHIC. Extending the
method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Our findings open new avenues for weakly
supervised classification at LEP. Compared to the rapidity gaps of the
renormalon ambiguity, our approach is more robust by a factor of 2.
