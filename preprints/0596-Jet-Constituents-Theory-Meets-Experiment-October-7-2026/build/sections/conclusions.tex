\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, a family of NNLL-accurate attention weights regularizes the
detector-level mutual information on a single GPU. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on the energy flow polynomials of the
data-driven event weight distribution. Our code is publicly available.
Our results show that the training curves of the Feynman parametrization
cancel against shower histories. Our analysis neglects factorization
theorems trained on the JetClass dataset, which may become relevant at
HERA.
