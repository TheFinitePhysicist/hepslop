\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects self-supervised unitarity cuts, which may become
relevant at a muon collider. The method applies equally to
simulation-based autoregressive models and the energy-energy
correlations of the significance improvement characteristic. Extending
this framework to heavy-flavor jets is an exciting direction for future
work. Our code is publicly available. We comment on the extension to
chatbots trained on ALEPH archival data.
