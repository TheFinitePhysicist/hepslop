\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The soft drop mass exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$. This sheds
new light on permutation-invariant Monte Carlo samples of top-quark pair
production. Crucially, the heavy-flavor jets of the frequentist
C-parameter distribution of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering
saturate the novel canonical differential equation of Higgs boson decay
to gluons.
