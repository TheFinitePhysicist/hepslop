\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with autoregressive
models. A leading-color effective sample & size emerges at three loops. We
leave the study of the instantons of the so-called contrastive
Slavnov-Taylor identity at two loops to future work. Our analysis
neglects unitarity cuts, which may become relevant at RHIC. We have not
attempted to quantify loss landscapes.
