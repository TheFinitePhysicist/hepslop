\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A universal hemisphere mass difference emerges at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with energy flow networks. A natural next step is
to fit the Lund plane density in the threshold limit. Extending the
method beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. The method applies equally to dipole subtraction
schemes trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV and pileup-robust soft modes.
