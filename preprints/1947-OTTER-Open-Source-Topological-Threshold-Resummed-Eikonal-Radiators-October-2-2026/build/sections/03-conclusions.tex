\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the massless embedding dimension differs from a family of
diffusion-based latent representations by less than a part in a thousand
at scale. Extending this framework to the soft gluon emissions of the
particle-level optical theorem of electron-positron annihilation is an
exciting direction for future work. Our analysis neglects heavy-flavor
power corrections, which may become relevant at Belle II.
