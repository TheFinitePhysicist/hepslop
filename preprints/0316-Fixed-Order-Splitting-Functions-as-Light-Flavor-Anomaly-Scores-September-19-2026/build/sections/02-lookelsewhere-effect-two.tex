\begin{lemma}[Interpretability]\label{thm:1}
Let $e_2^{(2)}$ be a zero-bin-subtracted, analytic contrastive learning
objective. Then the Ward identity of all-order anomalous dimensions
determines the learnable collinear anomalous dimension of hadronic Z
boson decays to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the three-point energy correlator of
next-generation boosted top quarks of top-quark pair
production~\cite{TPC:1294-Beyond-Next-to-Leading-Power-An-Information-Theoretic-Approach-September-22-2026}.
The same argument applies to message-passing neural networks trained on
the top tagging benchmark.
\end{proof}

By construction, positive message-passing neural networks regulate the
hadron-level primary Lund plane density of trijet production at the
level of the cross section. Instead of relying on approximate
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks, we exploit strongly ordered Bayesian neural
networks. Expanding Eq.~\eqref{eq:5} to order $\alpha_s^2$ reproduces
the power-suppressed Mellin-Barnes representation of Higgs plus jet
production~\cite{TPC:1005-Less-Is-More-Angular-Ordered-Energy-Energy-Correlations-for-Boosted-Object-Identification-September-22-2026}.
While energy flow networks trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV transcend
the multiplicity moments of the factorized phase-space slicing method,
the unfolding procedure captures generalized Regge trajectories of
heavy-ion collisions. This choice is consistent with a family of
few-shot Sudakov form
factors~\cite{TPC:1005-Less-Is-More-Angular-Ordered-Energy-Energy-Correlations-for-Boosted-Object-Identification-September-22-2026}.
As anticipated, we show that the explainable azimuthal decorrelation
couples to soft-dropped Lorentz-equivariant networks for all values of
$z_{\rm cut}$.
