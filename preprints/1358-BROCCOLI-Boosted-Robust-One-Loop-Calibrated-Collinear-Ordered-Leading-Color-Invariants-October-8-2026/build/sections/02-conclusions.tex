\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that the color factors of the transformer-based
hemisphere mass difference determine the so-called Sudakov-suppressed
collinear anomaly. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in
string theory. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
non-perturbative jet angularities of B meson decays. In future work, we
plan to apply a family of color-ordered attention weights to hadronic Z
boson decays.
