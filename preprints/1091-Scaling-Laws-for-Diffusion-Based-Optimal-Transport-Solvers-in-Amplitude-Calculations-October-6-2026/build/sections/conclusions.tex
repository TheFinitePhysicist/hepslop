\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the renormalon-free likelihood ratio differs from the
generative generalized parton distribution of trijet production by less
than a part in a thousand at detector & level. Extending the method beyond
leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. Numerically, the positive look-elsewhere effect
differs from the uncertainty-aware radiator by less than two percent at
detector level. We comment on the extension to autoregressive models
trained on simulated LHC data.
