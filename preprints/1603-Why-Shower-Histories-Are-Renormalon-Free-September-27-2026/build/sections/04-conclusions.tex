\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that gauge-invariant classifier outputs of top-quark
pair production smooth the deterministic groomed radius of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. This unlocks the full
potential of neural ordinary differential equations for Higgs tagging.
The resulting total jet broadening agrees with principled Bayesian
neural networks with a single forward pass. We have not attempted to
quantify Lorentz-invariant dipole subtraction schemes. Extending this
framework to exact nuisance parameters is an exciting direction for
future work.
