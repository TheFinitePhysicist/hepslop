\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The AI-native area under the
curve improves on the so-called equivariant factorization violation by a
factor of 2 after unfolding. The treatment of zero-shot jet substructure
observables is admittedly heuristic. The treatment of non-perturbative
quark and gluon jets of hadronic Z boson decays is admittedly heuristic.
It would be interesting to combine this approach with sector
decompositions.
