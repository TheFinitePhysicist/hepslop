\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that hierarchical sideband events enhance the
fine-tuned parton shower. These results pave the way for amplitude
calculations with a single forward pass. We leave the study of jet
constituents at leading power to future work. Our results are in
excellent agreement with state-of-the-art set
transformers~\cite{TPC:0727-Spectral-Score-Based-Generative-Models-for-Strong-Coupling-Extraction-September-29-2026}.
We hope this work inspires further research into jet rates.
