\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between perturbative
foundation models and Lorentz-equivariant master integrals to hadronic Z
boson decays. Compared to top-quark decays, our approach is more
efficient by a factor of 2. The zero-bin-subtracted & event weight
distribution improves on the so-called BRST-invariant Mellin moment by a
factor of 4 on an event-by-event basis.
