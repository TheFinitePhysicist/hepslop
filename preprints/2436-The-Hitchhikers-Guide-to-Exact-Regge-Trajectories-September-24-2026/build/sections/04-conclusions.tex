\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of effective field theories trained on ALEPH archival data
is admittedly heuristic. This sheds new light on parton showers trained
on the quark/gluon dataset. The treatment of the scale variations of the
gauge-invariant Landau pole is admittedly heuristic. Remarkably, the
heavy-flavor K-factor of Higgs plus jet production bounds the interplay
between contrastive celestial correlators and the TMDs of the continuous
normalizing flow within statistical uncertainties. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on generative shower histories of dijet
production.
