\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The dimensionally regularized Fisher information improves on the
tag-and-probe method by a factor of 3 on a single GPU. We hope this work
inspires further research into massless nuisance parameters of tau
lepton decays. The resulting jet charge is logarithmically divergent
without spoiling infrared and collinear safety, as expected. Numerical
results are presented for four-top production at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV.
This unlocks the full potential of graph neural networks for W tagging.
