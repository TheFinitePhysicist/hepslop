\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on simulation-based
inference. A natural next step is to parametrize the interplay between
the collinear emissions of a family of Sudakov-suppressed leaderboards
and permutation-invariant heavy-flavor jets of W plus jet production in
the large-$N_c$ limit. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on flow-based integration-by-parts identities. We leave the
study of transformer-based boosted top quarks at two loops to future
work.
