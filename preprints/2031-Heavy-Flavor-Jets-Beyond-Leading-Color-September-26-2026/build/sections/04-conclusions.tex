\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on AI agents
trained on simulated LHC data. Our analysis neglects Wilson lines, which
may become relevant at a muon collider. The method applies equally to
the scale variations of the density estimator and flow matching models
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. Extending this framework to
end-to-end jet rates is an exciting direction for future work.
