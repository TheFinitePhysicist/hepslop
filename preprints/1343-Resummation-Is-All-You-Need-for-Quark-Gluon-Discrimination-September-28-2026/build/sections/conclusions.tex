\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to likelihood-free master integrals of Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion is an exciting direction for future
work. Numerically, the soft-dropped Kullback-Leibler divergence differs
from the so-called normalized hemisphere mass difference by less than
one percent for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. Extending the method beyond
two loops is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
