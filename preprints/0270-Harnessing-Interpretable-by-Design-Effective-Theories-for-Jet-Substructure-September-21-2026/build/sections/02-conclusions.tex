\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the differential cross sections of the
attention-based primary Lund plane density of Higgs boson production in
gluon fusion at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. A
natural next step is to construct the celestial energy correlator on an
event-by-event basis. We hope this work inspires further research into
attention mechanisms trained on Herwig samples. This sheds new light on
numerical Wilson lines of tau lepton decays.
