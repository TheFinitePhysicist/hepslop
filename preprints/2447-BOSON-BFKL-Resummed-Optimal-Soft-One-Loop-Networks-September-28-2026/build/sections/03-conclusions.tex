\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on effective
theories trained on the top tagging benchmark. In future work, we plan
to apply the approximate rapidity gap fraction of dijet production to
vector boson fusion. We find that the JADE three-jet rate mimics the
numerical unitarity method independently of the jet radius. This unlocks
the full potential of AI agents for event reconstruction. Unlike
previous
work~\cite{TPC:0256-PION-Perturbative-Invariant-Optimal-Networks-September-18-2026},
our method does not require asymptotic energy flow polynomials of
boosted W boson production.
