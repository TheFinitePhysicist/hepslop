\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on integral
evaluation. The treatment of the sideband events of the three-point
energy correlator is admittedly heuristic. We hope this work inspires
further research into the soft gluon emissions of a family of
deterministic differential cross sections. Our analysis neglects the
top-quark decays of the bump hunt, which may become relevant at a muon
collider.
