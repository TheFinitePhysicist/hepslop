\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to next-to-leading-logarithmic dipole showers
and the Feynman integrals of the Lorentz-invariant jet shape. Our
analysis neglects collinear modes, which may become relevant at the LHC.
Our framework seamlessly integrates rotationally symmetric energy flow
networks and elegant subtraction schemes. We hope this work inspires
further research into closed-form master integrals.
