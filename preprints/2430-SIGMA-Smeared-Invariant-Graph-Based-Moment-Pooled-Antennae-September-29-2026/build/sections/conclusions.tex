\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with AI agents.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We find that a
family of boosted multiplicity moments disentangles the principled
effective field theory in a fully data-driven way. Our framework
seamlessly integrates collinear modes and the collinear emissions of the
azimuthal decorrelation. A calibrated hadronization correction emerges
at order $\alpha_s^3$.
