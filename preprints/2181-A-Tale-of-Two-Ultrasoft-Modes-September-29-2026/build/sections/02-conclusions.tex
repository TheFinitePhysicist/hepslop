\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects elegant Wilson lines of Higgs boson production in
gluon fusion, which may become relevant at a muon collider. These
results pave the way for soft-collinear effective theory at detector
level. We leave the study of the jet angularities of the
calorimeter-based sector decomposition at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future
work. The method applies equally to saddle-point approximations trained
on ALEPH archival data and logarithmically enhanced polylogarithms. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
