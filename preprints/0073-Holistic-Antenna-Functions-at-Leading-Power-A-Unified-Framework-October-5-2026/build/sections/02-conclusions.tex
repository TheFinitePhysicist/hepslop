\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for perturbative QCD to all orders in
$\alpha_s$. We observe a clear improvement over latent representations
without spoiling infrared and collinear safety. We hope this work
inspires further research into collinear-safe point cloud transformers.
This is just the tip of the iceberg. Extending this framework to smeared
lattice simulations is an exciting direction for future work.
