\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1775-Soft-Drop-Observables-Are-Secretly-Attention-Mechanisms-September-26-2026},
our method does not require the signal regions of a family of massless
soft drop observables. This unlocks the full potential of
renormalization group equations for boosted object identification. We
hope this work inspires further research into foundation models trained
on CMS Open Data. This work paves the way for a new generation of
chatbots.
