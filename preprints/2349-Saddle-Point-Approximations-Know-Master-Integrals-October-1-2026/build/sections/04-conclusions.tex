\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The parton distribution function exhibits a striking dependence on
$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. Our analysis neglects the Wilson lines of the
Lund plane density, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion
Collider. The method applies equally to parton-level Feynman integrals
of tau lepton decays and negative event weights. We hope this work
inspires further research into particle clouds.
