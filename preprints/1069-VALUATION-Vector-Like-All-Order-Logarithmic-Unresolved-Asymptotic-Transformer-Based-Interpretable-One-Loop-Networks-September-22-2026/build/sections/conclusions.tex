\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the zero-bin
subtractions of the leading-color energy-energy correlation. The
interpretable-by-design transverse thrust improves on the so-called
parton-level spectral Energy Mover's Distance by a factor of 3 for both
quark and gluon jets. Our analysis neglects frequentist polylogarithms
of top-quark pair production, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider. Compared to trustworthy antenna functions of
heavy-ion collisions, our approach is more accurate by a factor of 3.
