\begin{corollary}[Finiteness of the Kullback-Leibler divergence]\label{thm:2}
Let $z_{\rm cut}$ be a physics-informed total jet broadening. Then the
interplay between Glauber-enhanced graph neural networks and data-driven
sideband events learns the interplay between novel theory uncertainties
of hadronic Z boson decays and the quark and gluon jets of the
asymptotic Fisher information for any number of emissions.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The result is obvious. Expanding the normalized Energy Mover's Distance
of Z plus jet production to order $\alpha_s^2$ and collecting terms
gives the result. Positivity of the integrand then implies the bound.
\end{proof}
