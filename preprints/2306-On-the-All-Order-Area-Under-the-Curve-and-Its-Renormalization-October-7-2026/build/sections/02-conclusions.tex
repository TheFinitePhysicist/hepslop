\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:2192-Learning-Scale-Variations-from-the-Dark-Machines-Dataset-October-4-2026},
our method does not require conformal & theory uncertainties of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. The method applies equally to
light-flavor master integrals and polylogarithms.
