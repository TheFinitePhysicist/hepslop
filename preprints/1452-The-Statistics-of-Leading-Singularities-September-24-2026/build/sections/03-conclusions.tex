\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the energy flow polynomials of the
conditional normalizing flow. We observe a clear improvement over
Catani-Seymour dipoles on an event-by-event basis. The treatment of
energy flow networks trained on the LHC Olympics dataset is admittedly
heuristic. We discuss implications for anomaly detection at the Future
Circular Collider.
