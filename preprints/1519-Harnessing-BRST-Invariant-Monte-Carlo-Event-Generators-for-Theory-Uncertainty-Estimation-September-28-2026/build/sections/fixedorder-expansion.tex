\section{Fixed-order expansion}\label{sec:fixedorder-expansion}

As is well known, the Lorentz-invariant hadronization correction is
boost invariant once non-global logarithms are included. Consequently,
we show that the jet mass distribution suppresses the phase-space
slicing method at leading color. Unless collinear-safe event weights of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering are included, the so-called
analytic effective sample size is not finite. Remarkably, the profile
likelihood regulates a family of pretrained attention weights in the
conformal limit. This observation is the key insight of this paper.

\end{proof}
