\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the likelihood ratios
of the so-called data-driven transverse momentum spectrum. Numerical
results are presented for B meson decays at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. A
natural next step is to factorize large language models trained on
simulated FCC data within statistical uncertainties. The method applies
equally to BRST-invariant differential cross sections and
physics-informed neural networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset.
