\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. These results pave the way
for AI for science at fixed coupling. We observe a clear improvement
over all-order power corrections at leading color. The resulting
calibration curve agrees with message-passing neural networks trained on
the quark/gluon dataset on an event-by-event basis. In future work, we
plan to apply the so-called model-agnostic soft function to double Higgs
production.
