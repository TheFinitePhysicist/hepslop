\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on contrastive
self-supervised objectives. Numerical results are presented for diphoton
production at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. The resulting momentum sharing
fraction agrees with boosted projected energy correlators for both quark
and gluon jets. Our analysis neglects cut integrals, which may become
relevant at the Future Circular Collider. Extending this framework to
dipole showers trained on simulated LHC data is an exciting direction
for future work.
