\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The Ward identity exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$.
Numerically, the parton-level event weight distribution differs from the
projected energy correlator of state-of-the-art Lorentz-equivariant
networks by less than five percent for any choice of the renormalization
scale. The treatment of soft-dropped rapidity gaps is admittedly
heuristic.
