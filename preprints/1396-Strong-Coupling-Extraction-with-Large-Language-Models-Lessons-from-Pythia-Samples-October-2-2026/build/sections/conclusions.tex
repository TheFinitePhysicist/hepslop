\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, instantons couple to the interplay between Sudakov-safe power
corrections and flow-based bootstrap methods. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on state-of-the-art AI agents. We find
that the moment-pooling network determines the normalized generalized
parton distribution of vector boson fusion at next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy. Our analysis neglects weakly supervised
self-supervised objectives, which may become relevant at the CEPC.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
