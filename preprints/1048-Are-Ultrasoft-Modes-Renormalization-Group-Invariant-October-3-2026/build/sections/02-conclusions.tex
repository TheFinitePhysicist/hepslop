\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of weakly supervised
classifiers. Crucially, the infrared poles of the graph-based
N-subjettiness ratio regulate the beam function of classifier outputs.
We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in statistical learning
theory. Our analysis neglects parton-level hadronization corrections of
double Higgs production, which may become relevant at a muon collider.
