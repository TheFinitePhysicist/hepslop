\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of parton-level renormalons of Higgs boson decay to gluons
is admittedly heuristic. Our findings open new avenues for weakly
supervised classification at the High-Luminosity LHC. This work paves
the way for a new generation of weakly supervised classifiers. We hope
this work inspires further research into ROC curves.
