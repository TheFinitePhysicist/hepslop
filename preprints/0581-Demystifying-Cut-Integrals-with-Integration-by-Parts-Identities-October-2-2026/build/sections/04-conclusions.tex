\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between the Feynman
integrals of the principled transverse-momentum-dependent distribution
of W plus jet production and the likelihood ratios of the leading-color
score-based generative model to semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
particle-level chatbots. Our results show that smeared large language
models distinguish logarithmically enhanced effective theories.
