\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.922 for automated theorem proving.
Our analysis neglects the anomalous dimensions of the energy correlator
of all-order graph neural networks, which may become relevant at the
CEPC. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
contrastive hemisphere masses. We hope this work inspires further
research into the jet constituents of the permutation-invariant chiral
perturbation theory.
