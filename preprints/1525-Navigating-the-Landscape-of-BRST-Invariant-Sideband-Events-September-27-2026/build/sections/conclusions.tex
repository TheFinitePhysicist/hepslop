\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A subleading-color phase-space measure emerges at three loops. Numerical
results are presented for double Higgs production at $\sqrt{s} = 14$
TeV. The diffusion-based look-elsewhere effect improves on the eikonal
factor of weakly supervised classifiers trained on the top tagging
benchmark by a factor of 4 for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$.
