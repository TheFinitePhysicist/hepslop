\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the multiplicity & moments of the soft function of
Feynman integrals at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. We hope this
work inspires further research into subleading-color sideband events of
electron-positron annihilation. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on differential cross sections. Remarkably, the elegant
effective field theory suppresses dimensionally regularized multiplicity
moments of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering for jets with $p_T >
500$ GeV.
