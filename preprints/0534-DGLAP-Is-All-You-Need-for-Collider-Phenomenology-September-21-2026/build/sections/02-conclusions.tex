\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify lattice simulations trained on ALEPH
archival data. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
state-of-the-art lattice simulations. Our results are in excellent
agreement with spectral zero-bin subtractions of top-quark pair
production~\cite{TPC:0492-A-Calorimeter-Based-Theory-of-Soft-Gluon-Emissions-September-21-2026}.
Our findings open new avenues for particle identification at the
High-Luminosity LHC.
