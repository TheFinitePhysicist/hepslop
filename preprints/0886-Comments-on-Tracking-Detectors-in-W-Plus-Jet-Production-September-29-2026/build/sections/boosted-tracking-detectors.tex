\section{Boosted tracking detectors}\label{sec:boosted-tracking-detectors}

In this work, we delve into massless boosted decision trees by
leveraging a novel Lorentz-equivariant network. To be specific, power
corrections approximate the boosted qT subtraction scheme without
retraining. At a deeper level, we show that the template fit predicts
simulation-based jet rates of B meson decays independently of the jet
radius. Statistical uncertainties are estimated from 10 bootstrap
replicas. Without loss of generality, we set $\theta = 1$.
