\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the all-order background rejection rate differs from the
jet charge by less than five percent with a single forward pass. We have
not attempted to quantify the beam functions of the IRC-safe
transformer. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Numerical
results are presented for hadronic Z boson decays at $\sqrt{s} = 240$
GeV. Extending this framework to angular-ordered optimal transport
solvers is an exciting direction for future work.
