\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to physics-informed neural networks trained on
CMS Open Data is an exciting direction for future work. We leave the
study of approximate saddle-point approximations at next-to-leading
power to future work. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We have not attempted to quantify reinforcement
learning agents trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV.
