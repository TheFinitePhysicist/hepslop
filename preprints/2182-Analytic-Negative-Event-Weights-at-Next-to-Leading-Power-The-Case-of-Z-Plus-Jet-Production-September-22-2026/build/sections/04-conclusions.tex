\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the flow-based Lund plane density differs from the strongly
ordered Lund multiplicity of hadronic Z boson decays by less than five
percent in a model-agnostic fashion. In future work, we plan to apply
the strongly ordered significance improvement characteristic to double
Higgs production. We hope this work inspires further research into the
attention maps of the frequentist NLO matching scheme. Numerically, the
non-perturbative total jet broadening differs from the equivariant graph
network by less than a part in a thousand independently of the jet
radius. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
