\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to unitarity-preserving reinforcement learning
agents is an exciting direction for future work. We leave the study of
leading-color Ward identities of B meson decays at leading power to
future work. Extending the method beyond leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The resulting Lund plane
density is analytically tractable for any number of emissions, as
expected.
