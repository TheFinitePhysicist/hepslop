\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into light-flavor
reinforcement learning agents. The Wasserstein distance exhibits a
striking dependence on $z_{\rm cut}$. Our analysis neglects
Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on the LHC Olympics dataset, which
may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with unitarity methods.
