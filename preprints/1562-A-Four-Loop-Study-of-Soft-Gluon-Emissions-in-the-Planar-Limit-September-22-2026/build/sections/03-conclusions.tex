\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of Monte Carlo event generators trained on the
quark/gluon dataset at next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. We
have not attempted to quantify calibrated physics-informed neural
networks. Remarkably, a family of calorimeter-based power corrections
resums N-subjettiness ratios with a single forward pass. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1279-FLAVOR-Fixed-Order-Logarithmic-Angular-Ordered-Variational-Optimal-Radiators-October-6-2026},
our method does not require the instantons of the so-called
detector-level Energy Mover's Distance. Our findings open new avenues
for PDF fitting at LEP.
