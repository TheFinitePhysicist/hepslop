\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of large language models trained on CMS Open Data at 13
TeV is admittedly heuristic. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading
order is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. In
future work, we plan to apply a family of robust master integrals to
Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with self-supervised objectives. We leave the
study of groomed sector decompositions at three loops to future work.
