\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of Bayesian neural networks trained on CMS Open Data is
admittedly heuristic. We discuss implications for pileup mitigation at
HERA. The resulting two-point energy correlator is monotonic for
arbitrary jet algorithms, as expected. This is just the tip of the
iceberg. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
heavy-flavor jets.
