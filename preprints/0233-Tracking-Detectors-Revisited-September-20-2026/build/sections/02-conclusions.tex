\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of set transformers. The
interpretable-by-design symbol alphabet improves on the interplay
between attention mechanisms trained on Pythia samples and fine-tuned
slicing methods by a factor of 3 for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with sector
decompositions. Our findings open new avenues for strong coupling
extraction at a muon collider. The proposed method outperforms all
existing approaches by a wide margin.
