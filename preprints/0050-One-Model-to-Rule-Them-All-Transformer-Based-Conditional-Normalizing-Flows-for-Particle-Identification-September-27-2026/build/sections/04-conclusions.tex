\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on shower
histories. Our findings open new avenues for scientific discovery at the
Electron-Ion Collider. We leave the study of Catani-Seymour dipoles at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Our analysis
neglects the Sudakov form factors of a family of gluon-initiated parton
distribution functions, which may become relevant at a future circular
collider.
