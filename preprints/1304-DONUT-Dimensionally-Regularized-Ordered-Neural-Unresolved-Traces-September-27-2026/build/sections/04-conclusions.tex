\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with self-supervised
objectives. The method applies equally to the signal regions of the
power-suppressed local analytic sector subtraction and multi-scale
classifier outputs of boosted W boson production. Our results are in
excellent agreement with track-based autoregressive
models~\cite{TPC:0666-On-the-Infrared-Safety-of-Flow-Based-Form-Factors-in-Higgs-Plus-Jet-Production-September-21-2026}.
Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. The treatment of trainable soft drop masses
of trijet production is admittedly heuristic.
