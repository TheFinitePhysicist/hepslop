\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over robust calorimeter showers to all
orders in $\alpha_s$. The treatment of soft-dropped top-quark decays of
electron-positron annihilation is admittedly heuristic. This work paves
the way for a new generation of set transformers. The resulting neural
tangent kernel agrees with spectral embeddings for jets with $p_T > 500$
GeV. This unlocks the full potential of effective theories for
quark/gluon discrimination.
