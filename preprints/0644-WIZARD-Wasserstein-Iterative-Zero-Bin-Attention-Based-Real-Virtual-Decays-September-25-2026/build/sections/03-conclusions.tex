\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on unfolding. The
look-elsewhere effect exhibits a striking dependence on $x$. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with sector decompositions. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. We
hope this work inspires further research into groomed ROC curves of
vector boson fusion.
