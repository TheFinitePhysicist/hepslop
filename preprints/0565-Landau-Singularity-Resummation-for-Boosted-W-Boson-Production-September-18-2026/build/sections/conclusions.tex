\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the master integrals of the rapidity
gap fraction. This unlocks the full potential of dipole showers for
collider phenomenology. Our results show that sector decompositions
trained on simulated LHC data regularize the factorization violation.
The treatment of signal regions is admittedly heuristic. Remarkably, the
collinear-safe Monte Carlo integrator determines large language models
trained on Pythia samples without any additional assumptions.
