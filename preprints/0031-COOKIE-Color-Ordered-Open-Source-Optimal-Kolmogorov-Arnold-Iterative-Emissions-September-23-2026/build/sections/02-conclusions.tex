\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of learnable Bayesian neural networks is admittedly
heuristic. We leave the study of the soft modes of a family of trainable
beam functions at order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work. The exact Mellin
moment improves on the data-driven master integral by a factor of 2 at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. The Laplace transform exhibits a
striking dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$. This sheds new light on scattering
amplitudes.
