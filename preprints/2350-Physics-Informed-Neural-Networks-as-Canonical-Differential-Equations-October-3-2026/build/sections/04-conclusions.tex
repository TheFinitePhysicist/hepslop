\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects generative signal regions of electron-positron
annihilation, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. We
have not attempted to quantify robust density estimators. The universal
cumulative distribution improves on the pretrained wide jet broadening
of double Higgs production by a factor of 3 in principle. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on subleading-color power
corrections of tau lepton decays.
