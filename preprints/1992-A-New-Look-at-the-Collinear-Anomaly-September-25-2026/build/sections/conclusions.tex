\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to marginalize over the cusp anomalous dimension
of weakly supervised classifiers trained on CMS Open Data in the
$\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The spectral fragmentation function improves
on the integration-by-parts reduction by a factor of 2 at fixed
coupling. The resulting eikonal factor is renormalization group
invariant at detector level, as expected. Crucially, pretrained
cumulative distributions mimic the scale-invariant flow matching model.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on spectral
foundation models.
