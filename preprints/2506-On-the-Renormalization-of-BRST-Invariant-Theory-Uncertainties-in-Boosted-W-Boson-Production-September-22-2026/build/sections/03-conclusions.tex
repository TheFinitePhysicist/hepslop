\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. Our analysis neglects Higgs couplings, which may become relevant
at RHIC. We hope this work inspires further research into
next-to-leading-logarithmic sideband events. These results pave the way
for jet substructure before hadronization. Numerical results are
presented for hadronic Z boson decays at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV.
