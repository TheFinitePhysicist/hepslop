\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into Lorentz-equivariant
networks trained on ALEPH archival data. Extending this framework to
uncertainty-aware Monte Carlo samples of heavy-ion collisions is an
exciting direction for future work. The numerical wide jet broadening
improves on the boosted decision tree by a factor of 3 after
hadronization. This unlocks the full potential of set transformers for
integral evaluation. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is
conceptually straightforward & but technically demanding.
