\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the soft modes of the approximate sector shower,
which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider. We have not
attempted to quantify contrastive collinear modes. Our framework
seamlessly integrates equivariant graph networks trained on Pythia
samples and model-agnostic energy flow networks. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with energy flow networks. Numerical results
are presented for electron-positron annihilation at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$
GeV.
