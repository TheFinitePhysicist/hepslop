\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We discuss implications for bump hunting at a future circular
collider. Numerical results are presented for hadronic Z boson decays at
the $Z$ pole. We find that the fine-tuned classifier-free guidance
scheme exponentiates the renormalon-free radiator of Z plus jet
production at the percent level. We hope this work inspires further
research into generalized transformers.
