\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify renormalized leading singularities. A
natural next step is to resum N-subjettiness ratios to arbitrary
precision. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on
simulation-based inference. Our findings open new avenues for precision
QCD at the Electron-Ion Collider. We comment on the extension to
permutation-invariant score-based generative models.
