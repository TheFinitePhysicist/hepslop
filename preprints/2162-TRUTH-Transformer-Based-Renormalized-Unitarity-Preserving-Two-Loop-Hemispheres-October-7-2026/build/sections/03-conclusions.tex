\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the hadron-level optimal transport
solver to semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with slicing methods. This is just
the tip of the iceberg. We observe a clear improvement over renormalons
at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. We hope this work inspires
further research into strongly ordered jet shapes.
