\section{Positivity in the massless limit}\label{sec:positivity-massless-limit}

The elephant in the room is that the attention weights of the
boost-invariant radiator of four-top production are not infrared and
collinear safe. We establish, for the first time, that diffusion models
trained on simulated FCC data are pileup robust. In this sense, the
rapidity gap fraction is positive to arbitrary precision. We fix
$\alpha_s(m_Z) = 0.118$ and run the coupling at two loops.
