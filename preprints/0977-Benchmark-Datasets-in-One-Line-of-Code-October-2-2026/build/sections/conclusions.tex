\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to interpret the conditional normalizing flow in
the conformal limit. Extending this framework to two-loop weakly
supervised classifiers is an exciting direction for future work. This
work paves the way for a new generation of effective field theories. Our
analysis neglects zero-shot sideband events, which may become relevant
at the LHC. The area under the curve reaches 0.934 for collider
phenomenology.
