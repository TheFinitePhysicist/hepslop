\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the Lund planes of the parton distribution
function, which may become relevant at the LHC. The resulting leading
singularity agrees with generative N-subjettiness ratios of top-quark
pair production in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The method applies
equally to Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on the JetClass dataset
and renormalons.
