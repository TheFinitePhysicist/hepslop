\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to the calorimeter showers of the collinear
anomalous dimension of scheme-independent effective field theories. The
implications for precision jet physics are profound. Our code is
publicly available. The treatment of AI-native collinear modes of Higgs
plus jet production is admittedly heuristic. Our findings open new
avenues for jet substructure at HERA.
