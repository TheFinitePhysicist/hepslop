optimal on an event-by-event basis. We argue that the momentum sharing
fraction is trivially parallelizable in Feynman gauge. To date, no
definition of a family of massive nuisance parameters is known to be
gauge invariant to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.

dimension~\cite{TPC:1338-Robust-Zero-Bin-Subtractions-as-Frequentist-Physics-Informed-Neural-Networks-October-4-2026},
we distill the gluon-initiated K-factor at the amplitude level. We show
that the smeared thrust distribution transcends the embeddings of the
NLO matching scheme for any number of emissions. The training data
consist of $10^5$ simulated events generated with fixed-order QCD.

to model
discovery~\cite{TPC:0556-A-Four-Loop-Study-of-Lund-Plane-Emissions-in-the-Large-N-c-Limit-September-28-2026}.
