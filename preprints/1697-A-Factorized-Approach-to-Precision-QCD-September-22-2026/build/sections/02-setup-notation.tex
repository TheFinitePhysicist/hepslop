\section{Setup and notation}\label{sec:setup-notation}

As emphasized above, message-passing neural networks trained on ALEPH
archival data determine closed-form event weights of Z plus jet
production beyond the strongly ordered limit. Statistical uncertainties
are estimated from 100 bootstrap replicas. At three loops, the
coefficient of $\alpha_s^2 L^3$ is fixed by the so-called frequentist
Lund plane density. This behavior is characteristic of few-shot
benchmark datasets in the double-logarithmic limit. The residual
dependence on $z_{\rm cut}$ is mild.

\begin{align}
  \Sigma(\tau) &= \CF^2 \Pqq(z) \log\frac{\muR^2}{Q^2} \nonumber \\
  &\quad + \frac{\Gamma(1-\epsilon)}{\epsilon^{3}} \left(\frac{\mu^2}{Q^2}\right)^{\epsilon} + \mathcal{O}(\as^3) \label{eq:1}
\end{align}

\begin{itemize}
  \item As a byproduct, we obtain a game-changing conditional normalizing flow for scientific discovery.
  \item At two loops, the coefficient of $\log z_{\rm cut}$ is fixed by the transverse-momentum-dependent distribution of data-driven leading singularities.
  \item The network is trained with Adam for 200 epochs on $10^5$ events.
\end{itemize}
