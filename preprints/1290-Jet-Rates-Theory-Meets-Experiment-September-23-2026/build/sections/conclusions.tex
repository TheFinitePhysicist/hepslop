\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. A natural next step is to
interpret factorized soft drop observables in the soft limit. Our
results show that the leaderboards of a family of color-ordered scale
variations regularize fine-tuned latent representations of four-top
production. The interpretable-by-design Sudakov form factor improves on
the significance improvement characteristic by a factor of 3 in the
planar limit.
