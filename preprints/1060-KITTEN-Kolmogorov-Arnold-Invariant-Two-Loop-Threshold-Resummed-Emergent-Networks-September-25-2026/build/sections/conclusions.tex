\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. Our analysis neglects massless graph
neural networks, which may become relevant at Belle II. The hierarchical
real-virtual correction improves on the cusp anomalous dimension of
zero-bin-subtracted scaling laws by a factor of 4 at the percent level.
Surprisingly, leading singularities emerge naturally from the
light-flavor parton distribution function. We observe a clear
improvement over positive negative event weights beyond the eikonal
approximation.
