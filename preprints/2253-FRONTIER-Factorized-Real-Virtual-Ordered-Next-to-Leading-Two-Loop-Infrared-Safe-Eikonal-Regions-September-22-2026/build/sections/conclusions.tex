\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for new physics searches at a future circular
collider. We have not attempted to quantify effective theories trained
on ALEPH archival data. The treatment of message-passing neural networks
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV is admittedly heuristic. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on learnable classifier
outputs. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a
wide margin.
