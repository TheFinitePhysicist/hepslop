\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to renormalize collinear-safe autoregressive
models without spoiling infrared and collinear safety. Our analysis
neglects subtraction schemes trained on the quark/gluon dataset, which
may become relevant at Belle II. Extending this framework to Landau
poles is an exciting direction for future work. Our analysis neglects
Monte Carlo event generators trained on ALEPH archival data, which may
become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC.
