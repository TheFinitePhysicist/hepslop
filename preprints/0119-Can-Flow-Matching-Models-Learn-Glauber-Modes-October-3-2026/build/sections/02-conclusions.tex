\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this & approach with weakly supervised
classifiers. Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The resulting soft drop mass
is Sudakov safe after unfolding, as expected. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The treatment of
trainable multiplicity moments is admittedly heuristic.
