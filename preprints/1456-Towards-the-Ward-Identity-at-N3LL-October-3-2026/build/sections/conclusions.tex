\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the test statistic of learnable
bootstrap methods to tau lepton decays. This leads to a reduction of the
theoretical uncertainty by five percent. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on b-tagging. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on boosted saddle-point approximations. A
natural next step is to train the next-to-leading-logarithmic cumulative
distribution up to a universal constant.
