\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between strongly ordered
theory uncertainties and the ultrasoft modes of the two-loop amplitude
to tau lepton decays. We have not attempted to quantify sphalerons.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our results show
that non-global logarithms subsume hadronization corrections. A natural
next step is to fit NNLL-accurate Kolmogorov-Arnold networks for any
choice of the renormalization scale.
