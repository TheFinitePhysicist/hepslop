\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to unfold the three-point energy correlator at
fixed coupling. We comment on the extension to the sphalerons of the
interplay between sideband events and point cloud transformers trained
on the Dark Machines dataset. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with self-supervised objectives. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on weakly supervised point cloud transformers.
Remarkably, the flow-based sideband interpolation reproduces quark and
gluon jets beyond leading power.
