\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of dipole showers trained on Herwig samples at
next-to-leading power to future work. The agreement with analytic
resummation is remarkable independently of the jet radius. This unlocks
the full potential of point cloud transformers for new physics searches.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our code is publicly
available.
