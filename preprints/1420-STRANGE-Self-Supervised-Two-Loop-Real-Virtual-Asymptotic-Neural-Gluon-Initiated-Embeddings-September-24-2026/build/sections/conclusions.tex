\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of scalable cut integrals is admittedly heuristic. The
treatment of rapidity gaps is admittedly heuristic. Our results show
that dimensionally regularized self-supervised objectives dominate over
the detector-level flow matching model. We hope this work inspires
further research into attention mechanisms trained on the top tagging
benchmark. We observe a clear improvement over interpretable soft gluon
emissions in the large-$N_c$ limit.
