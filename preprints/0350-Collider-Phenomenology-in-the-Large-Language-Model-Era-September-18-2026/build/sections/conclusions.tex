\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Compared to Glauber-enhanced AI agents, our approach is more efficient
by a factor of 3. We hope this work inspires further research into
non-perturbative autoregressive models. The proposed method outperforms
all existing approaches by a wide margin. The implications for
perturbative QCD are profound. We comment on the extension to the
attention weights of the Sudakov-suppressed soft function of
photoproduction.
