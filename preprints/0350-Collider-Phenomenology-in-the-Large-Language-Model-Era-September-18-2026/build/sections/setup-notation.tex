\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Casimir scaling]\label{thm:4}
For every elegant transverse thrust, the heavy-quark effective theory is
renormalon free up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the planar limit. The remaining terms are
power suppressed. The result is obvious.
\end{proof}
