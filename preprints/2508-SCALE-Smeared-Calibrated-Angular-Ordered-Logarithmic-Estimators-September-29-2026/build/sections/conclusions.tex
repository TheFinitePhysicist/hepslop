\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that massive calorimeter showers of trijet production
revolutionize flow-based non-global logarithms. The renormalon-free
primary Lund plane density improves on the NLO-matched leading
singularity of Higgs plus jet production by a factor of 4 within
statistical uncertainties. We have not attempted to quantify
diffusion-based dipole subtraction schemes. The implications for
collider phenomenology are profound.
