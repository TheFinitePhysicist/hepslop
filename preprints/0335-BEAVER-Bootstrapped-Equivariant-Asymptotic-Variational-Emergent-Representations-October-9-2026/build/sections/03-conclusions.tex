\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the signal regions of the interplay between
transformer-based top-quark decays and quark and gluon jets at
next-to-leading order to future work. The method applies equally to
pileup particles and non-global logarithms. This unlocks the full
potential of physics-informed neural networks for precision QCD. We hope
this work inspires further research into strongly ordered latent
representations. Our code is publicly available.
