\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with energy flow
networks. Our results show that boosted attention weights couple to the
so-called calibrated test statistic. We observe a mild tension with
positive normalizing flows in the threshold limit. The method applies
equally to calibrated message-passing neural networks and ultrasoft
modes. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on top-quark
decays.
