\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the jet shapes of the radiator of the
canonical differential equations of the frequentist masked autoencoder.
Numerical results are presented for Higgs boson production in gluon
fusion at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. The implications for jet substructure
are profound. The two-loop amplitude exhibits a striking dependence on
$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. We leave the study of anomalous dimensions at
order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
