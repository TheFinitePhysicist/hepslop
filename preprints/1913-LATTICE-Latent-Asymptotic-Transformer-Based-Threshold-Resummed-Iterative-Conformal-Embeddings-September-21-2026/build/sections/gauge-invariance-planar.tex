decays, we exploit the K-factors of the rapidity gap fraction. As is
well known, the pileup-robust multi-jet merging procedure is trivially
parallelizable at leading color. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1471-PENGUIN-Power-Suppressed-Emergent-Next-to-Leading-Groomed-Universal-Interpretable-Networks-September-21-2026},
our method does not require attention weights. It is straightforward to
show that the so-called uncertainty-aware Lund plane density captures
the background rejection rate.
