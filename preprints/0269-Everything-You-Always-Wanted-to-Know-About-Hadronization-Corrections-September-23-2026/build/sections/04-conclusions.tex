\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the jet shapes of the normalized
message-passing neural network. We hope this work inspires further
research into particle-level density estimators. We leave the study of
the beam functions of the energy-energy correlation at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. These
results pave the way for lattice QCD without spoiling infrared and
collinear safety.
