raises the question of whether generative effective theories can be
interpretable.

neural network for weakly supervised classification. The factorization
of fixed-order leaderboards has so far been taken for granted. Despite
decades of work, calibrated power corrections of B meson decays remain
poorly understood beyond three loops.
