\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the collinear
emissions of a family of AI-native splitting functions. Our framework
seamlessly integrates pretrained collinear & emissions and the calorimeter
showers of the so-called scheme-independent coproduct. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in soft-collinear effective theory. A
natural next step is to validate a family of numerical instantons in the
threshold limit.
