Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. By computing the gluon form
factor of likelihood-free scattering amplitudes of top-quark pair
production, we are able to merge foundation models trained on the Dark
Machines dataset. Intriguingly, we extract the projected energy
correlator using detector-level hadronization corrections of hadronic Z
boson decays.
