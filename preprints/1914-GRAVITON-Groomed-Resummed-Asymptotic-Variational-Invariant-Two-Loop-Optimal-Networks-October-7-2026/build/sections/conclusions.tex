\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on renormalon-free
Mellin moments of boosted W boson production. Extending this framework
to template fits trained on Herwig samples is an exciting direction for
future work. The treatment of calibrated Gaussian processes is
admittedly heuristic. Numerical results are presented for deep inelastic
scattering at the $Z$ pole.
