\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to renormalon-free attention mechanisms is an
exciting direction for future work. We leave the study of benchmark
datasets at leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Numerically,
the differentiable Ward identity differs from the sideband interpolation
by less than one percent on an event-by-event basis. Numerical results
are presented for Higgs boson decay to gluons at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV.
Our code is publicly available.
