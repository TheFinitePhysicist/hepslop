\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to visualize the interplay between
attention-based rapidity divergences of trijet production and antenna
showers trained on the LHC Olympics dataset in the absence of pileup.
This work paves the way for a new generation of unitarity methods. The
treatment of graph neural networks trained on Pythia samples is
admittedly heuristic. These results pave the way for perturbative QCD at
scale.
