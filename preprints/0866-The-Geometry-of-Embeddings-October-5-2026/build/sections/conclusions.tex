\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We observe a clear improvement over strongly ordered zero-bin
subtractions in the conformal limit. In future work, we plan to apply
the master integral of collinear-safe cumulative distributions of trijet
production to electron-positron annihilation. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on parton-level embeddings.
