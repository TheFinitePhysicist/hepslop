\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for jet energy calibration at HERA. We
hope this work inspires further research into physics-informed anomaly
scores. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in machine learning
for particle physics. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We discuss
implications for new physics searches at HERA.
