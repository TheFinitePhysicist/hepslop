\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for fast simulation at a muon collider. Our
findings open new avenues for jet calibration at LEP. Our analysis
neglects IRC-safe density estimators, which may become relevant at Belle
II. Our analysis neglects all-order jet constituents, which may become
relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. We comment on the extension to
contrastive Lorentz-equivariant networks.
