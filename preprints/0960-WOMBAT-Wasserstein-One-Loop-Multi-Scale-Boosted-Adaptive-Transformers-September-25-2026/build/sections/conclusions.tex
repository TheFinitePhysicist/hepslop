\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the hierarchical azimuthal
decorrelation of dijet production to tau lepton decays. Extending this
framework to differentiable jet substructure observables is an exciting
direction for future work. We hope this work inspires further research
into BRST-invariant parton distribution functions of electron-positron
annihilation. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by
a wide margin.
