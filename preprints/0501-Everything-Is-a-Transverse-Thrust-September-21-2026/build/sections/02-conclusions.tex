\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for event reconstruction at the High-Luminosity
LHC. We find that the permutation-invariant jet mass distribution of
Drell-Yan production controls a family of light-flavor collinear modes
at the level of the cross section. Extending the method beyond leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. This is just the tip of the iceberg. We leave the study of
Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on simulated FCC data at leading
power to future work.
