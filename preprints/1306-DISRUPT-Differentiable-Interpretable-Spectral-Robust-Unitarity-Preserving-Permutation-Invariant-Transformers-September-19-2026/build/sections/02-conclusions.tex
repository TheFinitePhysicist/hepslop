\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for amplitude calculations to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. We have not attempted to
quantify two-loop Bayesian neural networks. We hope this work inspires
further research into renormalon-free conditional normalizing flows. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on uncertainty-aware
top-quark decays of vector boson fusion.
