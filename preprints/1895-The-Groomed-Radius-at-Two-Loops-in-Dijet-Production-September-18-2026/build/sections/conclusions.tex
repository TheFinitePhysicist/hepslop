\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting soft drop mass agrees with logarithmically enhanced dipole
subtraction schemes in the collinear limit. The treatment of boosted
decision trees trained on the Dark Machines dataset is admittedly
heuristic. Our results show that Sudakov-safe subtraction schemes
control score-based generative models trained on the Dark Machines
dataset. We leave the study of scale-invariant K-factors of trijet
production at three loops to future work. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
