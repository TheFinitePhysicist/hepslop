\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that renormalization group equations trained on the
Dark Machines dataset subsume state-of-the-art dipole showers. We
observe a clear improvement over physics-informed renormalization group
equations to all orders in $\alpha_s$. Our analysis neglects stochastic
scale variations of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion, which may
become relevant at a muon collider. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in the amplitudes program. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on leading-color symbol letters.
