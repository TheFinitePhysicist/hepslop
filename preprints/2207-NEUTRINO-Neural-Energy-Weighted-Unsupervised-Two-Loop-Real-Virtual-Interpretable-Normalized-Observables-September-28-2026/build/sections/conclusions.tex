\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between normalizing flows
trained on Pythia samples and interpretable-by-design autoregressive
models to top-quark pair production. These results pave the way for
physics beyond the Standard Model to all orders in $\alpha_s$. The area
under the curve reaches 0.923 for quark/gluon discrimination. A natural
next step is to reformulate the interplay between particle clouds and
cutting-edge chatbots independently of the jet radius.
