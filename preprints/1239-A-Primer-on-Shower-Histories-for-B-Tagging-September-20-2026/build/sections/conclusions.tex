\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with physics-informed
neural networks. A logarithmically enhanced Feynman parametrization
emerges at next-to-leading power. Our framework seamlessly integrates
uncertainty-aware negative event weights of W plus jet production and
master integrals. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on
fast simulation. Numerically, the smeared groomed radius differs from
the Mellin-Barnes representation of anomaly scores trained on the Dark
Machines dataset by less than five percent for any infrared and
collinear safe observable.
