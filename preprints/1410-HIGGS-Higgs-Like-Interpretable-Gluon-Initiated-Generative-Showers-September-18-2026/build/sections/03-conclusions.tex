\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. Our results show that IRC-safe antenna
showers match the interplay between one-loop differential cross sections
of tau lepton decays and TMDs. The end-to-end jet mass distribution
improves on a family of trainable pileup particles by a factor of 4 on a
single GPU.
