\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this
work inspires further research into learnable zero-bin subtractions. Our
findings open new avenues for track reconstruction at the LHC. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in the AI revolution. This
work paves the way for a new generation of physics-informed neural
networks.
