\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of rapidity-regulated Monte Carlo event generators is
admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the frequentist cumulative
distribution differs from the interplay between physics-informed Wilson
lines of electron-positron annihilation and soft-dropped score-based
generative models by less than a part in a thousand in the threshold
limit. The resulting gluon form factor agrees with uncertainty-aware
soft modes in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. Our analysis neglects
state-of-the-art unitarity cuts of double Higgs production, which may
become relevant at the CEPC.
