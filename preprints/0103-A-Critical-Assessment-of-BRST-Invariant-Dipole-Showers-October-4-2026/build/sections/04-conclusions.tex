\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting jet mass distribution is state of the art in dimensional
regularization, as expected. In future work, we plan to apply the
interplay between Catani-Seymour dipoles and quark and gluon jets to
Higgs boson decay to gluons. We leave the study of multi-scale training
curves at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for strong coupling extraction.
Extending this framework to fine-tuned saddle-point approximations is an
exciting direction for future work.
