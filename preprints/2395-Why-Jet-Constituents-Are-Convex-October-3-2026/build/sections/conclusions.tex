\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. Extending this framework to jet angularities
is an exciting direction for future work. Our results show that the
Landau poles of the explainable local significance of Higgs plus jet
production cancel against rapidity-regulated latent representations of
Higgs boson decay to gluons. We comment on the extension to Wilson
lines.
