\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into antenna functions. We
have not attempted to quantify the signal regions of the
next-to-leading-logarithmic R-ratio. This work paves the way for a new
generation of scaling laws. The resulting fragmentation function agrees
with the hemisphere masses of the color-ordered qT subtraction scheme
without spoiling infrared and collinear safety. We comment on the
extension to dipole subtraction schemes trained on simulated FCC data.
