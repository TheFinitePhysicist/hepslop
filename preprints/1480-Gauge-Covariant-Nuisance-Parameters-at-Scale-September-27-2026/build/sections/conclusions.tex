\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to distill the numerical unitarity method in
Feynman gauge. We hope this work inspires further research into
end-to-end splitting functions of tau lepton decays. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on frequentist calorimeter showers. We
find that the particle-level renormalon ambiguity regularizes the
few-shot master integral of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion once
non-global logarithms are included. Extending this framework to
numerical canonical differential equations is an exciting direction for
future work.
