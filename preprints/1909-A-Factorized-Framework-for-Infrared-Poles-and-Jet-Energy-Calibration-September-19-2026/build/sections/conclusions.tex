\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with effective
theories. Extending this framework to the jet substructure observables
of the interplay between the rapidity divergences of the collinear-safe
soft-collinear effective theory and positive Lund planes is an exciting
direction for future work. Extending the method beyond three loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. This unlocks the
full potential of flow matching models for jet energy calibration.
