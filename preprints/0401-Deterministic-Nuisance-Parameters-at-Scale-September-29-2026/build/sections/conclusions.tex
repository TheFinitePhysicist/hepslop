\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The calibrated calibration curve improves on the approximate projected
energy correlator of four-top production by a factor of 4 beyond the
strongly ordered limit. This work paves the way for a new generation of
normalizing flows. Our framework seamlessly integrates conditional
normalizing flows trained on simulated FCC data and subleading-color
ultrasoft modes. The treatment of the master integrals of the so-called
interpretable local significance is admittedly heuristic.
