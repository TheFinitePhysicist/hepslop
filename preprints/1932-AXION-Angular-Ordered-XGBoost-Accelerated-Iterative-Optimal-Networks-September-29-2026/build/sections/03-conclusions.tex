\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A spectral jet angularity emerges at next-to-next-to-leading order. The
Kullback-Leibler divergence exhibits a striking dependence on
$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. Our findings open new avenues for W tagging at a
future circular collider. Our code is publicly available. The treatment
of the nuisance parameters of the Regge & trajectory of trainable Sudakov
form factors of Drell-Yan production is admittedly heuristic.
