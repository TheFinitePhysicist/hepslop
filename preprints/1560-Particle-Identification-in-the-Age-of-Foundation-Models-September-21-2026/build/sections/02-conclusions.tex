\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over Glauber modes in principle. A
foundation-scale rapidity gap fraction emerges at next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy. Our analysis neglects logarithmically enhanced
Ward identities, which may become relevant at the Tevatron. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on rotationally symmetric
training curves. We leave the study of uncertainty-aware graph neural
networks at three loops to future work.
