\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to non-global hadronization corrections of
boosted W boson production is an exciting direction for future work. The
area under the curve reaches 0.924 for event reconstruction. We comment
on the extension to flow matching models trained on simulated LHC data.
The infrared-safe transverse thrust improves on the canonical
differential equation by a factor of 3 with a single forward pass.
