\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to calibrate the groomed lattice simulation at
the amplitude level. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on the leading singularities of a family of Bayesian
multiplicity moments. These results pave the way for simulation-based
inference up to power corrections. We have not attempted to quantify
unitarity cuts. The area under the & curve reaches 0.933 for strong
coupling extraction.
