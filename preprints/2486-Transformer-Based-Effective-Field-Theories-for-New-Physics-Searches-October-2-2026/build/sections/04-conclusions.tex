\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.933 for parton shower tuning. These
results pave the way for resummation in dimensional regularization.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. In contrast
to normalizing flows, the so-called quark-initiated jet function is
boost invariant by construction. Numerical results are presented for
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV.
