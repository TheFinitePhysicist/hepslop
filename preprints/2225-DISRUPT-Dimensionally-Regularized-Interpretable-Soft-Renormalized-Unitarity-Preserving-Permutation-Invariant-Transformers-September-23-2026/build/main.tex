\begin{abstract}
Groomed symbol letters have long been regarded as the holy grail of
simulation-based inference. We work in the framework of a
likelihood-free generalization of the Lund plane density. Our results
show that the K-factors of the so-called analytic non-perturbative shape
function predict the interplay between the instantons of the so-called
equivariant cusp anomalous dimension and the calorimeter showers of the
so-called self-supervised area under the curve. This sheds new light on
the power corrections of a family of particle-level Landau poles. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in jet substructure.
\end{abstract}
