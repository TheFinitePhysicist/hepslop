\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, fine-tuned infrared poles emerge naturally from the
massive energy-energy correlation of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering. Numerical results are presented for deep inelastic
scattering at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. This leads to a reduction of the
theoretical uncertainty by one percent. We have not attempted to
quantify antenna functions. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
