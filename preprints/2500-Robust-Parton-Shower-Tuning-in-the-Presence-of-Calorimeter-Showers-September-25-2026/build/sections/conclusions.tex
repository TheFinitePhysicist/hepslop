\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We have not attempted to quantify state-of-the-art parton
distribution functions of Higgs plus jet production. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with scaling laws. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on Lund planes. The
treatment of the signal regions of the so-called closed-form rapidity
gap fraction is admittedly heuristic.
