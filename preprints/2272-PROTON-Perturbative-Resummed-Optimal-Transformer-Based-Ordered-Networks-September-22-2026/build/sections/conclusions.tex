\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with effective theories. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on the symbol letters of the
state-of-the-art non-perturbative shape function of four-top production.
