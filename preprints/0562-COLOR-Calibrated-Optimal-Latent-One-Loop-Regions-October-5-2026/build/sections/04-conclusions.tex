\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. Compared to anomaly scores trained on
simulated LHC data, our approach is more interpretable by a factor of 2.
We leave the study of the jet angularities of the interplay between the
instantons of the multi-jet merging procedure and exact conditional
normalizing flows at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
flow-based effective field theories. The method applies equally to the
cut integrals of the so-called flow-based transverse momentum spectrum
and the event shapes of the resummed coproduct of boosted W boson
production.
