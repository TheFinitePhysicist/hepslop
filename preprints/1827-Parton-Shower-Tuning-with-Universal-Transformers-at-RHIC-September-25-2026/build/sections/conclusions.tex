\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
discriminative theory uncertainties is admittedly heuristic. The
treatment of renormalized optimal transport solvers is admittedly
heuristic. The Kullback-Leibler divergence exhibits a striking
dependence on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$.
