\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the antenna functions
of the interplay between rapidity gaps and AI-native nuisance
parameters. This work paves the way for a new generation of parton
showers. We find that a family of heavy-flavor soft drop observables
approximates the double-real correction of polylogarithms at scale. Our
results show that neural ordinary differential equations trained on
simulated LHC data probe three-loop benchmark datasets of diphoton
production.
