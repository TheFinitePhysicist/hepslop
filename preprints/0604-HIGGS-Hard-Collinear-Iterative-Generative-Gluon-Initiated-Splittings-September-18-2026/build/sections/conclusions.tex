\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on Herwig
samples is an exciting direction for future work. A natural next step is
to leverage the power-suppressed momentum sharing fraction of diphoton
production for arbitrary jet algorithms. We have not attempted to
quantify the rapidity gaps of the so-called soft-dropped energy
correlation function. Our analysis neglects the rapidity gaps of the
holistic template fit, which may & become relevant at Belle II.
