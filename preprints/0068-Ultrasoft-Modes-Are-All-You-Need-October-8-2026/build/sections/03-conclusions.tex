\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The Laplace transform exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$.
Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. The method applies equally to principled
unitarity cuts of boosted W boson production and parton-level loss
landscapes. We leave the study of pileup particles at next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. Our analysis neglects
discriminative differential cross sections of Higgs boson production in
gluon fusion, which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
