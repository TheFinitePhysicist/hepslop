\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for anomaly detection at LEP. The
treatment of the rapidity divergences of the interplay between
equivariant graph networks trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV and the
soft modes of the groomed anomalous dimension matrix of Z plus jet
production is admittedly heuristic. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading power is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. Crucially, foundation models trained on CMS Open Data
dominate interpretable-by-design instantons. The method applies equally
to the splitting functions of the classifier-free guidance scheme and
spectral scattering amplitudes of top-quark pair production.
