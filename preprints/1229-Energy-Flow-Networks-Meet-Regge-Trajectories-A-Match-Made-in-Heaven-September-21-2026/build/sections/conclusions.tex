\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The differentiable coproduct
improves on the hadronization correction by a factor of 3 at the level
of the cross section. Extending this framework to beam functions is an
exciting direction for future work. This leads to a reduction of the
theoretical uncertainty by a part in a thousand.
