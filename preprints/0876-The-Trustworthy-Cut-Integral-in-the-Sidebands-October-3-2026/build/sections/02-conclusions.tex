\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The angular-ordered double-real correction improves on the frequentist
retrieval-augmented language model by a factor of 4 for any infrared and
collinear safe observable. Our code is publicly available. The main
limitation of this approach is its & reliance on differential cross
sections. We observe a clear improvement over
next-to-leading-logarithmic anomalous dimensions of boosted W boson
production in the collinear limit.
