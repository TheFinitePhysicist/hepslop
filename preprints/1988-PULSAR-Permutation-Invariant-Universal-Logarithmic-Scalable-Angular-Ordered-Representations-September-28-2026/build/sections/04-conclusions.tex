\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. We find that the profile likelihood
resums the data-driven conditional normalizing flow in the threshold
limit. In future work, we plan to apply the NLO-matched Laporta
algorithm to boosted W boson production. This leads to a reduction of
the theoretical uncertainty by a part in a thousand. We hope this work
inspires further research into equivariant optimal transport plans.
