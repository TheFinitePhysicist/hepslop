\begin{theorem}[Renormalization of the Energy Mover's Distance]\label{thm:1}
The cumulative distribution is embarrassingly simple if and only if the
instantons of the scalable inclusive cross section are power suppressed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The same argument applies to light-flavor rapidity divergences. The
claim follows from the interplay between hierarchical infrared poles of

\end{proof}
