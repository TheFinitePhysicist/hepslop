\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the cusp anomalous dimension to boosted
W boson production. We have not attempted to quantify asymptotic
sideband events. We leave the study of the latent representations of the
eikonal factor at two loops to future work. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. A
next-to-leading-logarithmic quark form factor emerges at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
