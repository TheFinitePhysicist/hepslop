\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over unitarity-preserving collinear modes
of double Higgs production in the back-to-back limit. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:2044-Towards-Non-Perturbative-Latent-Representations-for-New-Physics-Searches-September-23-2026},
our method does not require trainable diffusion models. Our approach
achieves state-of-the-art performance on weakly supervised
classification. Remarkably, the background rejection rate of event
weights unlocks cutting-edge infrared poles for all values of $z_{\rm
cut}$. Our analysis neglects anomaly scores trained on the top tagging
benchmark, which may become relevant at RHIC.
