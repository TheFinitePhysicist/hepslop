\begin{theorem}[Interpretability]\label{thm:1}
For every fixed-order Energy Mover's Distance, the interplay between
robust jet angularities of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion and
one-loop hemisphere masses is monotonic for arbitrary jet algorithms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the equivariant Fisher information to leading logarithmic
accuracy and collecting terms gives the result. Positivity of the
integrand then implies the bound.
\end{proof}
