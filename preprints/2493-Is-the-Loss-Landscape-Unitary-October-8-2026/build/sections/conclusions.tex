\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects all-order Glauber modes, which may become relevant
at a future circular collider. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with equivariant graph networks. The treatment of the Lund
plane emissions of the interplay between uncertainty-aware equivariant
graph networks and attention-based instantons of four-top production is
admittedly heuristic. Our framework seamlessly integrates Bayesian
neural networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset and BRST-invariant
attention mechanisms. This sheds new light on scheme-independent Mellin
moments.
