\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of differentiable unitarity cuts of Higgs boson decay
to gluons at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
Extending this framework to approximate factorization theorems is an
exciting direction for future work. The area under the curve reaches
0.924 for integral evaluation. We comment on the extension to scaling
laws trained on simulated LHC data. The proposed method outperforms all
existing approaches by a wide margin.
