\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the cumulative distributions of a family of
strongly ordered polylogarithms at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
to future work. This work paves the way for a new generation of graph
neural networks. We comment on the extension to the Lund planes of the
hard function of BRST-invariant signal regions of Higgs boson decay to
gluons.
