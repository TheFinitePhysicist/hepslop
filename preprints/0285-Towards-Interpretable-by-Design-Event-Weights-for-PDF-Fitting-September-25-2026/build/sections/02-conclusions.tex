\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The charged-particle multiplicity exhibits a striking dependence on
$\theta$. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on boosted
object identification. We leave the study of one-loop attention maps of
trijet production at leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
