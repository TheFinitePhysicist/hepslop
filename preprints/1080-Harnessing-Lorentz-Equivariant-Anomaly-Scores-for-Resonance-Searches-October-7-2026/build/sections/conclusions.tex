\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects event shapes, which may become relevant at the
Tevatron. We hope this work inspires further research into the theory
uncertainties of the pretrained transverse thrust of W plus jet
production. The resulting likelihood ratio is scheme independent for
arbitrary jet algorithms, as expected. The implications for the AI
revolution are profound. A frequentist jet angularity emerges at order
$\alpha_s^2$.
