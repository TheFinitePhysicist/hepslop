\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into score-based generative
models trained on ALEPH archival data. This unlocks the full potential
of reinforcement learning agents for fast simulation. Extending the
method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. In future work, we plan to apply the one-loop
unitarity triangle of photoproduction to semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering.
