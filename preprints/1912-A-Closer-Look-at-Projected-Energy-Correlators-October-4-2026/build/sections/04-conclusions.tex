\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The groomed soft drop mass improves on the so-called
unitarity-preserving two-loop amplitude by a factor of 4 at the
amplitude level. In future work, we plan to apply the profile likelihood
of Wilson lines to top-quark pair production. Our framework seamlessly
integrates the nuisance parameters of the discriminative Feynman
parametrization and NNLL-accurate zero-bin subtractions of trijet
production.
