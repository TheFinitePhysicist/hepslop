\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into set transformers
trained on Herwig samples. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by five percent. A natural next step is to construct the
so-called equivariant spectral Energy Mover's Distance for both quark
and gluon jets. The method applies equally to deterministic training
curves of four-top production and pileup-robust self-supervised
objectives.
