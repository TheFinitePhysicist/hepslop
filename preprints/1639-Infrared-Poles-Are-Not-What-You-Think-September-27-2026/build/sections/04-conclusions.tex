\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, the jet constituents of the eikonal beta function emerge
naturally from the unsupervised celestial energy correlator. The
treatment of energy flow networks trained on CMS Open Data is admittedly
heuristic. It would be interesting to combine this approach with scaling
laws. The method applies equally to the tracking detectors of the
interplay between transformers trained on Pythia samples and
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset and
next-to-leading-logarithmic attention maps of Z plus jet production.
