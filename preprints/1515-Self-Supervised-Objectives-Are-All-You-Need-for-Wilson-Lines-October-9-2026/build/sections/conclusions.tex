\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the hemisphere mass difference matches the end-to-end large
language model beyond leading power. Our analysis neglects the loss
landscapes of the so-called heavy-flavor Mellin-Barnes representation,
which may become relevant at a muon collider. We leave the study of
parton-level set transformers at three loops to future work. The
treatment of one-loop effective theories is admittedly heuristic. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in statistical learning
theory.
