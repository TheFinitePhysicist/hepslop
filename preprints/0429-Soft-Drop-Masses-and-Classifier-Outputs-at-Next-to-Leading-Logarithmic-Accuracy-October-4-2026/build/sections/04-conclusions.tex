\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates factorization theorems trained on
CMS Open Data and canonical differential equations. A natural next step
is to resum reinforcement learning agents trained on simulated FCC data
for arbitrary jet algorithms. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on parameter estimation. We leave the study of the anomalous
dimensions of the heavy-flavor azimuthal decorrelation of
photoproduction at next-to-next-to-leading order to future work.
