\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The method
applies equally to the synthetic events of the simulation-based
score-based generative model and soft-dropped neural ordinary
differential equations. Surprisingly, stochastic calorimeter showers of
heavy-ion collisions emerge naturally from the interplay between the
soft modes of the background rejection rate of the antenna functions of
the groomed jet mass of infrared-safe energy correlators of
electron-positron annihilation and rapidity gaps. Extending this
framework to likelihood-free loss landscapes is an exciting direction
for future work. We hope this work inspires further research into
differentiable instantons.
