\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting nuisance parameter is insensitive to hadronization at
detector level, as expected. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on rapidity divergences. We have not attempted to quantify
color factors. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches
by a wide margin. This sheds new light on collinear-safe K-factors of B
meson decays.
