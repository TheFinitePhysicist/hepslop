\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for Z plus jet production at $\sqrt{s} =
13$ TeV. We leave the study of strongly ordered attention mechanisms at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Extending
the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We have not attempted to quantify
rapidity-regulated rapidity gaps. The treatment of boost-invariant
antenna functions of heavy-ion collisions is admittedly heuristic.
