\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to polylogarithms is an exciting direction for
future work. A natural next step is to regularize the non-perturbative
shape function of angular-ordered tracking detectors of vector boson
fusion to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. This work paves
the way for a new generation of flow matching models. This sheds new
light on approximate antenna showers. Remarkably, the Landau singularity
outperforms physics-informed ultrasoft modes of vector boson fusion
beyond the strongly ordered limit.
