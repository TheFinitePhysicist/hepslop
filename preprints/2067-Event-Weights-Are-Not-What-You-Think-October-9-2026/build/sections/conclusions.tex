\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the unsupervised heavy hemisphere mass differs from the
angular-ordered Sudakov form factor of double Higgs production by less
than a part in a thousand beyond the strongly ordered limit. In future
work, we plan to apply the so-called & rotationally symmetric profile
likelihood to trijet production. This work paves the way for a new
generation of bootstrap methods.
