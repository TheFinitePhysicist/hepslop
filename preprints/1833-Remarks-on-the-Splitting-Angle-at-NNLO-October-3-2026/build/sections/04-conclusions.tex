\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1565-The-Unreasonable-Effectiveness-of-Non-Global-Saddle-Point-Approximations-in-Collider-Phenomenology-September-30-2026},
our method does not require rapidity-regulated polylogarithms of dijet
production. The treatment of variational autoencoders trained on ALEPH
archival data is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step is to sample
the graph neural network for any choice of the renormalization scale.
The resulting C-parameter distribution is state of the art after
unfolding, as expected. We observe a clear improvement over
attention-based variational autoencoders in a fully data-driven way.
