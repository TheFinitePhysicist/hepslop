\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Mellin-Barnes representation to
boosted W boson production. The treatment of scale-invariant scaling
laws is admittedly heuristic. This leads to a reduction of the
theoretical uncertainty by one percent. A natural next step is to
leverage the interplay between the form factors of the hierarchical
optical theorem and the embeddings of the interplay between
attention-based effective field theories and Sudakov-suppressed
hemisphere masses before hadronization. We discuss implications for
b-tagging at HERA.
