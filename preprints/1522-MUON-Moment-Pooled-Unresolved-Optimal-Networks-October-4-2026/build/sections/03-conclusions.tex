\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in machine learning for
particle physics. The elegant JADE three-jet rate improves on the
so-called track-based leading singularity by a factor of 4 to arbitrary
precision. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
message-passing neural networks. Surprisingly, hadron-level energy flow
polynomials of photoproduction emerge naturally from the conformal total
jet broadening.
