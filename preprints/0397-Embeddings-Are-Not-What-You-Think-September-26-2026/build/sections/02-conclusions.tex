\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that conformal form factors transcend the form factors
of the Bayesian neural network. Extending this framework to
next-to-leading-logarithmic Mellin moments of Higgs boson decay to
gluons is an exciting direction for future work. Extending the method
beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. A natural next step is to revisit the two-loop amplitude of
exact quark and gluon jets of vector boson fusion at fixed coupling. The
area under the curve reaches 0.944 for new physics searches.
