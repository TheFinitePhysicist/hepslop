\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of weakly supervised sphalerons is admittedly heuristic.
Crucially, AI-native soft drop masses of four-top production
exponentiate approximate hemisphere masses of hadronic Z boson decays.
In future work, we plan to apply the pretrained graph neural network to
Higgs plus jet production. Extending this framework to BRST-invariant
score-based generative models is an exciting direction for future work.
