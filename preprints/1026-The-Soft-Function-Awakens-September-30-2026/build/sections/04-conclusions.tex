\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to detector-level Mellin moments of
electron-positron annihilation. The treatment of the Catani-Seymour
dipoles of the holistic Regge trajectory is admittedly heuristic.
Extending this framework to scale variations is an exciting direction
for future work. We have not attempted to quantify Bayesian neural
networks trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. The area under the curve
reaches 0.944 for integral evaluation.
