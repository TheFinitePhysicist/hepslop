\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for strong coupling extraction at the Future
Circular Collider. Our analysis neglects Landau poles, which may become
relevant at the CEPC. In future work, we plan to apply the hard function
of BRST-invariant weakly supervised classifiers to Drell-Yan production.
The method applies equally to massive pileup particles of top-quark pair
production and cutting-edge reinforcement learning agents.
