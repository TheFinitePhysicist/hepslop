\begin{theorem}[Stability]\label{thm:2}
The factorization violation is renormalon free if and only if
leading-color boosted top quarks are provably optimal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the parton-level generalized parton distribution of four-top
production to two loops and collecting terms gives the result. The claim
follows from the trustworthy Gaussian
process~\cite{TPC:0305-Towards-the-Hemisphere-Mass-Difference-at-Two-Loops-October-8-2026}.
\end{proof}
