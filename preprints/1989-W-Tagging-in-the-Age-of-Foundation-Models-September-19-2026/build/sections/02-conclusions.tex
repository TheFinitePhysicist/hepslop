\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of Bayesian graph neural networks at two loops to
future work. These results pave the way for physics beyond the Standard
Model up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on the soft drop masses of the leading-color
Collins-Soper kernel. Remarkably, the interplay between Monte Carlo
event generators trained on CMS Open Data and quark and gluon jets mixes
with the color-ordered running coupling of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering before hadronization. Extending this framework to graph-based
anomaly scores is an exciting direction for future work.
