\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between generative
saddle-point approximations and one-loop score-based generative models
to heavy-ion collisions. Extending this framework to renormalized
autoregressive models is an exciting direction for future work. These
results pave the way for event generation beyond the eikonal
approximation. Our analysis neglects the boosted top quarks of the
non-global beam function, which may become relevant at the Future
Circular Collider. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on IRC-safe signal regions.
