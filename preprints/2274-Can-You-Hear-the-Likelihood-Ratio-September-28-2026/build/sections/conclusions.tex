\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. This leads to a reduction of
the theoretical uncertainty by one percent. The resulting azimuthal
decorrelation is Sudakov safe in the planar limit, as expected. A
natural next step is to calibrate the scheme-independent quark form
factor of trijet production before hadronization.
