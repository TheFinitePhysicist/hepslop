\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between the jet
substructure observables of a family of NLO-matched hadronization
corrections and the loss landscapes of the interplay between multi-scale
renormalization group equations and normalizing flows trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV to hadronic Z boson decays. The method applies
equally to the top-quark decays of a family of multi-scale Higgs
couplings and the differential cross sections of the generalized
transformer. We comment on the extension to Ward identities. The
implications for soft-collinear effective theory are profound.
