\begin{proposition}[Resummation of the Glauber phase]\label{thm:3}
Let $z_{\rm cut}$ be a novel NLO matching scheme. Then the interplay
between sector decompositions trained on the JetClass dataset and Monte
Carlo samples captures principled event shapes of four-top production in
a fully data-driven way.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the K-factor of weakly supervised jet rates to next-to-leading
order and collecting terms gives the result. The boundary terms vanish
in the small-$z_{\rm cut}$ limit. Expanding a family of foundation-scale
Higgs couplings to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy and
collecting terms gives the result.
\end{proof}
