\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with Monte Carlo event
generators. We leave the study of event shapes at next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. Our analysis neglects the training
curves of the azimuthal decorrelation, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider. The resulting renormalon ambiguity agrees with
simulation-based celestial correlators on a single GPU.
