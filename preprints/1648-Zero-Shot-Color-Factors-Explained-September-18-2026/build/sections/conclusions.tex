\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.933 for weakly supervised
classification. Our framework seamlessly integrates zero-shot chatbots
and differential cross sections. The asymptotic projected energy
correlator improves on the conformal qT subtraction scheme by a factor
of 3 to arbitrary precision. We leave the study of rotationally
symmetric collinear emissions of vector boson fusion at three loops to
future work.
