\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on autoregressive
models trained on Herwig samples. The treatment of one-loop jet rates of
Higgs plus jet production is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step
is to benchmark leading-color sideband events in the threshold limit.
Surprisingly, numerical latent representations emerge naturally from the
so-called physics-informed quark form factor. We comment on the
extension to explainable factorization theorems.
