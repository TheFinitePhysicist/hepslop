\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on benchmark
datasets. Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The implications for the AI
revolution are profound. Our findings open new avenues for amplitude
reduction at the Electron-Ion Collider. We leave the study of groomed
energy correlators of W plus jet production at next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work.
