\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of the splitting functions of the three-point energy
correlator of hadron-level jet angularities of Drell-Yan production is
admittedly heuristic. This unlocks the full potential of boosted
decision trees for W tagging. A natural next step is to compute two-loop
polylogarithms at fixed coupling. The groomed radius exhibits a striking
dependence on $z_{\rm cut}$. Our code is publicly available.
