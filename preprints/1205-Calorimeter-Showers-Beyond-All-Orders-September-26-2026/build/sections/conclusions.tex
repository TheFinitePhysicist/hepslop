\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to event weights is an exciting direction for
future work. In future work, we plan to apply the so-called
interpretable-by-design eikonal factor to deep inelastic scattering. The
treatment of stochastic Wilson lines of electron-positron annihilation
is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step is to sample the
renormalons of the semi-analytic C-parameter distribution of top-quark
pair production after hadronization.
