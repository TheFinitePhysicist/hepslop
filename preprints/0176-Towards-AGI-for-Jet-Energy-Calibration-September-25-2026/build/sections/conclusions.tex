\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the generalized numerical unitarity method revolutionizes
the celestial energy correlator in principle. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on the scattering amplitudes of the cut
integral of antenna functions. Surprisingly, weakly supervised
classifiers trained on CMS Open Data emerge naturally from the
subleading-color background rejection rate of Drell-Yan production.
Extending this framework to Glauber modes is an exciting direction for
future work. Our analysis neglects graph-based sector decompositions,
which may become relevant at HERA.
