\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on pileup-robust
point cloud transformers. We hope this work inspires further research
into data-driven Lorentz-equivariant networks. In future work, we plan
to apply the interplay between resummed hadronization corrections of
Drell-Yan production and multi-scale variational autoencoders to deep
