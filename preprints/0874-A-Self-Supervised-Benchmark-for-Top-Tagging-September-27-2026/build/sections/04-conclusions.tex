\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into scaling laws trained on
the quark/gluon dataset. This is just the tip of the iceberg. Our
analysis neglects two-loop dipole showers, which may become relevant at
a future circular collider. A natural next step is to match the soft
modes of the Glauber-enhanced gluon form factor of semi-inclusive deep
inelastic scattering on an event-by-event basis. Our code is publicly
available.
