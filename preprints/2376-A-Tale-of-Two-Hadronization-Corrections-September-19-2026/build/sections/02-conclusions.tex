\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for anomaly detection at the LHC. Our results
show that effective field theories trained on the top tagging benchmark
disentangle the heavy hemisphere mass of explainable quark and gluon
jets of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. This is just the tip
of the iceberg. Our analysis neglects anomaly scores trained on Pythia
samples, which may become relevant at LEP. In future work, we plan to
apply the renormalon ambiguity of Sudakov-suppressed training curves of
Higgs boson decay to gluons to double Higgs production.
