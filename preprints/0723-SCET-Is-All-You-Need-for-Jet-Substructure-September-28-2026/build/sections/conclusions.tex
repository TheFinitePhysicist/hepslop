\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the energy-energy correlation to tau
lepton decays. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
autoregressive models. We hope this work inspires further research into
shower histories. Extending this framework to cutting-edge graph neural
networks is an exciting direction for future work. Numerically, the
Glauber-enhanced jet shape differs from the conditional normalizing flow
by less than one percent within statistical uncertainties.
