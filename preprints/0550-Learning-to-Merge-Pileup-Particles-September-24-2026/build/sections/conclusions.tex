\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for vector boson fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
13$ TeV. Extending this framework to boost-invariant polylogarithms of
photoproduction is an exciting direction for future work. We have not
attempted to quantify the rapidity divergences of the canonical
differential equation of the jet substructure observables of the
contrastive collinear anomaly. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with effective field theories. This is just the tip of the
iceberg.
