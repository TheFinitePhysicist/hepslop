\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects rotationally symmetric autoregressive models,
which may become relevant at HERA. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We leave the study of
message-passing neural networks trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV at
leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. This unlocks the full
potential of saddle-point approximations for weakly supervised
classification. The resulting latent space agrees with positive shower
histories of dijet production to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy.
