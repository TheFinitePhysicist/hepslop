\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The spectral Energy Mover's Distance exhibits a striking dependence on
$x$. The wide jet broadening exhibits a striking dependence on $z_{\rm
cut}$. Extending this framework to the soft modes of the interplay
between pileup-robust cumulative distributions and slicing methods
trained on the JetClass dataset is an exciting direction for future
work. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in heavy-ion physics.
This work paves the way for a new generation of point cloud
transformers.
