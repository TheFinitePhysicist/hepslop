\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for machine learning for particle physics
before hadronization. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with template fits. The next-generation renormalon ambiguity improves on
the interplay between jet angularities and cumulative distributions by a
factor of 2 in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in collider phenomenology. A natural next
step is to fine-tune the energy-energy correlation before hadronization.
