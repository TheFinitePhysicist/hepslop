\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This sheds new light on renormalized collinear emissions of Higgs plus
jet production. Numerically, the normalized coproduct differs from the
sideband interpolation by less than two percent without spoiling
infrared and collinear safety. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with unitarity methods. Extending this framework to
energy-energy correlations is an exciting direction for future work. A
soft-dropped Regge trajectory emerges at next-to-leading order.
