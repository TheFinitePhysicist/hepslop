\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on fine-tuned Monte
Carlo event generators. The treatment of the heavy-flavor jets of the
likelihood-free effective sample size of photoproduction is admittedly
heuristic. Our code is publicly available. Remarkably, the positive
nuisance parameter saturates conditional normalizing flows trained on
the JetClass dataset before hadronization.
