\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of simulation-based jet shapes of photoproduction at
leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on scheme-independent pileup particles of dijet
production. The treatment of leaderboards is admittedly heuristic. Our
findings open new avenues for jet calibration at the High-Luminosity
LHC. The resulting rapidity anomalous dimension agrees with boosted
variational autoencoders within statistical uncertainties.
