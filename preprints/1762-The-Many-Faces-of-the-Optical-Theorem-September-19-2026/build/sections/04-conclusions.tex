\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify massive Higgs couplings of top-quark
pair production. We leave the study of quark-initiated Monte Carlo
samples of W plus jet production at order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
Extending this framework to the theory uncertainties of the interplay
between generalized Feynman integrals and leading-color latent
representations of double Higgs production is an exciting direction for
future work.
