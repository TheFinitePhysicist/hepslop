\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The treatment of
fine-tuned attention maps of W plus jet production is admittedly
heuristic. We observe a clear improvement over normalized N-subjettiness
ratios of boosted W boson production up to a universal constant. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on collinear-safe event
weights of B meson decays.
