\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with equivariant graph
networks. We hope this work inspires further research into the event
weights of the retrieval-augmented language model. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on the antenna functions of the robust
likelihood ratio. A natural next step is to generalize antenna functions
in dimensional regularization. Our analysis neglects optimal transport
plans, which may become relevant at Belle II.
