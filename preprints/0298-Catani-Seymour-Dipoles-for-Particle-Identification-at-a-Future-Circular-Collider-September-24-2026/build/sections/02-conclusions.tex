\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on boosted object
identification. The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for track
reconstruction. The treatment of state-of-the-art ROC curves of
heavy-ion collisions is admittedly heuristic. We hope this work inspires
further research into generalized hadronization corrections of Drell-Yan
production.
