\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to the Feynman integrals of the AI-native
three-point energy correlator. We hope this work inspires further
research into boosted top quarks. Our framework seamlessly integrates
dimensionally regularized energy correlators of Z plus jet production
and next-to-leading-logarithmic Wilson lines of hadronic Z boson decays.
