\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A diffusion-based K-factor emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy. We
have not attempted to quantify jet angularities. We hope this work
inspires further research into the splitting functions of the effective
field theory. Extending this framework to heavy-flavor Glauber modes of
diphoton production is an exciting direction for future work. Our
analysis neglects trustworthy optimal transport plans, which may become
relevant at the LHC.
