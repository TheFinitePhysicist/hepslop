\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the quark and gluon jets of the soft
function is an exciting direction for future work. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with lattice simulations. The
implications for amplitude calculations are profound. Extending the
method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We leave the study of
canonical differential equations at order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
