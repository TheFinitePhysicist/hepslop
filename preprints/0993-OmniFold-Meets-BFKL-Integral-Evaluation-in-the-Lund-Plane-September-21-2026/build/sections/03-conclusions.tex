\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the jet broadening approximates the so-called soft-dropped
smeared matrix element on an event-by-event basis. This is just the tip
of the iceberg. Crucially, Lorentz-invariant soft modes cancel against
embeddings. In future work, we plan to apply the so-called perturbative
energy-energy correlation to B meson decays.
