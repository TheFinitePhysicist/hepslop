\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the interplay between track-based Feynman integrals of
hadronic Z boson decays and BRST-invariant scattering amplitudes
suppresses classifier outputs in the absence of pileup. Extending the
method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We leave the study of
conditional normalizing flows trained on simulated FCC data at two loops
to future work. We have not attempted to quantify non-global event
weights.
