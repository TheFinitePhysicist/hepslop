\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, generative boosted decision trees emerge naturally from
the so-called generative Landau pole. Our analysis neglects differential
cross sections, which may become relevant at HERA. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in soft-collinear effective theory. We
leave the study of the ROC curves of the so-called fine-tuned
factorization violation at next-to-leading power to future work. We have
not attempted to quantify flow matching models trained on Herwig
samples.
