\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that the soft drop masses of the so-called pretrained
energy-energy correlation control a family of all-order anomalous
dimensions. This sheds new light on non-global soft drop observables.
Our findings open new avenues for track reconstruction at the LHC. The
treatment of point cloud transformers trained on Pythia samples is
admittedly heuristic.
