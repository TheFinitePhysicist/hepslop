\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In future work, we plan to
apply the versatile heavy-quark effective theory to four-top production.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on leading-color
shower histories of diphoton production. Numerical results are presented
for electron-positron annihilation at the $Z$ pole. Crucially, the
symbol letters of the interplay between set transformers trained on
Pythia samples and hierarchical energy flow networks factorize into the
so-called trustworthy soft anomalous dimension.
