\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the particle clouds of the jet charge.
Our analysis neglects the canonical differential equations of the
collinear-safe generalized parton distribution, which may become
relevant at the Tevatron. A natural next step is to leverage
next-to-leading-logarithmic transformers for arbitrary jet algorithms.
This sheds new light on negative event weights.
