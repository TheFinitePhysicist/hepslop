\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The agreement with fixed-order QCD is remarkable in a model-agnostic
fashion. We hope this work inspires further research into track-based
template fits. Our analysis neglects parton showers trained on Pythia
samples, which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
Extending this framework to closed-form density estimators is an
exciting direction for future work. This leads to a reduction of the
theoretical uncertainty by five percent.
