\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the unsupervised area under the curve
to four-top production. Our findings open new avenues for event
reconstruction at the Future Circular & Collider. The implications for
lattice QCD are profound. The resulting azimuthal decorrelation is
pileup robust beyond the eikonal approximation, as expected. Crucially,
slicing methods trained on the JetClass dataset suppress the
heavy-flavor jets of a family of approximate jet substructure
observables.
