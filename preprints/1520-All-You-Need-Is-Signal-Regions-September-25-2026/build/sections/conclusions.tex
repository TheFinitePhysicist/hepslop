\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of flow-based subtraction schemes is admittedly heuristic.
Extending this framework to groomed dipole showers is an exciting
direction for future work. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with large language models. Our results show that smeared
N-subjettiness ratios of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion
determine a family of flow-based soft drop masses.
