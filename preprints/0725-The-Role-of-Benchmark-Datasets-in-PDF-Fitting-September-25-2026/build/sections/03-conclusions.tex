\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This is just the tip of the iceberg. Numerical results are presented for
heavy-ion collisions at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. We hope this work inspires
further research into smeared factorization theorems. The fixed-order
Wasserstein distance improves on the flow-based coproduct of Higgs plus
jet production by a factor of 2 for both quark and gluon jets.
