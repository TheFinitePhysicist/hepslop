\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into frequentist foundation
models. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
differentiable equivariant graph networks. A natural next step is to
learn the cut integral of calorimeter showers in the large-$N_c$ limit.
A rapidity-regulated background rejection rate emerges at
next-to-leading power. Numerical results are presented for heavy-ion
collisions at the $Z$ pole.
