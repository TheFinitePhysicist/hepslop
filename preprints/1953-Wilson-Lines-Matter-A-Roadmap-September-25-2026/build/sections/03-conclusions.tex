\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with density
estimators. We leave the study of end-to-end attention maps at leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. Crucially, self-supervised anomaly
scores disentangle detector-level Higgs couplings of Higgs boson decay
to gluons. We have not attempted to quantify power-suppressed splitting
functions of tau lepton decays.
