\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for event generation at the LHC. In future
work, we plan to apply a family of AI-native hadronization corrections
to Drell-Yan production. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on conformal polylogarithms. Remarkably, the
hadronization correction outperforms score-based generative models
trained on Pythia samples to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy.
