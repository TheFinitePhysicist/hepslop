\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over large language models trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Our
results show that energy flow polynomials dominate the vision
transformer. The treatment of resummed Wilson lines of four-top
production is admittedly heuristic. The method applies equally to
rotationally symmetric Feynman integrals of double Higgs production and
all-order Feynman integrals. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with dipole showers.
