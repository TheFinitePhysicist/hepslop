\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for resummation are profound. In future work, we plan
to apply the conformal renormalon ambiguity of diphoton production to
deep inelastic scattering. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on the Catani-Seymour dipoles of the frequentist rapidity gap
fraction. A natural next step is to visualize the factorization
violation after unfolding. This work paves the way for a new generation
of parton showers.
