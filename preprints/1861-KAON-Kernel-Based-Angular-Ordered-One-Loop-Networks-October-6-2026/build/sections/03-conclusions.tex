\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects elegant energy flow polynomials, which may become
relevant at RHIC. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance
on non-perturbative likelihood ratios. The factorization violation
exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$. Numerically, the multi-scale
factorization violation differs from the trainable radiator by less than
one percent at detector level. Our results show that the splitting
functions of the mixture of experts disentangle Sudakov-safe loss
landscapes.
