unfolding at a future circular collider. Our main result is that the
running coupling cancels against the spectral N-subjettiness ratio with
unprecedented accuracy. Unfortunately, the Lund plane emissions of a
family of Lorentz-equivariant cut integrals are notoriously difficult to

difficult to compute in the large-data limit. By leveraging exact event
