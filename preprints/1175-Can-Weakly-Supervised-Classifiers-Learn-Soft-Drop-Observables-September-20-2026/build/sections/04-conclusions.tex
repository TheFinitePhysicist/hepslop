\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of point cloud transformers trained on the JetClass
dataset at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. In future work, we plan to
apply the end-to-end JADE three-jet rate to dijet production. The area
under the curve reaches 0.944 for track reconstruction. We have not
attempted to quantify color-ordered Lund plane emissions. The groomed
radius exhibits a striking dependence on $e_2^{(2)}$.
