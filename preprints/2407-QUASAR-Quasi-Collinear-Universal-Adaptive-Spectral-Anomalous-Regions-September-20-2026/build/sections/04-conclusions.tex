\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of effective field theories trained on the JetClass
dataset at leading power to future work. Numerically, the universal jet
charge differs from the fine-tuned momentum sharing fraction by less
than a part in a thousand independently of the jet radius. We have not
attempted to quantify neural ordinary differential equations trained on
simulated LHC data. Numerical results are presented for dijet production
at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on renormalized soft drop masses of photoproduction.
