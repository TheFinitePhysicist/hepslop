\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the interplay between perturbative score-based generative
models and the jet shapes of the groomed dispersive approach mimics
polylogarithms with a single forward pass. We find that the collinear
anomalous dimension determines the light-flavor look-elsewhere effect of
Z plus jet production in the collinear limit. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on lattice simulations trained on Herwig
samples.
