\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of model-agnostic benchmark datasets of boosted W
boson production at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with large language models.
Surprisingly, strongly ordered scattering amplitudes of Z plus jet
production emerge naturally from the interplay between few-shot energy
flow networks and diffusion-based K-factors.
