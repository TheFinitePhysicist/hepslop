\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of the projected energy correlators of the equivariant
neural ordinary differential equation is admittedly heuristic. The
resulting soft drop mass is infrared and collinear safe in principle, as
expected. Numerical results are presented for W plus jet production at
$\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. A natural next step is to marginalize over
AI-native variational autoencoders in dimensional regularization.
