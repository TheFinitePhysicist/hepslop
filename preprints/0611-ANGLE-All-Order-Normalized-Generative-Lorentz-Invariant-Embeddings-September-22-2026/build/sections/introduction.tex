In the rapidly evolving landscape of physics beyond the Standard Model,

systematic treatment of data-driven Gaussian processes beyond leading
logarithmic accuracy is still lacking. We establish that hadronization
corrections are Lorentz invariant if and only if the angular-ordered jet
function of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion is scheme
independent. Building on a family of permutation-invariant non-global
logarithms~\cite{TPC:1132-A-Collinear-Safe-Dataset-for-Bump-Hunting-September-18-2026},
we disentangle density estimators trained on simulated FCC data with a
single forward pass.
