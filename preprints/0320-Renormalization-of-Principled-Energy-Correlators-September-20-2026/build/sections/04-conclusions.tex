\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to distill self-supervised objectives trained on
Herwig samples in the strongly ordered limit. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on attention-based Bayesian neural
networks. We have not attempted to quantify stochastic subtraction
schemes. Our analysis neglects quark-initiated dipole showers, which may
become relevant at a future circular collider. Extending the method
beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
