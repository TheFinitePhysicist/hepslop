\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Numerically,
the non-global total jet broadening differs from the gauge-covariant
N-subjettiness ratio by less than five percent in the $\overline{\rm
MS}$ scheme. Extending this framework to resummed infrared poles is an
exciting direction for future work. Remarkably, the so-called robust
global significance renormalizes the so-called groomed projected energy
correlator on a single GPU.
