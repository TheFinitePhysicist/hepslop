\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of rapidity gaps is admittedly heuristic. The proposed
method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. The
resulting projected energy correlator is renormalon free at leading
color, as expected. Extending this framework to neural ordinary
differential equations trained on Herwig samples is an exciting
direction for future work. The treatment of cumulative distributions is
admittedly heuristic.
