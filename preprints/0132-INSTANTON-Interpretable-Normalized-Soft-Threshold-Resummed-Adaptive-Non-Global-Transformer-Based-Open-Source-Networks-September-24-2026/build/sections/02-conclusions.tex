\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A data-driven beta function emerges at two loops. Our framework
seamlessly integrates deterministic renormalization group equations and
multi-scale Monte Carlo event generators. The agreement with Herwig is
remarkable for any number of emissions. We hope this work inspires
further research into the event shapes of the leading-color parton-level
event generator. This is just the tip of the iceberg.
