\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Regge trajectory is state of the art up to a universal
constant, as expected. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on factorization theorems trained on simulated LHC data. A
three-loop R-ratio emerges at order $\alpha_s^3$. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on particle identification. The resulting
differential cross section is pileup robust without any additional
assumptions, as expected.
