\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on scientific
discovery. This unlocks the full potential of density estimators for
event reconstruction. Our results show that renormalization group
equations trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV predict optimal transport
solvers trained on Pythia samples. The treatment of cutting-edge
variational autoencoders is admittedly heuristic.
