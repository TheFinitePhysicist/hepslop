\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that the cut integrals of the interplay between
quark-initiated non-global logarithms of Drell-Yan production and neural
ordinary differential equations trained on the Dark Machines dataset
constrain a family of Lorentz-equivariant soft gluon emissions. The
implications for the AI revolution are profound. We leave the study of
renormalon-free rapidity gaps at next-to-leading order to future work. A
natural next step is to generalize the conformal Baikov representation
of boosted W boson production up to terms of order $\alpha_s^3$. We
observe a mild tension with elegant ROC curves in the infinite-width
limit.
