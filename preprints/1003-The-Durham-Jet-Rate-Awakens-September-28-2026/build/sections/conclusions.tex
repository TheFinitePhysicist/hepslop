\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on bump hunting. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on particle-level jet
rates of top-quark pair production. We discuss implications for jet
energy calibration at HERA. Our code is publicly available. The
treatment of effective theories trained on ALEPH archival data is
admittedly heuristic.
