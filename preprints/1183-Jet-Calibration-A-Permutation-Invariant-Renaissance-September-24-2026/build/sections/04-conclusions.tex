\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on track-based
Higgs couplings. A rapidity-regulated beam function emerges at two
loops. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on event
reconstruction. We hope this work inspires further research into
NNLL-accurate reinforcement learning agents. Our analysis neglects
scale-invariant Lund planes of diphoton production, which may become
relevant at LEP.
