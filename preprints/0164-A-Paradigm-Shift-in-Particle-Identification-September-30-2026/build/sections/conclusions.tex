\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of IRC-safe anomalous dimensions at
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The
Lorentz-invariant three-point energy correlator improves on the scalable
celestial energy correlator by a factor of 4 in light-cone gauge. This
unlocks the full potential of density estimators for jet tagging.
