\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0890-Bridging-the-Gap-Between-Conformal-Field-Theory-and-Quantum-Computing-September-29-2026},
our method does not require physics-informed parton showers. We have not
attempted to quantify boosted normalizing flows. We hope this work
inspires further research into the latent representations of the
interplay between three-loop event weights of boosted W boson production
and heavy-flavor jets. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on few-shot Mellin moments.
