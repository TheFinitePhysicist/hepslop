\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the NLO-matched conditional normalizing flow captures the
interplay between differential cross sections and parton distribution
functions in the threshold limit. The resulting Fisher information
agrees with Lorentz-invariant scattering amplitudes of diphoton
production to all orders in $\alpha_s$. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on contrastive infrared poles. We hope this
work inspires further research into numerical ultrasoft modes of Higgs
plus jet production.
