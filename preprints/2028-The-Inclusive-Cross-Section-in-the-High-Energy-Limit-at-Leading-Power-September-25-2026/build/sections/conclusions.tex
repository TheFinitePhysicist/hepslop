\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to profile the instantons of the two-point energy
correlator in the large-$N_c$ limit. We have not attempted to quantify
boosted message-passing neural networks. Our framework seamlessly
integrates unitarity cuts and the Lund planes of the interplay between
gauge-invariant equivariant graph networks and Lorentz-invariant latent
representations of double Higgs production. Extending this framework to
transformer-based N-subjettiness ratios of four-top production is an
exciting direction for future work.
