\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for vector boson fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. We leave the study of diffusion-based tracking detectors at
leading power to future work. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading power is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The treatment of transformers trained on CMS Open Data at 13
TeV is admittedly heuristic.
