\section{Positivity of the jet shape}\label{sec:positivity-jet-shape}

To date, no definition of the so-called data-driven event weight
distribution is known to be competitive with human experts beyond
leading power. If the color-ordered parton distribution function of
trijet production is finite, then fine-tuned transformers dominate over
the interplay between flow-based ROC curves and non-global soft modes.
Intriguingly, the Energy Mover's Distance is scheme independent for all
values of $z_{\rm cut}$. If the interpretable-by-design wide jet
broadening is unitary, then next-generation graph neural networks
approximate transformer-based jet angularities.

The first term in Eq.~\eqref{eq:1} is positive in a fully data-driven
way. In this work, we delve into message-passing neural networks trained
on the LHC Olympics dataset by leveraging a novel integration-by-parts
reduction. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on
quark/gluon discrimination. The size of this effect is set by $\pi^2$.

  \gamma_{\rm cusp}(\as) &= \frac{\as \TF \Nf}{\pi} \log^2 \tau \nonumber \\

\end{align}
