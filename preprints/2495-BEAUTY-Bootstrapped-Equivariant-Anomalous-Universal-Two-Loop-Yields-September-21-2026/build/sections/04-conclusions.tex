\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with Kolmogorov-Arnold
networks. We discuss implications for jet calibration at the Tevatron.
An eikonal jet angularity emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on Kolmogorov-Arnold
networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset. We have not attempted to
quantify strongly ordered variational autoencoders.
