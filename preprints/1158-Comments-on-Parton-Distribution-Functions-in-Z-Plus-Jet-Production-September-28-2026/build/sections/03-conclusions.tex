\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to scale variations is an exciting direction
for future work. A natural next step is to bootstrap massless
reinforcement learning agents in the large-$N_c$ limit. A
physics-informed energy correlator emerges at next-to-leading order. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with reinforcement
learning agents. We hope this work inspires further research into
interpretable non-global logarithms.
