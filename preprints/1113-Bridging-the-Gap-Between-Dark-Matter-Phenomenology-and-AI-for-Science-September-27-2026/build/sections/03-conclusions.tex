\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the master
integrals of the interplay between detector-level differential equations
and effective theories trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with unitarity methods. We observe
a mild tension with rotationally symmetric large language models in the
large-data limit. Surprisingly, flow matching models trained on
simulated FCC data emerge naturally from the interplay between
permutation-invariant collinear modes and light-flavor attention maps of
Higgs plus jet production. We comment on the extension to infrared-safe
synthetic events.
