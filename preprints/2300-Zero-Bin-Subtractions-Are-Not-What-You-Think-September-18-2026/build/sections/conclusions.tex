\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects energy flow polynomials, which may become relevant
at HERA. The method applies equally to permutation-invariant
N-subjettiness ratios of Drell-Yan production and stochastic jet shapes
of vector boson fusion. The resulting eikonal factor is universal in
principle, as expected. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on soft drop masses.
