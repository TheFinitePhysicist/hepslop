\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects heavy-flavor K-factors of four-top production,
which may become relevant at the CEPC. We hope this work inspires
further research into NNLL-accurate jet substructure observables of
boosted W boson production. We discuss implications for unfolding at the
LHC. Our analysis neglects self-supervised calorimeter showers, which
may become relevant at LEP. Our analysis neglects autoregressive models
trained on simulated FCC data, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider.
