\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The multi-scale energy correlation function improves on the spectral
hadronization correction by a factor of 3 beyond leading power. A
scalable cusp anomalous dimension emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$.
Extending this framework to AI agents trained on simulated LHC data is
an exciting direction for future work. The treatment of hierarchical
projected energy correlators is admittedly heuristic. Our code is
publicly available.
