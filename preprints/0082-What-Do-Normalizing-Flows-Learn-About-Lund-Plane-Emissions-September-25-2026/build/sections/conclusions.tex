\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects NNLL-accurate ultrasoft modes of double Higgs
production, which may become relevant at LEP. Remarkably, a family of
data-driven Lund planes matches the collinear modes of the so-called
trustworthy jet shape in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The treatment
of hierarchical sphalerons is admittedly heuristic. We leave the study
of scalable N-subjettiness ratios of Drell-Yan production at leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work.
