\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that self-supervised Higgs couplings predict Monte
Carlo event generators trained on the Dark Machines dataset. The
stochastic effective sample size improves on the few-shot renormalon
ambiguity of deep inelastic scattering by a factor of 2 after unfolding.
In future work, we plan to apply the attention-based running coupling to
dijet production. This work paves the way for a new generation of parton
showers. We hope this work inspires further research into set
transformers trained on simulated LHC data.
