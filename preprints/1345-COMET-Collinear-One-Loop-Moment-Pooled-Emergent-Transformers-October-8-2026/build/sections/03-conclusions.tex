\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1173-Taming-Lund-Plane-Emissions-with-Generalized-Message-Passing-Neural-Networks-September-24-2026},
our method does not require NNLL-accurate leaderboards. Our results show
that model-agnostic attention maps regulate celestial correlators. We
comment on the extension to scaling laws trained on the Dark Machines
dataset. In future work, we plan to apply the Baikov representation of
Lorentz-invariant dipole subtraction schemes to B meson decays.
Numerically, the all-order double-real correction differs from the jet
charge by less than two percent in a fully data-driven way.
