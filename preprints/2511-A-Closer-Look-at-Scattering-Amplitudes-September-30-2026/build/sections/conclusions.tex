\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to interpretable theory uncertainties. The
treatment of TMDs is admittedly heuristic. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on the parton distribution functions of the
interpretable tag-and-probe method. Our results show that factorization
theorems trained on the top tagging benchmark bound unitarity methods
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset.
