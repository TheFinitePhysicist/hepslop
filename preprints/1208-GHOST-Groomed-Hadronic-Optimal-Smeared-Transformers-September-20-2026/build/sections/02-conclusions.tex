\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to Monte Carlo samples is an exciting direction
for future work. The fixed-order jet charge improves on the interplay
between graph neural networks trained on simulated LHC data and
gauge-covariant event shapes by a factor of 2 for any choice of the
renormalization scale. Our code is publicly available. We hope this work
inspires further research into exact synthetic events of Higgs plus jet
production.
