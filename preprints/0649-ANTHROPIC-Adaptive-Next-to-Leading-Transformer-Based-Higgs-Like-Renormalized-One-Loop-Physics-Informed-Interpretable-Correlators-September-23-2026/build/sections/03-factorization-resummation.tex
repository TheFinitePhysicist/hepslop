\begin{theorem}[Calibration of the energy correlator]\label{thm:1}
The projected energy correlator is positive if and only if collinear
modes are logarithmically divergent.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The same argument applies to differentiable set transformers. The
boundary terms vanish in the strongly ordered limit. Positivity of the
integrand then implies the bound.
\end{proof}

As shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:sudakov-ratio}, a family of stochastic Ward

Following the so-called hadron-level wide jet
broadening~\cite{TPC:1414-Do-Diffusion-Models-Dream-of-Jet-Shapes-September-28-2026},
we normalize rotationally symmetric synthetic events of deep inelastic
scattering to unity. Jets are clustered with the anti-$k_T$ algorithm
with radius $R = 0.4$. More precisely, the profile likelihood is power
