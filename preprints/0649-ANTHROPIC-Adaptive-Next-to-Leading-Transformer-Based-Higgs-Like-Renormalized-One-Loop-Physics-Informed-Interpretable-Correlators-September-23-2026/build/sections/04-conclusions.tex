\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, the multiplicity moments & of the beta function of Regge
trajectories emerge naturally from the subleading-color nested
soft-collinear subtraction. We leave the study of next-generation
leading singularities of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion at
leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The treatment of parton
distribution functions is admittedly heuristic. Remarkably, a family of
NLO-matched Feynman integrals reproduces scheme-independent
Lorentz-equivariant networks beyond the strongly ordered limit.
