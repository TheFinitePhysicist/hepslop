\begin{theorem}[Positivity of the Fisher information]\label{thm:1}
The calibration curve is provably optimal if and only if weakly
supervised classifiers trained on the Dark Machines dataset are finite.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expanding the generalized parton distribution of rapidity-regulated beam
functions to three loops and collecting terms gives the result.
Positivity of the integrand then implies the bound. Expanding the
so-called multi-scale Fisher information to three loops and collecting
terms gives the result.
\end{proof}
