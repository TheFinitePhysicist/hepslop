\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into detector-level boosted
decision trees. Extending this framework to message-passing neural
networks trained on the top tagging benchmark is an exciting direction
for future work. Our findings open new avenues for Higgs tagging at the
Electron-Ion Collider. This unlocks the full potential of template fits
for pileup mitigation. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on soft-dropped parton showers.
