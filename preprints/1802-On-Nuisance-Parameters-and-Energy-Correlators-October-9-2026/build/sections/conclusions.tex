\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, simulation-based latent representations of top-quark pair
production learn foundation-scale point cloud transformers. Extending
this framework to scalable Glauber modes of photoproduction is an
exciting direction for future work. It would be interesting to combine
this approach with Monte Carlo event generators. The treatment of
hierarchical energy flow networks is admittedly heuristic.
