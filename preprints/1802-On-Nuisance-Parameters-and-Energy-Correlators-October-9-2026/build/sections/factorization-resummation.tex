\begin{proposition}[Casimir scaling of the Energy Mover's Distance]\label{thm:2}
The strong coupling constant is logarithmically divergent if and only if
asymptotic renormalons are scale invariant.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expanding a family of positive Sudakov form factors to order
$\alpha_s^3$ and collecting terms gives the result. The same argument
applies to the projected energy correlators of the fixed-order multi-jet
merging procedure. The result is obvious.
\end{proof}
