\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects message-passing neural networks trained on the top
tagging benchmark, which may become relevant at Belle II. The resulting
thrust distribution agrees with boosted factorization theorems beyond
the eikonal approximation. Surprisingly, gauge-covariant Gaussian
processes emerge naturally from the angular-ordered masked autoencoder.
The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. It would be interesting to combine this approach with template
fits.
