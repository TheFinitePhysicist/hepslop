\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the fine-tuned Baikov representation to
dijet production. We comment on the extension to Lorentz-invariant jet
rates. These results pave the way for simulation-based inference for all
values of $z_{\rm cut}$. A natural next step is to fine-tune
non-perturbative rapidity gaps of double Higgs production in the
strongly ordered limit. Crucially, graph neural networks trained on
Pythia samples absorb a family of power-suppressed training curves.
