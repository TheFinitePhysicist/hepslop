\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with equivariant graph
networks. This work paves the way for a new generation of equivariant
graph networks. Extending this framework to rotationally symmetric
scattering amplitudes of deep inelastic scattering is an exciting
direction for future work. Our analysis neglects zero-shot
message-passing neural networks, which may become relevant at the Future
Circular Collider.
