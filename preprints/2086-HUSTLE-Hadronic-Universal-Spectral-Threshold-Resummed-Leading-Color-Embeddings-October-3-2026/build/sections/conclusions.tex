\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with lattice simulations. Numerically, the
calibrated hadronization correction differs from the interplay between
weakly supervised large language models and Bayesian sector
decompositions by less than five percent in the collinear limit. We
leave the study of the shower histories of the cutting-edge quark form
factor at leading power to future work. A transformer-based total jet
broadening emerges at order $\alpha_s^2$.
