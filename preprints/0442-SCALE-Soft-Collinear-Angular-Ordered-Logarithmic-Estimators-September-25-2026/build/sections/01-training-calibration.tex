trained on ALEPH archival data. Although we focus on Z plus jet
production, the argument is expected to hold more generally. For
brevity, we refer to anomaly scores trained on simulated LHC data as
density estimators. We stress that this conclusion holds only before
hadronization. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty
by two percent.
