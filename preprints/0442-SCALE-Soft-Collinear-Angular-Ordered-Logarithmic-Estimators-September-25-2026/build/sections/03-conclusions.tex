\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.944 for boosted object
identification. Our framework seamlessly integrates subleading-color set
transformers and subtraction schemes trained on Pythia samples. The
treatment of analytic unitarity cuts of boosted W boson production is
admittedly heuristic. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on asymptotic anomalous dimensions of boosted W boson
production.
