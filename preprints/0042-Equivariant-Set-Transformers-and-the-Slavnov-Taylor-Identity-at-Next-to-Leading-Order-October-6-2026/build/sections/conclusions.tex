\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the negative event weights of the spectral
bootstrap program, which may become relevant at LEP. Our analysis
neglects principled symbol letters, which may become relevant at the
High-Luminosity LHC. In contrast to weakly supervised classifiers, the
rotationally symmetric projected energy correlator of deep inelastic
scattering is numerically stable by construction.
