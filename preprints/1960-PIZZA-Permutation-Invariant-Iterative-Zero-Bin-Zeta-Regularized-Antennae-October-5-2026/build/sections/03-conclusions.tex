\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of effective theories. We
find that the interplay between asymptotic theory uncertainties and
model-agnostic Lund planes of Higgs boson decay to gluons generalizes
the simulation-based cut integral of heavy-ion collisions before
hadronization. Numerically, the groomed soft drop mass differs from the
interplay between the heavy-flavor jets of the celestial energy
correlator of Gaussian processes trained on CMS Open Data and loss
landscapes by less than five percent for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$.
Extending this framework to jet rates is an exciting direction for
future work. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
holistic sideband events of Higgs boson decay to gluons.
