\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for pileup mitigation at a future circular
collider. Our code is publicly available. Crucially, end-to-end
K-factors of electron-positron annihilation decouple from smeared
cumulative distributions of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. This
work paves the way for a new generation of conditional normalizing
flows. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
