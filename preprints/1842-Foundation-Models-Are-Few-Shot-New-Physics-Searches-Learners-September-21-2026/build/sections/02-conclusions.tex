\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A gauge-covariant soft drop mass emerges at next-to-next-to-leading
order. The resulting total jet broadening is causal in the threshold
limit, as expected. Our analysis neglects state-of-the-art hadronization
corrections of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering, which may
become relevant at a muon collider. Extending the method beyond three
loops is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
