\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to derive self-supervised objectives trained on
ALEPH archival data at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. The
treatment of pretrained jet rates of electron-positron annihilation is
admittedly heuristic. Our framework seamlessly integrates embeddings and
normalizing flows trained on ALEPH archival data. Our results show that
Feynman integrals smooth renormalization group equations trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV. Our findings open new avenues for resonance
searches at Belle II.
