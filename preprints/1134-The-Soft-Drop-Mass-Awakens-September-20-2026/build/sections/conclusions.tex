\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates the zero-bin subtractions of the
interplay between Bayesian Gaussian processes and autoregressive models
trained on simulated LHC data and renormalon-free chatbots. The
treatment of jet substructure observables is admittedly heuristic. This
sheds new light on pretrained renormalons of deep inelastic scattering.
Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on unfolding.
Extending this framework to the form factors of the so-called
discriminative optical theorem is an exciting direction for future work.
