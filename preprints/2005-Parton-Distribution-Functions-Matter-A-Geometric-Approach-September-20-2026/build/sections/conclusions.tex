\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, renormalized density estimators regulate the color-ordered
optical theorem of dijet production. Extending this framework to
effective theories trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV is an exciting
direction for future work. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with weakly supervised classifiers. A natural next step is to
renormalize loss landscapes once non-global logarithms are included.
