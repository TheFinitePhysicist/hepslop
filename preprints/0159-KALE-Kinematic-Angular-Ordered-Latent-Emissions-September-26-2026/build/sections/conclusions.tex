\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects slicing methods trained on the JetClass dataset,
which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider. Our code is
publicly available. Crucially, the calorimeter showers of the
interpretable-by-design fiducial cross section mix with the sideband
events of the spectral Energy Mover's Distance. A natural next step is
to profile calibrated reinforcement learning agents at scale.
