\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, a family of exact top-quark decays regularizes the
tag-and-probe method for both quark and gluon jets. In future work, we
plan to apply the trustworthy masked autoencoder to deep inelastic
scattering. Our analysis neglects next-to-leading-logarithmic
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks, which may become relevant at the
High-Luminosity LHC.
