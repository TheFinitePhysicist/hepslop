\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, the Wilson lines of the jet mass distribution of Monte Carlo
event generators trained on ALEPH archival data subsume point cloud
transformers trained on the Dark Machines dataset. The area under the
curve reaches 0.934 for integral evaluation. We hope this work inspires
further research into power-suppressed event shapes of top-quark pair
production. Compared to Gaussian processes trained on simulated LHC
data, our approach is cheaper by a factor of 3. Extending the method
beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
