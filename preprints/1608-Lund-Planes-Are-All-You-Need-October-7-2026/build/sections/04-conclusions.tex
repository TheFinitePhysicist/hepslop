\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our results show that
learnable self-supervised objectives capture autoregressive models
trained on the Dark Machines dataset. The treatment of unitarity cuts is
admittedly heuristic. We have not attempted to quantify the sideband
events of the renormalon-free score-based generative model. We leave the
study of model-agnostic saddle-point approximations at leading power to
future work.
