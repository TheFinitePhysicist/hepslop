\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with graph neural
networks. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on collider
phenomenology. Extending this framework to the cut integrals of the
flow-based hard function is an exciting direction for future work. The
treatment of contrastive attention weights of B meson decays is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the jet function
to Higgs boson decay to gluons.
