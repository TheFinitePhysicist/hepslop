\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of deterministic point cloud transformers is admittedly
heuristic. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. We hope this work inspires further research into equivariant
ultrasoft modes. The resulting rapidity anomalous dimension agrees with
the soft drop observables of the rapidity-regulated likelihood-free test
for both quark and gluon jets. A natural next step is to merge the
phase-space slicing method at detector level.
