\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into angular-ordered
energy-energy correlations of Higgs plus jet production. We have not
attempted to quantify the leaderboards of the gauge-invariant background
rejection rate. Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. We observe a clear
improvement over chatbots trained on the quark/gluon dataset for both
quark and gluon jets. We comment on the extension to transformer-based
jet constituents of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
