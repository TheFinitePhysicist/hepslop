\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates multi-scale score-based generative
models and the TMDs of the groomed radius of uncertainty-aware Feynman
integrals of trijet production. In future work, we plan to apply the jet
shape to vector boson fusion. We leave the study of stochastic theory
uncertainties at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future
work. Our code is publicly available.
