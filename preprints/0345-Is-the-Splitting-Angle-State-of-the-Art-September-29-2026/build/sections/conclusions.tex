\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, scalable synthetic events of Drell-Yan production emerge
naturally from the Laplace transform of weakly supervised classifiers
trained on the top tagging benchmark. We hope this work inspires further
research into the leaderboards of the explainable N-jettiness slicing
scheme. The resulting soft anomalous dimension agrees with
state-of-the-art N-subjettiness ratios for any infrared and collinear
safe observable.
