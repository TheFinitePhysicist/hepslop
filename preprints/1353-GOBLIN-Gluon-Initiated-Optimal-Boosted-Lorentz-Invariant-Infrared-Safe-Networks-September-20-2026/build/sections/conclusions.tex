\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The implications
for physics beyond the Standard Model are profound. Our approach
achieves state-of-the-art performance on integral evaluation. In future
work, we plan to apply the track-based Lund plane density to Higgs plus
jet production. Extending this framework to discriminative Glauber modes
is an exciting direction for future work.
