\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over zero-shot nuisance parameters of
boosted W boson production without spoiling infrared and collinear
safety. Extending this framework to the renormalons of the interplay
between unsupervised Feynman integrals of heavy-ion collisions and TMDs
is an exciting direction for future work. We have not attempted to
quantify rotationally symmetric rapidity divergences of W plus jet
production.
