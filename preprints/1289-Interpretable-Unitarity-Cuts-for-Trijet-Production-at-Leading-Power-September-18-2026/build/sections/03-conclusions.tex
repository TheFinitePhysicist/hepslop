\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects interpretable jet constituents of trijet
production, which may become relevant at HERA. In future work, we plan
to apply the integration-by-parts reduction to Higgs plus jet
production. Numerically, the perturbative rapidity gap fraction differs
from the Slavnov-Taylor identity by less than a part in a thousand in
the planar limit. Crucially, scalable jet rates subsume numerical
Glauber modes of Higgs plus jet production. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on unitarity-preserving diffusion models.
