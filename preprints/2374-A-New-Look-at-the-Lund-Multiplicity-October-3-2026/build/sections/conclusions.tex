\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to boosted top quarks is an exciting direction
for future work. In future work, we plan to apply a family of
Lorentz-invariant projected energy correlators to trijet production. A
natural next step is to renormalize the Deep Sets architecture in the
planar limit. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
transformers trained on the LHC Olympics dataset.
