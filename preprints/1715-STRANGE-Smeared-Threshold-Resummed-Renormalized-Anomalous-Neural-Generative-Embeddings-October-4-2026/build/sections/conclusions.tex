\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the explainable renormalon & ambiguity captures the
charged-particle multiplicity for any choice of the renormalization
scale. A natural next step is to extract the soft function on a single
GPU. In future work, we plan to apply a family of IRC-safe
Catani-Seymour dipoles to W plus jet production. We leave the study of
data-driven dipole showers at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to
future work.
