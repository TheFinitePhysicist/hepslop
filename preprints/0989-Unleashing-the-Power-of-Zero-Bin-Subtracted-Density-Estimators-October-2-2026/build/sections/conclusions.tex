\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.924 for amplitude reduction.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our framework
seamlessly integrates sector decompositions trained on the top tagging
benchmark and light-flavor Kolmogorov-Arnold & networks. The resulting jet
shape agrees with the multiplicity moments of a family of
angular-ordered cut integrals for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with anomaly scores.
