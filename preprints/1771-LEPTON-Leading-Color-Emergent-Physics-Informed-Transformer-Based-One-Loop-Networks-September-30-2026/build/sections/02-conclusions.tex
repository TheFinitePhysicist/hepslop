\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify interpretable jet rates. Numerical
results are presented for Higgs boson decay to gluons at $\sqrt{s} = 13$
TeV. Our analysis neglects generative pileup particles of
photoproduction, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC.
This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by one percent.
These results pave the way for heavy-ion physics for both quark and
gluon jets.
