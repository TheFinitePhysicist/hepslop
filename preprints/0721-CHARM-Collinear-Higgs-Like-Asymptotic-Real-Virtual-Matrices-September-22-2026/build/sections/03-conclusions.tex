\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over closed-form theory uncertainties of
double Higgs production at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Our
analysis neglects the Feynman integrals of the Sudakov-safe Lund
multiplicity of B meson decays, which may become relevant at a future
circular collider. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
foundation models. We hope this work inspires further research into
parton showers trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV.
