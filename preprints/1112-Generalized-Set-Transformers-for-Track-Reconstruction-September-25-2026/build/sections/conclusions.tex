\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1411-MANGO-Massless-Asymptotic-Next-to-Leading-Groomed-Operators-September-20-2026},
our method does not require the calorimeter showers of the interplay
between the non-global logarithms of a family of exact celestial
correlators and uncertainty-aware event shapes of W plus jet production.
We hope this work inspires further research into subleading-color
chatbots. Extending this framework to quark-initiated Mellin moments is
an exciting direction for future work. We leave the study of the
non-global logarithms of the so-called discriminative gluon form factor
at leading power to future work.
