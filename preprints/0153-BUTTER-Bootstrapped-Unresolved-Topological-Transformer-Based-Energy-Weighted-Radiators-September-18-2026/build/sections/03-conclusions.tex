\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on self-supervised
Catani-Seymour dipoles of deep inelastic scattering. The proposed method
outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. Extending the
method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. These results pave the way
for the amplitudes program after unfolding.
