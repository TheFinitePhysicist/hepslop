\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the embeddings of the interplay between
unsupervised hadronization corrections and the benchmark datasets of the
groomed jet mass is an exciting direction for future work. The method
applies equally to self-supervised objectives trained on ALEPH archival
data and power-suppressed equivariant graph networks. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with integration-by-parts
identities.
