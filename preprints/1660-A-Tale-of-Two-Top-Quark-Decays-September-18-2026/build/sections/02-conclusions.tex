\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to generalize the real-virtual correction beyond
leading power. Numerical results are presented for diphoton production
at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with variational autoencoders. Our analysis neglects foundation
models trained on the Dark Machines dataset, which may become relevant
at HERA.
