\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of optimal transport
solvers. We observe a clear improvement over energy flow networks
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV within statistical uncertainties. A
natural next step is to learn the quark-initiated event weight
distribution without retraining. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on calorimeter simulation. This unlocks the full potential
of dipole subtraction schemes for unfolding.
