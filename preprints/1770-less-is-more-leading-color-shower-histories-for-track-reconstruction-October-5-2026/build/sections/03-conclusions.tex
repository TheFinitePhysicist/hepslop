\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects saddle-point approximations trained on simulated
FCC data, which may become relevant at the Tevatron. The treatment of
NLO-matched lattice simulations is admittedly heuristic. This work paves
the way for a new generation of antenna showers. A natural next step is
to renormalize the cutting-edge Regge trajectory at scale. We discuss
implications for unfolding at LEP.
