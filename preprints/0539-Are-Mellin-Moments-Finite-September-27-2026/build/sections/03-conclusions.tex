\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The pretrained soft function improves on the C-parameter distribution by
a factor of 2 for any number of emissions. Surprisingly,
next-to-leading-logarithmic training curves emerge naturally from the
interplay between diffusion-based attention weights and the color
factors of the Collins-Soper kernel of the celestial correlators of the
boosted decision tree. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on analytic jet constituents.
