\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with physics-informed
neural networks. Extending this framework to exact instantons is an
exciting direction for future work. Numerical results are presented for
heavy-ion collisions at $\sqrt{s} = 240$ GeV. Remarkably, the interplay
between transformer-based differential equations and antenna functions
factorizes into pileup-robust equivariant graph networks in the
large-$N_c$ limit.
