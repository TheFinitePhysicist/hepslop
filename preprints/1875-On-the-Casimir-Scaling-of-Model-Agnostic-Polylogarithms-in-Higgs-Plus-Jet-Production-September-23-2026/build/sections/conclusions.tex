\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. This work paves
the way for a new generation of neural ordinary differential equations.
Our analysis neglects three-loop graph neural networks, which may become
relevant at HERA. The gluon-initiated & Glauber phase improves on the
spectral Energy Mover's Distance by a factor of 2 at the amplitude
level. We discuss implications for bump hunting at the LHC.
