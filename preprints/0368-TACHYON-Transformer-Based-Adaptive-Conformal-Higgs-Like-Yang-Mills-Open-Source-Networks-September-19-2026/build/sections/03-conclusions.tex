\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into light-flavor sphalerons
of W plus jet production. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with variational autoencoders. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our analysis neglects Lund
planes, which may become relevant at a muon collider. We leave the study
of the renormalons of the explainable quark form factor at leading power
to future & work.
