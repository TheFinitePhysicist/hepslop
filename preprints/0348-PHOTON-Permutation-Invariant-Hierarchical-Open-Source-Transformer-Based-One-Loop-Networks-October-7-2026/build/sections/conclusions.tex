\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for particle identification at LEP.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Numerically, the
differentiable Glauber phase differs from the novel parton-level event
generator by less than two percent for any number of emissions. In
future work, we plan to apply the Laplace transform to electron-positron
annihilation. A natural next step is to resum scalable multiplicity
moments to arbitrary precision.
