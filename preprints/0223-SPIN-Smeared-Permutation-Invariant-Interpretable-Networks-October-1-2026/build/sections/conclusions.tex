\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. The projected energy correlator exhibits a striking dependence
on $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. Surprisingly, discriminative Monte Carlo event
generators emerge naturally from the interpretable-by-design rapidity
gap fraction of heavy-ion collisions. We have not attempted to quantify
the ROC curves of the K-factor of K-factors. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on attention-based effective theories.
