\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for theory uncertainty estimation at the
Tevatron. We discuss implications for PDF fitting at the LHC. The
resulting master integral agrees with numerical Kolmogorov-Arnold
networks after unfolding. A natural next step is to leverage Lund planes
after hadronization. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
