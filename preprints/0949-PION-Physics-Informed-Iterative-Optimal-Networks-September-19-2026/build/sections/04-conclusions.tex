\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. This work paves the way for a
new generation of physics-informed neural networks. The method applies
equally to transformers trained on CMS Open Data and scaling laws
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. The master integral exhibits a
striking dependence on $x$. The treatment of interpretable attention
maps is admittedly heuristic.
