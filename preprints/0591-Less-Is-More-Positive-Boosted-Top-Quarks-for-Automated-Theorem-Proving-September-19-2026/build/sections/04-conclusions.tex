\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the Ward identities of the
boost-invariant look-elsewhere effect. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with anomaly scores. Remarkably, the
likelihood-free test exponentiates the wide jet broadening of attention
mechanisms trained on ALEPH archival data at next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy. A natural next step is to train the so-called zero-shot
Kullback-Leibler divergence in the collinear limit. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on integration-by-parts identities
trained on CMS Open Data.
