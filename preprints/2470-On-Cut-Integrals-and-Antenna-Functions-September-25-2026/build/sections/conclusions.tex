\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on frequentist
boosted decision trees. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with autoregressive models. We hope this work
inspires further research into reinforcement learning agents trained on
the top tagging benchmark.
