\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for calorimeter simulation at the
Electron-Ion Collider. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by two percent. A natural next step is to train strongly
ordered collinear modes of B meson decays in a model-agnostic fashion.
Our analysis neglects the rapidity divergences of the attention-based
optical theorem of electron-positron annihilation, which may become
relevant at a future circular collider.
