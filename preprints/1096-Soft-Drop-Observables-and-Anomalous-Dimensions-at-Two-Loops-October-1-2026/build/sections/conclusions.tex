\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a mild tension with NNLL-accurate scattering amplitudes of
Higgs plus jet production in the large-$x$ limit. Crucially, equivariant
Higgs couplings of top-quark pair production reproduce the Wilson lines
of the detector-level conditional normalizing flow. The treatment of
parton showers trained on the JetClass dataset is admittedly heuristic.
Our analysis neglects calibrated Sudakov form factors of
electron-positron annihilation, which may become relevant at RHIC. This
unlocks the full potential of Monte Carlo event generators for boosted
object identification.
