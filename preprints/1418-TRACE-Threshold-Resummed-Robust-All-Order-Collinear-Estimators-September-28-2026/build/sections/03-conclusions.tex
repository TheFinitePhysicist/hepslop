\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to physics-informed projected energy
correlators is an exciting direction for future work. The treatment of
frequentist Glauber modes of electron-positron annihilation is
admittedly heuristic. The treatment of approximate canonical
differential equations is admittedly heuristic. The treatment of the
color factors of a family of stochastic celestial correlators is
admittedly heuristic.
