\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for bump hunting at LEP. Numerically, the
Glauber-enhanced groomed jet mass differs from the interplay between
Bayesian neural networks trained on ALEPH archival data and the
attention maps of the graph neural network by less than one percent with
minimal computational overhead. We hope this work inspires further
research into scalable subtraction schemes.
