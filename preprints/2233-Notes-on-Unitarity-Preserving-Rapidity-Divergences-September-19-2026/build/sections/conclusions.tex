\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates Feynman integrals and particle-level
N-subjettiness ratios. This sheds new light on N-subjettiness ratios. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in conformal field theory. The
main limitation of this approach is its reliance on attention weights.
These results pave the way for simulation-based inference at leading
color.
