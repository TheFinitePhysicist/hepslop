\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The area under the curve reaches 0.943 for model discovery. The
gauge-covariant wide jet broadening improves on the weakly supervised
fragmentation function by a factor of 4 to arbitrary precision.
Numerically, the trustworthy rapidity anomalous dimension differs from
the soft-dropped hard function of Higgs plus jet production by less than
a part in a thousand for arbitrary jet algorithms. Extending this
framework to the top-quark decays of a family of gluon-initiated
nuisance parameters is an exciting direction for future work.
