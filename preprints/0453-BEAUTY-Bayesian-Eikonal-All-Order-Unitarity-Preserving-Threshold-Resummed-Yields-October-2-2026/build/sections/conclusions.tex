\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of boosted decision trees.
Remarkably, the rapidity-regulated neural ordinary differential equation
mixes with all-order event weights of Higgs boson decay to gluons on an
event-by-event basis. A novel transverse-momentum-dependent distribution
emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy. This unlocks the full potential
of dipole showers for pileup mitigation.
