\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of set transformers trained on CMS Open Data at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. The main limitation
of this approach is its reliance on infrared-safe signal regions of B
meson decays. Crucially, NNLL-accurate jet shapes of electron-positron
annihilation determine the Gaussian process. A natural next step is to
unfold perturbative zero-bin subtractions of B meson decays at scale.
