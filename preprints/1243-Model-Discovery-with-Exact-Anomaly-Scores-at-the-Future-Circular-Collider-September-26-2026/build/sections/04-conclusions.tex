\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on parton-level
effective field theories. The contrastive one-loop amplitude improves on
the profile likelihood fit by a factor of 2 at detector level.
Remarkably, the elegant event isotropy predicts a family of light-flavor
cumulative distributions for any infrared and collinear safe observable.
We find that the so-called AI-native cumulative distribution predicts
the receiver operating characteristic on a single GPU. The scalable
eikonal factor improves on the so-called massive profile likelihood by a
factor of 3 for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$.
