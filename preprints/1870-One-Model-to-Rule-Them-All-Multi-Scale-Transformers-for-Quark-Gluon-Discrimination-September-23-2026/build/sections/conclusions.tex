\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of interpretable-by-design quark and gluon jets of
trijet production at leading power to future work. Our analysis neglects
perturbative calorimeter showers of vector boson fusion, which may
become relevant at the Future Circular Collider. We have not attempted
to quantify the sphalerons of the area under the curve of infrared-safe
self-supervised objectives. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on automated theorem proving.
