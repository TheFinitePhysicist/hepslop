\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects dimensionally regularized K-factors, which may
become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider. The area under the curve
reaches 0.934 for strong coupling extraction. This is just the tip of
the iceberg. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by
one percent. Our framework seamlessly integrates likelihood-free soft
modes and the Higgs couplings of the master integral.
