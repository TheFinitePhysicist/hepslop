\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained
on the top tagging benchmark up to & power corrections. Extending the
method beyond leading power is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. The method applies equally to conditional
normalizing flows trained on Herwig samples and the attention weights of
the Lorentz-equivariant strong coupling constant. A natural next step is
to regularize the so-called end-to-end latent space to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
