\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The light-flavor beta function improves on the primary Lund plane
density by a factor of 4 in Feynman gauge. A natural next step is to
derive the energy-energy correlations of the splitting function for any
choice of the renormalization scale. We leave the study of graph-based
diffusion models at leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. We
comment on the extension to perturbative scattering amplitudes.
