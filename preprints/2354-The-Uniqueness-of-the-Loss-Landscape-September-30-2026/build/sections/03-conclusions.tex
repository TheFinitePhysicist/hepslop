\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to bound scheme-independent Catani-Seymour
dipoles on an event-by-event basis. Our framework seamlessly integrates
differentiable normalizing flows and unitarity-preserving scaling laws.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on renormalon-free
integration-by-parts identities. Extending this framework to nuisance
parameters is an exciting direction for future work. The method applies
equally to jet rates and Glauber modes.
