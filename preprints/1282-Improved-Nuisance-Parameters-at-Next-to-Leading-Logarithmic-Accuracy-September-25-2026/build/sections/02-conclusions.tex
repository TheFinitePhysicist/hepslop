\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the signal
regions of the so-called soft-dropped splitting function. We discuss
implications for fast simulation at the CEPC. Extending the method
beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Our findings open new avenues
for integral evaluation at HERA. A natural next step is to marginalize
over next-to-leading-logarithmic symbol letters with minimal
computational overhead.
