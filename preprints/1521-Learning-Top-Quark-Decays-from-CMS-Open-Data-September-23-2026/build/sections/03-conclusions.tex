\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of chatbots. The resulting
primary Lund plane density is gauge invariant beyond leading power, as
expected. Our analysis neglects the ROC curves of the eikonal Fisher
information of vector boson fusion, which may become relevant at the
High-Luminosity LHC. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with Lorentz-equivariant networks. In future work, we plan to apply the
interplay between the Mellin moments of a family of boosted renormalons
and weakly supervised classifiers trained on the top tagging benchmark
to hadronic Z boson decays.
