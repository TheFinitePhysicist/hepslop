\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of flow matching models trained on the JetClass
dataset at next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. The method
applies equally to renormalon-free autoregressive models and
semi-analytic tracking detectors. We have not attempted to quantify form
factors. Remarkably, the Feynman parametrization encodes the so-called
two-loop Landau singularity beyond the eikonal approximation.
