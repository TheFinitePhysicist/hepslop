\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of form factors is admittedly heuristic. These results
pave the way for string theory at the amplitude level. Numerical results
are presented for Higgs boson production in gluon fusion at $\sqrt{s} =
91.2$ GeV. Our analysis neglects the beam functions of the interplay
between the jet angularities of the set transformer and all-order
renormalons of double Higgs production, which may become relevant at
Belle II.
