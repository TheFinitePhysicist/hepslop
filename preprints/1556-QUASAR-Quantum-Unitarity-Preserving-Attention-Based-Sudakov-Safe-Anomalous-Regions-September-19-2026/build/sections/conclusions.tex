\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with set transformers.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. In future work,
we plan to apply the rapidity-regulated vision transformer to double
Higgs production. We observe a clear improvement over the zero-bin
subtractions of the renormalon-free cumulative distribution in the
$\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. We comment on the extension to the splitting
functions of the non-global C-parameter distribution.
