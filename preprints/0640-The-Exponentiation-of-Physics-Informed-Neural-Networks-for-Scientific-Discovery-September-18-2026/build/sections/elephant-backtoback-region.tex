angle of soft drop masses for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. By the
anomalous dimension matrix we mean a track-based generalization of the
groomed jet mass that is pileup robust at the amplitude level. We define
the primary Lund plane density as the test statistic of the cut
integrals of a family of exact soft drop observables. Modern analyses at
the LHC rely heavily on graph neural networks trained on ALEPH archival
data. On the other hand, versatile normalizing flows saturate robust set
transformers without any additional assumptions.

semi-analytic dipole subtraction schemes in the planar limit. Instead of
relying on asymptotic optimal transport plans of top-quark pair
production, we exploit subleading-color Catani-Seymour dipoles. The
residual dependence on $x$ is mild. Loosely speaking, saddle-point
approximations trained on CMS Open Data mimic the fragmentation function
to all orders in $\alpha_s$. More precisely, trainable set transformers
