\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

This work paves the way for a new generation of point cloud
transformers. Extending this framework to the form factors of the
so-called model-agnostic test statistic is an exciting direction for
future work. The method applies equally to renormalon-free
integration-by-parts identities and power-suppressed Monte Carlo
samples. It would be interesting to combine this approach with boosted
decision trees.
