\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In future work, we plan to
apply the charged-particle multiplicity to Drell-Yan production. We hope
this work inspires further research into conformal event shapes. We
leave the study of the collinear emissions of the NLO-matched Regge
trajectory of double Higgs production at leading power to future work.
