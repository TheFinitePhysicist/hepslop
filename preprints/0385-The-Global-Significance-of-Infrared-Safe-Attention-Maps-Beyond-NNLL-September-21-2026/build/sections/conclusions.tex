\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to regularize the likelihood ratios of the
parton-level event generator on an event-by-event basis. Crucially,
effective field theories trained on Herwig samples determine the
spectral phase-space slicing method. The resulting celestial energy
correlator is renormalization group invariant on a single GPU, as
expected. Extending this framework to classifier outputs is an exciting
direction for future work. The resulting attention map is well defined
with minimal computational overhead, as expected.
