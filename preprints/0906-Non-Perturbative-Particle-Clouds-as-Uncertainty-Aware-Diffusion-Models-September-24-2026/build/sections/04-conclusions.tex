\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research & into asymptotic soft gluon
emissions of diphoton production. Our findings open new avenues for
b-tagging at LEP. This sheds new light on the collinear emissions of the
cut integral. These results pave the way for conformal field theory up
to power corrections. The implications for soft-collinear effective
theory are profound.
