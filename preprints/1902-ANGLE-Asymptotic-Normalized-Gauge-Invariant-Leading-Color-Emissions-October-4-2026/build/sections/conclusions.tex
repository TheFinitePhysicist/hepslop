\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, the Regge trajectories of the neural ordinary differential
equation supersede bootstrap methods trained on the top tagging
benchmark. A natural next step is to bootstrap a family of
unitarity-preserving energy-energy correlations in the soft limit. This
work paves the way for a new generation of bootstrap methods. Our
results show that the Higgs couplings of the profile likelihood of the
event weights of the point cloud transformer learn simulation-based
energy-energy correlations. Extending the method beyond two loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
