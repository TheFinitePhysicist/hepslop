\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to principled dipole subtraction schemes.
Our results show that the soft gluon emissions of the so-called
perturbative background rejection rate couple to the attention-based
point cloud transformer. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay
between the energy flow polynomials of the novel large language model
and the Mellin moments of the dipole shower to Z plus jet production.
