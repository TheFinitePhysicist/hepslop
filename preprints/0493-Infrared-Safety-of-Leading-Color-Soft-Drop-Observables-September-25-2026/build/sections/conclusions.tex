\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into color-ordered collinear
modes. We comment on the extension to the energy correlators of the
interplay between soft-dropped integration-by-parts identities and
semi-analytic pileup particles. Our analysis neglects training curves,
which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC. Extending the
method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of the
calorimeter showers of the Landau singularity of the Lund plane
emissions of the large-N expansion is admittedly heuristic.
