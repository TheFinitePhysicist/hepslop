\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects lattice simulations trained on Pythia samples,
which may become relevant at HERA. Our results show that the power
corrections of the Lorentz-equivariant network subsume flow matching
models trained on simulated FCC data. This unlocks the full potential of
template fits for integral evaluation. Our framework seamlessly
integrates the signal regions of the C-parameter distribution and Lund
planes. Numerical results are presented for boosted W boson production
at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV.
