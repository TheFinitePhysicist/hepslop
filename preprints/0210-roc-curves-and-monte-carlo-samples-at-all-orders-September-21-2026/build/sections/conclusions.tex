\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify boosted likelihood ratios. We hope
this work inspires further research into energy correlators. The
treatment of rotationally symmetric quark and gluon jets of dijet
production is admittedly heuristic. The resulting cumulative
distribution agrees with uncertainty-aware Sudakov form factors of Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. Our
analysis neglects asymptotic celestial correlators, which may become
relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
