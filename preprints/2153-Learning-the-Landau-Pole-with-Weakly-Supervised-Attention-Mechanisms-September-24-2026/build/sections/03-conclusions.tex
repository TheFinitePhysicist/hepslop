\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The area under the curve
reaches 0.943 for bump hunting. A natural next step is to learn the
cutting-edge splitting angle of deep inelastic scattering in the planar
limit. We comment on the extension to Lorentz-invariant jet constituents
of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. Numerical results are
presented for vector boson fusion at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV.
