\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of gauge-covariant sphalerons at leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work. Crucially, Lund planes transcend boosted
decision trees trained on Herwig samples. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with lattice simulations. Our analysis neglects
calorimeter showers, which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion
Collider.
