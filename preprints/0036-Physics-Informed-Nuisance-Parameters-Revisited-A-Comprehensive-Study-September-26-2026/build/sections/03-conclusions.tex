\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into tracking detectors. Our
code is publicly available. We leave the study of scalable event weights
of boosted W boson production at next-to-leading order to future work.
Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:0882-W-Tagging-with-Logarithmically-Enhanced-Monte-Carlo-Event-Generators-at-the-High-Luminosity-LHC-September-18-2026},
our method does not require leading-color sector decompositions.
