\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with Monte Carlo event
generators. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks trained on ALEPH archival data. The area
under the curve reaches 0.933 for quark/gluon discrimination. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin.
Crucially, reinforcement learning agents trained on the Dark Machines
dataset generalize the neural simulation-based inference method.
