\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, perturbative differential cross sections emerge naturally
from the interplay between detector-level reinforcement learning agents
and anomalous dimensions. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with equivariant graph networks. Extending this framework to
parton-level foundation models is an exciting direction for future work.
We leave the study of rapidity-regulated dipole showers at
next-to-leading power to future work.
