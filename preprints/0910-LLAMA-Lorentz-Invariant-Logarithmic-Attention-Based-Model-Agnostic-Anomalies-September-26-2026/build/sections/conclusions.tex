\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to scale variations. A natural next step is
to generalize the attention-based cusp anomalous dimension in the
back-to-back limit. Extending the method beyond three loops is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on end-to-end energy flow
networks. The treatment of physics-informed instantons is admittedly
heuristic.
