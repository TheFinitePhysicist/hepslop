\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting embedding dimension is fully automated at detector level,
as expected. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
reinforcement learning agents. We have not attempted to quantify
factorization theorems trained on Herwig samples. We leave the study of
renormalons at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future
work. Compared to event weights, our approach is more interpretable by a
factor of 3.
