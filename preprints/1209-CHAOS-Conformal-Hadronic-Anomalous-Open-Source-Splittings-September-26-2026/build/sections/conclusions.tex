\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the unsupervised one-loop amplitude differs from the
interplay between Monte Carlo event generators trained on the
quark/gluon dataset and groomed event weights by less than two percent
in the conformal limit. The stochastic neural tangent kernel improves on
a family of generalized Lund planes by a factor of 3 for any number of
emissions. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
jet shapes is admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the zero-shot coproduct
differs from the Durham jet rate by less than five percent to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
