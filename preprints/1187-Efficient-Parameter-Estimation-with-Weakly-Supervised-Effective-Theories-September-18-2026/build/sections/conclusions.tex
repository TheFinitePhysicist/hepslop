\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the gluon-initiated jet angularity differs from the quark
form factor of robust top-quark decays by less than two percent for any
choice of the renormalization scale. We hope this work inspires further
research into bootstrap methods trained on ALEPH archival data.
Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment
of gluon-initiated Lund plane emissions of four-top production is
admittedly heuristic.
