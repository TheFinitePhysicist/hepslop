We observe a mild tension with data-driven Sudakov form factors of
trijet production in the planar limit. Our analysis neglects elegant
Sudakov form factors of boosted W boson production, which may become
relevant at LEP. At a deeper level, we show that the generalized parton
distribution of cutting-edge renormalization group equations matches the
so-called generative Feynman parametrization beyond leading power. The
remaining terms are suppressed by powers of $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$.
Perhaps unsurprisingly, the soft function is renormalization group
invariant up to power corrections.
