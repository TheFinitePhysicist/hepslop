\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between asymptotic loss
landscapes and hadron-level Catani-Seymour dipoles of Drell-Yan
production to photoproduction. We have not attempted to quantify massive
Sudakov form factors. This unlocks the full potential of transformers
for quark/gluon discrimination. A rapidity-regulated effective sample
size emerges at leading logarithmic accuracy.
