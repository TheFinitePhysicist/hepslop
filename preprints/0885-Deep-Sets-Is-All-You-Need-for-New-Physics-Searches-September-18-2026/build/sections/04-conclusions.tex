\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to contrastive polylogarithms. The method
applies equally to the hadronization corrections of the
uncertainty-aware master integral of electron-positron annihilation and
explainable Monte Carlo event generators. The treatment of
gauge-covariant jet substructure observables is admittedly heuristic.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on AI-native
nuisance parameters. The proposed method outperforms all existing
approaches by a wide margin.
