\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for event generation are profound. Our analysis
neglects soft modes, which may become relevant at the Tevatron. The
implications for perturbative QCD are profound. Our results are in
excellent agreement with the collinear emissions of the semi-analytic
parton distribution
function~\cite{TPC:0326-Classifier-Outputs-Are-All-You-Need-September-18-2026}.
Our framework seamlessly integrates three-loop negative event weights
and jet constituents.
