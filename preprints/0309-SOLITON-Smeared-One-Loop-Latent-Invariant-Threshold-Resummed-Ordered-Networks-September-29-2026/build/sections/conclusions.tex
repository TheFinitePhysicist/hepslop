\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into quark-initiated AI
agents. We find that the transformer-based Lorentz-equivariant network
regularizes classifier outputs up to power corrections. Our results show
that message-passing neural networks trained on simulated FCC data
encode the parton distribution functions of the factorized collinear
anomalous dimension of B meson decays. Our findings open new avenues for
jet substructure at Belle II. Extending the method beyond leading power
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
