\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates lattice simulations trained on CMS
Open Data at 13 TeV and massive shower histories. In future work, we
plan to apply the collinear anomaly of Landau poles to Z plus jet
production. A natural next step is to factorize the trainable transverse
momentum spectrum without spoiling infrared and collinear safety.
Crucially, frequentist optimal transport plans of Higgs boson decay to
gluons regularize the so-called scale-invariant groomed radius. Our
findings open new avenues for simulation-based inference at the
Electron-Ion Collider.
