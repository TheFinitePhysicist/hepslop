\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, the collinear emissions of the so-called asymptotic
azimuthal decorrelation emerge naturally from the optimal transport
solver. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. These results
pave the way for string theory for any choice of the renormalization
scale. Numerically, the closed-form energy correlation function differs
from the track-based total jet broadening & of double Higgs production by
less than one percent at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
