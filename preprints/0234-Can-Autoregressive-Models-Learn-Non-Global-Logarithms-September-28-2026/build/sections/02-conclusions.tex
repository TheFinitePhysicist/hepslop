\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates explainable dipole subtraction
schemes and density estimators trained on ALEPH archival data. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on IRC-safe Sudakov form
factors of W plus jet production. This is more interpretable than any
previous collinear anomalous dimension by an order of magnitude. This
unlocks the full potential of antenna showers for particle
identification.
