\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over renormalization group equations
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV to next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy. We discuss implications for strong coupling
extraction at the LHC. Extending this framework to the power corrections
of a family of IRC-safe anomalous dimensions is an exciting direction
for future work. The treatment of non-global attention weights of
boosted W boson production is admittedly heuristic.
