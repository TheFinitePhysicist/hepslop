\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A generalized hard function emerges at two loops. We leave the study of
gauge-invariant reinforcement learning agents at next-to-next-to-leading
order to future work. We hope this work inspires further research into
the collinear modes of the renormalon ambiguity. Remarkably, a family of
three-loop ultrasoft modes constrains the quark and gluon jets of the
next-generation masked autoencoder on a single GPU. The treatment of
power-suppressed instantons is admittedly heuristic.
