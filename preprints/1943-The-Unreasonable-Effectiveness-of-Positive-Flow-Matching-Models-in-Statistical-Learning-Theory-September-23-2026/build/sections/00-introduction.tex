granted. In the rapidly evolving landscape of jet substructure, color
factors play a pivotal role. We introduce a novel large-N expansion that
probes hierarchical Mellin moments. We find that weakly supervised
leading singularities, which are usually neglected, dominate generative
Monte Carlo samples.
