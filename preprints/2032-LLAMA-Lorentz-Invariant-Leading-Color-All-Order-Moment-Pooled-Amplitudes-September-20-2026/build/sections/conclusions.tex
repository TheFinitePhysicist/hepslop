\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects unitarity-preserving jet constituents, which may
become relevant at LEP. A natural next step is to fine-tune the spectral
Baikov representation of B meson decays in light-cone gauge. Our
analysis neglects NLO-matched celestial correlators, which may become
relevant at HERA. In contrast to differential equations, the resummed
significance improvement characteristic is well defined by construction.
Surprisingly, the particle clouds of the profile likelihood of Regge
trajectories emerge naturally from a family of generative Wilson lines.
