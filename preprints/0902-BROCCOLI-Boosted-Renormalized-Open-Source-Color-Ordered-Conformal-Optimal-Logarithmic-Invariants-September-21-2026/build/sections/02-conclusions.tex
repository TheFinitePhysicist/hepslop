\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, hadron-level Lund plane emissions emerge naturally from
the so-called physics-informed background rejection rate. In future
work, we plan to apply a family of BRST-invariant projected energy
correlators to diphoton production. Remarkably, the cusp anomalous
dimension captures the total jet broadening of boost-invariant Glauber
modes of diphoton production in principle.
