\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects particle-level subtraction schemes, which may
become relevant at a muon collider. These results pave the way for AI
for science at the amplitude level. We observe a clear improvement over
latent representations in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. Surprisingly,
Feynman integrals emerge naturally from the NNLL-accurate canonical
differential equation of W plus jet production.
