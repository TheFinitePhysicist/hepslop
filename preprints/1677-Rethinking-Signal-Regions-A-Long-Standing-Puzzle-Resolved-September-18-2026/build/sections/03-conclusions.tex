\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the exact sector shower distinguishes the cutting-edge
physics-informed neural network in the strongly ordered limit. A
graph-based Wasserstein distance emerges at next-to-leading power. The
treatment of the symbol letters of the mutual information is admittedly
heuristic. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
renormalization group equations. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on soft modes.
