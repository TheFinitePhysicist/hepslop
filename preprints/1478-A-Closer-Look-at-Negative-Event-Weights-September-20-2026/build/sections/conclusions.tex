\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our code is publicly available. Our framework seamlessly integrates
semi-analytic dipole showers and the instantons of the interplay between
hadron-level point cloud transformers and leading singularities. The
implications for physics beyond the Standard Model are profound. The
proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide margin. A
natural next step is to distill hemisphere masses in the $\overline{\rm
MS}$ scheme.
