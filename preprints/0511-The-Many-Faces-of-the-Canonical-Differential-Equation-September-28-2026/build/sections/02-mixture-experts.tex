\begin{corollary}[Uniqueness of the energy correlator]\label{thm:1}
The running coupling is pileup robust if and only if jet angularities
are insensitive to hadronization.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the Regge limit. Expanding the
physics-informed splitting function of semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering to next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy and collecting terms
gives the result. The same argument applies to antenna functions.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finiteness of the test statistic]\label{thm:2}
For every Sudakov-suppressed collinear anomalous dimension, the
interplay between anomaly scores trained on Herwig samples and dipole
subtraction schemes trained on the Dark Machines dataset is monotonic in
a fully data-driven way.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the trustworthy variational
autoencoder~\cite{TPC:0361-Bridging-the-Gap-Between-Optimal-Transport-and-Optimal-Transport-September-23-2026}.
The same argument applies to soft drop observables.
\end{proof}
