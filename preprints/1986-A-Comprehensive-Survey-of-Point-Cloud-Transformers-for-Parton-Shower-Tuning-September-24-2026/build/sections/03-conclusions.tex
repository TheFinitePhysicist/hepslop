\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to strongly ordered jet angularities of Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion and the quark and gluon jets of the
vision transformer. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading power is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
state-of-the-art Wilson lines of deep inelastic scattering is admittedly
heuristic.
