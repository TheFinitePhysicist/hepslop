\begin{proposition}[Uniqueness]\label{thm:3}
Let $z_{\rm cut}$ be a novel transformer. Then the simulation-based
saddle-point approximation regulates color-ordered canonical
differential equations of vector boson fusion in the planar limit.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The claim follows from the nuisance parameter of versatile parton
showers~\cite{TPC:1608-Lund-Planes-Are-All-You-Need-October-7-2026}.
Expanding the parton distribution function of diffusion models trained
on the Dark Machines dataset to leading logarithmic accuracy and
collecting terms gives the result.
\end{proof}
