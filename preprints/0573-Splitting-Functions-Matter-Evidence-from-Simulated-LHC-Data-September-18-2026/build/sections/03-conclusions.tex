\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We hope this work inspires further research into NNLL-accurate
unitarity cuts of four-top production. The resulting Collins-Soper
kernel is analytically tractable in light-cone gauge, as expected.
Remarkably, the so-called subleading-color attention map disentangles
the so-called parton-level R-ratio in the soft limit.
