\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with graph neural
networks. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
anomaly scores trained on Herwig samples. We discuss implications for
bump hunting at a future circular collider. Extending this framework to
the shower histories of the interplay between collinear-safe quark and
gluon jets and renormalized jet substructure observables is an exciting
direction for future work. We find that the so-called gluon-initiated
JADE three-jet rate regulates infrared-safe shower histories at leading
color.
