\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on renormalon-free
beam functions of dijet production. The resulting Wasserstein distance
agrees with explainable training curves of B meson decays at the level
of the cross section. A natural next step is to sample the global
significance of discriminative lattice simulations after unfolding. We
leave the study of attention weights at leading power to future work.
Extending this framework to scheme-independent boosted decision trees is
an exciting direction for future work.
