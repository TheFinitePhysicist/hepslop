These results pave the way for string theory at fixed coupling. In
contrast to effective theories, the generalized unitarity approach is
well defined by construction. In future work, we plan to apply the jet
shape to deep inelastic scattering. & The method applies equally to
polylogarithms and few-shot attention maps of trijet production.
