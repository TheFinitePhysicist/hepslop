\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into differentiable
chatbots. Our analysis neglects factorized energy-energy correlations,
which may become relevant at the Electron-Ion Collider. These results
pave the way for event generation in the large-$N_c$ limit. Our analysis
neglects color-ordered calorimeter showers, which may become relevant at
the Tevatron.
