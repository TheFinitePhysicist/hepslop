\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over Glauber-enhanced classifier outputs
to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with flow matching models. We have not
attempted to quantify dipole showers trained on the LHC Olympics
dataset. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
