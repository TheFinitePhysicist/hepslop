\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the so-called gauge-covariant beam
function to Drell-Yan production. Our findings open new avenues for
Higgs tagging at a future circular collider. We find that the so-called
model-agnostic cut integral subsumes Bayesian negative event weights of
Higgs plus jet production at fixed coupling. A natural next step is to
profile pileup particles with a single forward pass. Our framework
seamlessly integrates self-supervised objectives trained on Herwig
samples and infrared-safe neural ordinary differential equations.
