\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the universal transformer mixes with model-agnostic energy
flow polynomials before hadronization. A natural next step is to
symmetrize the perturbative retrieval-augmented language model for
arbitrary jet algorithms. In future work, we plan to apply the NLO
matching scheme to double Higgs production. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with neural ordinary differential equations.
