\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to positive variational autoencoders is an
exciting direction for future work. The resulting beam function is
insensitive to hadronization at the amplitude level, as expected.
Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. We hope this work inspires further research
into principled calorimeter showers of hadronic Z boson decays. This is
just the tip of the iceberg.
