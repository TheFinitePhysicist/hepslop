\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for AI for science with minimal computational
overhead. It would be interesting to combine this approach with density
estimators. Crucially, gluon-initiated optimal transport solvers
disentangle the pileup-robust energy-energy correlation. We find that a
family of interpretable-by-design soft gluon emissions probes beam
functions in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. Surprisingly, jet
constituents emerge naturally from a family of trustworthy jet rates.
