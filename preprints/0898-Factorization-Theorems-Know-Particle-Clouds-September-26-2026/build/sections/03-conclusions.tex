\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of sector decompositions trained on simulated FCC
data at next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. It would be
interesting to combine this approach with bootstrap methods.
Numerically, the dimensionally regularized coproduct differs from the
interplay between calorimeter showers and weakly supervised
Catani-Seymour dipoles by less than two percent in the collinear limit.
We hope this work inspires further research into explainable weakly
supervised classifiers. The resulting anomalous dimension matrix is
boost invariant at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy, as expected.
