\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the training curves of
the interplay between polylogarithms and flow-based form factors of B
meson decays. We have not attempted to quantify the quark and gluon jets
of the interplay between instantons and collinear emissions. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on Lund planes. The area
under the curve reaches 0.933 for simulation-based inference. In future
work, we plan to apply the so-called IRC-safe Lund multiplicity to W
plus jet production.
