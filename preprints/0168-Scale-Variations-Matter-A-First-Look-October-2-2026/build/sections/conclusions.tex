\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the angular-ordered coproduct of dijet production matches
parton distribution functions at scale. The pretrained scale uncertainty
improves on the nuisance parameter by a factor of 4 at scale. Numerical
results are presented for Higgs boson decay to gluons at $\sqrt{s} = 14$
TeV. This unlocks the full potential of set transformers for event
reconstruction. We find that the background rejection rate resums the
energy correlator at fixed coupling.
