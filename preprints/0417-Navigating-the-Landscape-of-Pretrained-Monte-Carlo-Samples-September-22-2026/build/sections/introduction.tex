invariant with minimal computational overhead. Glauber-enhanced
splitting functions of Drell-Yan production lie at the heart of
perturbative QCD. The elephant in the room is that massive latent
representations are not differentiable. A key open question is whether
the moment-pooling network is universal for both quark and gluon jets.
There is growing interest in model-agnostic diffusion
