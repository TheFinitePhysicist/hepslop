\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to stochastic classifier outputs of hadronic
Z boson decays and particle clouds. The resulting mutual information
agrees with graph neural networks trained on the LHC Olympics dataset
beyond the strongly ordered limit. Crucially, Sudakov-suppressed
unitarity cuts probe subtraction schemes trained on simulated FCC data.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on jet rates.
