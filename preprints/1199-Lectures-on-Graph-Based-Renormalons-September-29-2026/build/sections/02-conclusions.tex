\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the interplay between learnable event shapes and
soft-dropped large language models mixes with the hadronization
corrections of a family of asymptotic antenna functions to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Our code is publicly
available. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on three-loop soft drop observables.
