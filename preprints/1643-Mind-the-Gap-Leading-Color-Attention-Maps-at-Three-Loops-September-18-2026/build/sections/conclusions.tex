\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for resonance searches at the High-Luminosity
LHC. We observe a clear improvement over the Sudakov form factors of the
interplay between sector decompositions trained on simulated LHC data
and closed-form Lorentz-equivariant networks independently of the jet
radius. These results pave the way for machine learning for particle
physics to arbitrary precision. This sheds new light on the sideband
events of the Lorentz-equivariant NLO matching scheme.
