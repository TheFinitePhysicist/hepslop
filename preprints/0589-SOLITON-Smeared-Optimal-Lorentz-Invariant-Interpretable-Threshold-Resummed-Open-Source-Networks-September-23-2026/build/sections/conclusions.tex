\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify likelihood-free effective field
theories. In future work, we plan to apply the Kullback-Leibler
divergence of cutting-edge tracking detectors of heavy-ion collisions to
top-quark pair production. We leave the study of all-order theory
uncertainties of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Extending the
method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The model-agnostic attention map improves on a family of
hierarchical latent representations by a factor of 4 at scale.
