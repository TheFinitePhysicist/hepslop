\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results are in excellent agreement with data-driven differential
cross sections of hadronic Z boson
decays~\cite{TPC:0818-The-Many-Faces-of-the-Eikonal-Factor-September-23-2026}.
Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. A natural next step is to
reweight the principled simulation-based inference pipeline with
unprecedented accuracy.
