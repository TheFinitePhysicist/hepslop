\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for parameter estimation at RHIC. Our
results are in excellent agreement with non-global cut integrals of
hadronic Z boson
decays~\cite{TPC:2179-CASCADE-Conformal-All-Order-Sudakov-Safe-Color-Ordered-Asymptotic-Data-Driven-Embeddings-September-18-2026}.
Our analysis neglects the polylogarithms of the projected energy
correlator, which may become relevant at the Future Circular Collider.
