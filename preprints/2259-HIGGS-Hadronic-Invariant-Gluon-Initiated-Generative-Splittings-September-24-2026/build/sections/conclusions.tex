\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the transformer-based coproduct differs from the
discriminative dispersive approach by less than one percent up to power
corrections. We hope this work inspires further research into pretrained
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. Our findings open new avenues for event
reconstruction at the Future Circular Collider. This unlocks the full
potential of flow matching models for jet calibration.
