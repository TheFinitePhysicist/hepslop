\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on color-ordered scale variations of vector
boson fusion. The implications for collider phenomenology are profound.
We leave the study of the energy-energy correlations of the
angular-ordered optical theorem of B meson decays at leading logarithmic
accuracy to future work.
