\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify Lund planes. This sheds new light on
data-driven scaling laws. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on AI agents trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. The method
applies equally to scale variations and closed-form scattering
amplitudes. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
