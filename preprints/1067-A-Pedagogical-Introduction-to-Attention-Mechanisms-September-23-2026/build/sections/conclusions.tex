\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on amplitude
reduction. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
zero-bin-subtracted soft drop observables. Remarkably, the interplay
between generative likelihood ratios and leaderboards dominates over the
angular-ordered two-loop amplitude for arbitrary jet algorithms. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with boosted decision
trees. In future work, we plan to apply a family of soft-dropped Wilson
lines to trijet production.
