\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to all-order parton showers. We leave the
study of uncertainty-aware point cloud transformers at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on pileup particles. We discuss
implications for weakly supervised classification at the High-Luminosity
LHC. Numerical results are presented for diphoton production at
$\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV.
