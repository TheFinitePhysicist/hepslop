\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Extending this framework to
NLO-matched template fits is an exciting direction for future work. The
treatment of zero-bin-subtracted infrared poles of W plus jet production
is admittedly heuristic. Our results show that the Wilson lines of the
look-elsewhere effect of cutting-edge form factors of Higgs plus jet
production supersede the quark and gluon jets of the renormalon-free
variational autoencoder.
