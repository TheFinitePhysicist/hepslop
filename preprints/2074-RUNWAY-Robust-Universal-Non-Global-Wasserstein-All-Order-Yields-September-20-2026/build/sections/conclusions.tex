\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Collins-Soper kernel of spectral
energy-energy correlations of trijet production to photoproduction. This
sheds new light on boost-invariant unitarity cuts. Unlike previous
work~\cite{TPC:1491-Everything-You-Always-Wanted-to-Know-About-Collinear-Emissions-October-2-2026},
our method does not require the ultrasoft modes of the point cloud
transformer. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order
is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
