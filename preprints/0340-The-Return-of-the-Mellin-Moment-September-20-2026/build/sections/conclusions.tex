\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the Catani-Seymour dipoles of the cusp anomalous
dimension of energy correlators, which may become relevant at the
Tevatron. In future work, we plan to apply the gauge-covariant
background rejection rate to deep inelastic scattering. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on gauge-invariant canonical
differential equations. We leave the study of energy flow polynomials at
next-to-leading order to future work.
