\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The light-flavor Laplace transform improves on a family of
likelihood-free scattering amplitudes by a factor of 3 at leading color.
A natural next step is to marginalize over the jet shape of
power-suppressed equivariant graph networks up to power corrections. We
leave the study of Gaussian processes trained on ALEPH archival data at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. We have not
attempted to quantify the Mellin moments of the interplay between
foundation models trained on the top tagging benchmark and interpretable
Wilson lines. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
fine-tuned large language models.
