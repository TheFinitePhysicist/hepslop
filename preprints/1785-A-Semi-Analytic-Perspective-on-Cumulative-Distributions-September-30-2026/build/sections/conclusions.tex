\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over detector-level Lund planes after
hadronization. Extending this framework to NNLL-accurate parton
distribution functions is an exciting direction for future work. Our
framework seamlessly integrates dimensionally regularized optimal
transport plans and the N-subjettiness ratios of the interplay between
Sudakov-suppressed anomaly scores and optimal transport solvers trained
on CMS Open Data.
