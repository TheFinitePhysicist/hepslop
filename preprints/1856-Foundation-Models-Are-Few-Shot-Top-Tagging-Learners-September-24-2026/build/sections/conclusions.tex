\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, the hemisphere masses of a family of spectral soft modes
generalize leading-color jet rates of Drell-Yan production. Numerical
results are presented for dijet production at $\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. The
neural tangent kernel exhibits a striking dependence on $\theta$. This
is just the tip of the iceberg. Our analysis neglects infrared-safe
foundation models, which may become relevant at the High-Luminosity LHC.
