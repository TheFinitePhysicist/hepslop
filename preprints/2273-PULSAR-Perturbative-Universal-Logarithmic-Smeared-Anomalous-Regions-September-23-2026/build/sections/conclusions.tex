\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to generalized likelihood ratios is an exciting
direction for future work. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with neural ordinary differential equations. The method applies
equally to normalizing flows trained on ALEPH archival data and energy
flow polynomials. In future work, we plan to apply the eikonal factor to
Higgs boson decay to gluons. The area under the curve reaches 0.943 for
integral evaluation.
