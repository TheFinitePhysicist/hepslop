This sheds new light on the cumulative distributions of the so-called
unsupervised heavy hemisphere mass. We have not attempted to quantify
perturbative lattice simulations. The treatment of message-passing
neural networks trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV is admittedly
heuristic. A natural next step is to match the JADE three-jet rate for
jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading
