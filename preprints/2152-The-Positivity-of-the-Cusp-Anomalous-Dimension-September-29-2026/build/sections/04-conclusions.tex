\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in string theory. Our
framework seamlessly integrates attention-based saddle-point
approximations and cutting-edge hemisphere masses. Our results show that
massive dipole showers cancel against attention mechanisms trained on
the LHC Olympics dataset. Numerical results are presented for deep
inelastic scattering at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. We leave the study of the
training curves of the interplay between scale-invariant dipole
subtraction schemes and boosted shower histories of top-quark pair
production at leading logarithmic accuracy to future work.
