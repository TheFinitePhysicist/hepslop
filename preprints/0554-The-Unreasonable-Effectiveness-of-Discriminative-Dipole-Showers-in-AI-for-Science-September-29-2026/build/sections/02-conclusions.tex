\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for top-quark pair production at
$\sqrt{s} = 14$ TeV. We leave the study of embeddings at next-to-leading
power to future work. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on smeared antenna showers. In contrast to AI agents, the
so-called power-suppressed scale uncertainty is embarrassingly simple by
construction.
