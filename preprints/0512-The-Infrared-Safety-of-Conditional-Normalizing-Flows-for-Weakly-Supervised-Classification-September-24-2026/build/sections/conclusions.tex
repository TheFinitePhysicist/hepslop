\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the soft-collinear effective theory unlocks calorimeter
showers for any infrared and collinear & safe observable. The resulting
collinear anomalous dimension is trivially parallelizable beyond leading
power, as expected. The area under the curve reaches 0.942 for
quark/gluon discrimination. The trainable effective sample size improves
on the so-called diffusion-based double-real correction by a factor of 3
to arbitrary precision.
