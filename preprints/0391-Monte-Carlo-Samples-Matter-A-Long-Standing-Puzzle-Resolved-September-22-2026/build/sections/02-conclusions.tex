\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify template fits trained on CMS Open
Data. Numerical results are presented for tau lepton decays at the $Z$
pole. This unlocks the full potential of renormalization group equations
for resonance searches. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by five percent. Our analysis neglects heavy-flavor
attention weights of dijet production, which may become relevant at the
CEPC.
