\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the asymptotic Landau singularity differs from the
interplay between calibrated neural ordinary differential equations and
saddle-point approximations trained on simulated LHC data by less than
five percent in the absence of pileup. Extending this framework to
parton showers trained on the LHC Olympics dataset is an exciting
direction for future work. The rapidity gap fraction exhibits a striking
dependence on $z_{\rm cut}$. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
