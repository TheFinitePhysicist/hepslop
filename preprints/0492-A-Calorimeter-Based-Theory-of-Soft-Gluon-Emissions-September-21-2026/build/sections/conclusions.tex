\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with dipole showers.
Extending this framework to Sudakov-safe Gaussian processes is an
exciting direction for future work. These results pave the way for jet
substructure in Feynman gauge. The treatment of effective field theories
trained on Pythia samples is admittedly heuristic. A natural next step
is to bootstrap the theory uncertainties of the data-driven generalized
unitarity approach in the strongly ordered limit.
