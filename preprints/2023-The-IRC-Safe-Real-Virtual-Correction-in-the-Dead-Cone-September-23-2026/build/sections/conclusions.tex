\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the generative Lorentz-equivariant
network to Higgs plus jet production. Extending the method beyond two
loops is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our
analysis neglects effective theories trained on ALEPH archival data,
which may become relevant at the Tevatron. We hope this work inspires
further research into deterministic neural ordinary differential
equations. Our findings open new avenues for event generation at the
Future Circular Collider.
