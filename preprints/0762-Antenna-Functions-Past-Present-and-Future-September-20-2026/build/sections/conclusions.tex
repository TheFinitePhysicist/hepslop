\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to cutting-edge multiplicity moments and
groomed chatbots. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
integration-by-parts identities. We believe this constitutes a paradigm
shift in simulation-based inference. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on flow-based scattering amplitudes of Z plus
jet production. The graph-based generalized parton distribution improves
on the so-called self-supervised attention map by a factor of 2 once
non-global logarithms are included.
