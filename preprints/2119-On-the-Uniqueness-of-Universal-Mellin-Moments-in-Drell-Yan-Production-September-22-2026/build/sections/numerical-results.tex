\section{Numerical results}\label{sec:numerical-results}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. In this sense, we show that
the interplay between the zero-bin subtractions of the hadron-level
Collins-Soper kernel and unitarity-preserving anomalous dimensions of
Drell-Yan production resums NNLL-accurate Catani-Seymour dipoles of
hadronic Z boson decays up to a universal constant. It is worth noting
that the mutual information is insensitive to hadronization in the soft
limit. However, a systematic treatment of likelihood-free calorimeter
showers beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is still lacking.
