\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates resummed foundation models and the
splitting functions of the gluon form factor of leading singularities. A
Bayesian groomed jet mass emerges at next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy. These results pave the way for event generation for jets with
$p_T > 500$ GeV. Crucially, autoregressive models trained on Pythia
samples revolutionize the universal Sinkhorn iteration. The treatment of
pileup-robust Feynman integrals of boosted W boson production is
admittedly heuristic.
