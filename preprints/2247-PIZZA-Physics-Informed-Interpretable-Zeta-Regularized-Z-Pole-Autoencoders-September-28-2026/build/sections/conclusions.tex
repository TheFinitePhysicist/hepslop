\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with graph neural
networks. Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
approximate self-supervised objectives. The treatment of the form
factors of a family of eikonal heavy-flavor jets is admittedly
heuristic. Extending this framework to collinear emissions is an
exciting direction for future work.
