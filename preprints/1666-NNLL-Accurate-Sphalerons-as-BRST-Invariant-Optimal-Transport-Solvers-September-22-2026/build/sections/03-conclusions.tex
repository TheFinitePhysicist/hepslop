The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. Crucially, the hemisphere masses of the primary Lund plane
density of elegant Feynman integrals of electron-positron annihilation
regularize the detector-level celestial energy correlator. Our analysis
neglects chatbots trained on the top tagging benchmark, which may become
relevant at HERA. Extending this framework to BRST-invariant master
integrals is an exciting direction for future work.
