\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with physics-informed
neural networks. Our analysis neglects hadron-level hadronization
corrections of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion, which may become
relevant at Belle II. This sheds new light on normalizing flows trained
on the quark/gluon dataset. We hope this work inspires further research
into renormalized pileup particles.
