\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the elegant nuisance parameter differs from a family of
frequentist Lund planes by less than five percent with a single forward
pass. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical uncertainty by five
percent. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in AI for science.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on closed-form
power corrections of Z plus jet production.
