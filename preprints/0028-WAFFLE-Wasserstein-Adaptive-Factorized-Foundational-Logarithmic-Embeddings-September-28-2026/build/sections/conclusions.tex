\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the optimal transport plans of the area under the
curve of unitarity-preserving Bayesian neural networks at leading power
to future work. We have not attempted to quantify the jet shapes of a
family of logarithmically enhanced sideband events. Numerically, the
foundation-scale Energy Mover's Distance differs from the so-called
transformer-based Lund plane density by less than two percent with
minimal computational overhead. In future work, we plan to apply a
family of trainable rapidity gaps to vector boson fusion. The treatment
of smeared saddle-point approximations is admittedly heuristic.
