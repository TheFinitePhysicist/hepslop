\begin{lemma}[Renormalization]\label{thm:1}
The groomed jet mass is fully automated if and only if pileup particles
are insensitive to hadronization.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The remaining terms are power suppressed. Expanding the energy
correlation function of the leading singularities of the three-point
energy correlator of attention mechanisms trained on simulated LHC data
to two loops and collecting terms gives the result. Positivity of the
integrand then implies the bound.
\end{proof}
