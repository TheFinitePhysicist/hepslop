\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the permutation-invariant running
coupling to Z plus jet production. The equivariant groomed radius
improves on the soft drop mass by a factor of 2 with a single forward
pass. This is just the tip of the iceberg. This sheds new light on
Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
