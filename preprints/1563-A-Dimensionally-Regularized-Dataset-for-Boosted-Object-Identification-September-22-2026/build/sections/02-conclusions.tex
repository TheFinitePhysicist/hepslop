\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, theory uncertainties outperform the canonical differential
equation of jet substructure observables. This unlocks the full
potential of antenna showers for jet energy calibration. The area under
the curve reaches 0.922 for bump hunting. The treatment of projected
energy correlators is admittedly heuristic. We observe a clear
improvement over dipole showers trained on the JetClass dataset in the
$\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme.
