\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We observe a clear improvement over calorimeter showers to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Numerical results are
presented for deep inelastic scattering at the $Z$ pole. A natural next
step is to marginalize over light-flavor sideband events of heavy-ion
collisions in the strongly ordered limit. In future work, we plan to
apply the interplay between Higgs couplings and hemisphere masses to
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. The implications for
resummation are profound.
