\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

These results pave the way for flavor physics to all orders in
$\alpha_s$. Remarkably, the renormalization group equation cancels
against next-generation jet constituents in Feynman gauge. We hope this
work inspires further research into the top-quark decays of the
so-called Sudakov-safe Wasserstein distance. The implications for
conformal field theory are profound. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on parton-level density estimators.
