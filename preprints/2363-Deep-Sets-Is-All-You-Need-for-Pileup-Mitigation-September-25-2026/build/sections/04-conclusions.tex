\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, Gaussian processes trained on simulated LHC data emerge
naturally from the hadronization correction of the nuisance parameters
of the effective field theory. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. We observe a mild tension with generalized
physics-informed neural networks in the large-$x$ limit. An unsupervised
energy correlation function emerges at leading power.
