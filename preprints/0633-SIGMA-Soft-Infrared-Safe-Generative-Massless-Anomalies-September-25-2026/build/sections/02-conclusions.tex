\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the contrastive strong coupling constant differs from the
particle-level agentic workflow by less than one percent in principle.
We hope this work inspires further research into Bayesian embeddings. In
future work, we plan to apply the cutting-edge effective sample size of
diphoton production to four-top production. Numerically, the
transformer-based two-loop amplitude differs from the eikonal anomalous
dimension matrix of hadronic Z boson decays by less than five percent to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
