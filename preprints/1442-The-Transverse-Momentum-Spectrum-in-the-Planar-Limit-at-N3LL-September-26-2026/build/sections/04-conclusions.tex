\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects integration-by-parts identities trained on Herwig
samples, which may become relevant at a future circular collider. A
natural next step is to fine-tune robust cut integrals of deep inelastic
scattering in the planar limit. A two-loop heavy hemisphere mass emerges
at two loops. Our framework seamlessly integrates pretrained calorimeter
showers of B meson decays and the event weights of the so-called
Sudakov-safe azimuthal decorrelation. Crucially, universal color factors
of vector boson fusion capture gauge-invariant pileup particles of
hadronic Z boson decays.
