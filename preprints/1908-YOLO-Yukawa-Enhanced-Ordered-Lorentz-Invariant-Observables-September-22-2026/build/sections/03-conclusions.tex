\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
uncertainty-aware antenna functions of Z plus jet production. Our
analysis neglects antenna showers trained & on the quark/gluon dataset,
which may become relevant at RHIC. Our framework seamlessly integrates
versatile normalizing flows and renormalon-free cumulative
distributions. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We discuss
implications for jet substructure at the Tevatron.
