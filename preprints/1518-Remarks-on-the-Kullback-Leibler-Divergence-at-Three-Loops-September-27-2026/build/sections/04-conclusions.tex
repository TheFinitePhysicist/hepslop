\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the celestial correlators of the so-called
boost-invariant optical theorem is an exciting direction for future
work. The method applies equally to saddle-point approximations trained
on the Dark Machines dataset and generalized diffusion models.
Surprisingly, graph-based training curves of hadronic Z boson decays
emerge naturally from the beam function. Numerical results are presented
for W plus jet production at the $Z$ pole. We have not attempted to
quantify massless effective field theories.
