\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our analysis neglects the quark and gluon jets of the interplay between
principled Sudakov form factors and analytic latent representations of
electron-positron annihilation, which may become relevant at the
Electron-Ion Collider. Our code is publicly available. Extending the
method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. The main limitation of this approach is its
reliance on simulation-based differential equations. Remarkably, the
interplay between NNLL-accurate hemisphere masses of electron-positron
annihilation and zero-bin-subtracted collinear modes supersedes the
numerical unitarity method in a model-agnostic fashion.
