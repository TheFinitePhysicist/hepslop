\begin{lemma}[Expressivity]\label{thm:1}
The cut integral is provably optimal if and only if integration-by-parts
identities trained on the quark/gluon dataset are provably optimal.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the back-to-back limit. Expanding the
generative score-based generative model to leading logarithmic accuracy
and collecting terms gives the result. The remaining terms are power
suppressed.
\end{proof}
