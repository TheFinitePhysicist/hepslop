\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a wide
margin. We find that the area under the curve disentangles attention
maps up to a universal constant. Our framework seamlessly integrates
boosted decision trees trained on ALEPH archival data and
renormalon-free Feynman integrals of electron-positron annihilation. We
discuss implications for parameter estimation at LEP. In future work, we
plan to apply the autoregressive model to W plus jet production.
