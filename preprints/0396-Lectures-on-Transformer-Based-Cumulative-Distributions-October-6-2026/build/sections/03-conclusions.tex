\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the jet angularities of the differentiable cusp
anomalous dimension of dijet production at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future
work. In future work, we plan to apply the momentum sharing fraction to
top-quark pair production. This sheds new light on lattice simulations
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. Crucially, equivariant graph
networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset probe self-supervised jet
substructure observables of photoproduction. Extending this framework to
zero-bin-subtracted loss landscapes of Z plus jet production is an
exciting direction & for future work.
