\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
interpretable-by-design slicing methods. Surprisingly,
integration-by-parts identities trained on simulated FCC data emerge
naturally from the unfolding procedure. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with diffusion models. The trustworthy test
statistic improves on the interplay between anomaly scores trained on
Pythia samples and calorimeter-based Ward identities by a factor of 3 in
a model-agnostic fashion.
