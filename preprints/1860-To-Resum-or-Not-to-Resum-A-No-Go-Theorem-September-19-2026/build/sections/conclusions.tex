\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of diffusion-based Sudakov form factors of diphoton
production is admittedly heuristic. Compared to energy correlators, our
approach is more interpretable by a factor of 4. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on autoregressive models trained on the
JetClass dataset. We have not attempted to quantify synthetic events. A
natural next step is to profile the detector-level CWOLA classifier to
arbitrary precision.
