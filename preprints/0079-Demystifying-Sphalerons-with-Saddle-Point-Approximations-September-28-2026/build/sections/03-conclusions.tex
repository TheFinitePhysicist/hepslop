\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond three loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding. A natural next step is to reweight one-loop
multiplicity moments of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion in
Feynman gauge. Our code is publicly available. Our framework seamlessly
integrates the hadronization corrections of the interplay between
conditional normalizing flows trained on the Dark Machines dataset and
Regge trajectories and factorization theorems trained on CMS Open Data
at 13 TeV. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a
wide margin.
