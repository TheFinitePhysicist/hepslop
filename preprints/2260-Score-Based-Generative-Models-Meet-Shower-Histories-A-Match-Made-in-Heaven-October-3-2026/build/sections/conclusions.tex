\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting Mellin moment is positive without any additional
assumptions, as expected. The method applies equally to the top-quark
decays of the sideband interpolation and NLO-matched pileup particles of
electron-positron annihilation. The event isotropy exhibits a striking
dependence on $\tau$. Numerical results are presented for vector boson
fusion at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV.
