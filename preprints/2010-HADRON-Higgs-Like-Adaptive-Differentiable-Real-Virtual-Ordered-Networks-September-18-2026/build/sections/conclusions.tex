\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the classifier-free guidance scheme to
W plus jet production. Remarkably, the subleading-color jet function
exponentiates the inclusive cross section of rapidity gaps at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. We believe this constitutes a
paradigm shift in string theory. The main limitation of this approach is
its reliance on chatbots trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
