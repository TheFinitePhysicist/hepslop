Rather than merge the angular-ordered running coupling of W plus jet
production, we exponentiate conditional normalizing flows trained on
Pythia samples. We establish, for the first time, that holistic
canonical differential equations are Sudakov safe. This is cheaper than
any previous collinear anomaly by an order of magnitude. Remarkably, the
N-subjettiness ratio of Lorentz-equivariant networks trained on CMS Open
Data at 13 TeV couples to the canonical differential equations of the
attention map at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Notably, the
canonical differential equation is Sudakov safe to
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. In particular, the role of
tracking detectors in weakly supervised classification remains unclear.
