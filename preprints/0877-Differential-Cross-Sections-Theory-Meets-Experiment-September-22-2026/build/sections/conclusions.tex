\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for integral evaluation at Belle II. Extending
this framework to splitting functions is an exciting direction for
future work. We hope this work inspires further research into
subtraction schemes trained on Herwig samples. The method applies
equally to parton-level Higgs couplings of trijet production and
unsupervised jet substructure observables of hadronic Z boson decays.
