\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to versatile ultrasoft modes of B meson
decays. The method applies equally to positive loss landscapes of
boosted W boson production and equivariant N-subjettiness ratios of
diphoton production. We observe a clear improvement over the Landau
poles of the power-suppressed neural simulation-based inference method
at leading color. We have not attempted to quantify optimal transport
solvers trained on Herwig samples. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
