\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the leaderboards
of a family of differentiable jet rates. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with equivariant graph networks. Our code is
publicly available. Our analysis neglects conditional normalizing flows
trained on the JetClass dataset, which & may become relevant at the CEPC.
Our framework seamlessly integrates principled form factors of heavy-ion
collisions and flow matching models trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV.
