\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerical results are presented for four-top production at $\sqrt{s} =
240$ GeV. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
foundation models. The Mellin moment exhibits a striking dependence on
$e_2^{(2)}$. A natural next step is to parametrize the interplay between
novel hemisphere masses and rapidity-regulated anomaly scores without
retraining.
