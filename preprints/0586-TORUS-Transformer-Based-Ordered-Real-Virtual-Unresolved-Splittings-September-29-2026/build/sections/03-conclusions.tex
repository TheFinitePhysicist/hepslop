\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to revisit analytic Bayesian neural networks for
jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. In future work, we plan to apply the
closed-form phase-space slicing method to semi-inclusive deep inelastic
scattering. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We have not
attempted to quantify soft drop masses. Our code is publicly available.
