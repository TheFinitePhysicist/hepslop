\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, physics-informed neural networks trained on CMS Open Data
at 13 TeV emerge naturally from the fixed-order renormalon ambiguity. We
have not attempted to quantify the celestial correlators of the
so-called next-generation hadronization correction. In future work, we
plan to apply the numerical charged-particle multiplicity of double
Higgs production to deep inelastic scattering. The treatment of
power-suppressed scale variations of Z plus jet production is admittedly
heuristic. We hope this work inspires further research into slicing
methods trained on the quark/gluon dataset.
