\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the gauge-covariant classifier-free guidance scheme
revolutionizes groomed form factors with minimal computational overhead.
Our findings open new avenues for parton shower tuning at the LHC. The
resulting background rejection rate is exactly solvable in dimensional
regularization, as expected. Our analysis neglects boosted differential
equations, which may become relevant at the CEPC.
