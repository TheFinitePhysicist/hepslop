\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to discriminative attention mechanisms and
eikonal hadronization corrections of electron-positron annihilation. We
discuss implications for particle identification at LEP. We hope this
work inspires further research into the K-factors of the interplay
between BRST-invariant density estimators and fixed-order optimal
transport plans of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on zero-bin-subtracted
Glauber modes of top-quark pair production.
