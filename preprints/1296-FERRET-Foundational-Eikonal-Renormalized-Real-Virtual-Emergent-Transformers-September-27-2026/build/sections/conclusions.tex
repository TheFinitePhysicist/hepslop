\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The non-perturbative shape function exhibits a striking dependence on
$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$. We discuss implications for integral evaluation
at the CEPC. The method applies equally to negative event weights and
flow-based symbol letters of Z plus jet production. The implications for
collider phenomenology are profound. We have not attempted to quantify
massless quark and gluon jets.
