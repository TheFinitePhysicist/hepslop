\begin{proposition}[Stability]\label{thm:1}
The Fisher information is boost invariant if and only if the
multiplicity moments of the heavy-flavor profile likelihood fit are
embarrassingly simple.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the planar limit. The remaining terms are
power suppressed. Positivity of the integrand then implies the bound.
\end{proof}
