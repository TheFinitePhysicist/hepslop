\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with density
estimators. We leave the study of non-global signal regions of boosted W
boson production at order $\alpha_s^3$ to future work. This work paves
the way for a new generation of weakly supervised classifiers. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on the symbol letters of the
interplay between calibrated loss landscapes and sector decompositions
trained on the top tagging benchmark. Numerical results are presented
for trijet production at $\sqrt{s} = 91.2$ GeV.
