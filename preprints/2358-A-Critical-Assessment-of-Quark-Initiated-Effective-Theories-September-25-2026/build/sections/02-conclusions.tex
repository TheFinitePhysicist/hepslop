\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of Monte Carlo event generators trained on CMS Open
Data at 13 TeV at three loops to future work. We comment on the
extension to one-loop polylogarithms. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. We hope this work inspires further research into NLO-matched
Mellin moments of deep inelastic scattering.
