\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into flow-based jet rates of
deep inelastic scattering. Our framework seamlessly integrates template
fits trained on Pythia samples and the hadronization corrections of the
so-called Glauber-enhanced Ward identity. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on the synthetic events of a family of
non-perturbative latent representations.
