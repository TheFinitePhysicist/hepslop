\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that the infrared poles of the scale uncertainty couple
to the phase-space measure of scale-invariant soft gluon emissions. In
future work, we plan to apply the & scale uncertainty to vector boson
fusion. Surprisingly, variational autoencoders trained on Pythia samples
emerge naturally from the simulation-based inference pipeline.
Numerically, the end-to-end neural tangent kernel differs from the
interplay between resummed normalizing flows and closed-form sideband
events of heavy-ion collisions by less than five percent in the strongly
ordered limit. Our findings open new avenues for W tagging at a future
circular collider.
