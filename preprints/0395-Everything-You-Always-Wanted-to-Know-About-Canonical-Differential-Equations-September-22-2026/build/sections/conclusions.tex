\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in lattice QCD. This is
just the tip of the iceberg. Our framework seamlessly integrates Monte
Carlo event generators trained on the LHC Olympics dataset and scaling
laws trained on Herwig samples. The proposed method outperforms all
existing approaches by a wide margin. The treatment of deterministic
saddle-point approximations is admittedly heuristic.
