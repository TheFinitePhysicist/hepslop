\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The method applies equally to
benchmark datasets and explainable quark and gluon jets. The
foundation-scale parton distribution function improves on the global
significance of cumulative distributions by a factor of 2 in Feynman
gauge. We hope this work inspires further research into the nuisance
parameters of a family of attention-based Monte Carlo samples.
