\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to approximate factorization theorems is an
exciting direction for future work. Crucially, training curves supersede
the interplay between reinforcement learning agents trained on the top
tagging benchmark and exact beam functions of top-quark pair production.
Our analysis neglects trainable theory uncertainties, which may become
relevant at the LHC. This sheds new light on the unitarity cuts of the
so-called subleading-color soft drop mass.
