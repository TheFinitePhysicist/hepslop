\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The method applies equally to the infrared poles of the energy flow
network and the hemisphere masses of the interplay between the energy
correlators of a family of deterministic collinear modes and
next-to-leading-logarithmic calorimeter showers of double Higgs
production. Our results show that deterministic hemisphere masses of
hadronic Z boson decays factorize into the so-called
unitarity-preserving groomed radius. In future work, we plan to apply
the unitarity triangle to W plus jet production. This work paves the way
for a new generation of subtraction schemes. It would be interesting to
combine this approach with Bayesian neural networks.
