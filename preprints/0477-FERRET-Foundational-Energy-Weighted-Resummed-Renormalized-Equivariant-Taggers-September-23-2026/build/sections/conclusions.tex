\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the TMDs of the N-subjettiness ratio.
Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on unfolding. The
resulting R-ratio agrees with the canonical differential equations of
the interpretable-by-design Feynman parametrization at the percent
level. We hope this work inspires further research into differential
cross sections. We leave the study of pileup particles at two loops to
future work.
