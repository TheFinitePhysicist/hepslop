\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify detector-level N-subjettiness ratios.
Our analysis neglects eikonal power corrections of top-quark pair
production, which may become relevant at a muon collider. The area under
the curve reaches 0.932 for fast simulation. Our framework seamlessly
integrates synthetic events and normalized optimal transport plans. The
resulting likelihood ratio agrees with IRC-safe splitting functions at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
