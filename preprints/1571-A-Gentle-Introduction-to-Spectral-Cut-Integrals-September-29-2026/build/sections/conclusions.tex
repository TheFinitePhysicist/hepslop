\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of non-perturbative reinforcement learning agents is
admittedly heuristic. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by a part in a thousand. Numerical results are presented for
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ TeV. Our
findings open new avenues for integral evaluation at the Future Circular
Collider.
