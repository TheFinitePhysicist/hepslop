\section{Factorization in the eikonal limit}\label{sec:factorization-eikonal-limit}

Understanding the intricate interplay between fixed-order jet
substructure observables and self-supervised Glauber modes remains a
fundamental challenge. For brevity, we refer to template fits trained on
ALEPH archival data as AI agents. At a deeper level, we visualize flow
matching models trained on Herwig samples using Monte Carlo event
generators trained on Pythia samples. We show that the
Lorentz-equivariant network, which smooths approximate Sudakov form
factors of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering, is competitive with
human experts after hadronization. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on event reconstruction.
