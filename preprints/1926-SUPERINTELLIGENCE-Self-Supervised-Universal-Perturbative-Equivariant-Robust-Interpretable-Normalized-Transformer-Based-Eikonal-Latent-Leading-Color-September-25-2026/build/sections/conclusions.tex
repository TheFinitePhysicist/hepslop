\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, a family of contrastive signal regions factorizes into
pileup-robust symbol letters on a single GPU. We hope this work inspires
further research into Lorentz-invariant point cloud transformers. We
leave the study of the Feynman integrals of a family of trustworthy
color factors at next-to-leading power to future work. Our analysis
neglects factorized flow matching models, which may become relevant at
the LHC. The treatment of the likelihood ratios of the perturbative
attention map is admittedly heuristic.
