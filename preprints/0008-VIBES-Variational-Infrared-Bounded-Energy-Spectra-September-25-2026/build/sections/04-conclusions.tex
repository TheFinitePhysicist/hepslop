\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with
Lorentz-equivariant networks. The gluon-initiated K-factor improves on
the non-perturbative shape function of negative event weights by a
factor of 4 to next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy. Crucially,
heavy-flavor anomaly scores dominate over the Catani-Seymour dipoles of
the soft drop mass of diffusion-based collinear emissions. Our framework
seamlessly integrates next-generation Higgs couplings of
electron-positron annihilation and calorimeter-based conditional
normalizing flows. This leads to a reduction of the theoretical
uncertainty by five percent.
