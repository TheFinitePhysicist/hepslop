\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with variational
autoencoders. Our framework seamlessly integrates gauge-invariant
boosted decision trees and graph neural networks trained on CMS Open
Data. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on Higgs
tagging. We leave the study of trustworthy N-subjettiness ratios of
heavy-ion collisions at next-to-leading power to future work.
