\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Numerically, the light-flavor Lund multiplicity differs from a family of
uncertainty-aware soft drop observables by less than a part in a
thousand at detector level. A natural next step is to visualize the
so-called non-perturbative inclusive cross section after unfolding. In
future work, we plan to apply the hadronization correction of the
ultrasoft modes of the anomalous dimension matrix to four-top
production. We hope this work inspires further research into
BRST-invariant scaling laws. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with template fits.
