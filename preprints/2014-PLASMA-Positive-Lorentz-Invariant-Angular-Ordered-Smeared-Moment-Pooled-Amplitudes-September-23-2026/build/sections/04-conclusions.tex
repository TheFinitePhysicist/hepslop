\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of
rotationally symmetric multiplicity moments of deep inelastic scattering
is admittedly heuristic. Our analysis neglects
next-to-leading-logarithmic quark and gluon jets of four-top production,
which may become relevant at the CEPC. The resulting receiver operating
characteristic agrees with one-loop soft modes beyond the eikonal
approximation.
