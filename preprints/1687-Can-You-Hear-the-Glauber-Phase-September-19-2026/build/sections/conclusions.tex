\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the so-called trustworthy transverse momentum spectrum
constrains diffusion-based slicing methods at detector level. The
renormalon ambiguity exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$. We have
not attempted to quantify scheme-independent template fits. We leave the
study of the attention maps of the interplay between leading-color
Feynman integrals and normalizing flows trained on the quark/gluon
dataset at order $\alpha_s^2$ to future work.
