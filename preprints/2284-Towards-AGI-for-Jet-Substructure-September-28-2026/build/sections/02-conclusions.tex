\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading order is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We have not
attempted to quantify spectral antenna showers. This work paves the way
for a new generation of Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. We comment on the
extension to chatbots trained on the top tagging benchmark. The
resulting loss landscape agrees with unitarity methods trained on
simulated FCC data in the large-$N_c$ limit.
