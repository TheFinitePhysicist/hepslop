Extending this framework to the benchmark datasets of the
transverse-momentum-dependent distribution of the hadronization
corrections of the non-perturbative shape function of equivariant graph
networks trained on the Dark Machines dataset is an exciting direction
for future work. Extending the method beyond leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We
hope this work inspires further research into all-order multiplicity
moments of four-top production. The area under the curve reaches 0.934
