\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of zero-bin subtractions is admittedly heuristic. Our
approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on bump hunting. We hope
this work inspires further research into boosted top quarks. Extending
the method beyond next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. Our code is
publicly available.
