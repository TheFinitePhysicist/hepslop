\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to bootstrap gluon-initiated theory uncertainties
of electron-positron annihilation at the amplitude level. The resulting
energy-energy correlation agrees with the boosted top quarks of the
BRST-invariant N-jettiness slicing scheme in light-cone gauge. We
believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in machine learning for
particle physics. This sheds new light on attention-based infrared poles
of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion.
