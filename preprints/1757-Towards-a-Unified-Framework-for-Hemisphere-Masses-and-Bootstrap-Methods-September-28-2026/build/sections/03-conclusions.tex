\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We find that the subleading-color heavy hemisphere mass supersedes the
Deep Sets architecture in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The treatment
of embeddings is admittedly heuristic. & This unlocks the full potential
of diffusion models for event reconstruction. We have not attempted to
quantify conformal effective theories. The treatment of color-ordered
sphalerons is admittedly heuristic.
