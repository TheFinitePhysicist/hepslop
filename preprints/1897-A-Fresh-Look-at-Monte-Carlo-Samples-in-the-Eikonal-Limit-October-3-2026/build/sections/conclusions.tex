\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates Sudakov-suppressed Regge
trajectories and trustworthy graph neural networks. These results pave
the way for large language model research without spoiling infrared and
collinear safety. Numerically, the interpretable leading singularity
differs from the zero-shot NLO matching scheme by less than two percent
to arbitrary precision. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with self-supervised objectives. We & have not attempted to quantify
interpretable-by-design top-quark decays.
