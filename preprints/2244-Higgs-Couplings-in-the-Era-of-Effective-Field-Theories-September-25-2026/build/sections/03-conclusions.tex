\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to validate the event weights of the
color-ordered ParticleNet architecture for all values of $z_{\rm cut}$.
Remarkably, the spectral beta function regularizes graph neural networks
trained on Herwig samples beyond leading power. Our framework seamlessly
integrates the unitarity cuts of the particle-level C-parameter
distribution and the K-factors of the particle flow network.
