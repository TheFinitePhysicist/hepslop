\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify leading-color rapidity divergences of
semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. We hope this work inspires
further research into power-suppressed ultrasoft modes of deep inelastic
scattering. In future work, we plan to apply the graph neural network to
electron-positron annihilation. This work paves the way for a new
generation of Monte Carlo event generators.
