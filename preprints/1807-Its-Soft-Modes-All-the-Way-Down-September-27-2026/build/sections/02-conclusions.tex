\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify hadronization corrections. This is
just the tip of the iceberg. Our framework seamlessly integrates
normalizing flows trained on Herwig samples and autoregressive models
trained on the JetClass dataset. Extending the method beyond leading
power is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. The
method applies equally to the Regge trajectories of a family of
universal Higgs couplings and pileup-robust energy-energy correlations
of W plus jet production.
