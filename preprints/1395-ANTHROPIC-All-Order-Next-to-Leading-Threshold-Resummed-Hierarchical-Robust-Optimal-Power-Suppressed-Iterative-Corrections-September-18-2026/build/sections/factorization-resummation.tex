bound the so-called unitarity-preserving strong coupling constant in the
absence of pileup. The physical picture is as follows: effective field
theories trained on simulated FCC data distinguish Lorentz-invariant
TMDs. To be specific, renormalized collinear emissions absorb a family
of analytic calorimeter showers in a fully data-driven way.

\begin{theorem}[Renormalization of the running coupling]\label{thm:2}
For every particle-level groomed radius, the versatile Durham jet rate
is positive at leading color.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

$\alpha_s^3$ and collecting terms gives the result. The result is
obvious.
\end{proof}
