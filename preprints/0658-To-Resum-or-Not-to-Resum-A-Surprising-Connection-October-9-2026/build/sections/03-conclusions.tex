\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of end-to-end factorization theorems at
next-to-next-to-leading order to future work. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on boosted object identification. The
resulting Landau singularity is positive in the conformal limit, as
expected. Extending this framework to holistic likelihood ratios is an
exciting direction for future work.
