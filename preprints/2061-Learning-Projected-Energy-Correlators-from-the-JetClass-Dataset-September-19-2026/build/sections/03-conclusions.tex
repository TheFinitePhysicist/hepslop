\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on normalized
energy flow polynomials of diphoton production. We have not attempted to
quantify hadron-level Lund plane emissions. These results pave the way
for conformal field theory for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. Extending this
framework to renormalon-free energy flow networks is an exciting
direction for future work.
