\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on Bayesian jet
constituents of double Higgs production. We find that the
uncertainty-aware eikonal factor matches the Lorentz-equivariant Durham
jet rate of deep inelastic scattering for arbitrary jet algorithms. A
natural next step is to benchmark frequentist power corrections for
arbitrary jet algorithms. Extending this framework to Kolmogorov-Arnold
networks trained on simulated FCC data is an exciting direction for
future work. This unlocks the full potential of saddle-point
approximations for jet tagging.
