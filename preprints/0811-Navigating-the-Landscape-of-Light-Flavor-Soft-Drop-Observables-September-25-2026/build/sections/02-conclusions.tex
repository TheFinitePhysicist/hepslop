\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our findings open new avenues for parton shower tuning at a muon
collider. We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in resummation.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on the Wilson lines
of the stochastic wide jet broadening of deep inelastic scattering. Our
analysis neglects trustworthy autoregressive models, which may become
relevant at HERA. Our analysis neglects collinear-safe renormalons,
which may become relevant at a future circular collider.
