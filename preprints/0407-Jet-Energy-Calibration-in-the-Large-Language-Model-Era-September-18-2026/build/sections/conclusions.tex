\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. The treatment of Lund plane
emissions is admittedly heuristic. We leave the study of boosted
decision trees trained on Herwig samples at next-to-next-to-leading
order to future work. The area under the curve reaches 0.922 for top
tagging. We have not attempted to quantify semi-analytic heavy-flavor
jets of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering.
