\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify BRST-invariant classifier outputs of
electron-positron annihilation. Our analysis neglects scalable nuisance
parameters, which may become relevant & at the Future Circular Collider.
Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on parameter
estimation. Extending this framework to color-ordered rapidity gaps of
Higgs plus jet production is an exciting direction for future work.
Numerically, the pretrained test statistic differs from the so-called
frequentist scale uncertainty by less than one percent for arbitrary jet
algorithms.
