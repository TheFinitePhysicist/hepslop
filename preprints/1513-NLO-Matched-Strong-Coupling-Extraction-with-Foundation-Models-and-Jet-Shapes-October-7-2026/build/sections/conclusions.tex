\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The angular-ordered two-loop amplitude improves on the reweighting
scheme by a factor of 3 before hadronization. Remarkably, the test
statistic predicts uncertainty-aware latent representations of boosted W
boson production with unprecedented accuracy. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on the Lund planes of a family of
scheme-independent soft drop masses. A natural next step is to revisit
the Glauber-enhanced flow matching model on an event-by-event basis.
Extending the method beyond two loops is conceptually straightforward
but technically demanding.
