\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to equivariant ultrasoft modes of Z plus jet
production is an exciting direction for future work. We have not
attempted to quantify beam functions. The treatment of asymptotic
factorization theorems is admittedly heuristic. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on versatile sector decompositions. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with attention mechanisms.
