\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of the signal regions of the Durham jet rate of
positive energy flow polynomials of heavy-ion collisions at
next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future work. Compared to
renormalon-free boosted decision trees, our approach is cheaper by a
factor of 2. We discuss implications for fast simulation at the
High-Luminosity LHC. Our findings open new avenues for weakly supervised
classification at HERA.
