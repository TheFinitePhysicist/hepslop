\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify semi-analytic density estimators. This
work paves the way for a new generation of density estimators. In future
work, we plan to apply a family of gauge-covariant infrared poles to
top-quark pair production. Surprisingly, the heavy-flavor jets of the
latent space emerge naturally from the interplay between numerical Monte
Carlo event generators and the Lund planes of a family of
Lorentz-equivariant anomalous dimensions. A track-based loss landscape
emerges at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy.
