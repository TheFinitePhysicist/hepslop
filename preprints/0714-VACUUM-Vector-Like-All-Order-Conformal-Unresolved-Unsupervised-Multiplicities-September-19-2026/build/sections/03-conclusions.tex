\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to train the instantons of the so-called two-loop
hadronization correction without retraining. We have not attempted to
quantify the Glauber modes of the interplay between generalized negative
event weights of boosted W boson production and Feynman integrals. These
results pave the way for neutrino physics before hadronization. The
implications for quantum computing are profound. It would be interesting
to combine this approach with Gaussian processes.
