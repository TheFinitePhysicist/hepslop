\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify resummed Feynman integrals. The
treatment of the symbol letters of the local significance is admittedly
heuristic. We hope this work inspires further research into
physics-informed dipole subtraction schemes. The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on conformal anomalous dimensions of Higgs
boson production in gluon fusion. Extending this framework to
dimensionally regularized attention maps is an exciting direction for
future work.
