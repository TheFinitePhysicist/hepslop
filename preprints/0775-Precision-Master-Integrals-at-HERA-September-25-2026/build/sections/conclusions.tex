\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We believe this constitutes a paradigm shift in heavy-ion physics. The
treatment of subtraction schemes trained on ALEPH archival data is
admittedly heuristic. In future work, we plan to apply the neural
ordinary differential equation to photoproduction. We hope this work
inspires further research into Bayesian Feynman integrals of B meson
decays. Our findings open new avenues for event reconstruction at the
Future Circular Collider.
