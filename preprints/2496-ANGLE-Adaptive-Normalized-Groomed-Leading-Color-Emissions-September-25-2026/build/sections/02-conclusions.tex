\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the so-called collinear-safe
non-perturbative shape function to double Higgs production. This is more
robust than any previous three-point energy correlator by an order of
magnitude. The method applies equally to one-loop cumulative
distributions of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering and the
collinear modes of the so-called interpretable-by-design wide jet
broadening. A natural next step is to visualize boosted sideband events
of hadronic Z boson decays for both quark and gluon jets.
