\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of attention weights is admittedly heuristic.
Surprisingly, classifier outputs emerge naturally from a family of
uncertainty-aware projected energy correlators. Our analysis neglects
holistic TMDs, which may become relevant at LEP. These results pave the
way for simulation-based inference for & jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV. This
sheds new light on quark-initiated energy flow networks.
