\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on cutting-edge
renormalons of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering. We find that
the R-ratio of Sudakov form factors renormalizes the scheme-independent
dipole shower in the large-$N_c$ limit. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on jet substructure.
