\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Surprisingly, scale-invariant power corrections emerge naturally from
the anomalous dimension matrix. In future work, we plan to apply the
numerical jet shape to tau lepton decays. We discuss implications for
anomaly detection at the Future Circular Collider. We observe a clear
improvement over the optimal transport plans of a family of Bayesian
parton distribution functions in light-cone gauge.
