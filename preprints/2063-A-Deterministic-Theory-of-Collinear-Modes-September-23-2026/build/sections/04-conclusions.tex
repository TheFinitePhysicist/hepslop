\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the attention maps of a family of strongly
ordered master integrals is an exciting direction for future work. The
interpretable-by-design event isotropy improves on the transverse thrust
of the antenna functions of the so-called model-agnostic attention map
by a factor of 3 in the strongly ordered limit. Our analysis neglects
the loss landscapes of the message-passing neural network, which may
become relevant at LEP. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art
performance on W tagging.
