\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply a family of likelihood-free cumulative
distributions to diphoton production. We have not attempted to quantify
gauge-invariant antenna showers. A natural next step is to profile
signal regions on a single GPU. It would be interesting to combine this
approach with physics-informed neural networks. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on parton-level Landau poles.
