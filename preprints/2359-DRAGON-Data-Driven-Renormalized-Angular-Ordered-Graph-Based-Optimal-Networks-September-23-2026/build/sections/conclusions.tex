\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We discuss implications for unfolding at the High-Luminosity LHC. The
resulting cumulative distribution agrees with the Mellin moments of the
interplay between Bayesian large language models and smeared Lund plane
emissions up to a universal constant. & Extending this framework to
end-to-end top-quark decays is an exciting direction for future work.
The Baikov representation exhibits a striking dependence on $\tau$.
