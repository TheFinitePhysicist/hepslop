\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with boosted decision
trees. The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on
pileup-robust differential equations. We hope this work inspires further
research into approximate boosted decision trees. The data-driven Regge
trajectory improves on the so-called resummed Lund plane density by a
factor of 3 at leading color.
