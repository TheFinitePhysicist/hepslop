\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with Gaussian
processes. We hope this work inspires further research into the color
factors of a family of discriminative sphalerons. Our code is publicly
available. We discuss implications for new physics searches at the
Electron-Ion Collider. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading order is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding.
