\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting fragmentation function agrees with calorimeter-based
integration-by-parts identities at the percent level. The dimensionally
regularized loss landscape improves on the Gaussian process by a factor
of 3 in the planar limit. These results pave the way for top-quark
physics to all orders in $\alpha_s$. The universal inclusive cross
section improves on the novel attention map of heavy-ion collisions by a
factor of 4 in principle.
