\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We comment on the extension to slicing methods trained on ALEPH archival
data. Our findings open new avenues for amplitude reduction at the
Electron-Ion Collider. Extending the method beyond
next-to-next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding. Compared to versatile
N-subjettiness ratios, our approach is more accurate by a factor of 3.
