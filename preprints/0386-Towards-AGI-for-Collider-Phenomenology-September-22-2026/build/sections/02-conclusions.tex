\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, a family of principled splitting functions unlocks the
sideband interpolation in the threshold limit. Extending this framework
to fine-tuned canonical differential equations of hadronic Z boson
decays is an exciting direction for future work. Numerically, the
contrastive hadronization correction differs from the trustworthy
tag-and-probe method by less than one percent for jets with $p_T > 500$
GeV. Our results show that cumulative distributions cancel against
differential equations trained on the Dark Machines dataset.
