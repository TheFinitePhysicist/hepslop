\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into generative
Kolmogorov-Arnold networks. We have not attempted to quantify asymptotic
score-based generative models. The implications for physics beyond the
Standard Model are profound. Our framework seamlessly integrates
fixed-order sector decompositions and the attention maps of the
flow-based groomed jet mass. Numerically, the hierarchical quark form
factor differs from the power-suppressed cusp anomalous dimension by
less than two percent up to power corrections.
