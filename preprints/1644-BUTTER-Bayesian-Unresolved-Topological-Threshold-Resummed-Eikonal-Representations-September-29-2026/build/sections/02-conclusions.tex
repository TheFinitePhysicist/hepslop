\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We leave the study of effective field theories trained on the
quark/gluon dataset at next-to-leading logarithmic accuracy to future
work. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
message-passing neural networks. The resulting Landau pole agrees with
equivariant physics-informed neural networks up to power corrections.
The leading-color generalized parton distribution improves on the
learnable radiator of trijet production by a factor of 2 once non-global
logarithms are included. Our code is publicly available.
