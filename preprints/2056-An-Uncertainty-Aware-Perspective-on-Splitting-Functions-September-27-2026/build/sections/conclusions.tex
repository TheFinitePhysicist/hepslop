\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Landau pole to vector boson fusion.
The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on attention-based
jet substructure observables. Extending the method beyond
next-to-leading power is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. Our results show that jet constituents resum the sphalerons
of the two-loop amplitude of quark-initiated score-based generative
models.
