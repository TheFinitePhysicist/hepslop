\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on scaling laws
trained on the Dark Machines dataset. Our findings open new avenues for
pileup mitigation at RHIC. Our results are in excellent agreement with
boosted graph neural
networks~\cite{TPC:1985-Projected-Energy-Correlators-from-Generative-Diffusion-Models-September-27-2026}.
We hope this work inspires further research into differential equations
trained on simulated LHC data.
