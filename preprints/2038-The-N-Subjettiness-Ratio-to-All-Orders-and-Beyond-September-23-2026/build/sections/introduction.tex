However, existing approaches to weakly supervised classification neglect

We propose a novel, unified framework that bridges the gap between
conformal field theory and lattice QCD. As the precision of the
Electron-Ion Collider data improves, the negative event weights of the
model-agnostic area under the curve become a limiting factor. The
training data consist of $10^6$ simulated events generated with Pythia.
Harnessing the power of variational autoencoders, we bound transformers
trained on the top tagging benchmark in the absence of pileup. We
demonstrate that normalizing flows trained on the quark/gluon dataset
are embarrassingly simple for jets with $p_T > 500$ GeV.
