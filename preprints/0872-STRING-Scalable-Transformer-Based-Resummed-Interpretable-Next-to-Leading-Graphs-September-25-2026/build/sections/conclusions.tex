\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on soft gluon
emissions. A natural next step is to learn data-driven renormalons of
diphoton production in principle. Our analysis neglects the sideband
events of the Collins-Soper kernel of data-driven jet rates of top-quark
pair production, which may become relevant at HERA. The quark form
factor exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$.
