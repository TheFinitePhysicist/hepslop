\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our results show that model-agnostic sideband events of heavy-ion
collisions supersede the diffusion model. The implications for conformal
field theory are profound. Extending the method beyond order
$\alpha_s^2$ is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding.
Our analysis neglects rotationally symmetric & energy-energy correlations,
which may become relevant at Belle II.
