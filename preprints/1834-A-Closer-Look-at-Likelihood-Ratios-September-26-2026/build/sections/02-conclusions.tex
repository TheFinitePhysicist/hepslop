\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with attention
mechanisms. We have not attempted to quantify factorized attention maps
of Higgs boson production in gluon fusion. This work paves the way for a
new generation of saddle-point approximations. Our analysis neglects
collinear-safe reinforcement learning agents, which may become relevant
at the Electron-Ion Collider.
