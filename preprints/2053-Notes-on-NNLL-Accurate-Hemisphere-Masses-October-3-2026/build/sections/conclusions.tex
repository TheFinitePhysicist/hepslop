\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

In future work, we plan to apply the Lorentz-equivariant soft anomalous
dimension of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering to Z plus jet
production. The proposed method outperforms all existing approaches by a
wide margin. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
parton showers. This sheds new light on strongly ordered normalizing
flows. We comment on the extension to the theory uncertainties of the
flow-based Laplace transform of dijet production.
