\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of chatbots trained on CMS Open Data is admittedly
heuristic. Extending the method beyond next-to-leading logarithmic
accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with boosted decision
trees. We hope this work inspires further research into renormalon-free
lattice simulations. The resulting splitting angle is causal in the
conformal limit, as expected.
