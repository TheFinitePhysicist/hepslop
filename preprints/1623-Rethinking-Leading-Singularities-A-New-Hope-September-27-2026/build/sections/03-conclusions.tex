\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the cut integrals of a family of robust jet
rates is an exciting direction for future work. The main limitation of
this approach is its reliance on foundation-scale unitarity methods. It
would be interesting to combine & this approach with graph neural
networks. Our findings open new avenues for new physics searches at the
LHC. This unlocks the full potential of bootstrap methods for model
discovery.
