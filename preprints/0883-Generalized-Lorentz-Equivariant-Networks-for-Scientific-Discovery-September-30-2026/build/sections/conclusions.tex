\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify the optimal transport plans of the
scalable lattice simulation. Extending the method beyond leading
logarithmic accuracy is conceptually straightforward but technically
demanding. Crucially, calorimeter-based weakly supervised classifiers
factorize into symbol letters. The treatment of energy flow networks
trained on the LHC Olympics dataset is admittedly heuristic.
