\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting transverse thrust agrees with K-factors beyond the eikonal
approximation. Extending the method beyond order $\alpha_s^3$ is
conceptually straightforward but technically demanding. We hope this
work inspires further research into projected energy correlators. A
natural next step is to bootstrap chatbots trained on ALEPH archival
data in the planar limit. The resulting Regge trajectory is monotonic
within statistical uncertainties, as expected.
