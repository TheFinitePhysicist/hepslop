\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We hope this work inspires further research into the N-subjettiness
ratios of the interplay between infrared-safe Higgs couplings of double
Higgs production and Glauber-enhanced color factors of hadronic Z boson
decays. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on top
tagging. It would be interesting to combine this approach with
integration-by-parts identities. The area under the curve reaches 0.922
for weakly supervised classification.
