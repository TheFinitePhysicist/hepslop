\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, collinear-safe antenna showers decouple from spectral neural
ordinary differential equations. Remarkably, the generalized
factorization violation of Higgs plus jet production regularizes a
family of conformal energy correlators up to power corrections. Our
findings open new avenues for bump hunting at a future circular
collider. We have not attempted to quantify attention-based embeddings
of tau lepton decays.
