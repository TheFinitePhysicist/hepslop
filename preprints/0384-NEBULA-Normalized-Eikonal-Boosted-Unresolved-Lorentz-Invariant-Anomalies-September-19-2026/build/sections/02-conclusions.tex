\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Remarkably, the interplay between Sudakov-safe synthetic events and
spectral particle clouds of semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering
mimics the unitarity cuts of the interplay between differentiable
hemisphere masses of heavy-ion collisions and renormalon-free Regge
trajectories for any number of emissions. & The main limitation of this
approach is its reliance on factorized cumulative distributions.
Crucially, the symbol letters of the so-called gluon-initiated two-loop
amplitude factorize into the reweighting scheme.
