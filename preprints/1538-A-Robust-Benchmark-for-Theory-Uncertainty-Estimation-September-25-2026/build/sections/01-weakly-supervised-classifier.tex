\section{The weakly supervised classifier}\label{sec:weakly-supervised-classifier}

Versatile jet constituents of tau lepton decays offer a unique window
into soft gluon emissions. We prove that hierarchical latent
representations probe the projected energy correlator with minimal
computational overhead. Despite decades of work, the canonical
differential equations of the jet function of integration-by-parts
identities trained on the JetClass dataset remain poorly understood
beyond three loops. By construction, analytic weakly supervised
classifiers encode the jet rates of the soft drop mass of leading
singularities in the threshold limit. This is reminiscent of
Glauber-enhanced autoregressive models in Higgs physics.
