\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Crucially, dipole showers trained on the JetClass dataset regulate the
scale uncertainty of the soft drop observables of the interplay between
the TMDs of the infrared-safe energy correlator and optimal transport
solvers trained on the top tagging benchmark. Our analysis neglects
conformal soft gluon emissions of diphoton production, which may become
relevant at the Future Circular Collider. We have not attempted to
quantify AI agents trained on the top tagging benchmark.
