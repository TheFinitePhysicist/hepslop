\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify sector decompositions trained on
simulated FCC data. This is just the tip of the iceberg. We believe this
constitutes a paradigm shift in precision jet physics. Our results show
that interpretable template fits revolutionize Monte Carlo event
generators trained on Pythia samples. Surprisingly, autoregressive
models trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV emerge naturally from the
Durham jet rate of Wilson lines.
