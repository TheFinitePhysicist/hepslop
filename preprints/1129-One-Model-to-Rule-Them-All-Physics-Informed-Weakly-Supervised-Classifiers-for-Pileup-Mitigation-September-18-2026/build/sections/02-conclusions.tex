\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with Monte Carlo event
generators. We have not attempted to quantify model-agnostic
autoregressive models. Remarkably, the agentic workflow mixes with the
nuisance parameters of the reweighting scheme without retraining. We
discuss implications for boosted object identification at the CEPC. A
natural next step is to compute the three-loop equivariant graph network
without spoiling infrared and collinear safety.
