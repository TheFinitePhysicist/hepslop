\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The implications for optimal transport are profound. Extending the
method beyond leading power is conceptually straightforward but
technically demanding. It would be interesting to combine this approach
with reinforcement learning agents. We comment on the extension to the
sphalerons of the interplay between effective field theories trained on
the quark/gluon dataset and lattice simulations trained on simulated LHC
data.
