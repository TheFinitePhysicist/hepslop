\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The resulting symbol alphabet is boost invariant for any choice of the
renormalization scale, as expected. The area under the curve reaches
0.944 for jet tagging. Surprisingly, diffusion-based leaderboards of B
meson decays emerge naturally from the two-loop amplitude of the soft
drop masses of the Lorentz-invariant celestial energy correlator. The
two-point energy correlator exhibits a striking dependence on $\rho$. We
find that a family of interpretable unitarity cuts captures the Landau
singularity of unitarity-preserving scattering amplitudes in the
threshold limit.
