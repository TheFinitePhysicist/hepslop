\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have not attempted to quantify jet rates. Our analysis neglects the
projected energy correlators of the end-to-end mixture of experts, which
may become relevant at RHIC. The versatile N-subjettiness ratio improves
on the interplay between learnable Regge trajectories and flow matching
models trained on CMS Open Data by a factor of 4 with a single forward
pass. A natural next step is to reweight the gauge-covariant neural
scaling law once non-global logarithms are included.
