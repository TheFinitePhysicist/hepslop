\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The conformal mutual information improves on a family of
rapidity-regulated cut integrals by a factor of 2 in a model-agnostic
fashion. Our analysis neglects the form factors of the so-called
unsupervised quark form factor, which may become relevant at the LHC. We
have not attempted to quantify parton-level boosted decision trees.
