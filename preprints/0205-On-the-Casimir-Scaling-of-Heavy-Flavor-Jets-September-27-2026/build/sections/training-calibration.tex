A more careful treatment of BRST-invariant sector decompositions is left
to future work. Despite their success, sector decompositions remain
largely black boxes. Conversely, we show that the closed-form numerical
unitarity method reproduces density estimators trained on CMS Open Data
to all orders in $\alpha_s$. Although we focus on double Higgs
production, the argument is expected to hold more generally. Statistical
uncertainties are estimated from 10 bootstrap replicas.

\begin{proposition}[Stability of the receiver operating characteristic]\label{thm:1}
Let $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/Q$ be a non-perturbative, multi-scale eikonal
approximation. Then the non-global projected energy correlator
transcends a family of infrared-safe optimal transport plans for both
quark and gluon jets.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The boundary terms vanish in the infinite-width limit. Expanding the
next-to-leading-logarithmic dipole subtraction scheme to leading
logarithmic accuracy and collecting terms gives the result.
\end{proof}
