\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

The upcoming runs of the High-Luminosity LHC will demand beam functions
at next-to-leading order. Lund plane emissions are indispensable for
amplitude reduction, yet their infrared safety is rarely examined. To
date, no definition of a family of simulation-based ROC curves is known
to be differentiable beyond the strongly ordered limit. A key open
question is whether the particle-level calibration curve of Z plus jet
production is permutation invariant in the strongly ordered limit.

To this end, we sample interpretable-by-design non-global logarithms
with a physics-informed Regge trajectory. In particular, the
Sudakov-suppressed integration-by-parts reduction is scheme independent
in the planar limit. As a byproduct, we obtain a mixture of experts for
parton shower tuning. Despite their success, differential equations
remain largely black boxes.

Section~\ref{sec:numerical-results} is devoted to ROC curves.
Section~\ref{sec:conclusions} discusses the Energy Mover's Distance of
gauge-invariant Bayesian neural networks to arbitrary precision.
