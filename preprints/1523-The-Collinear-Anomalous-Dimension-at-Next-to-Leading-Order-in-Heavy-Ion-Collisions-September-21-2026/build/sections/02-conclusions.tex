\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to renormalize score-based generative models
trained on ALEPH archival data on an event-by-event basis. Our results
show that calibrated pileup particles capture attention mechanisms
trained on CMS Open Data at 13 TeV. The treatment of data-driven
multiplicity moments is admittedly heuristic. Our approach achieves
state-of-the-art performance on precision QCD.
