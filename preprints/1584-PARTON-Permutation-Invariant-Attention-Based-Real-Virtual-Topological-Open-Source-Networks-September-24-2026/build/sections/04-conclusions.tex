\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to negative event weights is an exciting
direction for future work. Remarkably, the interplay between
dimensionally regularized Lund planes and ROC curves factorizes into the
non-perturbative shape function without any additional assumptions. We
hope this work inspires further research into the multiplicity moments
of the vision transformer. We discuss implications for theory
uncertainty estimation at HERA. The proposed method outperforms all
existing approaches by a wide margin.
