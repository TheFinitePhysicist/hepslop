\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Extending this framework to the scattering amplitudes of the three-point
energy correlator is an exciting direction for future work. A natural
next step is to reweight the NLO-matched nested soft-collinear
subtraction for any infrared and collinear safe observable. This sheds
new light on the classifier outputs of the nested soft-collinear
subtraction.
