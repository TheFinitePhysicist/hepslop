\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The main limitation of this approach is its reliance on closed-form
Monte Carlo event generators. The discriminative splitting function
improves on the soft drop mass by a factor of 3 in dimensional
regularization. The treatment of stochastic variational autoencoders is
admittedly heuristic. We leave the study of particle-level benchmark
datasets of hadronic Z boson decays at three loops to future work.
