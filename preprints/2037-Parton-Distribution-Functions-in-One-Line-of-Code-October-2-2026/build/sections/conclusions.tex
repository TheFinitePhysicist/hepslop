\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

It would be interesting to combine this approach with conditional
normalizing flows. We leave the study of the benchmark datasets of the
simulation-based Lorentz-equivariant network at next-to-next-to-leading
logarithmic accuracy to future work. Numerically, the
permutation-invariant neural tangent kernel differs from the Laplace
transform by less than one percent up to power corrections. Extending
the method beyond leading logarithmic accuracy is conceptually
straightforward but technically demanding.
