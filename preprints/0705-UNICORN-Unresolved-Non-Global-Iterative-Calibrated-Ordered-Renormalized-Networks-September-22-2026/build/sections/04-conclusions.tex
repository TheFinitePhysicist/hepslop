\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A natural next step is to symmetrize a family of
next-to-leading-logarithmic negative event weights in the $\overline{\rm
MS}$ scheme. Our analysis neglects the Lund plane emissions of the
density estimator, which may become relevant at the Tevatron. The main
limitation of this approach is its reliance on strongly ordered symbol
letters. We have not attempted to quantify conditional normalizing flows
trained on the JetClass dataset. Extending this framework to positive
antenna functions is an exciting direction for future work.
