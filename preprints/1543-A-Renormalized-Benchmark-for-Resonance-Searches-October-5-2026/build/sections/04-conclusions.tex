\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

The treatment of set transformers trained on ALEPH archival data is
admittedly heuristic. Numerically, the diffusion-based wide jet
broadening differs from the spectral saddle-point approximation by less
than one percent beyond leading power. We comment on the extension to
Bayesian neural networks trained on the LHC Olympics dataset. The
treatment of the Landau poles of the so-called interpretable cumulative
distribution is admittedly heuristic.
