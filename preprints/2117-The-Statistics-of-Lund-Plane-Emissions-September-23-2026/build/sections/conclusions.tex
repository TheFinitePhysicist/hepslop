\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

Our framework seamlessly integrates jet substructure observables and
reinforcement learning agents trained on Herwig samples. The treatment
of rapidity-regulated subtraction schemes is admittedly heuristic. It
would be interesting to combine this approach with self-supervised
objectives. We discuss implications for top tagging at the Electron-Ion
Collider. In future work, we plan to apply the interplay between
foundation-scale conditional normalizing flows and smeared effective
theories to vector boson fusion.
